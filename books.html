    Ajahn Sujin Boriharnwanaket Reference Library

    A Survey of Paramattha Dhammas A comprehensive and concise account of the Buddha's teachings on ultimate realities. The Perfections Leading to Enlightenment An exploration of the pāramīs and their development in daily life. Taking Refuge in Buddhism Focuses on building true faith through the understanding of the Dhamma. Mettā: Loving-kindness in Buddhism A direct approach to the practical application of mettā as a reality, not just words. Realities and Concepts Extracted from A Survey of Paramattha Dhammas, focusing on the distinction between truth and naming. Buddhism in Daily Life (Ajahn Sujin) Practical guidance on seeing dhammas in ordinary experience rather than special postures.

    Nina van Gorkom Reference Library

    Abhidhamma in Daily Life An essential introduction to the Abhidhamma, defining absolute realities and encouraging their recognition in ordinary experience. Cetasikas A detailed study of the 52 mental factors (cetasikas) that accompany the mind, helping identify defilements. Buddhism in Daily Life (Nina van Gorkom) Written in Thailand, this book explores how the Buddha's teachings on generosity, sincerity, and tolerance are lived.
