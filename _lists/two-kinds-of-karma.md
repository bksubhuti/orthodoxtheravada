### Overview
[Kamma](/glossary/kamma/) (Sanskrit: Karma) is driven by volition (*[cetanā](/glossary/cetana/)*). In orthodox Theravāda, all intentional actions of body, speech, and mind are fundamentally divided into two ethical categories based on the mental roots that produce them and the results (*vipāka*) they yield.

### The List
1. **Kusala-kamma** - Wholesome Action: Intentional action rooted in non-greed, non-hatred, and non-delusion. It generates pleasant results in this life or future lives, leading to fortunate rebirths and facilitating the path to awakening.
2. **Akusala-kamma** - Unwholesome Action: Intentional action rooted in greed (*[lobha](/glossary/lobha/)*), hatred (*[dosa](/glossary/dosa/)*), and delusion (*[moha](/glossary/moha/)*). It generates painful results, leading to suffering and rebirth in the lower realms.

### Quote
> **Lobho nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya, doso nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya, moho nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya. Yaṃ, bhikkhave, lobhapakataṃ kammaṃ lobhajaṃ lobhanidānaṃ lobhasamudayaṃ, taṃ kammaṃ akusalaṃ taṃ kammaṃ sāvajjaṃ taṃ kammaṃ dukkhavipākaṃ, taṃ kammaṃ kammasamudayāya saṃvattati, na taṃ kammaṃ kammanirodhāya saṃvattati. Alobho nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya, adoso nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya, amoho nidānaṃ kammānaṃ samudayāya. Yaṃ, bhikkhave, alobhapakataṃ kammaṃ alobhajaṃ alobhanidānaṃ alobhasamudayaṃ, taṃ kammaṃ kusalaṃ taṃ kammaṃ anavajjaṃ taṃ kammaṃ sukhavipākaṃ, taṃ kammaṃ kammanirodhāya saṃvattati, na taṃ kammaṃ kammasamudayāya saṃvattati.**  
> Greed is a cause for the origination of deeds, hatred is a cause for the origination of deeds, and delusion is a cause for the origination of deeds. Monks, whatever kamma is fashioned through greed, born of greed, caused by greed, and originated by greed—that kamma is the unwholesome, that kamma is blameable, that kamma has suffering as its fruit; that kamma leads to the origination of deeds, that kamma does not lead to the cessation of deeds. Non-greed is a cause for the origination of deeds, non-hatred is a cause for the origination of deeds, and non-delusion is a cause for the origination of deeds. Monks, whatever kamma is fashioned through non-greed, born of non-greed, caused by non-greed, and originated by non-greed—that kamma is the wholesome, that kamma is blameless, that kamma has happiness as its fruit; that kamma leads to the cessation of deeds, it does not lead to the origination of deeds.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Sammādiṭṭhi](/glossary/sammaditthi/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (MN 9) – Ven. Sāriputta defines right view as discerning the unwholesome and its root, the wholesome and its root.
* **Canonical**: *Paṭhamanidāna Sutta* (AN 3, A-iii 1053-1055) – Kamma born of greed, hatred, delusion is unwholesome with painful result; kamma born of non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion is wholesome with happy result.
* **Commentary**: *Aṭṭhasālinī* – Provides the exhaustive Abhidhammic breakdown of how consciousness generates these two kammic forces.
