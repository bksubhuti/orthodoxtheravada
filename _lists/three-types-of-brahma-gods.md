### Overview
When a meditator fully masters the First Fine-Material Absorption (*Paṭhama [Jhāna](/glossary/jhana/)*), the resulting [kamma](/glossary/kamma/) guarantees their rebirth in the highest celestial planes known as the [Brahma](/glossary/brahma/) Worlds (*Rūpa-loka*). The [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) divides the inhabitants of this first jhānic plane into three specific tiers, depending on whether their concentration during life was weak, medium, or superior.

### The List
1. **Brahmapārisajjā** - Devas of Brahma's Retinue: The lowest tier. Inhabited by practitioners who developed a basic, weak mastery of the First Jhāna. They act as the ordinary citizens and followers in the celestial court.
2. **Brahmapurohitā** - Devas of Brahma's Ministers: The intermediate tier. Inhabited by practitioners who developed a medium mastery of the First Jhāna. They function as advisers and officials to the great king.
3. **Mahābrahmā** - The Great Brahmas: The highest, most luminous tier of this plane. Inhabited by those who perfected the ultimate, superior mastery of the First Jhāna. They possess immense radiance and long lifespans, though they are still impermanent and subject to kamma.

### Quote
> **Paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ parittaṃ bhāvetvā brahmapārisajjānaṃ devānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti. Paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ majjhimaṃ bhāvetvā brahmapurohitānaṃ devānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti. Paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ paṇītaṃ bhāvetvā mahābrahmānaṃ devānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti.**  
> Having cultivated the first jhāna to a limited degree, they reappear in companionship with the Brahma-councilor devas. Having cultivated the first jhāna to a middle-standing degree, they reappear in companionship with the Brahma's minister devas. Having cultivated the first jhāna to an exalted degree, they reappear in companionship with the Great Brahma devas.  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Vibh 1024, Dhammahadayavibhaṅga, Uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavāra) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=439&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=UGHhua1oYW1h4bmDIGpoxIFuYeG5gyBwYXJpdHRh4bmDIGJoxIF2ZXR2xIEgYnJhaG1hcMSBcmlzYWpqxIFuYeG5gyBkZXbEgW5h4bmD)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)* (Vibh 1024, Vibh 3314:2-3316:2) – Weak, medium and exalted first jhāna lead to rebirth among the Brahmapārisajjā, Brahmapurohitā and Mahābrahmā devas respectively.
* **Ṭīkā-layer handbook**: *[Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)* (Abhidh-s 386:1, Chapter 5) – "Brahmapārisajjā brahmapurohitā mahābrahmā ceti paṭhamajjhānabhūmi": the three planes of the first-absorption sphere.
