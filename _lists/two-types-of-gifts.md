### Overview
Giving (*[dāna](/glossary/dana/)*) is the first of the Dukanipāta's chapter on gifts. The Buddha says: "Monks, there are these two kinds of gifts: the giving of material things and the gift of the Dhamma. Of these two kinds of gifts, the foremost is the gift of the Dhamma" (A-ii 248:1-5; the same in Iti 98, It 994:1-2). The Aṭṭhakathā explains that *dāna* is the thing that is given (*deyyadhamma*), or the volition that has an object, the name for the giving up of one's possessions (Mp-ii 264:1-2).

### The List
1. **Āmisadāna** - The Gift of Material Things: the giving of the four requisites, as things given (*cattāro paccayā diyyanakavasena*, Mp-ii 264:3).
2. **Dhammadāna** - The Gift of the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/): one who teaches and gives to others the practice that leads to the attainment of the Deathless (*amatapattipaṭipadaṃ kathetvā deti*, Mp-ii 264:4). It is the foremost of the two (A-ii 248:5).


### Quote
> **“Dvemāni, bhikkhave, dānāni. Katamāni dve? Āmisadānañca dhammadānañca. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, dve dānāni. Etadaggaṃ, bhikkhave, imesaṃ dvinnaṃ dānānaṃ yadidaṃ dhammadānan” ti.**  
> 142. “Monks, there are these two kinds of gifts. What are the two? The giving of material things and the gift of the Dhamma. These are the two kinds of gifts. The foremost of these two kinds of gifts is the gift of the Dhamma.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Dāna Sutta* (AN 2.142, A-ii 247:1 "3. Dānavaggo", para 142, A-ii 248:1-5); the same teaching in the *Itivuttaka* (Iti 98, It 994:1-2).
* **Canonical**: *[Dhammapada](/glossary/dhammapada/)* (Dhp 354, Dhp 1144:1) - "Sabbadānaṃ dhammadānaṃ jināti": the gift of Dhamma excels all gifts.
* **Commentary**: *Manorathapūraṇī* (Mp-ii 264:1-4).
