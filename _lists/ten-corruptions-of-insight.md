### Overview
During advanced *[vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/)* (insight) meditation, as the mind becomes highly concentrated and begins to see the rapid arising and passing away of phenomena, a practitioner will invariably experience profound, blissful side-effects. The danger is that the meditator will mistake these byproducts for [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). They are called "corruptions" (*[upakkilesa](/glossary/upakkilesa/)*) because attachment to them halts all spiritual progress.

### The List
1. **Obhāsa** - Illumination: Experiencing brilliant, blinding mental light or auras.
2. **Ñāṇa** - Knowledge: Experiencing extraordinarily sharp, rapid intellectual understanding of phenomena.
3. **[Pīti](/glossary/piti/)** - Joy / Rapture: Experiencing overwhelming physical thrill and ecstasy.
4. **[Passaddhi](/glossary/passaddhi/)** - Tranquility: Experiencing an impossibly deep, unshakeable calmness of body and mind.
5. **[Sukha](/glossary/sukha/)** - Bliss: Experiencing a profound, subtle happiness that surpasses all worldly pleasure.
6. **Adhimokkha** - Resolve / Extreme Faith: Experiencing a surging, unshakeable devotion and faith.
7. **Paggaha** - Exertion: Experiencing perfectly balanced, effortless energy that never tires.
8. **Upaṭṭhāna** - Assurance / Mindfulness: Experiencing perfectly sharp, unwavering mindfulness.
9. **[Upekkhā](/glossary/upekkha/)** - Equanimity: Experiencing supreme, effortless neutrality toward all objects.
10. **Nikanti** - Attachment: The actual subtle craving and delight that arises in response to the first nine experiences, causing the mind to stick to them.

### Quote
> **Kathaṃ dhammuddhaccaviggahitaṃ mānasaṃ hoti ? Aniccato manasikaroto obhāso uppajjati, obhāso dhammoti obhāsaṃ āvajjati, tato vikkhepo uddhaccaṃ. Aniccato manasikaroto ñāṇaṃ uppajjati, pīti uppajjati, passaddhi uppajjati, sukhaṃ uppajjati, adhimokkho uppajjati, paggaho uppajjati, upaṭṭhānaṃ uppajjati, upekkhā uppajjati, nikanti uppajjati,‘ nikanti dhammo ’ ti nikantiṃ āvajjati. Tato vikkhepo uddhaccaṃ.**  
> How is the heart seized by restlessness concerning the Dhamma? In one attending as inconstant, illumination arises. He adverts to the illumination thus: “Illumination is the Dhamma.” From that, distraction and restlessness arise. In one attending as inconstant, knowledge arises, rapture arises, tranquility arises, pleasure arises, decision arises, exertion arises, establishment arises, equanimity arises, attachment arises. He adverts to the attachment thus: “Attachment is the Dhamma.” From that, distraction and restlessness arise.  
>
