### Overview
The Buddha named two laywomen (*[Upāsikā](/glossary/upasika/)*), Khujjuttarā and Nandamātā of Veḷukaṇḍaka, as the scale and measure for his female lay disciples (A-ii 234:2).

### The List
1. **Khujjuttarā:** A female lay follower who served Queen Sāmāvatī as her attendant (Mp-i 1308:2). The Buddha declared her foremost among learned laywomen (A-i 248:1, *Bahussutā*); the Aṭṭhakathā says she taught five hundred women the Dhamma and established them in the fruit of stream-entry (Mp-i 1318:8).
2. **Nandamātā Veḷukaṇḍakī:** A female lay follower who established an offering endowed with six factors for the Saṅgha headed by Sāriputta and Moggallāna (A-vi 253:2).

### Quote
> **‘ tādisī homi yādisī khujjuttarā ca upāsikā veḷukaṇḍakiyā ca nandamātā ’ ti. Esā, bhikkhave, tulā etaṃ pamāṇaṃ mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ yadidaṃ khujjuttarā ca upāsikā veḷukaṇḍakiyā ca nandamātā” ti.**  
> ‘May I be such as the female lay follower Khujjuttarā and Nandamātā, Velukaṇḍaka’s mother!’ This is the scale, this is the measure for my female lay disciples, that is to say, Khujjuttarā the female lay follower and Nandamātā, Velukaṇḍaka’s mother.”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 2, Āyācanavagga, para 134) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_02&page_number=88&name=dukanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCYIHTEgWRpc8SrIGhvbWkgecSBZGlzxKsga2h1amp1dHRhcsSBIGNh)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Āyācanavagga* (AN 2, A-ii 234:2) – The scale and measure for female lay disciples: Khujjuttarā and Nandamātā of Veḷukaṇḍaka.
* **Canonical**: *Buddhavaṃsa* (Bv 3145:1) - "Nandamātā ca uttarā, aggupaṭṭhikupāsikā" (Nanda's mother and Uttarā were the chief female lay attendants). This is the same pair as AN 2: Uttarā is Khujjuttarā, whose name is Uttarā; she was called *khujjā* ("hunchbacked") because she was born so (*sā jātakāle khujjā ahosīti khujjuttarā tveva paññāyittha*, It-a 147:7), and the garland-maker addresses her as "amma uttare" (Mp-i 1308:3). The Buddhavaṃsa-aṭṭhakathā repeats the same two names for the prediction of the earlier Buddhas, "Uttarā nandamātā ca, aggā hessantupaṭṭhikā" (Bv-a 1058:1), with *ca* joining the two women.
* **Not the same text**: the *Uttarā, Nanda's mother* of the Etadagga (A-i 250:1, "Jhāyīnaṃ yadidaṃ uttarānandamātā", foremost of the meditators) is a woman of Rājagaha, daughter of Puṇṇa's household, whose story is in Mp-i 1319:1-1334:3; the Nandamātā of AN 2 and AN 6 is "of Veḷukaṇḍaka" (A-vi 253:2; Mp-vi 599:1), a Non-returner who knew the three Piṭakas (Pj-ii 1639:2-3). The commentaries do not say that they are one person, so this page does not identify them.
* **Canonical**: *Etadagga Vagga* (AN 1, A-i 247:1, 248:1) – Visākhā Migāramātā is foremost among givers, Khujjuttarā among the learned. Khujjuttarā's story is in the Manorathapūraṇī (Mp-i 1265:1 "Khujjuttarā-sāmāvatīvatthu" to 1318:8) and the Itivuttaka commentary (It-a 146:4-153:4).
