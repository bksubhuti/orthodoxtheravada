### Overview
The Analytical Knowledges (*Paṭisambhidā*) represent the pinnacle of scholarly and experiential wisdom. While an ordinary [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/) possesses the wisdom to end suffering, an Arahat endowed with these four analytical knowledges possesses an unmatched, piercing comprehension of doctrine, causality, language, and teaching methodology. Great disciples like Venerable Sāriputta were masters of these knowledges.

### The List
1. **Atthapaṭisambhidā** - Analytical Knowledge of Meaning / Result: The precise understanding of the meaning of words, the results of causes ([kamma](/glossary/kamma/)), and the exact nature of phenomena that have been produced by conditions.
2. **Dhammapaṭisambhidā** - Analytical Knowledge of Doctrine / Cause: The precise understanding of the teaching, the causes that produce results, and the Noble Path.
3. **Niruttipaṭisambhidā** - Analytical Knowledge of Language: The perfect mastery of the original grammatical structure and etymology of the Pāḷi language, allowing for flawless communication of the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/).
4. **Paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā** - Analytical Knowledge of Perspicuity: The overarching, spontaneous insight into the previous three knowledges, allowing the teacher to instantly answer any question and synthesize concepts effortlessly.

> ## vsm du p 70
> **dutiyacatukke catasso paṭisambhidā nāma atthādīsu pabhedagatāni cattāri ñāṇāni.**  
>In the second quartet, the fourfold analytical knowledges (paṭisambhidā) are four kinds of knowledge related to the distinctions in meaning (attha), and so on.   
>**vuttañhetaṃ – ‘‘atthe ñāṇaṃ atthapaṭisambhidā.**  
>It has been said: "Knowledge of meaning is attha-paṭisambhidā.  
>**dhamme ñāṇaṃ dhammapaṭisambhidā.**   
>Knowledge of Dhamma is dhamma-paṭisambhidā.   
>**tatradhammaniruttābhilāpe ñāṇaṃ niruttipaṭisambhidā.**   
>nowledge of the expression in language for that Dhamma is nirutti-paṭisambhidā.   
>**ñāṇesu ñāṇaṃ paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā’’ti (vibha. 718).**   
>Knowledge of these knowledges is paṭibhāna-paṭisambhidā."
