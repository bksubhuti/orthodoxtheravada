### Overview
The sixth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes all consciousness based on the presence of two specific mental factors: Applied thought (*[Vitakka](/glossary/vitakka/)*, the initial mounting of the mind onto an object) and Sustained thought (*[Vicāra](/glossary/vicara/)*, the continued stroking or anchoring on that object). The Aṭṭhakathā explains the terms at As 856:1-7.

### The List
1. **Savitakkasavicārā dhammā** - States with applied and sustained thought: the states in the plane with both (*savitakkasavicārabhūmi*) of the sense sphere, the fine-material sphere and the supramundane, with the vitakka and vicāra themselves excluded (Dhs 1768:2-3). *Savitakkā* = occurring together with vitakka; *savicārā* = with vicāra (As 856:1-3).
2. **Avitakkavicāramattā dhammā** - States without applied thought but with sustained thought only: the states in the plane of vicāra-only (*avitakkavicāramattabhūmi*) of the fine-material sphere and the supramundane, with the vicāra itself excluded (Dhs 1769:2-3). *Vicāramattā*: of vitakka and vicāra, only vicāra is the measure; they do not go beyond vicāra in association with vitakka (As 856:5-7).
3. **Avitakkaavicārā dhammā** - States without applied or sustained thought: the states in the plane of neither (*avitakkaavicārabhūmi*) of the sense, fine-material, immaterial and supramundane spheres, together with all matter and the unconditioned element ([Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/)) (Dhs 1770:2-4). Those without both are *avitakkaavicārā* (As 856:4).

### Quote
> **(Ka) savitakkasavicārā dhammā. (Kha) avitakkavicāramattā dhammā. (Ga) avitakkaavicārā dhammā.**  
> 6.(Ka) Mental phenomena with applied thought and sustained thought. (Kha) Mental phenomena with only sustained thought, without applied thought. (Ga) Mental phenomena without applied thought and without sustained thought.  
>
