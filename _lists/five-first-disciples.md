### Overview
The *Pañcavaggiyā* (The Group of Five Monks) were the ascetic companions who attended the Bodhisatta while he strove (*padhānapahitattaṃ upaṭṭhahiṃsu*, Vin-iii 116:5). When he gave up severe austerity they thought of him as one who had turned to abundance and abandoned the striving (*bāhulliko padhānavibbhanto*, M-i 972:4). After his awakening the Buddha went to the Deer Park at Isipatana and taught them first (Vin-iii 116:6, 132:1).

### The List
1. **Aññāsikoṇḍañña:** The first to see the Dhamma when the Wheel of Dhamma was set in motion (Vin-iii 142:1). The Buddha's udāna "aññāsi vata, bho koṇḍañño" gave him the name Aññāsikoṇḍañña (Vin-iii 143:9-10). In the Aṭṭhakathā he is the brahmin youth, the youngest of those who read the marks of the newborn Bodhisatta, who declared that he would surely become a Buddha (Ap-a 731:5).
2. **Vappa:** Taught by the Buddha after Koṇḍañña's ordination, he and Bhaddiya received the Dhamma-eye together and were ordained with "ehi bhikkhu" (Vin-iii 145:1-146:5).
3. **Bhaddiya:** As with Vappa (Vin-iii 145:2).
4. **Mahānāma:** He and Assaji received the Dhamma-eye together, after Vappa and Bhaddiya, while the three monks went on alms for all six (Vin-iii 147:1-8).
5. **Assaji:** Later, seen on alms round in Rājagaha by Sāriputta, whom his pleasing manner impressed (Vin-iii 305:5).

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Dhammacakkappavattana [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (SN 56.11) – The first sermon delivered to these five (S-v 2474, "1. Dhammacakkappavattanasuttaṃ"). Only Koṇḍañña is named in it; the other four names are in the Vinaya.
* **Canonical**: *Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta* (SN 22.59, S-iii 322) – The second sermon. The Vinaya Mahāvagga's account of it ends with the minds of the five monks freed from the taints (Vin-iii 153:3).
* **Canonical**: Vinaya Mahāvagga (Vin-iii 132-147) names Koṇḍañña, Vappa and Bhaddiya, then Mahānāma and Assaji, in the order of their attainment.
