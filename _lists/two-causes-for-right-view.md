### Overview
Right View (*[Sammādiṭṭhi](/glossary/sammaditthi/)*) is the forerunner of the entire Noble Eightfold Path. In the Mahāvedalla Sutta, Ven. Sāriputta, answering Ven. Mahākoṭṭhita, names the two conditions for its arising (M-i 1515:1). The Papañcasūdanī explains that this right view is both insight right view and path right view (Ps-i 2635:1).

### The List
1. **Parato ghoso** - The Voice of Another: Listening to the beneficial [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) from another (*sappāyadhammassavanaṃ*, Ps-i 2635:2). The Aṭṭhakathā notes that Paccekabuddhas and Omniscient Buddhas have no such condition (Ps-i 2635:7).
2. **Yoniso manasikāro** - Wise Attention: One's own careful attention (*attano upāyamanasikāro*, Ps-i 2635:3).

### Quote
> **“Dve kho, āvuso, paccayā sammādiṭṭhiyā uppādāya – parato ca ghoso, yoniso ca manasikāro. Ime kho, āvuso, dve paccayā sammādiṭṭhiyā uppādāyā” ti.**  
> “There are two conditions, friend, for the arising of right view: the sound from another and rational application of mind.” “These are the two conditions, friend, for the arising of right view.”  
>
