### Overview
The sixteen knowledges of insight are a traditional arrangement of the stages of *[vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/)* (insight) from the Visuddhimagga and the commentaries. No text in TPR counts them as a single list of sixteen (searches for *soḷasa vipassanāñāṇa* and *soḷasavidhañāṇa*: 0 sentences in all layers); each of the sixteen names is found in the Aṭṭhakathā, and the commentaries group the middle ones as eight knowledges plus a ninth, the conformity knowledge (*aṭṭhannaṃ ñāṇānaṃ vasena … navamañca saccānulomikañāṇaṃ*), which is called the purification of knowledge and vision of the practice (Vism-ii 1653:1-3). Insight that has reached its peak ends in the change-of-lineage knowledge, which is the conclusion of the insight leading to emergence (*vuṭṭhānagāminī vipassanā*, Vism-ii 1860:1-2).

### The List
1. **Nāmarūpapariccheda-ñāṇa** - Knowledge defining mentality-materiality, the beginning of "the knowledge and vision of things as they really are", which is incipient insight (*nāmarūpaparicchedañāṇaṃ ādiṃ katvā taruṇavipassanā*, Mp-v 47:2). The commentaries also call it *nāmarūpavavatthānañāṇa* (Paṭis-a 179:1).
2. **Paccayapariggaha-ñāṇa** - Knowledge discerning the conditions of mentality-materiality (Vism-ii 1447:1); the Paṭisambhidāmagga calls it *dhammaṭṭhitiñāṇa* (Paṭis 9:1).
3. **Sammasana-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of comprehension: "the understanding that defines past, future and present states in brief is knowledge of comprehension" (Paṭis 10:1).
4. **Udayabbayānupassanā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of contemplation of arising and passing away (Vism-ii 1653:2).
5. **Bhaṅgānupassanā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of contemplation of dissolution (Vism-ii 1653:2).
6. **Bhayatupaṭṭhāna-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of the appearance of terror (Vism-ii 1653:2).
7. **Ādīnavānupassanā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of contemplation of danger (Vism-ii 1653:2).
8. **Nibbidānupassanā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of contemplation of disenchantment (Vism-ii 1653:2).
9. **Muñcitukamyatā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of the desire for deliverance (Vism-ii 1653:2).
10. **Paṭisaṅkhānupassanā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of contemplation of reflection (Vism-ii 1653:2).
11. **Saṅkhārupekkhā-ñāṇa** - Knowledge of equanimity about formations (Vism-ii 1653:2).
12. **Anuloma-ñāṇa** - Conformity knowledge, "the ninth, the knowledge in conformity with the truths" (Vism-ii 1653:3); it is the conclusion of insight that takes formations as its object (Vism-ii 1860:1).
13. **Gotrabhū-ñāṇa** - Change-of-lineage knowledge, the conclusion of the insight leading to emergence (Vism-ii 1860:2).
14. **Magga-ñāṇa** - Path knowledge.
15. **Phala-ñāṇa** - Fruition knowledge. In the Visuddhimagga's simile of the man who escapes a burning house, the knowledge of the desire for deliverance is looking for the way out, conformity is seeing the way, change-of-lineage is leaving the house, path knowledge is running away fast and fruition knowledge is standing in a safe place (Vism-ii 1827:1-3).
16. **Paccavekkhaṇa-ñāṇa** - Reviewing knowledge: the reviews are nineteen in all, fifteen for the three trainees and four for the Arahant, who has no review of remaining defilements (Abhidh-s-t 1781:8).


### Quote
> **Aṭṭhannaṃ pana ñāṇānaṃ vasena sikhāppattā vipassanā, navamañca saccānulomikañāṇanti ayaṃ paṭipadāñāṇadassanavisuddhi nāma. Aṭṭhannanti cettha upakkilesavimuttaṃ vīthipaṭipannavipassanāsaṅkhātaṃ udayabbayānupassanāñāṇaṃ, bhaṅgānupassanāñāṇaṃ, bhayatupaṭṭhānañāṇaṃ, ādīnavānupassanāñāṇaṃ, nibbidānupassanāñāṇaṃ, muñcitukamyatāñāṇaṃ, paṭisaṅkhānupassanāñāṇaṃ, saṅkhārupekkhāñāṇanti imāni aṭṭha ñāṇāni veditabbāni. Navamaṃ saccānulomikañāṇanti anulomassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ.**  
> 737. Now, insight reaches its culmination with the eight knowledges, and knowledge in conformity with truth is ninth; these are what is called purification by knowledge and vision of the way. “The eight” should be understood as follows: (1) knowledge of contemplation of rise and fall, which is insight free from imperfections and steady on its course, (2) knowledge of contemplation of dissolution, (3) knowledge of appearance as terror, (4) knowledge of contemplation of danger, (5) knowledge of contemplation of dispassion, (6) knowledge of desire for deliverance, (7) knowledge of contemplation of reflection, and (8) knowledge of equanimity about formations. “The ninth, knowledge in conformity with truth” is a name for conformity.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Paṭisambhidāmagga* (Paṭis 9:1-10:1, an early list of knowledges with its own names: *dhammaṭṭhitiñāṇa*, *sammasane ñāṇa*, *udayabbayānupassane ñāṇa*, and so on).
* **Commentary**: *Visuddhimagga* (Vism-ii 1447:1, 1653:1-3, 1827:1-3, 1860:1-2); *Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā* (Paṭis-a 179:1); *Manorathapūraṇī* (Mp-v 47:2); *Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī* (Abhidh-s-t 1781:8). The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha counts ten insight knowledges (sammasana to anuloma), a different arrangement (Abhidh-s 815:1).
