### Overview
As a meditator engages in strict *vipassanā* (insight) meditation, their realization does not happen all at once. According to the Mahāvihāra tradition, the mind passes through a highly predictable, standardized sequence of sixteen distinct experiential knowledges (*ñāṇa*), charting the exact psychological journey from mundane observation to the supramundane realization of Nibbāna.

### The List
1. **Nāmarūpapariccheda-ñāṇa:** Knowledge defining mentality-materiality.
2. **Paccayapariggaha-ñāṇa:** Knowledge discerning the conditions/causes of mentality-materiality.
3. **Sammasana-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of comprehending the three characteristics (impermanence, suffering, non-self).
4. **Udayabbaya-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of arising and passing away (the crucial turning point of insight).
5. **Bhaṅga-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of dissolution (seeing only the vanishing of phenomena).
6. **Bhaya-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of terror/fear (seeing the dissolving aggregates as dangerous).
7. **Ādīnava-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of danger/disadvantage.
8. **Nibbidā-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of revulsion/disenchantment toward all formations.
9. **Muñcitukamyatā-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of the desire for deliverance.
10. **Paṭisaṅkhā-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of reflective contemplation (re-evaluating the 3 characteristics to escape).
11. **Saṅkhārupekkhā-ñāṇa:** Knowledge of equanimity toward all formations (perfect, fearless neutrality).
12. **Anuloma-ñāṇa:** Conformity knowledge (the mind aligns completely with truth).
13. **Gotrabhū-ñāṇa:** Change-of-lineage knowledge (the mind leaves the worldly lineage and strikes Nibbāna for the first time).
14. **Magga-ñāṇa:** Path knowledge (the supramundane flash that destroys fetters).
15. **Phala-ñāṇa:** Fruition knowledge (the peaceful result of the path).
16. **Paccavekkhaṇa-ñāṇa:** Reviewing knowledge (the subsequent intellectual review of what was just attained and the defilements abandoned).

### Textual References
* **Commentary**: *Visuddhimagga* (Chapters XVIII–XXII) – Buddhaghosa's magnus opus details the exact emotional and cognitive markers of every single step on this list.
