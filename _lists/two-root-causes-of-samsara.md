### Overview
While the cycle of dependent origination (*[paṭiccasamuppāda](/glossary/paticcasamuppada/)*) has twelve links, orthodox [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) and commentarial methods often isolate these two specific links as the primary "roots" (*mūla*) of the entire wheel of suffering. They are the twin engines driving rebirth and kammic accumulation.

### The List
1. **[Avijjā](/glossary/avijja/)** - Ignorance: The fundamental inability to see the Four Noble Truths. It acts as the root from the past that drives present conditioning, blinding beings to the reality of their suffering.
2. **[Taṇhā](/glossary/tanha/)** - Craving: The unquenchable thirst for sensual pleasure, existence, and non-existence. It acts as the root in the present that guarantees future rebirth, binding beings to the wheel of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).

### Quote
> **Pubbā koṭi na paññāyati avijjāyaca bhavataṇhāya ca, tattha avijjānīvaraṇaṃ taṇhāsaṃyojanaṃ.**  
> No past term is evident of ignorance and of craving in regard to the continuation of existence. Herein, ignorance is the hindrance and craving the fetter.  
>  
> — [Nettippakaraṇa](/glossary/nettippakarana/) (Nett 764:2, Nayasamuṭṭhāna) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_20&page_number=91&name=nettippakara%E1%B9%87ap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=UHViYsSBIGtv4bmtaSBuYSBwYcOxw7HEgXlhdGkgYXZpamrEgXlhY2EgYmhhdmF0YeG5h2jEgXlh)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Nettippakaraṇa* (Nett 764:2-4) – Names ignorance and craving for existence together: no first point of either is evident; ignorance is the hindrance (*nīvaraṇa*) and craving the fetter (*saṃyojana*).
* **Canonical**: *Paṭhamabhava Sutta* (AN 3, A-iii 832-834, CST para 77) – Kamma is the field, consciousness the seed, craving the moisture; consciousness of beings hindered by ignorance and fettered by craving is established, and renewed existence arises.
* **Canonical**: *Paṭisambhidāmagga* (Paṭis 174:2) and *Dīgha Nikāya* (D-iii 1308:1) – “Avijjā ca bhavataṇhā ca” as a pair.
* **Commentary**: *Dīghanikāya ṭīkā* (Sv-pt-ii 363:3) – “vaṭṭamūlaṃ avijjā taṇhā”: ignorance and craving are the root of the round.
