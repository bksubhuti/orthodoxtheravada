### Overview
While the cycle of dependent origination (*[paṭiccasamuppāda](/glossary/paticcasamuppada/)*) has twelve links, orthodox [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) and commentarial methods often isolate these two specific links as the primary "roots" (*mūla*) of the entire wheel of suffering. They are the twin engines driving rebirth and kammic accumulation.

### The List
1. **[Avijjā](/glossary/avijja/)** - Ignorance: The fundamental inability to see the Four Noble Truths. It acts as the root from the past that drives present conditioning, blinding beings to the reality of their suffering.
2. **[Taṇhā](/glossary/tanha/)** - Craving: The unquenchable thirst for sensual pleasure, existence, and non-existence. It acts as the root in the present that guarantees future rebirth, binding beings to the wheel of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Bhava](/glossary/bhava/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 3.76) – Identifies ignorance as the covering and craving as the fetter that causes beings to endlessly wander.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XVII) – Explicitly designates ignorance and craving as the two outstanding roots of the round of rebirths.
