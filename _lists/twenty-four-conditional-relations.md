### Overview
The *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)* (the "Great Book", *mahāpakaraṇa*) is the last of the seven books of the Abhidhamma in the Aṭṭhasālinī's list (As 113:2-4). It teaches twenty-four conditions (*catuvīsati paccayā*, As 932:3; Abhidh-s 740:1). A condition is defined as that which has the characteristic of being helpful: a phenomenon that is helpful to the persistence or the arising of another is called its condition (Paṭṭh-a 1918:1). The Aṭṭhasālinī says that when the Buddha contemplated the Mahāpakaraṇa with its twenty-four universal conditions, his omniscient knowledge found its full scope, as a great fish finds room only in the great ocean, and six-coloured rays came out of his body (As 113:4-5, 114:1).

### The List
Each condition is explained in the Paṭṭhāna commentary (Paṭṭh-a 1917:1-1944:1) as a phenomenon that is helpful in a particular way:
1. **[Hetu-paccaya](/glossary/hetu-paccaya/)** - Root condition (Paṭṭh-a 1917:1).
2. **[Ārammaṇa-paccaya](/glossary/arammana-paccaya/)** - Object condition: helpful by way of being the object (Paṭṭh-a 1920:1).
3. **[Adhipati-paccaya](/glossary/adhipati-paccaya/)** - Predominance condition: helpful in the sense of being the chief (Paṭṭh-a 1921:1).
4. **[Anantara-paccaya](/glossary/anantara-paccaya/)** - Immediate contiguity condition: helpful by way of immediate succession (Paṭṭh-a 1922:1).
5. **[Samanantara-paccaya](/glossary/samanantara-paccaya/)** - Proximity condition: "whichever is the anantara condition, that very one is the samanantara condition" (Paṭṭh-a 1923:1); the two are one thing under two names.
6. **[Sahajāta-paccaya](/glossary/sahajata-paccaya/)** - Conascence condition: helpful by arising together, like a lamp to its light (Paṭṭh-a 1924:1).
7. **[Aññamañña-paccaya](/glossary/annamanna-paccaya/)** - Mutuality condition: helpful by mutually producing and supporting (Paṭṭh-a 1925:1).
8. **[Nissaya-paccaya](/glossary/nissaya-paccaya/)** - Support (dependence) condition: helpful as a foundation or support, as the earth is for trees or paintings (Paṭṭh-a 1926:1).
9. **[Upanissaya-paccaya](/glossary/upanissaya-paccaya/)** - Decisive support condition: a phenomenon that depends on another for its occurrence; the commentary distinguishes, among others, immediate decisive support (*anantarūpanissaya*) and natural decisive support (*pakatūpanissaya*) (Paṭṭh-a 1927:1-1929:1).
10. **[Purejāta-paccaya](/glossary/purejata-paccaya/)** - Prenascence condition: helpful by having arisen first and persisting (Paṭṭh-a 1930:1).
11. **[Pacchājāta-paccaya](/glossary/pacchajata-paccaya/)** - Postnascence condition: immaterial phenomena that arise later and support the material phenomena that arose before, as the craving for food of young vultures supports their bodies (Paṭṭh-a 1931:1).
12. **[Āsevana-paccaya](/glossary/asevana-paccaya/)** - Repetition condition: helpful by repetition, making the following phenomena skilled and strong, like repeated study of a text (Paṭṭh-a 1932:1).
13. **[Kamma-paccaya](/glossary/kamma-paccaya/)** - [Kamma](/glossary/kamma/) condition: helpful by the activity that is the effort of the mind (Paṭṭh-a 1933:1).
14. **[Vipāka-paccaya](/glossary/vipaka-paccaya/)** - Result condition: resultant phenomena helpful by their quiet and effortless nature (Paṭṭh-a 1934:1).
15. **[Āhāra-paccaya](/glossary/ahara-paccaya/)** - Nutriment condition: the four nutriments, helpful by supporting material and immaterial phenomena (Paṭṭh-a 1935:1).
16. **[Indriya-paccaya](/glossary/indriya-paccaya/)** - Faculty condition: the twenty faculties other than the female and male faculties, helpful by way of predominance (Paṭṭh-a 1936:1).
17. **[Jhāna-paccaya](/glossary/jhana-paccaya/)** - [Jhāna](/glossary/jhana/) condition: the seven jhāna factors, helpful by closely contemplating the object (Paṭṭh-a 1937:1).
18. **[Magga-paccaya](/glossary/magga-paccaya/)** - Path condition: the twelve path factors, helpful as the way out (Paṭṭh-a 1938:1).
19. **[Sampayutta-paccaya](/glossary/sampayutta-paccaya/)** - Association condition: immaterial phenomena that are helpful by association, having one base, one object, one arising and one cessation (Paṭṭh-a 1939:1).
20. **[Vippayutta-paccaya](/glossary/vippayutta-paccaya/)** - Dissociation condition: material phenomena for the immaterial and the immaterial for the material, without that sharing (Paṭṭh-a 1940:1).
21. **[Atthi-paccaya](/glossary/atthi-paccaya/)** - Presence condition: helpful by being present, supporting a like phenomenon (Paṭṭh-a 1941:1).
22. **[Natthi-paccaya](/glossary/natthi-paccaya/)** - Absence condition: immaterial phenomena that have just ceased, helpful by giving room for the next immaterial phenomena to arise (Paṭṭh-a 1942:1).
23. **[Vigata-paccaya](/glossary/vigata-paccaya/)** - Disappearance condition: the same phenomena, helpful by having disappeared (Paṭṭh-a 1943:1).
24. **[Avigata-paccaya](/glossary/avigata-paccaya/)** - Non-disappearance condition: the presence-condition phenomena themselves, helpful by not having disappeared (Paṭṭh-a 1944:1).
