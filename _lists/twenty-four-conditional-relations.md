### Overview
The *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)* is the seventh and final book of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Piṭaka. It represents the absolute pinnacle of Buddhist philosophy. While Dependent Origination shows *what* arises, the 24 Conditions (*Paccaya*) explain the exact mathematical physics of *how* phenomena interact, support, and condition one another. It is the Buddhist unified theory of causality.

### The List
1. **[Hetu-paccaya](/glossary/hetu-paccaya/)** - Root condition (greed, non-greed, etc.).
2. **[Ārammaṇa-paccaya](/glossary/arammana-paccaya/)** - Object condition (sight, sound, etc.).
3. **[Adhipati-paccaya](/glossary/adhipati-paccaya/)** - Predominance condition.
4. **[Anantara-paccaya](/glossary/anantara-paccaya/)** - Proximity condition.
5. **[Samanantara-paccaya](/glossary/samanantara-paccaya/)** - Contiguity condition.
6. **[Sahajāta-paccaya](/glossary/sahajata-paccaya/)** - Conascence (arising together) condition.
7. **[Aññamañña-paccaya](/glossary/annamanna-paccaya/)** - Mutuality condition.
8. **[Nissaya-paccaya](/glossary/nissaya-paccaya/)** - Support condition.
9. **[Upanissaya-paccaya](/glossary/upanissaya-paccaya/)** - Decisive support condition.
10. **[Purejāta-paccaya](/glossary/purejata-paccaya/)** - Prenascence (arising before) condition.
11. **[Pacchājāta-paccaya](/glossary/pacchajata-paccaya/)** - Postnascence (arising after) condition.
12. **[Āsevana-paccaya](/glossary/asevana-paccaya/)** - Repetition condition.
13. **[Kamma-paccaya](/glossary/kamma-paccaya/)** - [Kamma](/glossary/kamma/) condition.
14. **[Vipāka-paccaya](/glossary/vipaka-paccaya/)** - Result condition.
15. **[Āhāra-paccaya](/glossary/ahara-paccaya/)** - Nutriment condition.
16. **[Indriya-paccaya](/glossary/indriya-paccaya/)** - Faculty condition.
17. **[Jhāna-paccaya](/glossary/jhana-paccaya/)** - [Jhāna](/glossary/jhana/) condition.
18. **[Magga-paccaya](/glossary/magga-paccaya/)** - Path condition.
19. **[Sampayutta-paccaya](/glossary/sampayutta-paccaya/)** - Association condition.
20. **[Vippayutta-paccaya](/glossary/vippayutta-paccaya/)** - Dissociation condition.
21. **[Atthi-paccaya](/glossary/atthi-paccaya/)** - Presence condition.
22. **[Natthi-paccaya](/glossary/natthi-paccaya/)** - Absence condition.
23. **[Vigata-paccaya](/glossary/vigata-paccaya/)** - Disappearance condition.
24. **[Avigata-paccaya](/glossary/avigata-paccaya/)** - Non-disappearance condition.

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *Paṭṭhāna* – A massive, multi-volume text mapping every conceivable interaction of these 24 conditions. Tradition states the Buddha's body emitted six-colored rays of light while contemplating this matrix.
