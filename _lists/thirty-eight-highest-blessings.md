### Overview
In ancient India, people endlessly debated what constituted a true "blessing" or "omen of good fortune" (*maṅgala*), often pointing to sights, sounds, or rituals. A deity approached the Buddha to resolve the debate. The Buddha redefined blessings entirely, laying out 38 progressive steps of moral and spiritual development, starting from basic social ethics and culminating in the realization of [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/).

### The List
*(From the verses 3-12 of the Maṅgala Sutta, Khp 54-82; the Paramatthajotikā counts them as 38, Pj-i 767:1: "dasahi gāthāhi aṭṭhatiṃsa mahāmaṅgalāni")*
1. **Asevanā ca bālānaṃ** - Not consorting with the foolish.
2. **Paṇḍitānañca sevanā** - Consorting with the wise.
3. **Pūjā ca pūjaneyyānaṃ** - Paying homage to those worthy of homage.
4. **Patirūpadesavāso ca** - Living in a suitable place.
5. **Pubbe ca katapuññatā** - Having previously made merit.
6. **Attasammāpaṇidhi ca** - Directing oneself rightly.
7. **Bāhusaccañca** - Ample learning.
8. **Sippañca** - Skill.
9. **Vinayo ca susikkhito** - A well-trained Vinaya (discipline).
10. **Subhāsitā ca yā vācā** - Well-spoken words.
11. **Mātāpitu upaṭṭhānaṃ** - Support for mother and father.
12. **Puttadārassa saṅgaho** - The inclusion (support) of wife and children.
13. **Anākulā ca kammantā** - Peaceful occupations.
14. **Dānañca** - Giving.
15. **Dhammacariyā ca** - Righteous conduct.
16. **Ñātakānañca saṅgaho** - The inclusion (support) of relatives.
17. **Anavajjāni kammāni** - Blameless actions.
18. **Ārati** - Non-delight in evil, through the mind alone (Pj-i 687:1).
19. **Virati pāpā** - Refraining from evil through body and speech (Pj-i 687:2).
20. **Majjapānā ca saṃyamo** - Restraint from drinking intoxicants.
21. **Appamādo ca dhammesu** - Diligence in phenomena (dhammas).
22. **Gāravo ca** - Respect.
23. **Nivāto ca** - Humility.
24. **Santuṭṭhi ca** - Contentment.
25. **Kataññutā** - Gratitude.
26. **Kālena dhammassavanaṃ** - Hearing the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) at the right time.
27. **Khantī ca** - Forbearance.
28. **Sovacassatā** - Being easy to admonish.
29. **Samaṇānañca dassanaṃ** - Seeing ascetics.
30. **Kālena dhammasākacchā** - Dhamma discussion at the right time.
31. **Tapo ca** - Asceticism.
32. **Brahmacariyañca** - The holy life.
33. **Ariyasaccāna dassanaṃ** - Seeing the Noble Truths.
34. **Nibbānasacchikiriyā ca** - The realization of [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/).
35. **Phuṭṭhassa lokadhammehi cittaṃ yassa na kampati** - When touched by worldly phenomena, a mind that does not quake.
36. **Asokaṃ** - Sorrowless.
37. **Virajaṃ** - Dustless.
38. **Khemaṃ** - Safe.

### Quote
> **“Bahū devā manussā ca, maṅgalāni acintayuṃ; Ākaṅkhamānā sotthānaṃ, brūhi maṅgalamuttamaṃ”. 3. “Asevanā ca bālānaṃ, paṇḍitānañca sevanā; Pūjā ca pūjaneyyānaṃ, etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ.**  
> “Many devas and humans have pondered on blessings, desiring safety; tell me the highest blessing.” “Not consorting with the foolish, and consorting with the wise, and paying homage to those worthy of homage — this is the highest blessing.  
>
