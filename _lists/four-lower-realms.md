### Overview
The lower realms (*[apāya](/glossary/apaya/)*) are the states of loss into which beings are reborn through unwholesome [kamma](/glossary/kamma/). The Ratana Sutta says that the noble disciple who has the attainment of vision (*dassanasampadā*), and who will not take an eighth existence, is "freed from the four lower realms" (*catūhapāyehi ca vippamutto*), and the Khuddakapāṭha-aṭṭhakathā names the four: "the four lower realms are hell, the animal realm, the realm of ghosts and the asura host" (*cattāro apāyā nāma nirayatiracchānapettivisayaasurakāyā*).

The suttas often describe rebirth after death in "a state of loss, a bad destination, the nether world, hell" (*apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ*). The Visuddhimagga and the Samantapāsādikā first take all four words as names for hell, and then, as an alternative (*atha vā*), as the four lower realms: *apāya* shows the animal realm, *[duggati](/glossary/duggati/)* the realm of ghosts, *vinipāta* the asura host, and *niraya* hell itself.

### The List
1. **[Niraya](/glossary/niraya/)** - Hell: "a place without any enjoyment" (*natthi ettha assādasaññito ayoti nirayo*), of many kinds, such as Avīci.
2. **Tiracchānayoni** - The animal realm: a state of loss because it is apart from a good destination, but not called a bad destination, since powerful beings such as nāga kings are born there.
3. **Pettivisaya** - The realm of ghosts (*[peta](/glossary/peta/)*): both a state of loss and a bad destination, since it is a destination of suffering; ghosts of great power even have mansions.
4. **Asurakāya** - The asura host (*[asura](/glossary/asura/)*): called *vinipāta*, "the nether world", because it has fallen away from all prosperity. In the Pāthika Sutta (DN 24) the lowest of them are named the Kālakañcika asuras (*kālakañcikā nāma asurā sabbanihīno asurakāyo*).

### Quote
> **Catūhapāyehi ca vippamutto, chaccābhiṭhānāni abhabba kātuṃ; Idampi saṅghe ratanaṃ paṇītaṃ, etena saccena suvatthi hotu.**  
> He is also fully freed from the four states of woe, and therefore, incapable of committing the six major wrongdoings. This precious jewel is the Saṅgha. By this asseveration of the truth may there be happiness.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Ratana [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (Khp 6; [Suttanipāta](/glossary/suttanipata/) 2.1, v. 234) – The noble disciple is freed from the four lower realms.
* **Canonical**: *Saṅgīti Sutta* (DN 33, D-iii 1620–1623) – The first four of the nine inopportune times for the holy life are rebirth in hell, the animal realm, the realm of ghosts and the asura host.
* **Commentary**: *[Paramatthajotikā](/glossary/paramatthajotika/)* (Khuddakapāṭha-aṭṭhakathā, Pj-i 885) – Names the four lower realms.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Vism-ii 232–233) and *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* (Sp-i 723–724) – Explain *apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ* as the four lower realms.
* **See also**: [The 5 Destinations of Rebirth](/lists/five-destinations-of-rebirth/) (*pañca gatiyo*, MN 12): hell, the animal realm, the realm of ghosts, human beings and devas.
