### Overview
The thirteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā classifies states by the *object* (*ārammaṇa*) they take: a limited, an exalted or an immeasurable object (As 863:1-2).

### The List
1. **Parittārammaṇā dhammā** - States having limited objects: the mind and mental factors that arise taking limited states (*paritta dhammā*) as object (Dhs 1797:2).
2. **Mahaggatārammaṇā dhammā** - States having exalted objects: the mind and mental factors that arise taking exalted states (*mahaggata dhammā*) as object (Dhs 1798:2). In the later exposition the answer is: the base of infinite consciousness and the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception (Dhs 2221:2).
3. **Appamāṇārammaṇā dhammā** - States having immeasurable objects: the mind and mental factors that arise taking immeasurable states (*appamāṇa dhammā*, i.e. the paths, fruits and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/), Dhs 1796:2) as object (Dhs 1799:2). In the later exposition the answer is: the four paths and the four fruits (Dhs 2222:2).

### Quote
> **(Ka) parittārammaṇā dhammā. (Kha) mahaggatārammaṇā dhammā. (Ga) appamāṇārammaṇā dhammā.**  
> 13.(Ka) Mental phenomena with limited objects. (Kha) Mental phenomena with exalted objects. (Ga) Mental phenomena with immeasurable objects.  
>
