### Overview
The fifth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena based on their relationship to the defilements/corruptions (*[kilesa](/glossary/kilesa/)*). The Aṭṭhakathā explains it at As 855:1-6.

### The List
1. **Saṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikā dhammā** - States that are defiled and defiling: the three unwholesome roots (greed, hatred, delusion), the defilements that are one with them, the mental aggregates associated with them, and the bodily, verbal and mental action arisen from them (Dhs 1765:2-5). The Aṭṭhakathā: *saṃkilesa* afflicts and torments; *saṃkiliṭṭhā* are those endowed with it; *saṃkilesikā* are those that are fit to be defiled by making themselves its object (As 855:1-3).
2. **Asaṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikā dhammā** - States that are undefiled but defiling: the wholesome and indeterminate states with outflows of the three planes, and the five aggregates (Dhs 1766:2-3). The Aṭṭhakathā: the remaining two terms follow the method of the earlier triad (As 855:6).
3. **Asaṃkiliṭṭhaasaṃkilesikā dhammā** - States that are undefiled and not defiling: the supramundane paths, their fruits, and the unconditioned element ([Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/)) (Dhs 1767:2).

### Quote
> **(Ka) saṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikā dhammā. (Kha) asaṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikā dhammā. (Ga) asaṃkiliṭṭhaasaṃkilesikā dhammā.**  
> 5.(Ka) Mental phenomena that are defiled and defiling. (Kha) Mental phenomena that are undefiled, but are defiling. (Ga) Mental phenomena that are undefiled and not defiling.  
>
