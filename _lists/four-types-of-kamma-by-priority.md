### Overview
Among the many deeds a person has done, which one ripens first and gives the next rebirth? The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha divides kamma "by the order of giving results" (*pākadānapariyāyena*) into four: weighty, proximate, habitual and done-and-stored (Abhidh-s 438:1). The Ṭīkā explains the order as the order in which the results are given (*vipākadānānukkamena*): if there is no weighty kamma, the proximate one ripens; if not, the habitual; if not, the kaṭattā kamma (Abhidh-s-t 1348:6-7).

### The List
1. **Garuka kamma** - Weighty [Kamma](/glossary/kamma/): among weighty and not-weighty kamma, the weighty one, wholesome or unwholesome, ripens first, like a great flood overwhelming a little water (Abhidh-s-t 1348:1). On the unwholesome side it is the five *ānantariya* deeds such as matricide, on the wholesome side the *mahaggata* kamma, the jhānas (Abhidh-s-t 1348:1; Mp-iii 476:2). When it is present, other kamma, wholesome or unwholesome, cannot ripen; it alone gives the rebirth-linking, as a pebble or iron ball thrown into a lake cannot float on the surface but sinks (Mp-iii 476:3-5).
2. **Āsanna kamma** - Death-Proximate Kamma: if there is no weighty kamma, the kamma near to death, recollected at the time of death, ripens first (Abhidh-s-t 1348:3).
3. **Āciṇṇa kamma** - Habitual Kamma: if there is not even that, the kamma that was habitual, whether good conduct or bad, ripens first (Abhidh-s-t 1348:4). The Aṭṭhakathā describes this as the kamma that is *bahula*, done over a long time by repeated practice (Mp-iii 477:1-3).
4. **Kaṭattā kamma** - Kamma that was done (and stored): if none of the earlier three exists, this kamma, which has obtained repetition, draws the rebirth-linking (Abhidh-s-t 1348:5-6).

The Ṭīkā notes that the Abhidhammāvatāra and other books put the habitual kamma before the proximate, but that the Saṅgaha puts the proximate first because, as with cattle leaving a pen, the one nearest to the gate (the nearness of death) goes out first (Abhidh-s-t 1348:8-9).


### Quote
> **Garukaṃ āsannaṃ āciṇṇaṃ kaṭattākammañceti pākadānapariyāyena.**  
> 51. Weighty kamma (garukaṃ), death-proximate kamma (āsannaṃ), habitual kamma (āciṇṇaṃ), and reserve kamma (kaṭattākammañceti)—thus it is classified by way of the order of ripening (pākadānapariyāyena).
