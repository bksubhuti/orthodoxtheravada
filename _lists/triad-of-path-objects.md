### Overview
The sixteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā analyzes the specific relationship that consciousness has with the Noble Eightfold Path. It differentiates between minds that merely reflect upon the Path, minds that are actively rooted in it, and minds that are entirely dominated by it.

### The List
1. **Maggārammaṇā dhammā** - States with the path as object: Consciousness and its concomitants that arise focusing on the Noble Path (Dhs 1038). The Aṭṭhakathā names the eight knowledge-associated consciousnesses of trainees and non-trainees when they review the paths they have personally penetrated (As 2427:1).
2. **Maggahetukā dhammā** - States with the path as root: The mental states of one endowed with the Noble Path that are associated with the path-factors and with the path-roots: non-greed, non-hatred and non-delusion (Dhs 1039). The four noble paths are always path-rooted (As 2427:2).
3. **Maggādhipatino dhammā** - States with the path as predominant factor: Consciousness and its concomitants that arise having taken the Noble Path as the predominant condition (Dhs 1040), for example when one reviews a path one has penetrated and makes it weighty (As 2427:1), or develops the path with investigation (*vīmaṃsā*) as the dominant factor (Dhs 1040); the Aṭṭhakathā adds energy (As 2427:3).

### Quote
> **(Ka) maggārammaṇā dhammā. (Kha) maggahetukā dhammā. (Ga) maggādhipatino dhammā.**  
> 16.(Ka) Mental phenomena having the path as their object. (Kha) Mental phenomena having the path as a root. (Ga) Mental phenomena having the path as a predominant condition.  
>
