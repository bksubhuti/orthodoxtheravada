### Overview
While ordinary Arahats possess profound wisdom, a Fully Awakened Buddha possesses an intellect of unparalleled scope. The *[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)* outlines fourteen specific analytical knowledges. Eight of these are shared with disciples (*sāvakasādhāraṇāni*) and six are not (*asādhāraṇāni sāvakehi*), as the passage itself states (Paṭis 674:4–5).

### The List
**The 8 knowledges shared with disciples (*sāvakasādhāraṇāni*):**
1. **Dukkhe ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of suffering.
2. **Dukkhasamudaye ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the origin of suffering.
3. **Dukkhanirodhe ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the cessation of suffering.
4. **Dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the path leading to the cessation of suffering.
5. **Atthapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the discrimination of meanings.
6. **Dhammapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the discrimination of mental phenomena (*dhamma*).
7. **Niruttipaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the discrimination of language.
8. **Paṭibhānapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the discrimination of perspicuity.

**The 6 knowledges not shared with disciples (*asādhāraṇāni sāvakehi*):**
9. **Indriyaparopariyatte ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the penetration of others' faculties.
10. **Sattānaṃ āsayānusaye ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of beings' biases and underlying tendencies.
11. **Yamakapāṭihīre ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the Twin Marvel.
12. **Mahākaruṇāsamāpattiyā ñāṇaṃ:** Knowledge of the attainment of Great Compassion.
13. **Sabbaññutaññāṇaṃ:** Omniscient knowledge.
14. **Anāvaraṇañāṇaṃ:** Unobstructed knowledge.

(Paṭis 674:2–5, Samantacakkhu passage.)

### Quote
> **Cuddasa buddhañāṇāni. Dukkhe ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, dukkhasamudaye ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhe ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, atthapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, dhammapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, niruttipaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, paṭibhānapaṭisambhide ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, indriyaparopariyatte ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, sattānaṃ āsayānusaye ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, yamakapāṭihīre ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, mahākaruṇāsamāpattiyā ñāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, sabbaññutaññāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ, anāvaraṇañāṇaṃ buddhañāṇaṃ – imāni cuddasa buddhañāṇāni.**  
> There are fourteen kinds of an Enlightened One’s knowledge. Knowledge of suffering is an Enlightened One’s knowledge; knowledge of the origin of suffering is an Enlightened One’s knowledge; knowledge of the cessation of suffering is an Enlightened One’s knowledge; knowledge of the path leading to the cessation of suffering is an Enlightened One’s knowledge; knowledge of the discrimination of meanings … of the discrimination of mental phenomena … of the discrimination of language … of the discrimination of perspicuity … knowledge of the penetration of others’ faculties … knowledge of beings’ biases and underlying tendencies … knowledge of the Twin Marvel … knowledge of the attainment of Great Compassion … omniscient knowledge … unobstructed knowledge is an Enlightened One’s knowledge—these are the fourteen kinds of an Enlightened One’s knowledge.  
>
