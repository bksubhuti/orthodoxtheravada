### Overview
Lay support is vital to anchor the monastic path. The Buddha named two laymen (*[Upāsaka](/glossary/upasaka/)*), the householder Citta and Hatthaka of Āḷavī, as the scale and measure for his male lay disciples (A-ii 233:3); the Buddhavaṃsa calls them the chief lay attendants (*aggupaṭṭhākupāsakā*, Bv 3144:1).

### The List
1. **[Citta](/glossary/citta-base/) (the householder of Macchikāsaṇḍa):** Born in Macchikāsaṇḍa in Magadha (Mp-i 1187:3), he soon attained the fruit of non-returning (*anāgāmiphala*, Mp-i 1188:5). The Buddha declared him foremost among lay speakers of Dhamma (A-i 236:1, *Dhammakathika*).
2. **Hatthaka of Āḷavī:** A prince of Āḷavī who passed from hand to hand between the yakkha Āḷavaka, the Buddha and the king's men, and so was named Hatthaka (Mp-i 1199:11). The Buddha declared him foremost among those who win over an assembly through the four means of popularity (A-i 237:1; Mp-i 1195:1).

### Quote
> **‘ tādiso homi yādiso citto ca gahapati hatthako ca āḷavako ’ ti. Esā, bhikkhave, tulā etaṃ pamāṇaṃ mama sāvakānaṃ upāsakānaṃ yadidaṃ citto ca gahapati hatthako ca āḷavako” ti.**  
> ‘May I be such as the householder Citta and Hatthaka of Āḷavī!’ This is the scale, this is the measure for my male lay disciples, that is to say, the householder Citta and Hatthaka of Āḷavī.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Āyācanavagga* (AN 2, A-ii 233:2-3) – The scale and measure for male lay disciples: Citta and Hatthaka of Āḷavī.
* **Canonical**: *Buddhavaṃsa* (Bv 3144:1) – Citta and Hatthāḷavaka as the chief lay attendants of Gotama Buddha.
* **Canonical**: *Etadagga Vagga* (AN 1, A-i 235:1, 236:1, 237:1) – Anāthapiṇḍika is foremost among givers, Citta among speakers of Dhamma, Hatthaka among those who win over an assembly. Their stories are in the Manorathapūraṇī (Mp-i 1186-1201).
