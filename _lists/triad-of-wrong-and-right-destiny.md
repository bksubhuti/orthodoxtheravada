### Overview
The fifteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena based on whether they lock a being into an unalterable destiny. Certain states are fixed (*niyata*) because, once present, they give their result immediately after the dissolution of the aggregates (As 865:2, 865:5).

### The List
1. **Micchattaniyatā dhammā** - States with fixed wrong destiny: The five actions with immediate retribution (*pañca kammāni ānantarikāni*) and wrong view that is fixed in wrongness (*micchādiṭṭhiniyatā*) (Dhs 1035). The Aṭṭhakathā explains them as wrong in nature and determinate because they give result right after the breakup of the aggregates (As 865:1-3).
2. **Sammattaniyatā dhammā** - States with fixed right destiny: The four paths not included in the round of existences (Dhs 1036). They are determinate because they give their fruit immediately (*anantarameva phaladānena*, As 865:5).
3. **Aniyatā dhammā** - States with unfixed destiny: Leaving those aside, the remaining wholesome, unwholesome and indeterminate states of the sensuous, form, formless and unincluded planes, the aggregates, all materiality, and the unconditioned element (Dhs 1037).

### Quote
> **(Ka) micchattaniyatā dhammā. (Kha) sammattaniyatā dhammā. (Ga) aniyatā dhammā.**  
> 15.(Ka) Mental phenomena fixed in wrongness. (Kha) Mental phenomena fixed in rightness. (Ga) Unfixed mental phenomena.  
>
