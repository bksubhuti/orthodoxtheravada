### Overview
The Ten Perfections (*[Pāramī](/glossary/parami/)*) are the absolute prerequisites for attaining Supreme Buddhahood. A [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/) must cultivate these ten virtues over countless lifetimes, pushing them to three levels of intensity: ordinary, higher (sacrificing limbs), and ultimate (sacrificing one's own life). 

### The List
1. **[Dāna](/glossary/dana/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Generosity: Sacrificing wealth, family, limbs, and life for the benefit of others.
2. **[Sīla](/glossary/sila/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Virtue: Flawless moral conduct, even at the cost of one's life.
3. **[Nekkhamma](/glossary/nekkhamma/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Renunciation: Abandoning worldly pleasures and the household life to seek truth.
4. **[Paññā](/glossary/panna/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Wisdom: The relentless pursuit of understanding the ultimate nature of reality.
5. **[Viriya](/glossary/viriya/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Energy: Heroic, unyielding effort in the face of impossible odds.
6. **[Khanti](/glossary/khanti/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Patience: Enduring intense physical pain, insults, and abuse without a single thought of anger.
7. **[Sacca](/glossary/sacca/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Truthfulness: Keeping one's promises and never speaking a lie, even to save one's life.
8. **[Adhiṭṭhāna](/glossary/adhitthana/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Determination: Unshakable resolve to achieve awakening, never giving up the goal.
9. **[Mettā](/glossary/metta/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Loving-kindness: Boundless goodwill toward all beings, even toward one's murderers.
10. **[Upekkhā](/glossary/upekkha/) Pāramī** - The Perfection of Equanimity: Perfect mental balance, remaining unshaken by praise, blame, pleasure, or pain.

### Quote
> **“Dānaṃ sīlañca nekkhammaṃ, paññāvīriyañca kīdisaṃ; Khantisaccamadhiṭṭhānaṃ, mettupekkhā ca kīdisā. 77. “Dasa pāramī tayā dhīra, kīdisī lokanāyaka; Kathaṃ upapāramī puṇṇā, paramatthapāramī kathaṃ”.**  
> What kind were your giving, virtue, renunciation, wisdom, and effort? What kind were your patience, truthfulness, determination, loving-kindness, and equanimity? Wise One, World's Chief Leader, what kind of ten perfections were fulfilled by you? How were the secondary perfections fulfilled, and how were the supreme perfections fulfilled?”  
>
