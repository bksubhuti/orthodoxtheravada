### Overview
Every human being possesses a unique psychological disposition based on their past [kamma](/glossary/kamma/). In Theravāda meditation, a skilled teacher (*[kalyāṇamitta](/glossary/kalyanamitta/)*) analyzes a student's behavior, posture, and eating habits to determine their temperament (*carita*), and then prescribes a specific meditation subject to cure it.

### The List
1. **Rāgacarita** - The Lustful Temperament: Driven by attachment, desire for beauty, and pleasant sensory experiences. (Cured by meditating on the foulness of the body).
2. **Dosacarita** - The Hating Temperament: Driven by anger, irritation, fault-finding, and impatience. (Cured by meditating on loving-kindness and color kasinas).
3. **Mohacarita** - The Deluded Temperament: Driven by confusion, doubt, spaciness, and lack of clarity. (Cured by mindfulness of breathing).
4. **Saddhācarita** - The Faithful Temperament: The wholesome parallel to lust. Driven by natural devotion and trust. (Enhanced by recollecting the Buddha, [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/), and [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/)).
5. **Buddhicarita** - The Intelligent Temperament: The wholesome parallel to hate. Driven by analytical thinking, inquiry, and a desire for truth. (Enhanced by mindfulness of death and element analysis).
6. **Vitakkacarita** - The Speculative Temperament: The parallel to delusion. Driven by endless overthinking, restlessness, and theoretical wandering. (Cured by mindfulness of breathing).

### Quote
> **Idāni attano cariyānukūlanti ettha cariyāti cha cariyā rāgacariyā, dosacariyā, mohacariyā, saddhācariyā, buddhicariyā, vitakkacariyāti.**  
> Now, as to the phrase 'suitable to his own temperament' (attano cariyānukūlaṃ): here, temperament (cariyā) refers to six kinds of temperament: greedy temperament, hating temperament, deluded temperament, faithful temperament, intelligent temperament, and speculative temperament.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Vism, Cariyāvaṇṇanā, para 43; Aṭṭhakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_01&page_number=98&name=visuddhimaggo+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=NDMuIElkxIFuaSBhdHRhbm8gY2FyaXnEgW51a8WrbGFudGkgZXR0aGEgY2FyaXnEgXRp)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mahāniddesa* (Nidd-i 2012:6) – The Buddha knows beings as rāgacarita, dosacarita, mohacarita, vitakkacarita, saddhācarita or ñāṇacarita, and gives each a remedy. The *Visuddhimagga* list above has buddhicarita where the Niddesa has ñāṇacarita.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter III) – The definitive guide for identifying these temperaments based on how a person sweeps a floor, wears their robes, or eats their food.
