### Overview
In the Tatiya Saddhammasammosa Sutta the Buddha names five things that lead to the decline and disappearance of the true Dhamma (*saddhammassa sammosāya antaradhānāya saṃvattanti*, A-v 1054:1). Its counterpart, five things that lead to the Dhamma's continuance, follows at A-v 1059:1.

### The List
1. **Duggahitaṃ suttantaṃ pariyāpuṇanti dunnikkhittehi padabyañjanehi** - Monks memorize discourses learned incorrectly, with misplaced words and phrases; when the words and phrases are misplaced, the meaning is misinterpreted (A-v 1054:3-4).
2. **Bhikkhū dubbacā honti** - Monks are hard to speak to, possessed of qualities that make them hard to admonish, unforbearing, and do not take instruction respectfully (A-v 1055:1).
3. **Bahussutā … na sakkaccaṃ suttantaṃ paraṃ vācenti** - The learned monks (bahussutā āgatāgamā dhammadharā vinayadharā mātikādharā) do not carefully teach the discourse to others; after their passing the discourse is cut off at the root and without a refuge (A-v 1056:1-2).
4. **Therā bhikkhū bāhulikā honti sāthalikā** - Elder monks are luxurious and lax, leaders in backsliding, shirk the duty of seclusion and do not arouse energy to attain the unattained; the later generation follows their example (A-v 1057:1-3).
5. **Saṅgho bhinno hoti** - The Saṅgha is split; its members abuse, revile, reject and abandon one another, the unconfident do not gain confidence and some of the confident change (A-v 1058:1-3).

### Quote
> **“Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhammā saddhammassa sammosāya antaradhānāya saṃvattanti. Katame pañca? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhū duggahitaṃ suttantaṃ pariyāpuṇanti dunnikkhittehi padabyañjanehi. Dunnikkhittassa, bhikkhave, padabyañjanassa atthopi dunnayo hoti.**  
> “Mendicants, these five things lead to the decline and disappearance of the true Dhamma. What five? It’s when, mendicants, the monks memorize discourses that they learned incorrectly, with misplaced words and phrases. When the words and phrases are misplaced, mendicants, the meaning is misinterpreted.  
>
