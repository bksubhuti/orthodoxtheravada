### Overview
A resident monk (*āvāsiko [bhikkhu](/glossary/bhikkhu/)*) is a monk who lives at a monastery. The Āvāsika Sutta (AN 5.231), the first sutta of the Āvāsika Vagga, gives five qualities by which such a monk is to be esteemed (*bhāvanīyo*), and the five opposite qualities by which he is not. The suttas that follow in the same chapter give other sets of five for a resident monk.

### The List
1. **Ākappasampanno hoti vattasampanno** - Accomplished in deportment and in his duties. The Manorathapūraṇī explains *ākappasampanno* as "accomplished in the deportment of a recluse" (*samaṇākappena sampanno*).
2. **Bahussuto hoti sutadharo** - Learned, and one who retains what he has learned.
3. **Paṭisallekhitā hoti paṭisallānārāmo** - One who effaces the defilements, and who delights in seclusion.
4. **Kalyāṇavāco hoti kalyāṇavākkaraṇo** - A good speaker, with a pleasing delivery.
5. **Paññavā hoti ajaḷo aneḷamūgo** - Wise, not dull and not stupid.

### Quote
> **“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato āvāsiko bhikkhu bhāvanīyo hoti. Katamehi pañcahi? Ākappasampanno hoti vattasampanno; bahussuto hoti sutadharo; paṭisallekhitā hoti paṭisallānārāmo; kalyāṇavāco hoti kalyāṇavākkaraṇo; paññavā hoti ajaḷo aneḷamūgo. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato āvāsiko bhikkhu bhāvanīyo hotī” ti.**  
> “Mendicants, a resident monk possessed of five qualities is esteemed. Which five? He is accomplished in appearance and accomplished in conduct; he is learned and a memorizer of what he has learned; he is one who eradicates defilements and delights in seclusion; he is a good speaker and of pleasing delivery; he is wise, bright, and clever. Mendicants, a resident monk possessed of these five qualities is esteemed.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Āvāsika [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 5.231) – Five qualities by which a resident monk is esteemed, and five by which he is not. The next suttas in the Āvāsika Vagga give further sets of five: by which a resident monk is dear to his fellow monks (AN 5.232, Piya Sutta), and by which he graces the monastery (AN 5.233, Sobhana Sutta).
* **Commentary**: *[Manorathapūraṇī](/glossary/manorathapurani/)* (Mp-v 473) – Explains *ākappasampanno* as *samaṇākappena sampanno*, and *abhāvanīyo* as *vaḍḍhanīyo na hoti*.
