### Overview
The *Āsavas* (taints, corruptions, or effluents) are the most deeply ingrained psychological impurities. The Aṭṭhakathā explains *āsava* as what "flows" (from the eye … to the mind, and from the dhammas up to the gotrabhū, in space up to the highest plane of existence), and says they are called *āsava* like liquor and the like that have long been kept fermenting (*cirapārivāsiyaṭṭhena madirādayo*, As 878:2-8). The total eradication of these taints is the defining characteristic of an [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/) (often called a *Khīṇāsava*, "One whose taints are destroyed").

### The List
1. **Kāmāsava** - The Taint of Sensual Desire: The taint of sensual desire.
2. **Bhavāsava** - The Taint of Existence: The taint of being (existence).
3. **Diṭṭhāsava** - The Taint of Views: The taint of views. *(Note: the Suttas give only three taints, without views: M-i 276:4, D-iii 1362:1; the fourfold list is in the Abhidhamma, Dhs 1878:2.)*
4. **Avijjāsava** - The Taint of Ignorance: The taint of ignorance.

### Quote
> **Katame dhammā āsavā? Cattāro āsavā – kāmāsavo, bhavāsavo, diṭṭhāsavo, avijjāsavo.**  
> 1102. What states are taints? There are four taints: the taint of sensual desire, the taint of being, the taint of views, and the taint of ignorance.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs 1102, Āsavagocchaka) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=221&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTEwMi4gS2F0YW1lIGRoYW1txIEgxIFzYXbEgT8%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Sabbāsava [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (MN 2, M-i 55 "2. Sabbāsavasuttaṃ") – Teaches how taints are abandoned (by seeing, restraining, using, enduring, avoiding, removing, developing); it is not where the four are listed.
* **Canonical**: *Saṅgīti Sutta* (DN 33, D-iii 1362:1) and *Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta* (MN 9, M-i 276:4) – "Tayo āsavā – kāmāsavo, bhavāsavo, avijjāsavo".
* **Abhidhamma**: *Dhammasaṅgaṇī* (Dhs 1877 "Āsavagocchakaṃ") and its Aṭṭhakathā (As 878) – the fourfold list and the meaning of *āsava*.
