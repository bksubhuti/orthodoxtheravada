### Overview
The [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Piṭaka is the third basket of the Pāḷi Canon. Unlike the Suttas, which use conventional language (people, places, stories), the Abhidhamma is a highly technical, mathematical breakdown of ultimate reality (*paramattha [dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/)*). It consists of seven distinct treatises.

### The List
1. **[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)** - Classification of Dhammas: The foundational book, detailing the matrix (mātikā) of all reality and extensively analyzing every type of consciousness and mental factor.
2. **[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)** - The Book of Analysis: Provides a deep Abhidhammic breakdown of core [sutta](/glossary/sutta/) concepts like the aggregates, sense bases, and dependent origination.
3. **[Dhātukathā](/glossary/dhatukatha/)** - Discourse on Elements: A specialized text mapping all phenomena into the framework of aggregates, sense bases, and elements.
4. **[Puggalapaññatti](/glossary/puggalapannatti/)** - Designation of Individuals: A unique Abhidhamma text that classifies various types of persons (worldlings, noble disciples, etc.).
5. **[Kathāvatthu](/glossary/kathavatthu/)** - Points of Controversy: Taught in outline by the Buddha as the mātikā and expounded by Moggaliputtatissa Thera, to refute wrong views (As 50:13, 55:1; Kv-a 543).
6. **[Yamaka](/glossary/yamaka/)** - The Book of Pairs: A highly complex logical text designed to resolve ambiguities and test a student's grasp of ultimate terminology.
7. **[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)** - The Book of Conditional Relations: The massive, crowning achievement of the Abhidhamma, detailing the 24 universal conditions of causality that govern all existence.

### Quote
> **Dhammasaṅgaho, vibhaṅgo, dhātukathā, puggalapaññatti, kathāvatthu, yamakaṃ, paṭṭhānanti idaṃ abhidhammapiṭakaṃ nāma.**  
> The Dhammasaṅgaṇī, Vibhaṅga, Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, and Paṭṭhāna are called the Abhidhamma Pitaka (abhidhammapiṭakaṃ).

### Textual References
* **Commentary**: *Aṭṭhasālinī* (As 151:10) – "Dhammasaṅgaṇīādīni satta pakaraṇāni abhidhammapiṭakaṃ nāma"; and As 122:5 – the Buddha taught the Abhidhamma to his mother and the deities in Tāvatiṃsa. The seven are named in the Quote (Samantapāsādikā, also Sumaṅgalavilāsinī, Sv-i 120:5).
