### Overview
The fourteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena based on their inherent nobility, ethical quality, and overall desirability on the path to liberation.

### The List
1. **Hīnā dhammā** - Inferior states: All unwholesome consciousnesses and mental factors (rooted in greed, hatred, and delusion). They are low, base, and lead to suffering.
2. **Majjhimā dhammā** - Medium states: All mundane wholesome states, mundane resultant states, mundane functional states, and all physical matter. They are intermediate—better than unwholesome states, but still bound within [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).
3. **Paṇītā dhammā** - Superior states: The supramundane paths, fruits, and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). They are supreme, flawless, and represent the ultimate goal.

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)* (Mātikā 14) – Provides a definitive hierarchy of all reality, placing the supramundane above all conditioned existence.
