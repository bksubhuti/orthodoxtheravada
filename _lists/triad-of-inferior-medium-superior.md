### Overview
The fourteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā sorts all states into inferior, medium and superior. The Aṭṭhakathā explains that *hīnā* are vile unwholesome states, *majjhimā* are those between the inferior and the superior (As 864:1-2).

### The List
1. **Hīnā dhammā** - Inferior states: the twelve unwholesome consciousness-arisings (*dvādasa akusalacittuppādā*) (Dhs 2223:2); in the expanded answer, the three unwholesome roots, the defilements one with them, the associated aggregates and the action arisen from them (Dhs 1800:2-5).
2. **Majjhimā dhammā** - Medium states: the wholesome, the resultant and the functional-indeterminate in the three planes, and all matter (Dhs 2224:2); in the expanded answer, the wholesome and indeterminate states with outflows and the five aggregates (Dhs 1801:2).
3. **Paṇītā dhammā** - Superior states: the four paths not included in the round of existences, the four fruits of the life of a recluse, and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Dhs 2225:2).

### Quote
> **Katame dhammā hīnā? Dvādasa akusalacittuppādā – ime dhammā hīnā. 1424. Katame dhammā majjhimā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā majjhimā. 1425. Katame dhammā paṇītā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā paṇītā.**  
> 1423. What mental phenomena are inferior? The twelve unwholesome consciousness-arisings—these mental phenomena are inferior. 1424. What mental phenomena are medium? The wholesome in the three spheres, the result in the three spheres, the functional indeterminate in the three spheres, and all materiality—these mental phenomena are medium. 1425. What mental phenomena are superior? The four paths, not included in the round of existences, the four fruits of the life of a recluse, and Nibbāna—these mental phenomena are superior.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs 1423–1425, Tikamātikā 14 exposition) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=271&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTQyMy4gS2F0YW1lIGRoYW1txIEgaMSrbsSBPw%3D%3D)
