### Overview
Similar to the Taints, the Fetters (*[Saṃyojana](/glossary/samyojana/)*) are the specific defilements that bind beings to the wheel of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/). This Dyad isolates the precise mental factors that act as these binding chains.

### The List
1. **Saṃyojanā dhammā** - States that are fetters: The Suttas list ten fetters (including lust for form and for the formless, and restlessness). The [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) (Dhammasaṅgaṇī §1118) gives its own ten: sensual passion, aversion, conceit, views, doubt, adherence to rites and rituals, desire for continued existence, jealousy, stinginess and ignorance.
2. **No saṃyojanā dhammā** - States that are not fetters: Everything else in reality. All consciousnesses, the remaining mental factors, matter, and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). 

### Quote
> **Katame dhammā saṃyojanā? Dasa saṃyojanāni – kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ, mānasaṃyojanaṃ, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ, vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ, sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ, bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, issāsaṃyojanaṃ, macchariyasaṃyojanaṃ, avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ.**  
> 1118. What states are fetters? There are ten fetters: the fetter of sensual passion, the fetter of aversion, the fetter of conceit, the fetter of views, the fetter of doubt, the fetter of adherence to rites and rituals, the fetter of desire for continued existence, the fetter of jealousy, the fetter of stinginess, and the fetter of ignorance.  
>
