### Overview
When a monk finds resentment (*āghāta*) arising toward a person, the Buddha gives him five ways to remove it completely (*sabbaso paṭivinetabbo*, A-v 1091:1). They are five alternative remedies, any of which is to be applied to the object, the person, toward whom the resentment has arisen (Mp-v 331:2).

### The List
1. **[Mettā](/glossary/metta/) bhāvetabbā** - Developing loving-kindness toward that person; the Aṭṭhakathā says it is developed by way of the jhānas, *tikacatukkajjhānavasena* (Mp-v 331:3).
2. **[Karuṇā](/glossary/karuna/) bhāvetabbā** - Developing compassion toward that person, in the same way as mettā (Mp-v 331:4).
3. **[Upekkhā](/glossary/upekkha/) bhāvetabbā** - Developing equanimity toward that person, by way of the jhānas, *catukkapañcakajjhānavasena* (Mp-v 331:5).
4. **Asatiamanasikāro āpajjitabbo** - Non-attention: applying forgetfulness and non-attention to that person, so that he does not appear to the mind, as if separated by a wall (Mp-v 331:7).
5. **Kammassakatā adhiṭṭhātabbā** - Establishing the ownership of kamma in that person: "This venerable one is the owner of kamma, heir of kamma, kamma as origin, kamma as relative, and has kamma as his refuge. Whatever kamma he does, good or evil, of that he will be the heir" (A-v 1091:11-12).


### Quote
> **“Pañcime, bhikkhave, āghātapaṭivinayā yattha bhikkhuno uppanno āghāto sabbaso paṭivinetabbo. Katame pañca? Yasmiṃ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, mettā tasmiṃ puggale bhāvetabbā; evaṃ tasmiṃ puggale āghāto paṭivinetabbo. Yasmiṃ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, karuṇā tasmiṃ puggale bhāvetabbā; evaṃ tasmiṃ puggale āghāto paṭivinetabbo. Yasmiṃ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, upekkhā tasmiṃ puggale bhāvetabbā; evaṃ tasmiṃ puggale āghāto paṭivinetabbo. Yasmiṃ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, asatiamanasikāro tasmiṃ puggale āpajjitabbo; evaṃ tasmiṃ puggale āghāto paṭivinetabbo. Yasmiṃ, bhikkhave, puggale āghāto jāyetha, kammassakatā tasmiṃ puggale adhiṭṭhātabbā – ‘ kammassako ayamāyasmā kammadāyādo kammayoni kammabandhu kammappaṭisaraṇo, yaṃ kammaṃ karissati kalyāṇaṃ vā pāpakaṃ vā tassa dāyādo bhavissatī ’ ti; evaṃ tasmiṃ puggale āghāto paṭivinetabbo. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca āghātapaṭivinayā, yattha bhikkhuno uppanno āghāto sabbaso paṭivinetabbo” ti.**  
> “Monks, these are the five ways of removing annoyance by which, when annoyance has arisen for a bhikkhu, it is to be completely subdued. What are these five? Monks, when annoyance arises towards an individual, loving-kindness (mettā) is to be developed towards that individual; thus, annoyance towards that individual is to be subdued. Monks, when annoyance arises towards an individual, compassion is to be developed towards that individual; thus, annoyance towards that individual is to be subdued. Monks, when annoyance arises towards an individual, equanimity (upekkhā) is to be developed towards that individual; thus, annoyance towards that individual is to be subdued. Monks, when annoyance arises towards an individual, non-attention (asatiamanasikāro) to that individual is to be applied; thus, annoyance towards that individual is to be subdued. Monks, when annoyance arises towards an individual, the understanding of kamma ownership (kammassakatā) is to be established in that individual: ‘This venerable one is the owner of kamma, heir of kamma, kamma as origin, kamma as relative, and has kamma as his refuge. Whatever kamma he does, whether good or evil, of that he will be the heir’; thus, annoyance towards that individual is to be subdued. Monks, these are the five ways of removing annoyance by which, when annoyance has arisen for a bhikkhu, it is to be completely subdued.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Paṭhama Āghātapaṭivinaya [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 5.161, A-v 1090:1 "1. Paṭhamaāghātapaṭivinayasuttaṃ"), spoken by the Buddha to the monks ("Pañcime, bhikkhave"). The following sutta (AN 5.162, A-v 1093:1) is spoken by Venerable Sāriputta and gives a different five.
* **Commentary**: Manorathapūraṇī (Mp-v 331:1-8), which also explains why muditā is not in this list (Mp-v 331:6).
