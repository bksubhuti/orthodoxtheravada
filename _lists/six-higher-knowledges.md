### Overview
Through the mastery of the Fourth [Jhāna](/glossary/jhana/), the mind becomes incredibly malleable, luminous, and powerful. A master meditator can then direct this concentrated mind to acquire supernatural faculties (*[Abhiññā](/glossary/abhinna/)*). The first five are mundane and can be achieved by non-Buddhists; the sixth is supramundane and represents ultimate Buddhist awakening.

### The List
1. **[Iddhividha](/glossary/iddhividha/)** - Psychic Powers: The ability to multiply the body, walk through walls, dive into the earth, walk on water, or fly cross-legged through the air.
2. **[Dibbasota](/glossary/dibbasota/)** - The Divine Ear: Clairaudience. The ability to hear sounds both human and divine, whether near or incredibly far away.
3. **[Cetopariyañāṇa](/glossary/cetopariyanana/)** - Telepathy: The ability to penetrate and read the minds of others, knowing immediately if their mind contains lust, hatred, concentration, or liberation.
4. **[Pubbenivāsānussatiñāṇa](/glossary/pubbenivasanussatinana/)** - Recollection of Past Lives: The ability to remember one's own previous existences, spanning hundreds of thousands of eons, recalling names, diets, and experiences.
5. **[Dibbacakkhu](/glossary/dibbacakkhu/)** - The Divine Eye: Clairvoyance. The ability to see the passing away and rebirth of other beings, directly observing how they fare according to their [kamma](/glossary/kamma/).
6. **[Āsavakkhayañāṇa](/glossary/asavakkhayanana/)** - Knowledge of the Destruction of the Taints: The realization of the Four Noble Truths and the permanent eradication of all defilements. This is the attainment of Arahatship.

### Quote
> **Kāyampi cittampi ekavavatthānatā sukhasaññañca lahusaññañca adhiṭṭhānavasena ijjhanaṭṭhe paññā iddhividhe ñāṇaṃ. 51. Vitakkavipphāravasena nānattekattasaddanimittānaṃ pariyogāhaṇe paññā sotadhātuvisuddhiñāṇaṃ. 52. Tiṇṇannaṃ cittānaṃ vipphārattā indriyānaṃ pasādavasena nānattekattaviññāṇacariyā pariyogāhaṇe paññā cetopariyañāṇaṃ. 53. Paccayappavattānaṃ dhammānaṃ nānattekattakammavipphāravasena pariyogāhaṇe paññā pubbenivāsānussatiñāṇaṃ. 54. Obhāsavasena nānattekattarūpanimittānaṃ dassanaṭṭhe paññā dibbacakkhuñāṇaṃ. 55. Catusaṭṭhiyā ākārehi tiṇṇannaṃ indriyānaṃ vasībhāvatā paññā āsavānaṃ khaye ñāṇaṃ.**  
> 50. Understanding of the meaning of succeeding by defining body and cognizance as one and by steadying easy perception and quick perception by the power of the determination is knowledge of the kinds of success. 51. Understanding of fathoming sound signs in their difference and unity by the power of the extension of thought is knowledge of purification of the ear principle. 52. Understanding of fathoming the behavior of consciousness in its difference and unity by means of confidence in the faculties due to the extension of three types of cognizance is knowledge of encompassing the minds of others. 53. Understanding of fathoming states conditionally-arisen through the power of the extension of actions in their difference and unity is knowledge of recollection of past life. 54. Understanding of seeing the meaning as signs of visible objects in their difference and unity by means of illumination is knowledge of the divine eye. 55. Understanding as mastery of three faculties in sixty-four ways is knowledge about exhaustion of the taints.  
>
