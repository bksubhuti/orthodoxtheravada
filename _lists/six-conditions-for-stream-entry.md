### Overview
While the Path to Stream-Entry is typically defined by four factors, the Buddha also taught six benefits (*ānisaṃsā*) of realizing the Fruit of Stream-Entry (*Sotāpattiphala*).

### The List
1. **Saddhammaniyato hoti** - Certain of the True [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/): One is fixed (*niyata*) in the true Dhamma.
2. **Aparihānadhammo hoti** - Not liable to decline: One is unable to retrogress.
3. **Pariyantakatassa dukkhaṃ hoti** - Suffering has a limit: A limit has been placed on one's suffering.
4. **Asādhāraṇena ñāṇena samannāgato hoti** - Endowed with uncommon knowledge: One possesses knowledge that is not shared with ordinary people.
5. **Hetu cassa sudiṭṭho** - The cause is well seen.
6. **Hetusamuppannā ca dhammā** - And the things arisen from a cause (are also well seen).

### Quote
> **“Chayime, bhikkhave, ānisaṃsā sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya. Katame cha? Saddhammaniyato hoti, aparihānadhammo hoti, pariyantakatassa dukkhaṃ hoti, asādhāraṇena ñāṇena samannāgato hoti, hetu cassa sudiṭṭho, hetusamuppannā ca dhammā.**  
> “Monks, these are the six benefits in realizing the fruit of stream-entry. What six? One is certain of the true Dhamma; is unable to retrogress; there is a limit placed on their suffering; is possessed of uncommon knowledge; and their cause is well seen, along with causally-originated mental phenomena.  
>
