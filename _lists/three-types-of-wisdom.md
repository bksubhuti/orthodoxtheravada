### Overview
Wisdom (*[paññā](/glossary/panna/)*) is classed in the Saṅgīti Sutta in several triads. This page is the second of the two triads of wisdom there, in the order the sutta gives: wisdom gained by thinking, by learning, and by development (D-iii 1385:1).

### The List
1. **Cintāmayā paññā** - Wisdom gained by thinking: in the Visuddhimagga's explanation, wisdom acquired for oneself without hearing from another, produced by one's own power of thinking (Vism-ii 291:1); the Vibhaṅga's definition is that one attains it "parato asutvā", without hearing from another (Sv-iii 743:3 quotes it).
2. **Sutamayā paññā** - Wisdom gained by learning: wisdom acquired by hearing from another, produced by the power of what is heard (Vism-ii 291:2).
3. **Bhāvanāmayā paññā** - Wisdom gained by development: wisdom produced through development (*bhāvanā*) that has reached absorption (*appanā*) (Vism-ii 291:3).

### Quote
> **“Aparāpi tisso paññā – cintāmayā paññā, sutamayā paññā, bhāvanāmayā paññā.**  
> “There are three other kinds of wisdom: wisdom gained by thinking, wisdom gained by learning, and wisdom gained by development.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 33, Saṅgīti Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_03&page_number=183&name=p%C4%81thikavaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIEFwYXLEgXBpIHRpc3NvIHBhw7HDscSBIOKAkyBjaW50xIFtYXnEgQ%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Saṅgīti [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 33) – Venerable Sāriputta recites the Dhamma to the monks in this sutta (D-iii 1301:1); this is the second triad of paññā (the first, D-iii 1384:1, is sekkhā, asekkhā, nevasekkhānāsekkhā). Also in the Vibhaṅga (Vibh 2153:1, with definitions from Vibh 2348:1).
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XIV, "14. Khandhaniddeso", Vism-ii 273:1) – Explains the three at Vism-ii 291:1-3.
