### Overview
The Four Unwholesome Universal Mental Factors (*Sabbākusalasādhāraṇā Cetasikā*) are the four mental factors that the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha says are found in all twelve unwholesome consciousnesses (*[akusala](/glossary/akusala/) [citta](/glossary/citta-base/)*) (Abhidh-s 115:1). The Ṭīkā explains why: whoever does any unwholesome deed, such as killing, is deluded and so sees no danger in it (*mohena anādīnavadassāvī*), is shameless and so feels no disgust at it (*ahirikena ajigucchanto*), has no fear of wrongdoing and so is unafraid (*anottappena anottappanto*), and is restless and so unquiet (*uddhaccena avūpasanto*); therefore these four are found in all unwholesome states (Abhidh-s-t 1074:2).

### The List
1. **[Moha](/glossary/moha/)** - Delusion: in the Dhammasaṅgaṇī, not knowing, not seeing, non-penetration, foolishness, lack of clear comprehension, *avijjā* and so on, the root of the unwholesome (*akusalamūlaṃ*) (Dhs 892:2).
2. **[Ahirika](/glossary/ahirika/)** - Shamelessness: not being ashamed of what one should be ashamed of, not being ashamed of entering upon evil unwholesome states (*na hirīyati hiriyitabbena na hirīyati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā*, Dhs 889:2).
3. **[Anottappa](/glossary/anottappa/)** - Moral Dreadlessness: not being afraid of what one should be afraid of, not being afraid of entering upon evil unwholesome states (*na ottappati ottappitabbena …*, Dhs 890:2).
4. **[Uddhacca](/glossary/uddhacca/)** - Restlessness: restlessness of mind, lack of calming, a scattered mind, agitation of mind (*cittassa uddhaccaṃ avūpasamo cetaso vikkhepo bhantattaṃ cittassa*, Dhs 937:2).


### Quote
> **Akusalesu pana moho ahirikaṃ anottappaṃ uddhaccañcāti cattārome cetasikā sabbākusalasādhāraṇā nāma, sabbesupi dvādasā kusalesu labbhanti.**  
> 20. Among the unwholesome (akusala), however, these four mental factors—delusion (moha), moral shamelessness (ahirika), lack of fear of wrongdoing (anottappa), and restlessness (uddhacca)—are called universal to all unwholesome states; they are found in all twelve unwholesome types of consciousness.  
>

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)* defines each of the four separately within its analysis of the unwholesome cittas (Dhs 889:2, 890:2, 892:2, 937:2); it does not name them as a set of four universals.
* **Handbook and Ṭīkā**: *[Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)* (Chapter II, §20, Abhidh-s 115:1) names the set, *sabbākusalasādhāraṇā* (a term found only in the Ṭīkā layer: 0 Mūla, 0 Aṭṭhakathā, 9 Ṭīkā sentences); *Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī* (Abhidh-s-t 1074:1-2) explains it.
