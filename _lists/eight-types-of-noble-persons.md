### Overview
When taking refuge in the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/), one is not taking refuge in ordinary, unenlightened monks, but specifically in the "Noble Saṅgha" (*[Ariyasaṅgha](/glossary/ariyasangha/)*). The Buddha defined this supreme community as consisting of eight specific types of individuals, divided into four pairs based on whether they are experiencing the active Path-moment (*[magga](/glossary/magga/)*) or the resulting Fruit-moment (*phala*).

### The List
1. **[Sotāpanna](/glossary/sotapanna/)** - The Stream-Enterer (Fruit): The individual who has realized the fruit, permanently cutting the first three fetters.
2. **Sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno** - The one practicing for the realization of the Fruit of Stream-Entry: The individual in the exact mind-moment of the first path.
3. **[Sakadāgāmī](/glossary/sakadagami/)** - The Once-Returner (Fruit): The individual who has realized the fruit, heavily weakening lust and hatred.
4. **Sakadāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno** - The one practicing for the realization of the Fruit of Once-Returning: The individual in the exact mind-moment of the second path.
5. **[Anāgāmī](/glossary/anagami/)** - The Non-Returner (Fruit): The individual who has realized the fruit, permanently destroying all sensual lust and anger.
6. **Anāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno** - The one practicing for the realization of the Fruit of Non-Returning: The individual in the exact mind-moment of the third path.
7. **[Arahā](/glossary/arahat/)** - The Fully Awakened One (Fruit): The individual who has permanently destroyed all defilements.
8. **Arahattāya paṭipanno** - The one practicing for Arahatship: The individual in the exact mind-moment of the fourth path.

### Quote
> **“Aṭṭhime bhikkhave, puggalā āhuneyyā pāhuneyyā dakkhiṇeyyā añjalikaraṇīyā anuttaraṃ puññakkhettaṃ lokassa? Katame aṭṭha? Sotāpanno, sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, sakadāgāmī, sakadāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, anāgāmī, anāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, arahā, arahattāya paṭipanno.**  
> “Monks, these eight individuals are worthy of offerings, worthy of hospitality, worthy of offerings, worthy of reverential salutation, the unsurpassed field of merit for the world. What eight? A stream-enterer, one practicing in realizing the fruit of stream-entry, a once-returner, one practicing for realization of the fruit of once-returning, a non-returner, one practicing for realization of the fruit of non-returning, an Arahant, one practicing for arahantship.  
>
