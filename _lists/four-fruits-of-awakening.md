### Overview
Following immediately after the lightning-strike of a Path-moment (*[Magga](/glossary/magga/)*) comes its exact resultant consciousness, known as the Fruit (*Phala*). While the Path-moment does the active work of killing defilements, the Fruit-moment is the peaceful experience of liberation resulting from that destruction.

### The List
1. **Sotāpattiphala** - The Fruit of Stream-Entry: The immediate result of the first path. The individual is now officially a *[Sotāpanna](/glossary/sotapanna/)* (Stream-enterer), safe from rebirth in the lower realms.
2. **Sakadāgāmiphala** - The Fruit of Once-Returning: The immediate result of the second path. The individual is now a *[Sakadāgāmī](/glossary/sakadagami/)*, destined to return to the human world only one more time.
3. **Anāgāmiphala** - The Fruit of Non-Returning: The immediate result of the third path. The individual is now an *[Anāgāmī](/glossary/anagami/)*, completely free from all human anger and sexual desire.
4. **Arahattaphala** - The Fruit of Arahatship: The immediate result of the fourth path. The individual is a fully awakened [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/). Their mind is perfectly pure, and they will attain [Parinibbāna](/glossary/parinibbana/) at death.

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mahāli [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 6) – The Buddha defines the realization of these specific fruits as the purpose of the holy life.
* **[Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/)**: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)* – Classifies these four fruits as the ultimate unconditioned results (*vipāka*) of the supramundane paths.
