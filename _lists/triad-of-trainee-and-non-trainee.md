### Overview
The eleventh triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena as belonging to learners (*sekkha*), to non-learners (*asekkha*), or to neither.

### The List
1. **Sekkhā dhammā** - States belonging to Trainees: The four paths (not included in the round of existences) and the three lower fruits of the life of a recluse (Dhs 1023).
2. **Asekkhā dhammā** - States belonging to Non-Trainees: The uppermost fruit, the fruit of arahantship (Dhs 1024).
3. **Nevasekkhanāsekkhā dhammā** - States belonging to neither Trainees nor Non-Trainees: Leaving those aside, the remaining wholesome, unwholesome and indeterminate states of the sensuous, form and formless planes, the aggregates, all materiality, and the unconditioned element, [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Dhs 1025).

### Quote
> **(Ka) sekkhā dhammā. (Kha) asekkhā dhammā. (Ga) nevasekkhanāsekkhā dhammā.**  
> 11.(Ka) Mental phenomena of learners. (Kha) Mental phenomena of non-learners. (Ga) Mental phenomena neither of learners nor of non-learners.  
>
