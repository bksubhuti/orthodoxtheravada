### Overview
When monastics gather, the Buddha strictly forbade them from engaging in "animal talk" (gossip about kings, wars, women, and food). Instead, he mandated that all conversation must center on these ten profound subjects. The Buddha says that if the monks spoke on these ten topics, taking them one by one, they would outshine the splendor of even the sun and moon (AN 10.69).

### The List
1. **Appicchakathā** - Talk on wanting little: Discussing the virtues of having few desires.
2. **Santuṭṭhikathā** - Talk on contentment: Discussing being satisfied with whatever requisites are offered.
3. **Pavivekakathā** - Talk on seclusion: Discussing the physical and mental benefits of withdrawing from crowds.
4. **Asaṃsaggakathā** - Talk on aloofness / non-entanglement: Discussing the dangers of socializing and getting entangled with laypeople or other monks.
5. **Vīriyārambhakathā** - Talk on arousing energy: Discussing heroic effort and the overcoming of laziness.
6. **Sīlakathā** - Talk on virtue: Discussing the perfection of moral discipline.
7. **Samādhikathā** - Talk on concentration: Discussing the jhānas and the unification of mind.
8. **Paññākathā** - Talk on wisdom: Discussing insight into the arising and passing away of phenomena.
9. **Vimuttikathā** - Talk on liberation: Discussing the destruction of the fetters and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/).
10. **Vimuttiñāṇadassanakathā** - Talk on the knowledge and vision of liberation: Discussing the final confirmation of an [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/)'s awakening.

### Quote
> **“Dasayimāni, bhikkhave, kathāvatthūni. Katamāni dasa? Appicchakathā, santuṭṭhikathā, pavivekakathā, asaṃsaggakathā, vīriyārambhakathā, sīlakathā, samādhikathā, paññākathā, vimuttikathā, vimuttiñāṇadassanakathāti – imāni kho, bhikkhave, dasa kathāvatthūni.**  
> “Monks, these are ten topics for discussion. What are the ten? Talk on fewness of desires, talk on contentment, talk on seclusion, talk on non-entanglement, talk on arousing persistence, a discussion on virtuous behavior, a discussion on concentration, a discussion on wisdom, talk on liberation, and talk on the knowledge and vision of liberation. These, monks, are the ten topics for discussion.  
>
