### Overview
The Five Spiritual Faculties (*[Indriya](/glossary/indriya/)*) are the controlling mental factors that direct a practitioner toward awakening. For successful meditation, these faculties must be highly developed and carefully balanced. If one faculty overpowers its opposite, the mind falls off the path.

### The List
1. **Saddhindriya** - The Faculty of Faith: Deep, reasoned confidence in the Buddha's awakening. If strong while wisdom is weak, one is *muddhappasanna*, trusting in what is not worthy of trust (Vism-i 870:8).
2. **Vīriyindriya** - The Faculty of Energy: The effort exerted to overcome unwholesome states and arouse wholesome ones. If strong while concentration is weak, it falls into restlessness (*uddhacca*; Vism-i 870:12).
3. **Satindriya** - The Faculty of Mindfulness: Unwavering, continuous presence of mind. It is wanted everywhere, since it guards the mind against restlessness (from faith, energy and wisdom) and against laziness (from concentration) (Vism-i 870:23-24).
4. **Samādhindriya** - The Faculty of Concentration: The unification of the mind on a single object. If strong while energy is weak, it falls into laziness (*kosajja*; Vism-i 870:11).
5. **Paññindriya** - The Faculty of Wisdom: The penetrative insight into impermanence, suffering, and non-self. If strong while faith is weak, one goes to the side of the cunning (*kerāṭikapakkha*; Vism-i 870:9).

### Quote
> **“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṃ, vīriyindriyaṃ, satindriyaṃ, samādhindriyaṃ, paññindriyaṃ – imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyānī” ti.**  
> “Mendicants, there are these five faculties. What five? The faculty of faith, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, and the faculty of wisdom—these, indeed, mendicants, are the five faculties.”  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 48.1, Suddhika Sutta, Indriyasaṃyutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_05&page_number=170&name=mah%C4%81vaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFBhw7FjaW3EgW5pLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIGluZHJpecSBbmku)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Indriya Saṃyutta* (SN 48) – The collection of discourses on these five faculties; SN 48.1 is "1. Suddhikasuttaṃ" (S-v 1153:1).
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter IV, "Pathavīkasiṇaniddeso", Vism-i 870:1-24) – Explains the balancing of the faculties (*indriyasamattapaṭipādanaṃ*): faith with wisdom, energy with concentration, with mindfulness needed everywhere.
