### Overview
The 37 Factors of Awakening (*[Bodhipakkhiyadhamma](/glossary/bodhipakkhiyadhamma/)*) represent the grand synthesis of the Buddha's entire practical teaching. Shortly before his [Parinibbāna](/glossary/parinibbana/), the Buddha gathered the monks and declared that these specific dhammas, discovered by his own direct knowledge, must be thoroughly learned, practiced, and maintained to ensure the holy life lasts long in the world.

### The List
The 37 factors are an amalgamation of seven distinct sub-lists:
1. **The 4 Foundations of Mindfulness ([Satipaṭṭhāna](/glossary/satipatthana/)):** Mindfulness of body, feelings, mind, and phenomena. (4)
2. **The 4 Right Efforts ([Sammappadhāna](/glossary/sammappadhana/)):** The effort to prevent, abandon, develop, and maintain. (4)
3. **The 4 Bases of Spiritual Power ([Iddhipāda](/glossary/iddhipada/)):** Concentration driven by desire, energy, mind, and investigation. (4)
4. **The 5 Spiritual Faculties ([Indriya](/glossary/indriya/)):** Faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom as controlling faculties. (5)
5. **The 5 Spiritual Powers ([Bala](/glossary/bala/)):** The same five factors developed until they cannot be shaken by opposition. (5)
6. **The 7 Factors of Awakening ([Bojjhaṅga](/glossary/bojjhanga/)):** Mindfulness, investigation, energy, rapture, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity. (7)
7. **The Noble 8-Fold Path (Ariyaṭṭhaṅgika [Magga](/glossary/magga/)):** Right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, and concentration. (8)
*(4 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 8 = 37)*

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Mahāparinibbāna](/glossary/mahaparinibbana/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 16) – The Buddha formally entrusts these 37 factors to the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) as the essence of the dispensation.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XXII) – Explains how all 37 factors arise simultaneously in the mind at the exact moment of supramundane path-attainment.
