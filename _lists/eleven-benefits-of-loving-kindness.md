### Overview
Loving-kindness (*[Mettā](/glossary/metta/)*) is not merely a soft emotion; it is a powerful, protective, and highly rewarding mental force. The Buddha guaranteed that a practitioner who actively develops the liberation of the mind by loving-kindness until it becomes their vehicle and foundation will experience these eleven specific, worldly and spiritual benefits.

### The List
1. **Sukhaṃ supati** - One sleeps happily.
2. **Sukhaṃ paṭibujjhati** - One wakes happily.
3. **Na pāpakaṃ supinaṃ passati** - One does not see an evil dream.
4. **Manussānaṃ piyo hoti** - One is dear and appealing to human beings.
5. **Amanussānaṃ piyo hoti** - One is dear and appealing to non-human beings (devas, spirits).
6. **Devatā rakkhanti** - The deities actively protect them.
7. **Nāssa aggi vā visaṃ vā satthaṃ vā kamati** - Fire, poison, and weapons cannot harm them.
8. **Tuvaṭaṃ cittaṃ samādhiyati** - Their mind achieves deep concentration ([samādhi](/glossary/samadhi/)) very quickly.
9. **Mukhavaṇṇo vippasīdati** - Their complexion becomes bright, clear, and radiant.
10. **Asammūḷho kālaṃ karoti** - They die peacefully, completely unconfused and without terror.
11. **Uttari appaṭivijjhanto brahmalokūpago hoti** - If they do not penetrate higher (i.e., do not attain Arahatship in this life), they are guaranteed rebirth in the [Brahma](/glossary/brahma/) world.

### Quote
> **Katame ekādasa? Sukhaṃ supati, sukhaṃ paṭibujjhati, na pāpakaṃ supinaṃ passati, manussānaṃ piyo hoti, amanussānaṃ piyo hoti, devatā rakkhanti, nāssa aggi vā visaṃ vā satthaṃ vā kamati, tuvaṭaṃ cittaṃ samādhiyati, mukhavaṇṇo vippasīdati, asammūḷho kālaṃ karoti, uttari appaṭivijjhanto brahmalokūpago hoti.**  
> What eleven? One sleeps happily, one awakens happily, one sees no evil dream, one is pleasant to people, one is pleasant to non-human beings, the devas protect one, a fire, poison, or a weapon does not touch one, the mind is concentrated quickly, one's complexion clears, one dies unconfused, and if one does not penetrate any further, one is headed for the Brahma world.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 11.15, Mettā Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_11&page_number=542&name=ek%C4%81dasakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=S2F0YW1lIGVrxIFkYXNhPw%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mettā [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 11.15) – The Buddha tells the monks that eleven benefits are to be expected for one whose liberation of the mind by loving-kindness (*mettācetovimutti*) is cultivated and developed.
