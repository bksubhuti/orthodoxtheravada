### Overview
The Divine Abodes (*[Brahmavihāra](/glossary/brahmavihara/)*) are the four immeasurables (*appamaññā*) named in DN 33 (D-iii 1415:1), each pervading the world with a mind of that quality (D-iii 1415:2; A-v 1294:4-5). Developing them leads to rebirth in a Brahma-realm (A-v 1294:6, "cattāro brahmavihāre"). The Visuddhimagga (Chapter IX) gives the common purpose of the four: the happiness of insight and the success of existence (Vism-i 1941:1), and for each the function and the quality it removes (Vism-i 1940:1-8).

### The List
1. **[Mettā](/glossary/metta/)** - Loving-kindness: Its characteristic is the occurrence of a benevolent aspect (*hitākārappavatti*); its success is the subsiding of ill-will (*byāpāda*) (Vism-i 1940:1-2).
2. **[Karuṇā](/glossary/karuna/)** - Compassion: Its characteristic is the occurrence of the aspect of removing others' suffering (*dukkhāpanayanākārappavatti*); its success is the subsiding of cruelty (*vihiṃsā*) (Vism-i 1940:3-4).
3. **[Muditā](/glossary/mudita/)** - Sympathetic Joy: Its characteristic is gladness (*pamodana*), its function is non-envy (*anissāyana*); its success is the subsiding of discontent (*arati*) (Vism-i 1940:5-6).
4. **[Upekkhā](/glossary/upekkha/)** - Equanimity: Its characteristic is the occurrence of a neutral aspect towards beings (*majjhattākārappavatti*), seen with the thought "beings are the owners of their kamma"; its success is the subsiding of aversion and attachment (*paṭighānunaya*) (Vism-i 1940:7-8).

### Quote
> **“Catasso appamaññā . Idhāvuso, bhikkhu mettāsahagatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati. Karuṇāsahagatena cetasā … pe … muditāsahagatena cetasā … pe … upekkhāsahagatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati.**  
> “The four immeasurables. Here, a bhikkhu dwells pervading one quarter with a mind of loving-kindness. He dwells pervading one quarter with a mind of compassion… with a mind of sympathetic joy… with a mind of equanimity.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Saṅgīti [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 33, D-iii 1415:1-2) – "Catasso appamaññā", the four immeasurables. *Doṇabrāhmaṇa Sutta* (AN 5.192, A-v 1288:1; the passage at A-v 1294:4-6) – the four pervaded and called "cattāro brahmavihāre". The Mettā Sutta (Sn 1.8) is on loving-kindness only and does not list the four.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter IX, "9. Brahmavihāraniddeso", Vism-i 1736:1; the characteristics and purposes at Vism-i 1940:1-8, 1941:1-4) – The full meditation manual on the four subjects.
