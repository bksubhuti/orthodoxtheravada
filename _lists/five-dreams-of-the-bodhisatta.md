### Overview
Before his full awakening, while he was still only a Bodhisatta (*pubbeva sambodhā anabhisambuddhassa bodhisattasseva sato*), five great dreams (*mahāsupinā*) appeared to the Tathāgata. In the Mahāsupina Sutta (AN 5.196) the Buddha tells the monks the five dreams and what each foretold (*tassa abhisambodhāya*, "for that awakening").

### The List
1. **The Great Earth as a Bed:** The great earth was his great bed, the Himalaya, king of mountains, was his pillow, his left hand rested in the eastern sea, his right hand in the western sea, and both feet in the southern sea. *(Meaning: he awakened to the unsurpassed full awakening.)*
2. **The Tiriyā Grass from his Navel:** A grass called *tiriyā* rose from his navel and stood touching the sky. *(Meaning: having awakened to the noble eightfold path, he proclaimed it well to devas and humans.)*
3. **The White Worms covering his Legs:** White worms with black heads crawled up over his feet and covered his legs up to the knees. *(Meaning: many white-clothed householders went to him for refuge for life.)*
4. **The Four Birds of Different Colors:** Four birds of different colors came from the four directions, fell at his feet and all became white. *(Meaning: the four castes, khattiyas, brahmins, vessas and suddas, go forth in the Dhamma and Vinaya taught by the Tathāgata and realize the unsurpassed deliverance.)*
5. **Walking on a Mountain of Dung:** He walked back and forth on top of a great mountain of dung without being smeared by it. *(Meaning: he receives robes, almsfood, lodging and medicine, and uses them without being tied to them, infatuated or engrossed, seeing the danger and knowing the escape.)*

### Quote
> **“Tathāgatassa, bhikkhave, arahato sammāsambuddhassa pubbeva sambodhā anabhisambuddhassa bodhisattasseva sato pañca mahāsupinā pāturahesuṃ. Katame pañca? Tathāgatassa, bhikkhave, arahato sammāsambuddhassa pubbeva sambodhā anabhisambuddhassa bodhisattasseva sato ayaṃ mahāpathavī mahāsayanaṃ ahosi, himavā pabbatarājā bibbohanaṃ ahosi, puratthime samudde vāmo hattho ohito ahosi, pacchime samudde dakkhiṇo hattho ohito ahosi, dakkhiṇe samudde ubho pādā ohitā ahesuṃ.**  
> “Monks, before his full awakening, while he was still a Bodhisatta and only an unenlightened Bodhisatta, five great dreams appeared to the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the Perfectly Self-Awakened One. What five? Before the full awakening of the Tathāgata—the Arahant, the Perfectly Self-Awakened One—while he was still a Bodhisatta, this great earth was his great bed; the Himalaya, king of mountains, was his pillow; his left hand rested in the eastern sea; his right hand rested in the western sea; and both feet rested in the southern sea.  
>
