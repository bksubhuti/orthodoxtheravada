### Overview
While wholesome and unwholesome actions are driven by roots (greed, non-greed, etc.), much of our daily mental life consists of automated, mechanical cognition. These eighteen consciousnesses (*Ahetuka [Citta](/glossary/citta-base/)*) operate entirely without roots. They are primarily the passive results of past [kamma](/glossary/kamma/) (seeing, hearing) or mere functional processes.

### The List
**7 Unwholesome-Resultant Consciousnesses ([Akusala](/glossary/akusala/) Vipāka):**
1-4. Eye, ear, nose and tongue consciousness, each accompanied by equanimity.
5. Body-consciousness accompanied by pain.
6. Receiving consciousness accompanied by equanimity.
7. Investigating consciousness accompanied by equanimity.

**8 Wholesome-Resultant Rootless Consciousnesses ([Kusala](/glossary/kusala/) Vipāka):**
8-11. Eye, ear, nose and tongue consciousness, each accompanied by equanimity.
12. Body-consciousness accompanied by pleasure.
13. Receiving consciousness accompanied by equanimity.
14. Investigating consciousness accompanied by happiness.
15. Investigating consciousness accompanied by equanimity.

**3 Rootless Functional Consciousnesses (Ahetuka Kiriya):**
16. Five-sense-door adverting consciousness accompanied by equanimity.
17. Mind-door adverting consciousness accompanied by equanimity.
18. Smile-producing consciousness (*hasituppādacitta*) accompanied by happiness.

### Quote
> **Upekkhāsahagataṃ cakkhuviññāṇaṃ, tathā sotaviññāṇaṃ, ghānaviññāṇaṃ, jivhāviññāṇaṃ, dukkhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ, upekkhāsahagataṃ sampaṭicchanacittaṃ, upekkhāsahagataṃ santīraṇacittañceti imāni sattapi akusalavipākacittāni nāma. 10. Upekkhāsahagataṃ kusalavipākaṃ cakkhuviññāṇaṃ, tathā sotaviññāṇaṃ, ghānaviññāṇaṃ, jivhāviññāṇaṃ, sukhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ, upekkhāsahagataṃ sampaṭicchanacittaṃ, somanassasahagataṃ santīraṇacittaṃ, upekkhāsahagataṃ santīraṇacittañceti imāni aṭṭhapi kusalavipākāhetukacittāni nāma. 11. Upekkhāsahagataṃ pañcadvārāvajjanacittaṃ, tathā manodvārāvajjanacittaṃ, somanassasahagataṃ hasituppādacittañceti imāni tīṇipi ahetukakiriyacittāni nāma. 12. Icceva sabbathāpi aṭṭhārasāhetukacittāni samattāni.**  
> 9. Eye-consciousness accompanied by equanimity (cakkhuviññāṇaṃ); likewise ear-consciousness (sotaviññāṇaṃ), nose-consciousness (ghānaviññāṇaṃ), tongue-consciousness (jivhāviññāṇaṃ); body-consciousness accompanied by pain (dukkhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ); receiving consciousness accompanied by equanimity; and investigating consciousness accompanied by equanimity. These seven are called unwholesome-resultant consciousnesses. 10. Eye-consciousness accompanied by equanimity, which is a wholesome resultant; likewise ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness; body-consciousness accompanied by pleasure (sukhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ); receiving consciousness accompanied by equanimity; investigating consciousness accompanied by happiness; and investigating consciousness accompanied by equanimity. These eight are called rootless wholesome-resultant consciousnesses. 11. Five-sense-door adverting consciousness accompanied by equanimity; likewise mind-door adverting consciousness; and smile-producing consciousness accompanied by happiness. These three are called rootless functional consciousnesses. 12. Thus, in every way, the eighteen types of rootless consciousness (aṭṭhārasāhetukacittāni) are complete.
