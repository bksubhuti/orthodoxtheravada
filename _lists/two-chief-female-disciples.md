### Overview
Mirroring the structure of the male order, the [Bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/) [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) features its own two designated Chief Female Disciples (*Aggasāvikā*). The Buddhavaṃsa names them as Gotama Buddha's chief female disciples (Bv 3142:1).

### The List
1. **Khemā:** Formerly the chief queen of King Bimbisāra, intoxicated with her own beauty; the Buddha created a celestial maiden who aged and collapsed before her eyes (Mp-i 1067-1068). She was foremost among nun disciples of great wisdom (A-i 220:1, *[Paññā](/glossary/panna/)*).
2. **Uppalavaṇṇā:** Named for a complexion like the interior of a blue lotus (Mp-i 1075:1). She was foremost among nun disciples with psychic power (A-i 221:1, *[Iddhividha](/glossary/iddhividha/)*).

### Quote
> **Khemā uppalavaṇṇā ca, bhikkhunī aggasāvikā.**  
> Khemā and Uppalavaṇṇā were the chief female disciples.  
>  
> — [Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/) (Bv 27, Gotamabuddhavaṃsa) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_12&page_number=381&name=buddhava%E1%B9%83sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=S2hlbcSBIHVwcGFsYXZh4bmH4bmHxIEgY2EsIGJoaWtraHVuxKsgYWdnYXPEgXZpa8SBLg%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Etadagga Vagga* (AN 1, A-i 220:1 and 221:1) – Khemā is foremost of those of great wisdom, Uppalavaṇṇā of those with psychic power. Their stories are told in the Manorathapūraṇī (Mp-i 1065-1077).
* **Canonical**: *Dukanipāta* (A-ii 232:3) – The two are named together as the scale and measure for bhikkhunīs.
