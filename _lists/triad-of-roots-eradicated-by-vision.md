### Overview
The ninth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā builds directly on the eighth, but instead of classifying the general mental states, it specifically categorizes the underlying *roots* (*hetu*—greed, hatred, delusion) driving those states, tracking exactly when these roots are permanently severed by the supramundane paths.

### The List
1. **Dassanena pahātabbahetukā dhammā** - States having roots to be abandoned by vision: The greed, hatred and delusion unified with the three fetters, identity view, uncertainty and adherence to rites and rituals (Dhs 1017), together with the associated defilements, aggregates and actions. The first path abandons them.
2. **Bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā dhammā** - States having roots to be abandoned by cultivation: The remaining greed, hatred and delusion (*avaseso lobho, doso, moho*, Dhs 1018), together with the associated defilements, aggregates and actions.
3. **Neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā dhammā** - States having roots to be abandoned by neither vision nor cultivation: Leaving those aside, all the remaining wholesome, unwholesome and indeterminate states of the sensuous, form, formless and unincluded planes, the aggregates, all materiality and the unconditioned element (Dhs 1019).

### Quote
> **(Ka) dassanena pahātabbahetukā dhammā. (Kha) bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā dhammā. (Ga) neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā dhammā.**  
> 9.(Ka) Mental phenomena having causes eradicated by the First Path. (Kha) Mental phenomena having causes eradicated by the Higher Three Paths. (Ga) Mental phenomena having causes eradicated neither by the First Path nor by the Higher Three Paths.  
>
