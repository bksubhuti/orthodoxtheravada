### Overview
The Buddha told King Pasenadi of Kosala that four things should not be looked down upon or disparaged because they are young (*daharāti na uññātabbā, daharāti na paribhotabbā*). The verses that follow give the reason for each.

### The List
1. **A young prince (Khattiya):** A well-born, famous aristocrat should not be despised for being young. He may come to gain the throne, and in his anger he will lash out with royal punishment, so one should avoid him to protect one's life.
2. **A small snake (Uraṅga):** A serpent should not be despised for being young. It glides along powerfully and may bite a fool, man or woman, at any time, so one should avoid it to protect one's life.
3. **A small fire (Aggi):** A fire should not be despised for being young. Once it has obtained fuel it becomes a huge fire and may burn a fool, man or woman, at any time, so one should avoid it to protect one's life.
4. **A young monk ([Bhikkhu](/glossary/bhikkhu/)):** A bhikkhu possessed of virtue (*sīlasampanno*) should not be despised for being young. When he burns with his power, those who offended him have no children, livestock or heirs to find wealth, and become like palm-tree stumps (SN 3.1, verses after the quote). The verses end by saying that the wise person should treat the serpent, the fire, the famous aristocrat and the virtuous bhikkhu properly.

### Quote
> **“Cattāro kho me, mahārāja, daharāti na uññātabbā, daharāti na paribhotabbā. Katame cattāro? Khattiyo kho, mahārāja, daharoti na uññātabbo, daharoti na paribhotabbo. Urago kho, mahārāja, daharoti na uññātabbo, daharoti na paribhotabbo. Aggi kho, mahārāja, daharoti na uññātabbo, daharoti na paribhotabbo. Bhikkhu, kho, mahārāja, daharoti na uññātabbo, daharoti na paribhotabbo. Ime kho, mahārāja, cattāro daharāti na uññātabbā, daharāti na paribhotabbā” ti.**  
> “Great King, these four should not be looked down upon or disparaged because they are young. What four? An aristocrat, Great King, should not be looked down upon or disparaged because he is young. A serpent, Great King, should not be looked down upon or disparaged because it is young. A fire, Great King, should not be looked down upon or disparaged because it is young. A bhikkhu, Great King, should not be looked down upon or disparaged because he is young. These four, Great King, should not be looked down upon or disparaged because they are young.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Dahara [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (SN 3.1) – King Pasenadi asks the Buddha if he claims full awakening despite being young. The Buddha replies with these four examples of things that must never be despised for their youth.
