### Overview
The Stations of Mastery (*Abhibhāyatana*) are advanced meditative attainments where a practitioner completely masters their perception of physical forms. The Aṭṭhakathā explains them as the causes of mastering both the opposing states and the objects (Sumaṅgalavilāsinī). Four are on limited or immeasurable forms, beautiful or ugly; four are on the pure colours blue, yellow, red and white.

### The List
1. **Perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally, limited, beautiful or ugly:** One masters small external objects while still maintaining internal perception of form.
2. **Perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally, immeasurable, beautiful or ugly:** One masters vast external objects while still maintaining internal perception of form.
3. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally, limited, beautiful or ugly:** One masters small external objects without any internal perception of form.
4. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally, immeasurable, beautiful or ugly:** One masters vast external objects without any internal perception of form.
5. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally as blue (nīla):** One attains mastery over the blue kasina.
6. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally as yellow (pīta):** One attains mastery over the yellow kasina.
7. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally as red (lohita):** One attains mastery over the red kasina.
8. **Not perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally as white (odāta):** One attains mastery over the white kasina.

### Quote
> **“Aṭṭha kho imāni, ānanda, abhibhāyatanāni. Katamāni aṭṭha? Ajjhattaṃ rūpasaññī eko bahiddhā rūpāni passati parittāni suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇāni. ‘ Tāni abhibhuyya jānāmi passāmī ’ ti evaṃsaññī hoti. Idaṃ paṭhamaṃ abhibhāyatanaṃ.**  
> “Ānanda, there are these eight stations of mastery. Which eight? Perceiving form internally, one sees forms externally, limited, beautiful or ugly; having mastered them, one perceives thus: ‘I know, I see.’ This is the first station of mastery. (tr. Claude)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Mahāparinibbāna](/glossary/mahaparinibbana/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 16) – The Buddha explains these stations to Ānanda shortly before his passing.
* **Commentary**: *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī* (Aṭṭhaabhibhāyatanavaṇṇanā) – Explains each station, and refers to the *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* for the kasina practice.
