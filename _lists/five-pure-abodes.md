### Overview
The Pure Abodes (*Suddhāvāsā*) are five realms of the Fine-Material World (Rūpa-loka) in which Non-returners (*Anāgāmīs*) are reborn. The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha-ṭīkā says that Non-returners, having developed the fifth jhāna, arise in the five Pure Abodes according to the gradation of the faculties beginning with faith (*saddhādiindriyavemattatānukkamena*, Abhidh-s-t 1388:1). Those reborn there are of the kinds who attain parinibbāna without returning (e.g. *antarāparinibbāyī*, Mp-vii 817:1).

### The List
1. **Avihā** - "They do not fall from their own success and do not decline" (Vibh-a 2312:2).
2. **Atappā** - "They torment no being" (Vibh-a 2312:3).
3. **Sudassā** - "Beautiful in appearance, handsome, pleasing" (Vibh-a 2312:4).
4. **Sudassī** - "They see well, or their seeing is beautiful" (Vibh-a 2312:5).
5. **Akaniṭṭhā** - "Eldest in all qualities and in the success of existence; none here is youngest" (Vibh-a 2312:6).

### Quote
> **“Pañca suddhāvāsā – avihā, atappā, sudassā, sudassī, akaniṭṭhā.**  
> “There are five Pure Abodes: Avihā, Atappā, Sudassā, Sudassī, and Akaniṭṭha.”  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 33, Saṅgīti Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_03&page_number=198&name=p%C4%81thikavaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MzE4LuKAmOKAmCBQYcOxY2Egc3VkZGjEgXbEgXPEgSDigJMgYXZpaMSBLCBhdGFwcMSBLA%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Saṅgīti [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 33, D-iii 1486:1, para 318) – The list as quoted above.
* **Canonical**: *Mahāpadāna [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 14) – The Buddha tells how he went to the Avihā devas, where many devas came to him and told him of the earlier Buddhas (D-ii 220:4-8).
* **Commentary**: *Vibhaṅga Aṭṭhakathā* (Vibh-a 2312:2-6) explains the five names; *Itivuttaka Aṭṭhakathā* (It-a 252:2) says that those who arise in Avihā and the others are of five kinds of Non-returners by place of rebirth.
