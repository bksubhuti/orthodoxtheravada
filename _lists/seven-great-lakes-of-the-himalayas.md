### Overview
The Aṭṭhakathā describe the Himālaya as five hundred yojanas high, adorned with eighty-four thousand peaks and five hundred rivers, and name seven great lakes (*mahāsarā*) in it, each fifty yojanas in length, breadth and depth and one hundred and fifty yojanas in circumference. The list is commentarial; the seven names do not occur in the Mūla texts as a list.

### The List
1. **Anotatta** - Lake Anotatta: encircled by five peaks (Sudassana, Cittakūṭa, Kāḷakūṭa, Gandhamādana, Kelāsa), so that the sun and moon light it only through the gaps between the mountains. Buddhas, Paccekabuddhas, powerful disciples and sages bathe there (Ud-a 1185-1186).
2. **Kaṇṇamuṇḍa** - Lake Kaṇṇamuṇḍa.
3. **Rathakāra** - Lake Rathakāra.
4. **Chaddanta** - Lake Chaddanta.
5. **Kuṇāla** - Lake Kuṇāla.
6. **Mandākinī** - Lake Mandākinī.
7. **Sīhapapāta** - Lake Sīhapapāta (spelt Sīhappapāta in Mp-viii 92:2).

### Quote
> **Ayaṃ tāva jambudīpo dasasahassayojanaparimāṇo, tattha catusahassayojanaparimāṇo padeso udakena ajjhotthaṭo mahāsamuddoti saṅkhaṃ gato, tisahassayojanappamāṇe manussā vasanti, tisahassayojanappamāṇe himavā patiṭṭhito ubbedhena pañcayojanasatiko caturāsītikūṭasahassapaṭimaṇḍito samantato sandamānapañcasatanadīvicitto, yattha āyāmavitthārena ca gambhīrato ca paṇṇāsapaṇṇāsayojanā diyaḍḍhayojanasataparimaṇḍalā anotattadaho kaṇṇamuṇḍadaho rathakāradaho chaddantadaho kuṇāladaho mandākinidaho sīhappapātadahoti satta mahāsarā patiṭṭhahanti.**  
> First, this Jambudīpa is ten thousand yojanas in extent. In it, a region of four thousand yojanas is covered by water and has come to be known as the great ocean. In an area of three thousand yojanas, humans dwell. In an area of three thousand yojanas, the Himalaya is established, being five hundred yojanas in height, adorned with eighty-four thousand peaks, and varied with five hundred rivers flowing all around. In this Himalaya, seven great lakes are established—Anotatta Lake, Kaṇṇamuṇḍa Lake, Rathakāra Lake, Chaddanta Lake, Kuṇāla Lake, Mandākinī Lake, and Sīhappapāta Lake—each being fifty by fifty yojanas in length, breadth, and depth, and one hundred and fifty yojanas in circumference.  
>
