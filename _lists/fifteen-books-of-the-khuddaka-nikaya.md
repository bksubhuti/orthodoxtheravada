### Overview
The Khuddaka Nikāya (The Collection of Minor Texts) is the fifth and final collection of the [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/) Piṭaka. Despite its name, it contains some of the most profound, beloved, and ancient texts in the entire Theravāda canon, including poetry, spontaneous utterances, and past-life stories.

### The List
1. **[Khuddakapāṭha](/glossary/khuddakapatha/)** - The Minor Readings (A handbook for novices).
2. **[Dhammapada](/glossary/dhammapada/)** - The Path of Truth (The most famous collection of the Buddha's verses).
3. **[Udāna](/glossary/udana/)** - Inspired Utterances (Spontaneous verses of joy).
4. **[Itivuttaka](/glossary/itivuttaka/)** - The "Thus it was said" Discourses.
5. **[Suttanipāta](/glossary/suttanipata/)** - The Group of Discourses (Contains highly ancient, archaic texts).
6. **[Vimānavatthu](/glossary/vimanavatthu/)** - Stories of the Celestial Mansions (Heavenly karma).
7. **[Petavatthu](/glossary/petavatthu/)** - Stories of the Hungry Ghosts (Hellish karma).
8. **[Theragāthā](/glossary/theragatha/)** - Verses of the Elder Monks (Poetry of awakened monks).
9. **[Therīgāthā](/glossary/therigatha/)** - Verses of the Elder Nuns (Poetry of awakened nuns).
10. **[Jātaka](/glossary/jataka/)** - Birth Stories (Tales of the Buddha's previous lives).
11. **[Niddesa](/glossary/niddesa/)** - The Exposition (An ancient commentary on the Suttanipāta).
12. **[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)** - The Path of Discrimination (An Abhidhamma-style analytical text).
13. **[Apadāna](/glossary/apadana/)** - The Biographical Stories (Biographies of the Arahats).
14. **[Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)** - The Chronicle of Buddhas (Lineage of the 24 past Buddhas).
15. **[Cariyāpiṭaka](/glossary/cariyapitaka/)** - The Basket of Conduct (Stories illustrating the [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/)'s Perfections).

### Textual References
