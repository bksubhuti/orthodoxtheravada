### Overview
To achieve access concentration and full absorption (*[jhāna](/glossary/jhana/)*), a meditator's environment and daily habits must be perfectly calibrated. In the Visuddhimagga (Chapter IV, "Sattasappāyā"), Venerable Buddhaghosa lists seven "suitabilities" (*sappāya*). One should avoid the seven unsuitable ones and cultivate the suitable ones, for so practising, absorption comes before long (Vism-i 856-859, 866).

### The List
1. **Āvāsa-sappāya** - Suitable Abode: A dwelling where the sign (*nimitta*) arises and becomes stable, mindfulness is established and the mind concentrates; one may stay three days in each of several dwellings to find it (Vism-i 860).
2. **Gocara-sappāya** - Suitable Alms Resort: An alms village not far, within about one and a half *kosa* to the north or south of the lodging, where alms-food is easily obtained (Vism-i 861).
3. **Bhassa-sappāya** - Suitable Speech: Talk that falls under the thirty-two kinds of animal talk (*tiracchānakathā*) is unsuitable; talk based on the ten topics of talk (*dasakathāvatthu*) is suitable, and even that in measure (Vism-i 862).
4. **Puggala-sappāya** - Suitable Persons: A person who does not engage in animal talk and has virtue and other qualities, relying on whom the unconcentrated mind concentrates or the concentrated mind becomes steadier; one who is bodily fussy and talks animal talk is unsuitable (Vism-i 863).
5. **Bhojana-sappāya** - Suitable Food: One person finds sweet food suitable, another sour; the suitable food is that which, taken, brings ease and the mind concentrates or becomes steadier (Vism-i 864).
6. **Utu-sappāya** - Suitable Climate: To one person cool weather is suitable, to another warm; the suitable weather is that which, followed, brings ease and the same result for the mind (Vism-i 864).
7. **Iriyāpatha-sappāya** - Suitable Posture: To one person walking is suitable, to another lying, standing or sitting; examine for three days, and the posture in which the mind concentrates or becomes steadier is the suitable one (Vism-i 865).

### Quote
> **Tatrāyaṃ rakkhaṇavidhi – Āvāso gocaro bhassaṃ, puggalo bhojanaṃ utu; Iriyāpathoti sattete, asappāye vivajjaye. Sappāye satta sevetha, evañhi paṭipajjato; Nacireneva kālena, hoti kassaci appanā.**  
> 59. Herein, this is the method of guarding: Abode, resort, speech, person, food, weather, and posture—these seven, if unsuitable, should be avoided. One should cultivate the seven suitable ones; for to one practicing in this way, before long, absorption will occur.
