### Overview
In the same sutta, as the counterpart of the five causes of decline, the Buddha names five things that lead to the continuation, persistence and non-disappearance of the true [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) (*saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammosāya anantaradhānāya saṃvattanti*, A-v 1059:1). Each is the opposite of an item in the decline list (A-v 1054-1058).

### The List
1. **Suggahitaṃ suttantaṃ pariyāpuṇanti sunikkhittehi padabyañjanehi** - Monks memorize discourses learned correctly, with well-placed words and phrases; when the words and phrases are well placed, the meaning is correctly interpreted (A-v 1059:3-4).
2. **Bhikkhū suvacā honti** - Monks are easy to admonish, have qualities that make them easy to admonish, are patient, and take instruction respectfully (A-v 1060:1).
3. **Bahussutā … sakkaccaṃ suttantaṃ paraṃ vācenti** - The learned monks (bahussutā āgatāgamā dhammadharā vinayadharā mātikādharā) carefully make others recite the discourse; after their passing the discourse is not cut off at the root and has a refuge (A-v 1061:1-2).
4. **Therā bhikkhū na bāhulikā honti na sāthalikā** - Elder monks are not indulgent or slack, and are leaders in seclusion; they rouse energy for attaining the unattained, and those who come after them follow their example (A-v 1062:1-3).
5. **Saṅgho samaggo sammodamāno avivadamāno ekuddeso phāsuṃ viharati** - The Saṅgha lives in harmony, appreciating each other, without disputes, with a single recitation, so that the unconfident gain confidence and the confident grow in it (A-v 1063:1-3).

### Quote
> **“Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhammā saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammosāya anantaradhānāya saṃvattanti. Katame pañca? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhū suggahitaṃ suttantaṃ pariyāpuṇanti sunikkhittehi padabyañjanehi. Sunikkhittassa, bhikkhave, padabyañjanassa atthopi sunayo hoti. Ayaṃ, bhikkhave, paṭhamo dhammo saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammosāya anantaradhānāya saṃvattati.**  
> “Mendicants, these five things lead to the continuation, persistence, and enduring of the true Dhamma. What five? It’s when, mendicants, the monks memorize discourses that have been learned correctly, with well-placed words and phrases. When the words and phrases are well organized, mendicants, the meaning is correctly interpreted. This, mendicants, is the first thing that leads to the continuation, persistence, and enduring of the true Dhamma.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 5.156, Tatiya Saddhammasammosa Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_05&page_number=158&name=pa%C3%B1cakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFBhw7FjaW1lLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIGRoYW1txIEgc2FkZGhhbW1hc3NhIOG5rWhpdGl5xIE%3D)
