### Overview
Directly countering the roots of decline, the Buddha outlined the five defining virtues that successfully anchor and prolong the life of the true [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/). Monks who embody these five traits serve as the absolute defenders of the dispensation, keeping the light of truth shining clearly in the world.

### The List
1. **Flawless Scriptural Mastery:** He memorizes and studies the discourses with perfect precision, grasping the exact Pāḷi grammatical structures and contextual meaning.
2. **Perfect Teachability:** He is humble, cooperative, and easy to advise, gratefully accepting the corrections and guidance of wise elders.
3. **Active Mentorship:** He takes his institutional duties seriously, tirelessly instructioning his novices and students to ensure scriptural literacy continues.
4. **Protecting the Lineage:** He organizes regular recitations and memory circles to keep the complex matrix of the [Tipiṭaka](/glossary/tipitaka/) alive in human memory.
5. **Radical Simplicity:** He remains content with basic requisites, strictly avoids material hoarding, and devotes his nights and days to deep insight meditation.

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Sammosa [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 5.156) – The Buddha details these five protective factors that prevent the confusion and disappearance of the true teaching.
