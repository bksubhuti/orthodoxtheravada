### Overview
Time is cyclical in Theravāda cosmology, measured in vast cosmic cycles called *Kappas* (Aeons). Most aeons are dark, empty cycles where no Buddha arises. However, our current aeon is an exceptionally rare, auspicious cycle known as a *Bhaddakappa* (Fortunate Aeon) because exactly five Fully Awakened Buddhas are destined to manifest within it, illuminating the universe.

### The List
1. **Kakusandha Buddha:** The first Buddha of our aeon, his lifespan was forty thousand years (D-ii 13:4); a brahmin of the Kassapa clan.
2. **Koṇāgamana Buddha:** The second Buddha of our aeon, his lifespan was thirty thousand years (D-ii 13:5); a brahmin of the Kassapa clan.
3. **Kassapa Buddha:** The third Buddha of our aeon, his lifespan was twenty thousand years (D-ii 13:6); a brahmin of the Kassapa clan.
4. **Gotama Buddha:** Our Buddha, who speaks of himself in DN 14 (D-ii 10:7): his lifespan is short, "he who lives long lives a hundred years, or a little more" (D-ii 13:7-8); a khattiya of the Gotama clan.
5. **[Metteyya](/glossary/metteyya/) Buddha:** The future Buddha: "among humans living 80,000 years, a Blessed One named Metteyya will arise in the world" (Cakkavatti Sutta, DN 26, D-iii 247:1); the Aṭṭhakathā says "metteyyo uppajjissati" (Sv-ii 16:14).

### Quote
> **Imasmiṃ bhaddakappe kakusandho, koṇāgamano, kassapo, gotamo amhākaṃ sammāsambuddhoti cattāro buddhā uppannā, metteyyo uppajjissati. Evamayaṃ kappo pañcabuddhuppādapaṭimaṇḍitattā sundarakappo sārakappoti bhagavā imaṃ kappaṃ thomento evamāha.**  
> In this auspicious aeon, four Buddhas—Kakusandha, Koṇāgamana, Kassapa, and our Blessed One, Gotama—have arisen, and Metteyya will arise. The Blessed One, praising this aeon, said: thus this aeon is an auspicious aeon, an excellent aeon, adorned by the arising of five Buddhas.  
>  
> — Sumaṅgalavilāsinī (Dīgha-aṭṭhakathā, Aṭṭhakathā layer) on the Mahāpadāna Sutta (DN 14) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_di_02&page_number=5&name=mah%C4%81vagga%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=SW1hc21p4bmDIGJoYWRkYWthcHBlIGtha3VzYW5kaG8sIGtv4bmHxIFnYW1hbm8sIGthc3NhcG8sIGdvdGFtbw%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mahāpadāna [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 14) – The Buddha recounts six past Buddhas (Vipassī, Sikhī, Vessabhū, Kakusandha, Koṇāgamana, Kassapa) and himself, with their clans, lifespans and so on; the first three of this list are named there as arisen "imasmiññeva bhaddakappe" (D-ii 10:4-7). Metteyya is not named in DN 14; he is in the Cakkavatti Sutta, DN 26 (D-iii 247). The Aṭṭhakathā (Sv-ii 15:1, 16:14-15) gives the count of five for this aeon.
