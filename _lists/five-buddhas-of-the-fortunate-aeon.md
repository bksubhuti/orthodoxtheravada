### Overview
Time is cyclical in Theravāda cosmology, measured in vast cosmic cycles called *Kappas* (Aeons). Most aeons are dark, empty cycles where no Buddha arises. However, our current aeon is an exceptionally rare, auspicious cycle known as a *Bhaddakappa* (Fortunate Aeon) because exactly five Fully Awakened Buddhas are destined to manifest within it, illuminating the universe.

### The List
1. **Kakusandha Buddha:** The first Buddha of our aeon, appearing when human lifespans spanned forty thousand years.
2. **Koṇāgamana Buddha:** The second Buddha of our aeon, appearing when human lifespans spanned thirty thousand years.
3. **Kassapa Buddha:** The third Buddha of our aeon, appearing when human lifespans spanned twenty thousand years.
4. **Gotama Buddha:** Our historical Buddha. He appeared during a time of decline when human lifespans spanned a mere hundred years, discovering the path amidst intense ignorance.
5. **[Metteyya](/glossary/metteyya/) Buddha:** The future Buddha. He currently resides in the Tusita heaven, waiting for the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) of Gotama Buddha to completely disappear and human lifespans to cycle back up before descending for his final rebirth.

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mahāpadāna [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 14) – The Buddha details the lineages, trees, and lifespans of the prominent Buddhas of the past.
