### Overview
The Buddha pointed out two types of individuals who are hard to find in the world: the one who is first to do a kindness, and the one who is grateful and thankful for a kindness done.

### The List
1. **Pubbakārī** - The first doer / The initiator of help: One who does a kindness first (*paṭhamaṃ upakārassa kārako*). The Aṭṭhakathā says he makes the perception, “I give a debt” (*iṇaṃ demī*) (Mp-ii 245:3).
2. **Kataññūkatavedī** - The grateful and reciprocating one: One who, knowing what was done for him, does a kindness afterwards (*tena kataṃ ñatvā pacchā kārako*). The Aṭṭhakathā says he makes the perception, “I will cause the debt to be discharged” (*iṇaṃ jīrāpemī*) (Mp-ii 245:2-3).

### Quote
> **“Dveme, bhikkhave, puggalā dullabhā lokasmiṃ. Katame dve? Yo ca pubbakārī, yo ca kataññū katavedī. Ime kho, bhikkhave, dve puggalā dullabhā lokasmin” ti.**  
> “Monks, these two individuals are hard to find in the world. Which two? The one who is first to do a kindness, and the one who is grateful and thankful for a kindness done. Monks, these two individuals are hard to find in the world.”  
>
