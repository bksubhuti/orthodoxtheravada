### Overview
The Five Strands of Sensual Pleasure (*Kāmaguṇā*) are five classes of sense object: forms, sounds, odours, flavours and tangibles that are wanted, lovely, pleasant, agreeable, connected with sensual desire and provocative of lust. In MN 13 the pleasure and happiness arising dependent on them is called the gratification of sensual pleasures (M-i 509:5), and the danger and the escape follow. Elsewhere the Buddha says: "Saṅkapparāgo purisassa kāmo" (S-i 417:1; A-vi 622:1), "Man's sensuality is the intention of lust", and Sn 586:1 begins "Pañca kāmaguṇā loke".

### The List
1. **Rūpā** - Forms / Sights: Forms cognizable by the eye that are *iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṃhitā rajanīyā* (wanted, lovely, pleasant, agreeable, connected with sensual desire, provocative of lust).
2. **Saddā** - Sounds: Sounds cognizable by the ear (*sotaviññeyyā saddā*), of the same kind.
3. **Gandhā** - Odors / Smells: Odours cognizable by the nose (*ghānaviññeyyā gandhā*), of the same kind.
4. **Rasā** - Tastes: Flavours cognizable by the tongue (*jivhāviññeyyā rasā*), of the same kind.
5. **Phoṭṭhabbā** - Tactile sensations: Tangibles cognizable by the body (*kāyaviññeyyā phoṭṭhabbā*), of the same kind.

### Quote
> **Pañcime, bhikkhave, kāmaguṇā. Katame pañca? Cakkhuviññeyyā rūpā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṃhitā rajanīyā, sotaviññeyyā saddā … pe … ghānaviññeyyā gandhā … jivhāviññeyyā rasā … kāyaviññeyyā phoṭṭhabbā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṃhitā rajanīyā – ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca kāmaguṇā.**  
> Monks, there are these five cords of sensual pleasure. What are the five? Forms cognizable by the eye that are wanted, lovely, pleasant, agreeable, connected with sensual desire, and provocative of lust. Sounds cognizable by the ear… Odours cognizable by the nose… Flavours cognizable by the tongue… Tangibles cognizable by the body that are wanted, lovely, pleasant, agreeable, connected with sensual desire, and provocative of lust—these, monks, are the five cords of sensual pleasure.  
>
