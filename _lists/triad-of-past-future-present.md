### Overview
The eighteenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā classifies states as past, future or present. The Dhammasaṅgaṇī states that, [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) excepted, all states may be past, future or present, and that Nibbāna should not be called past, future or present (Dhs 2231:1-2).

### The List
1. **Atītā dhammā** - Past states: those that are past, ceased, vanished, changed, set, having arisen and vanished, included in the past portion: form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness (Dhs 1812:2). Aṭṭhakathā: those that have passed after attaining their own nature or the moment of arising and so on (As 868:1).
2. **Anāgatā dhammā** - Future states: those that are unborn, not become, not produced, included in the future portion: the five aggregates (Dhs 1813:2). Aṭṭhakathā: those that have not reached either of those (As 868:2).
3. **Paccuppannā dhammā** - Present states: those that are born, become, produced, arisen, included in the present portion: the five aggregates (Dhs 1814:2). Aṭṭhakathā: those that have arisen dependent on such and such a cause (As 868:3).

### Quote
> **(Ka) atītā dhammā. (Kha) anāgatā dhammā. (Ga) paccuppannā dhammā.**  
> 18.(Ka) Past mental phenomena. (Kha) Future mental phenomena. (Ga) Present mental phenomena.  
>
