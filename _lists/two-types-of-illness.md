### Overview
The Buddha categorized affliction into two distinct domains. While humans spend vast amounts of time and resources trying to cure physical ailments, they remain entirely ignorant of the chronic mental disease that afflicts everyone except an [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/).

### The List
1. **Kāyika-roga** - Physical Illness: Ailments affecting the physical body (*[rūpa](/glossary/rupa/)*). The Buddha notes that people can enjoy freedom from physical illness for a year, ten years, or even a hundred years.
2. **Cetasika-roga** - Mental Illness: Ailments affecting the mind, specifically the presence of defilements (greed, hatred, delusion). The Buddha states that it is hard to find anyone in the world free from mental illness for even a single moment, except for those whose mental taints are destroyed (Arahats).

### Quote
> **“Dveme, bhikkhave, rogā. Katame dve? Kāyiko ca rogo cetasiko ca rogo. Te, bhikkhave, sattā sudullabhā lokasmiṃ ye cetasikena rogena muhuttampi ārogyaṃ paṭijānanti, aññatra khīṇāsavehi.**  
> “Monks, there are these two kinds of disease (rogo). What two? Physical disease and mental disease. Physical disease and mental disease. But those beings in the world are hard to find who claim to be healthy in the case of mental disease even for a moment, apart from those whose defilements are destroyed.  
>
