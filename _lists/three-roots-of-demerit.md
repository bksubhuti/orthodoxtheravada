### Overview
*(Note: This list is identical in its ultimate doctrinal components to item 24, "The 3 Roots of Unwholesome Action." In the broader schema of the Suttas and [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), it functions as the definitive diagnostic triad for identifying how demerit (*[akusala](/glossary/akusala/)*) is birthed within the mind.)*

### The List
1. **[Lobha](/glossary/lobha/)** - Greed: The mental factor of attraction and clinging, pulling objects into the ego-continuum.
2. **[Dosa](/glossary/dosa/)** - Hatred: The mental factor of aversion and violence, pushing objects away with anger.
3. **[Moha](/glossary/moha/)** - Delusion: The foundational spiritual blindness that prevents the mind from perceiving the Four Noble Truths.

### Quote
> **“Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, akusalamūlāni. Katamāni tīṇi? “Yadapi, bhikkhave, lobho tadapi akusalamūlaṃ; yadapi luddho abhisaṅkharoti kāyena vācāya manasā tadapi akusalaṃ; yadapi luddho lobhena abhibhūto pariyādinnacitto parassa asatā dukkhaṃ uppādayati vadhena vā bandhanena vā jāniyā vā garahāya vā pabbājanāya vā balavamhi balattho itipi tadapi akusalaṃ. Itissame lobhajā lobhanidānā lobhasamudayā lobhapaccayā aneke pāpakā akusalā dhammā sambhavanti.**  
> “Monks, there are these three roots of the unwholesome. What are these three? “Monks, greed itself is a root of the unwholesome; whatever a greedy person generates by means of body, speech, or mind, that too is unwholesome; whatever suffering a greedy person—his mind overcome by greed, his mind obsessed by it—wrongly inflicts on another person through beating, imprisonment, confiscation, placing blame, or banishment, thinking, ‘I have power. I want power,’ that too is unwholesome. Thus, many evil, unwholesome states—born of greed, originating from greed, arising from greed, conditioned by greed—come to be.  
>
