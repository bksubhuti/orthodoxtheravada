### Overview
The seventh triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā classifies states as accompanied by rapture (*[pīti](/glossary/piti/)*), by pleasant feeling (*[sukha](/glossary/sukha/)*), or by equanimity (*[upekkhā](/glossary/upekkha/)*). The Aṭṭhakathā says *upekkhā* here means neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling, and that the triad takes two terms from the triad of feeling to show the difference between pleasure without rapture and pleasure with rapture (As 857:1-5).

### The List
1. **Pītisahagatā dhammā** - States accompanied by rapture: the states in the plane of rapture (*pītibhūmi*) of the sense, fine-material and supramundane spheres, with the rapture itself excluded; the associated feeling, perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1771:2-3). *Pītisahagatā* = gone together with rapture, i.e. associated with it (As 857:1).
2. **Sukhasahagatā dhammā** - States accompanied by pleasure: the states in the plane of pleasure (*sukhabhūmi*) of the sense, fine-material and supramundane spheres, with the pleasant feeling itself excluded; the associated perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1772:2-3).
3. **Upekkhāsahagatā dhammā** - States accompanied by equanimity: the states in the plane of equanimity (*upekkhābhūmi*) of the sense, fine-material, immaterial and supramundane spheres, with the equanimity (neutral feeling) itself excluded; the associated perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1773:2-3).

### Quote
> **(Ka) pītisahagatā dhammā. (Kha) sukhasahagatā dhammā. (Ga) upekkhāsahagatā dhammā.**  
> 7.(Ka) Mental phenomena associated with rapture. (Kha) Mental phenomena associated with pleasant feeling. (Ga) Mental phenomena associated with onlooking-equanimity.  
>
