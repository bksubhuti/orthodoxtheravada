### Overview
According to orthodox Theravāda commentary, all Fully Awakened Buddhas teach the exact same [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) and possess the same ultimate enlightenment. However, the duration of their [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/) training in [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/) varies depending on their dominant spiritual faculty.

### The List
1. **Paññādhika Bodhisatta** - Wisdom-predominant: A Bodhisatta who leans heavily on the faculty of wisdom. Because their understanding is sharp, they require the shortest time to perfect the Pāramīs (four incalculable eons and 100,000 great eons). Our Buddha Gotama fulfilled the perfections for four incalculable eons and a hundred thousand eons (Bv-a 4000:2), the period the commentary assigns to this type.
2. **Saddhādhika Bodhisatta** - Faith-predominant: A Bodhisatta who leans on the faculty of faith and devotion. They require a medium length of time to perfect the Pāramīs (eight incalculable eons and 100,000 great eons).
3. **Vīriyādhika Bodhisatta** - Energy-predominant: A Bodhisatta who leans on the faculty of sheer effort and heroic striving. They require the longest time to perfect the Pāramīs (sixteen incalculable eons and 100,000 great eons) before attaining Buddhahood.

### Quote
> **Buddhānaṃ, ānanda, heṭṭhimaparicchedena cattāri asaṅkhyeyyāni kappasatasahassañca, majjhimaparicchedena aṭṭha asaṅkhyeyyāni kappasatasahassañca, uparimaparicchedena soḷasa asaṅkhyeyyāni kappasatasahassañca. Ete ca bhedā paññādhikasaddhādhikavīriyādhikavasena ñātabbā. Paññādhikānañhi saddhā mandā hoti, paññā tikkhā. Saddhādhikānaṃ paññā majjhimā hoti, saddhā balavā. Vīriyādhikānaṃ saddhāpaññā mandā, vīriyaṃ balavanti.**  
> “For the Buddhas, Ānanda, the lowest duration is four aeons and a hundred thousand aeons, the medium is eight aeons and a hundred thousand aeons, and the highest is sixteen aeons and a hundred thousand aeons. These distinctions should be understood according to the Buddha of Wisdom, the Buddha of Faith, and the Buddha of Energy. Indeed, the Buddha of Wisdom has little faith but great wisdom; the Buddha of Faith has medium wisdom but great faith; the Buddha of Energy has little faith and wisdom but great energy.”  
>  
> — Aṭṭhakathā: [Paramatthajotikā](/glossary/paramatthajotika/) (Suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā, Khaggavisāṇa Sutta, Pj II 205) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_05_01&page_number=43&name=suttanip%C4%81taa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=QnVkZGjEgW5h4bmDLCDEgW5hbmRhLCBoZeG5reG5rWhpbWFwYXJpY2NoZWRlbmEgY2F0dMSBcmkgYXNh4bmFa2h5ZXl5xIFuaSBrYXBwYXNhdGFzYWhhc3Nhw7FjYSw%3D)

### Textual References
* **Commentary**: *[Paramatthajotikā](/glossary/paramatthajotika/)* (Pj-ii 205:3-7) – Gives the three durations (four, eight, sixteen incalculables and a hundred thousand eons) and says they are to be understood by wisdom, faith and energy; the same sentence is at Cp-a 2060:3 ("yathākkamaṃ", in that order), Ap-a 1207:4 and, in the Dīgha ṭīkā, Sv-pt-i 307:1-3.
* **Commentary**: *[Madhuratthavilāsinī](/glossary/madhuratthavilasini/)* (Commentary to the [Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)) – States how long particular Buddhas fulfilled the perfections: Gotama, four incalculables and a hundred thousand eons (Bv-a 4000:2); Anomadassī, sixteen (Bv-a 2265:3).
