### Overview
The second triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes states by their association (*sampayutta*) with pleasant, painful or neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling (*[vedanā](/glossary/vedana/)*).

### The List
1. **Sukhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā** - States associated with pleasant feeling: the states in the plane of pleasure (*sukhabhūmi*) of the sense, fine-material and supramundane spheres, with the pleasant feeling itself excluded; the associated perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1756:2-3).
2. **Dukkhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā** - States associated with painful feeling: the states in the plane of pain (*dukkhabhūmi*) of the sense sphere, with the painful feeling itself excluded; the associated perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1757:2-3).
3. **Adukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā** - States associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling: the states in the plane of neutral feeling of the sense, fine-material, immaterial and supramundane spheres, with that feeling itself excluded; the associated perception, formations and consciousness aggregates (Dhs 1758:2-3). Aṭṭhakathā: pleasant has the characteristic of experiencing the desirable, painful of the undesirable, neither of the reverse of both (As 852:6).

### Quote
> **(Ka) sukhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā. (Kha) dukkhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā. (Ga) adukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttā dhammā.**  
> 2.(Ka) Mental phenomena conjoined with pleasant feeling. (Kha) Mental phenomena conjoined with painful feeling. (Ga) Mental phenomena conjoined with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant.  
>
