### Overview
The Three Gateways to Liberation (*Vimokkha-mukha*) are the specific avenues through which the mind finally breaks free from [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/) and realizes [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). They directly correspond to the profound realization of the Three Characteristics (*[Tilakkhaṇa](/glossary/tilakkhana/)*) during the climax of insight meditation (*[vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/)*).

### The List
1. **Suññato vimokkho** - The Emptiness Liberation: Achieved by contemplating the non-self (*[anattā](/glossary/anatta/)*) nature of phenomena. By seeing all aggregates as empty of a soul or controller, the mind enters liberation through the gateway of emptiness.
2. **Animitto vimokkho** - The Signless Liberation: Achieved by contemplating the impermanent (*[anicca](/glossary/anicca/)*) nature of phenomena. By seeing that no conditioned thing is stable enough to grasp as a "sign" of security, the mind enters liberation through the gateway of the signless.
3. **Appaṇihito vimokkho** - The Desireless Liberation: Achieved by contemplating the suffering (*[dukkha](/glossary/dukkha/)*) inherent in phenomena. By seeing the oppression of all conditioned things, the mind completely drops all craving and enters liberation through the gateway of the desireless.

### Quote
> **Tīṇi kho panimāni vimokkhamukhāni lokaniyyānāya saṃvattanti. Sabbasaṅkhāre paricchedaparivaṭumato samanupassanatāya animittāya ca dhātuyā cittasampakkhandanatāya, sabbasaṅkhāresu manosamuttejanatāya appaṇihitāya ca dhātuyā cittasampakkhandanatāya, sabbadhamme parato samanupassanatāya suññatāya ca dhātuyā cittasampakkhandanatāya – imāni tīṇi vimokkhamukhāni lokaniyyānāya saṃvattanti.**  
> 219. Bhikkhus, there are these three gateways to liberation that lead to the outlet from the world. By seeing all formations as limited and circumscribed and by the mind entering into the signless element; by the bestirring of the mind with respect to all formations and by the mind entering into the desireless element; by seeing all things as alien and by the mind entering into the voidness element—these three gateways to liberation lead to the outlet from the world.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)* – Details the precise alignment of the three characteristics with these three liberations.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XXI) – Explains how the climax of insight routes the meditator through one of these specific gateways based on their dominant faculty.
