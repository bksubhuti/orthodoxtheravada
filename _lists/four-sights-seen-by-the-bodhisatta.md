### Overview
Before his going forth, the Bodhisatta saw four sights on drives to the park (*uyyānabhūmi*): an old man, a sick man, a dead man and one who had gone forth. The Mahāpadāna Sutta (DN 14) tells this of Prince Vipassī, the Buddha of ninety-one aeons ago; the Jātaka Aṭṭhakathā (Nidānakathā) tells it of Prince Siddhattha and says the devas manifested the four figures as precursors (*pubbanimitta*) of his awakening, seen only by the Bodhisatta and the charioteer (Ja-a-i 694:4-5). It also notes that the Dīghabhāṇakas say he saw all four signs (*cattāri nimittāni*) on the same day (Ja-a-i 695:8).

### The List
1. **Jiṇṇa** - An old man (*purisaṃ jiṇṇaṃ gopānasivaṅkaṃ bhoggaṃ daṇḍaparāyanaṃ pavedhamānaṃ*): bent like a roof-gable, leaning on a staff, tottering (D-ii 101:1). Ja-a-i 694:4 (*jarājajjaraṃ … devaputtaṃ*, an old man manifested by the devas).
2. **Ābādhika** - A sick man (*purisaṃ ābādhikaṃ dukkhitaṃ bāḷhagilānaṃ*): lying in his own excrement and urine, lifted up by some and laid down by others (D-ii 108:1). Ja-a-i 695:1 (*byādhitaṃ purisaṃ*).
3. **Kālaṅkata** - A dead man (*petaṃ kālaṅkataṃ*): a large crowd assembled and a funeral pyre being made with cloths of various colours; the charioteer explains that his mother, father and relatives will no longer see him (D-ii 113:1-10). Ja-a-i 695:3 (*kālakataṃ*).
4. **Pabbajita** - One who has gone forth (*purisaṃ bhaṇḍuṃ pabbajitaṃ kāsāyavasanaṃ*): a shaven-headed man in yellow robes, described as good in righteous conduct, peaceful conduct, skilful and meritorious action, harmlessness and compassion (D-ii 118:1-7). Ja-a-i 695:5-6 (*sunivatthaṃ supārutaṃ pabbajitaṃ*).

### Quote
> **“Addasā kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro uyyānabhūmiṃ niyyanto purisaṃ jiṇṇaṃ gopānasivaṅkaṃ bhoggaṃ daṇḍaparāyanaṃ pavedhamānaṃ gacchantaṃ āturaṃ gatayobbanaṃ.**  
> Monks, as Prince Vipassī was driving to the park, he saw an old man, bent like a roof-gable, decrepit, leaning on a staff, tottering as he went, afflicted, his youth long gone.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Mahāpadāna [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (DN 14, D-ii 4 "1. Mahāpadānasuttaṃ") – The four sights of the past Buddha Vipassī, in four separate sections (old man D-ii 101, sick man 108, dead man 113, one gone forth 118). No single Mūla passage lists all four; the quote above gives the first.
* **Commentary**: *[Jātaka](/glossary/jataka/) Aṭṭhakathā, Nidānakathā* (Ja-a-i 694-695) – The same four sights for Prince Siddhattha, the old man "in the way mentioned in the Mahāpadāna Sutta" (Ja-a-i 694:7).
