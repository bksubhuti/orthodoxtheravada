### Overview
In the Devadūta Sutta (MN 130) the Buddha describes the Great Hell (*mahānirayo*) in verse. The verse gives four structural features of it: its four doors, its iron ramparts, its iron roof and its iron floor. The sutta goes on to say that flames surge from each wall to the opposite wall and that the being there feels painful feelings, but does not die until the bad kamma is exhausted (M-iii 806). The title "torments" is the site's own name; the texts do not call these four a set of torments.

### The List
1. **Catudvāro** - Four doors: "Four are its corners, four its doors, divided into suitable quarters" (*catukkaṇṇo catudvāro, vibhatto bhāgaso mito*).
2. **Ayopākārapariyanto** - Surrounded by iron ramparts.
3. **Ayasā paṭikujjito** - Shut in with an iron roof.
4. **Ayomayā bhūmi** - Its floor is made of iron, "burnt, accompanied by heat" (*jalitā tejasāyutā*); the heat covers a hundred yojanas all around (M-iii 805).
