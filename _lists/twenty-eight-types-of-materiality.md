### Overview
This is the Abhidhamma list of the types of materiality (*[Rūpa](/glossary/rupa/)*): four great elements and twenty-four derived types, twenty-eight in all (Abhidh-s 524:1).

### The List
**The 4 Primary Elements (Mahābhūta):**
1. Earth Element (Solidity).
2. Water Element (Cohesion).
3. Fire Element (Temperature).
4. Wind Element (Motion).

**The 24 Derived Elements (Upādā Rūpa):**
5-9. The Five Sensitivities (Eye, Ear, Nose, Tongue, Body).
10-13. The Four Sense Objects (Form, Sound, Odor, Taste).
14-15. The Two Sexes (Femininity, Masculinity).
16. The Heart-base (Seat of consciousness).
17. Physical Life-faculty (Vitality).
18. Nutritive Essence.
19. Space Element.
20-21. Bodily and Verbal Intimation (Communication).
22-24. Lightness, Pliancy, Wieldiness of matter.
25-28. Production, Continuity, Decay, and Impermanence of matter.

### Quote
> **Jātirūpameva panettha upacayasantatināmena pavuccatīti ekādasavidhampetaṃ rūpaṃ aṭṭhavīsatividhaṃ hoti sarūpavasena. 17. Kathaṃ – Bhūtappasādavisayā, bhāvo hadayamiccapi; Jīvitāhārarūpehi, aṭṭhārasavidhaṃ tathā. Paricchedo ca viññatti, vikāro lakkhaṇanti ca; Anipphannā dasa ceti, aṭṭhavīsavidhaṃ bhave.**  
> 16. Here, the materiality of birth (jātirūpameva) is indeed spoken of (pavuccatīti) by the names of growth and continuity; thus these eleven kinds of materiality become twenty-eight by way of their specific properties (sarūpavasena). 17. How? Elements (bhūta), sensitive organs (pasāda), objects (visaya), sex (bhāvo), and the heart (hadaya); together with the materiality of life (jīvita) and nutriment (āhāra)—thus concrete matter is eighteenfold. Limitation (paricchedo), intimation (viññatti), mutability (vikāro), and characteristics (lakkhaṇa); these ten are non-produced; thus there would be twenty-eight in all.  
>
