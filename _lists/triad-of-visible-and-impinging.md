### Overview
The twenty-second and final triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā is a strictly physical and perceptual classification. It divides the entirety of reality based on two properties: visibility (can it be seen by the eye?) and impingement (can it strike a sense organ and cause physical friction?).

### The List
1. **Sanidassanasappaṭighā dhammā** - States that are visible and impinging: This refers exclusively to the Visible Form base (*Rūpāyatana*). It can be seen by the eye and it physically strikes the eye-sensitivity.
2. **Anidassanasappaṭighā dhammā** - States that are invisible but impinging: This includes the five physical sense organs (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body) and the remaining external objects (sound, smell, taste, touch). They cannot be seen, but they physically strike each other.
3. **Anidassanaappaṭighā dhammā** - States that are invisible and non-impinging: The four mental aggregates (feeling, perception, formations, consciousness), whatever form is invisible, non-impinging and included in the mental-phenomena base (*dhammāyatana*), and the unconditioned element, [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Dhs 1058). They cannot be seen, and they do not impinge on a sense organ.

### Quote
> **(Ka) sanidassanasappaṭighā dhammā. (Kha) anidassanasappaṭighā dhammā. (Ga) anidassanaappaṭighā dhammā.**  
> 22.(Ka) Mental phenomena that are both visible and impinging. (Kha) Mental phenomena that are invisible but impinging. (Ga) Mental phenomena that are invisible and non-impinging.  
>
