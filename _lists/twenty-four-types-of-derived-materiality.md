### Overview
The Visuddhimagga gives derived materiality (*upādārūpaṃ*, [Rūpa](/glossary/rupa/)) as twenty-four kinds (Vism-ii 323:1); with the four great elements this makes the twenty-eight of Vism-ii 358:1. The Dhammasaṅgaṇī's list of derived form (Dhs 595) names twenty-three, without the heart-basis (*hadayavatthu*).

### The List (Core Derived Materiality)
**The 5 Sensitive Phenomena (Pasāda Rūpa):**
1. Eye-sensitivity, 2. Ear-sensitivity, 3. Nose-sensitivity, 4. Tongue-sensitivity, 5. Body-sensitivity.
**The 4 Objective Phenomena (Gocara Rūpa):**
6. Visible Form, 7. Sound, 8. Odor, 9. Taste. *(Note: Touch is excluded here because it is composed of 3 of the Great Elements).*
**The 2 Sexual Phenomena (Bhāva Rūpa):**
10. Femininity, 11. Masculinity.
**The Heart Phenomenon (Hadaya Rūpa):**
12. Heart-base (the physical seat of the mind).
**The Life Phenomenon ([Jīvita](/glossary/jivita/) Rūpa):**
13. Physical vitality/Life faculty.
**The Nutritional Phenomenon (Āhāra Rūpa):**
14. Nutritive essence.
**The 1 Space Phenomenon:** 15. Space element.
**The 2 Communicating Phenomena:** 16. Bodily intimation, 17. Verbal intimation.
**The 3 Mutable Phenomena:** 18. Lightness, 19. Pliancy, 20. Wieldiness.
**The 4 Characteristics:** 21. Production, 22. Continuity, 23. Decay, 24. Impermanence.

### Quote
> **Upādārūpaṃ catuvīsatividhaṃ – cakkhu, sotaṃ, ghānaṃ, jivhā, kāyo, rūpaṃ, saddo, gandho, raso, itthindriyaṃ, purisindriyaṃ, jīvitindriyaṃ, hadayavatthu, kāyaviññatti, vacīviññatti, ākāsadhātu, rūpassa lahutā, rūpassa mudutā, rūpassa kammaññatā, rūpassa upacayo, rūpassa santati, rūpassa jaratā, rūpassa aniccatā, kabaḷīkāro āhāroti.**  
> Derived form is of twenty-four kinds: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, form, sound, fragrance, flavor, feminine faculty, masculine faculty, life faculty, heart-basis, bodily intimation, vocal intimation, space element, lightness of form, pliancy of form, wieldiness of form, growth of form, continuity of form, decay of form, impermanence of form, and physical nutriment.
