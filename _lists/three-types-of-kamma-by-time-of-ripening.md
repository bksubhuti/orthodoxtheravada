### Overview
Not all intentional actions yield immediate results. In orthodox [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), [kamma](/glossary/kamma/) is highly structural, and the precise moment of volition (*[cetanā](/glossary/cetana/)*) within the cognitive process determines exactly when a kammic seed will bear fruit. This framework explains why bad things sometimes happen to good people and vice versa.

### The List
1. **Diṭṭhadhammika-vedanīya-kamma** - Immediately Effective Kamma: Kamma that ripens within this very life. If it does not find the opportunity to ripen in this lifetime, it becomes defunct (Ahosi Kamma).
2. **Upapajja-vedanīya-kamma** - Subsequently Effective Kamma: Kamma that ripens exclusively in the very next lifetime immediately following the present one. It dictates the next rebirth or experiences therein. If it cannot operate in the next life, it becomes defunct.
3. **Aparāpariya-vedanīya-kamma** - Indefinitely Effective Kamma: Kamma that ripens in any lifetime from the second future life onwards. As long as the cycle of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/) continues, this kamma never expires and waits for the right conditions to bear fruit.

### Quote
> **Tattha pariyuṭṭhānaṃ diṭṭhadhammavedanīyaṃ vā kammaṃ upapajjavedanīyaṃ vā kammaṃ aparāpariyāyavedanīyaṃ vā kammaṃ, evaṃ kammaṃ tidhā vipaccati diṭṭhe vā dhamme upapajje vā apare vā pariyāye.**  
> Herein, obsession is action can be experienced in the here and now, or it is action in the next rebirth, or it is action in a succession of rebirths. Thus, action ripens in these three ways: in the here and now or in the next rebirth or in a succession of rebirths.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Paṭhamasañcetanika [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 10.217) – The Buddha says volitional kamma is experienced “in this very life, in the next rebirth, or on some subsequent occasion” (*diṭṭheva dhamme upapajje vā apare vā pariyāye*).
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XIX, §685) – Buddhaghosa provides the technical breakdown of how the seven javana mind-moments align with these three timeframes.
