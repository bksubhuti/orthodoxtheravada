### Overview
The Three Refuges (*[Tisaraṇa](/glossary/tisarana/)*) constitute the foundational act of entering the Buddhist dispensation. Taking refuge is not merely a ritual; it is a profound cognitive shift where a person formally recognizes that the Buddha, his Teaching, and his Awakened Community are the only true sources of safety from the suffering of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).

### The List
1. **Buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi** - I go to the Buddha for refuge: Taking safe harbor in the Fully Awakened One, recognizing him as the supreme teacher and the discoverer of the path.
2. **Dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi** - I go to the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) for refuge: Taking safe harbor in the Doctrine and the Path itself, recognizing it as the ultimate truth that liberates the mind.
3. **Saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi** - I go to the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) for refuge: Taking safe harbor in the Community of Awakened Disciples, recognizing them as the living proof of the path and the supreme field of merit.

### Quote
> **Buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi; Dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi; Saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.**  
> I go for refuge to the Buddha. I go for refuge to the Dhamma. I go for refuge to the Saṅgha.  
>  
> — [Khuddakapāṭha](/glossary/khuddakapatha/) (Khp 1, Saraṇattaya) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_01&page_number=1&name=khuddakap%C4%81%E1%B9%ADhap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=QnVkZGhh4bmDIHNhcmHhuYdh4bmDIGdhY2NoxIFtaTs%3D)
