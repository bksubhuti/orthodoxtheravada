### Overview
Hearing the true teaching (*Dhammassavana*) is the fundamental catalyst for awakening. The Buddha taught the monks that there are five benefits in listening to the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) (AN 5.202).

### The List
1. **Assutaṃ suṇāti** - One hears what one has not heard before: Expanding one's knowledge base by learning new discourses, rules, or meditation techniques.
2. **Sutaṃ pariyodāpeti** - One clarifies what one has already heard: Deepening one's understanding of familiar teachings, cleaning up misconceptions.
3. **Kaṅkhaṃ vitarati** - One overcomes doubt: Dispelling paralyzing skepticism regarding the Buddha, the path, or one's own practice through the clarity of the exposition.
4. **Diṭṭhiṃ ujuṃ karoti** - One straightens one's views: Aligning one's personal philosophy with Right View (*[Sammādiṭṭhi](/glossary/sammaditthi/)*), dropping eternalist or annihilationist tendencies.
5. **Cittamassa pasīdati** - One's mind becomes serene: The mind acquires confidence (*pasīdati*) through listening to the Dhamma.

### Quote
> **“Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṃsā dhammassavane. Katame pañca? Assutaṃ suṇāti, sutaṃ pariyodāpeti, kaṅkhaṃ vitarati, diṭṭhiṃ ujuṃ karoti, cittamassa pasīdati.**  
> “Monks, there are these five benefits in listening to the Dhamma. What five? He hears what he has not heard before; he clarifies what is heard; he overcomes doubt; he makes his view straight; and his mind acquires confidence.  
>
