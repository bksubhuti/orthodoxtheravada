### Overview
For communication to be considered "Right Speech" in Theravāda, it must pass a stringent five-part test. In the Vācā Sutta (AN 5.198) the Buddha says that speech endowed with five factors is well-spoken (*subhāsitā*), not badly spoken, blameless and not faulted by the wise (*anavajjā ca ananuvajjā ca viññūnaṃ*).

### The List
1. **Kālena bhāsitā hoti** - It is spoken at the right time: The speech is spoken at the proper time.
2. **Saccā bhāsitā hoti** - It is spoken in truth: The speech is true.
3. **Saṇhā bhāsitā hoti** - It is spoken gently: The speech is delivered with a mild and pleasant tone, avoiding harsh, aggressive, or abusive language.
4. **Atthasaṃhitā bhāsitā hoti** - It is spoken beneficially: The speech has a useful purpose. The speech is connected with benefit (*attha*).
5. **Mettacittena bhāsitā hoti** - It is spoken with a mind of loving-kindness: The speech is spoken with a mind of goodwill.

### Quote
> **“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgatā vācā subhāsitā hoti, no dubbhāsitā, anavajjā ca ananuvajjā ca viññūnaṃ. Katamehi pañcahi? Kālena ca bhāsitā hoti, saccā ca bhāsitā hoti, saṇhā ca bhāsitā hoti, atthasaṃhitā ca bhāsitā hoti, mettacittena ca bhāsitā hoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi aṅgehi samannāgatā vācā subhāsitā hoti, no dubbhāsitā, anavajjā ca ananuvajjā ca viññūnan” ti.**  
> “Monks, a statement endowed with five factors is well-spoken, not badly stated. It is blameless and unfaulted by the wise. Which five? It is spoken at the right time. It is spoken in truth. It is spoken gently. It is spoken beneficially. It is spoken with a mind of loving-kindness. Monks, a statement endowed with these five factors indeed is well-spoken, not badly stated. It is blameless and unfaulted by the wise.”  
>
