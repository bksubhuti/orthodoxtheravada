### Overview
The Five Precepts (*[Pañcasīla](/glossary/pancasila/)*) constitute the absolute baseline of moral conduct for a Theravāda Buddhist layperson. They are not commandments imposed by a creator, but voluntary training rules undertaken to avoid generating bad [kamma](/glossary/kamma/), protect others, and build a foundation of mental purity necessary for meditation.

### The List
1. **Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ** - The training rule of refraining from killing living beings.
2. **Adinnādānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ** - The training rule of refraining from taking what is not given.
3. **Kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ** - The training rule of refraining from sexual misconduct.
4. **Musāvādā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ** - The training rule of refraining from false speech.
5. **Surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ** - The training rule of refraining from liquor, wine and intoxicants, the basis of heedlessness.

### Quote
> **Pañca sikkhāpadāni – pāṇātipātā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, adinnādānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, musāvādā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ.**  
> 703. The five Training Rules are: the training rule of Refraining from killing living creatures; the training rule of refraining from taking what is not given; the training rule of refraining from sexual misconduct; the training rule of Refraining from false speech; the training rule of abstaining from drinking distilled and fermented drinks that cause heedlessness.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Vibhaṅga* (Vibh 703, Sikkhāpadavibhaṅga, Abhidhammabhājanīya) – The five training rules in the words quoted above.
* **Canonical**: *Abhisanda [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 8.39, A-viii 449-450) – The same five abstentions are given there as five "great gifts" (*mahādānāni*), each giving immeasurable beings freedom from fear, enmity and affliction. It uses the wording "pāṇātipātā paṭivirato", not the training-rule formula above.
