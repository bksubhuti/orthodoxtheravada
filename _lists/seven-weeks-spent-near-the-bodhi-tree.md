### Overview
Immediately following his full enlightenment on the full moon of Vesākha, the Buddha spent seven weeks (*sattasattāha*; the commentary counts the 49th day, Ja-a-i 772:1) at and near the [Bodhi](/glossary/bodhi/) tree, "experiencing the bliss of liberation" (*vimuttisukhapaṭisaṃvedī*). The Vinaya Mahāvagga records only four of the weeks (the Bodhi tree, Ajapāla banyan, Mucalinda and Rājāyatana, Vin-iii 6, 25-41); the other three (the Unblinking Gaze, the Jewel Walk and the Jewel House) are in the Jātaka Nidānakathā (Ja-a-i 761-762).

### The List
1. **Pallanka-sattāha:** Spent the first week sitting entirely cross-legged directly under the **Bodhi tree**, experiencing the absolute bliss of defilement destruction.
2. **Animisa-sattāha:** Week of the Unblinking Gaze. After the twin miracle that quelled the devas' doubt, he stood a little north-east of the seat and gazed at the seat and the Bodhi tree with unblinking eyes for a week, thinking "here I attained omniscience"; the place became the Animisacetiya (Ja-a-i 760-761).
3. **Caṅkamana-sattāha:** Week of the Walk. He created a walkway between the seat and the place where he had stood, a jewelled walk (*ratanacaṅkama*) running east to west, and walked there for a week; the place became the Ratanacaṅkamacetiya (Ja-a-i 761:2).
4. **Ratanaghara-sattāha:** Week of the Jeweled House. The devas created a jewel house (*ratanagharaṃ*) to the north-west of the Bodhi tree; sitting cross-legged there he spent the week contemplating the Abhidhamma Piṭaka, especially the Paṭṭhāna (Ja-a-i 762:1). The Ābhidhammikas say the "jewel house" means the place where he contemplated the seven treatises (762:2).
5. **Ajapāla-nigrodha-sattāha:** Week under the Banyan Tree. He went to the Ajapāla banyan (Ja-a-i 762:5); there Māra's three daughters, Taṇhā, Arati and Ragā, approached him and he paid them no heed (Ja-a-i 764-765). A *huṃhuṅkajātika* brahmin also came to him there (Ud 32:1).
6. **Mucalinda-sattāha:** Week under the Mucalinda Tree. A great out-of-season cloud brought seven days of rain and cold wind; the snake-king **Mucalinda** surrounded the Buddha's body seven times with his coils and spread his great hood above his head (Ud 99:1-2; Vin-iii 34).
7. **Rājāyatana-sattāha:** Week under the Rājāyatana Tree. Spent completing his fast, at the conclusion of which two merchant brothers, Tapussa and Bhallika, approached to offer rice cakes, becoming the very first lay followers in the world.

### Quote
> **Evaṃ satthā iminā pāṭihāriyena devatānaṃ vitakkaṃ vūpasametvā pallaṅkato īsakaṃ pācīnanissite uttaradisābhāge ṭhatvā “imasmiṃ vata me pallaṅke sabbaññutaññāṇaṃ paṭividdhan” ti cattāri asaṅkhyeyyāni kappasatasahassañca pūritānaṃ pāramīnaṃ phalādhigamaṭṭhānaṃ pallaṅkaṃ bodhirukkhañca animisehi akkhīhi olokayamāno sattāhaṃ vītināmesi, taṃ ṭhānaṃ animisacetiyaṃ nāma jātaṃ. Atha pallaṅkassa ca ṭhitaṭṭhānassa ca antarā caṅkamaṃ māpetvā puratthimapacchimato āyate ratanacaṅkame caṅkamanto sattāhaṃ vītināmesi, taṃ ṭhānaṃ ratanacaṅkamacetiyaṃ nāma jātaṃ. Catutthe pana sattāhe bodhito pacchimuttaradisābhāge devatā ratanagharaṃ māpayiṃsu, tattha pallaṅkena nisīditvā abhidhammapiṭakaṃ visesato cettha anantanayaṃ samantapaṭṭhānaṃ vicinanto sattāhaṃ vītināmesi.**  
> Thus, having quelled the devas’ doubt with this miracle, the Teacher stood in the north-east direction, slightly angled from the seat, and thinking, “It was here that I attained omniscience,” he gazed at the Bodhi-tree without blinking for one week, contemplating the seat and the tree, which were the fruition of the perfections he had fulfilled over four incalculable aeons and a hundred thousand aeons. That location became known as the Unblinking Shrine (animisacetiyaṃ). Then, having created a walkway between the seat and the place where he had stood, he spent one week walking along the Jewel Walkway (ratanacaṅkama) extending from east to west. That location became known as the Jewel Walkway Shrine (ratanacaṅkamacetiyaṃ). In the fourth week, however, the devas created a Jewel House (ratanaghara) to the north-west of the Bodhi-tree. Sitting cross-legged inside that house, he spent one week contemplating the Abhidhamma Piṭaka, especially the peerless Paṭṭhāna (Conditional Relations).

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/)* ([Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/) Piṭaka) – four weeks only: Bodhikathā (Vin-iii 5-6), Ajapālakathā (Vin-iii 25-26), Mucalindakathā (Vin-iii 33-34), Rājāyatanakathā (Vin-iii 41-42). Not in this source: the Unblinking, Jewel Walk and Jewel House weeks.
* **Commentary**: *Jātaka Aṭṭhakathā*, Nidānakathā (Ja-a-i 759-773) – all seven weeks in order; 772:1 counts the 49th day.
