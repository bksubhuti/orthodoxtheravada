### Overview
In the Mahākhandhaka, section 63 "Upasampadāvidhi" (Vin-iii 668:1), the Buddha learned that persons with leprosy, boils, eczema, consumption and epilepsy had been ordained, and he allowed that the candidate for higher ordination (*upasampadā*) be asked about the thirteen obstructive things (*terasa antarāyike dhamme*, Vin-iii 669:1-3). The questions are put in the midst of the Saṅgha after the candidate has been instructed by a monk agreed upon by the Saṅgha: "This is a time for truth-speaking, a time for fact-speaking. When I ask you in the midst of the Saṅgha about what is, you should say 'It is' (*atthi*) if it is so; you should say 'It is not' (*natthi*) if it is not so. Do not be at a loss, do not be abashed" (Vin-iii 677:1-4). In the assembly, the monk asks the Saṅgha for leave to ask, and then asks the questions (Vin-iii 680:1-7).

### The 13 Questions
1. **Kuṭṭhaṃ?** - Leprosy.
2. **Gaṇḍo?** - Boils.
3. **Kilāso?** - Eczema (the skin disease that is not cracking and not oozing, lotus-petal in colour, which makes the body spotted like a cow's, Sp-iii 203:1).
4. **Soso?** - Consumption.
5. **Apamāro?** - Epilepsy. (The commentary calls these five diseases, leprosy and so on, "prevalent", and one overcome by them is not to be let go forth, Sp-iii 200:3.)
6. **Manussosi?** - Are you a human being?
7. **Purisosi?** - Are you a man?
8. **Bhujissosi?** - Are you a free man?
9. **Aṇaṇosi?** - Are you free of debt?
10. **Nasi rājabhaṭo?** - Are you not a king's servant? The commentary says a king's servant is anyone who depends on the king's food and wages, whether a minister, a chief minister or an attendant, with or without a position (Sp-iii 206:5).
11. **Anuññātosi mātāpitūhi?** - Have your parents given permission?
12. **Paripuṇṇavīsativassosi?** - Are you twenty years old (complete)?
13. **Paripuṇṇaṃ te pattacīvaraṃ?** - Are your bowl and robes complete?

After these thirteen the sutta adds "What is your name? What is your preceptor's name?" (Vin-iii 669:14-15); these two are not counted among the thirteen.


### Quote
> **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, upasampādentena terasa antarāyike dhamme pucchituṃ. Evañca pana, bhikkhave, pucchitabbo – “santi te evarūpā ābādhā – kuṭṭhaṃ, gaṇḍo, kilāso, soso, apamāro? Manussosi? Purisosi? Bhujissosi? Aṇaṇosi? Nasi rājabhaṭo? Anuññātosi mātāpitūhi? Paripuṇṇavīsativassosi? Paripuṇṇaṃ te pattacīvaraṃ? Kiṃnāmosi? Konāmo te upajjhāyo” ti?**  
> “I allow you, monks, to ask a candidate for higher ordination about the stumbling-blocks.” And thus, monks, should he be asked: “Do you have such diseases as these: leprosy, boils, eczema, consumption, epilepsy? Are you a human being? Are you a man? Are you a freeman? Are you free of debt? Are you not in the royal service? Have you your parents’ consent? Are you full twenty years of age? Are you complete as to bowl and robes? What is your name? What is the name of your preceptor?”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/)*, Mahākhandhaka, section 63 "Upasampadāvidhi" (Vin-iii 668:1; para 125, Vin-iii 669:1-15, the thirteen questions); the instruction of the candidate (Vin-iii 677:1-4) and the questioning in the Saṅgha (Vin-iii 680:1-7).
* **Commentary**: *Samantapāsādikā* (Sp-iii 200:3, 203:1, 206:5; Sp-v 583:4 on the motion "ahaṃ itthannāmaṃ antarāyike dhamme puccheyyaṃ").
