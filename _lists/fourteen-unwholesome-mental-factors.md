### Overview
The Fourteen Unwholesome Mental Factors (*[Akusala](/glossary/akusala/) Cetasikā*) are the complete psychological toxins of the Theravāda system. They arise exclusively within the 12 unwholesome consciousnesses, rendering the mind morally blameworthy and generating painful future kammic ripening (*vipāka*). The first four are universal across all twelve unwholesome cittas; the others are found only in particular cittas (Abhidh-s 115–126).

### The List
**The 4 Unwholesome Universals (Sabbākusalasādhāraṇā), found in all twelve unwholesome cittas (Abhidh-s 115):**
1. **[Moha](/glossary/moha/)** - Delusion / Ignorance: The foundational darkness that blinds the mind to the Four Noble Truths.
2. **[Ahirika](/glossary/ahirika/)** - Shamelessness: The lack of moral conscience or internal recoil from committing evil deeds.
3. **[Anottappa](/glossary/anottappa/)** - Moral Dreadlessness: The complete disregard for the bad consequences or external blame of wrongdoing.
4. **[Uddhacca](/glossary/uddhacca/)** - Restlessness: The inner agitation or flutter of the mind that prevents it from settling calmly on an object.

**The 3 factors of the greed-rooted cittas (*lobhamūle tayo*, Abhidh-s 123): lobha in the eight lobha cittas (§21), diṭṭhi in the four view-associated (§22), māna in the four view-dissociated (§23):**
5. **[Lobha](/glossary/lobha/)** - Greed / Attachment: The basic pulling force that clings to sensory objects or existence.
6. **[Diṭṭhi](/glossary/ditthi/)** - Wrong View: Holding dogmatic, un-orthodox, or mistaken views regarding [kamma](/glossary/kamma/), self, or liberation.
7. **[Māna](/glossary/mana/)** - Conceit: The psychological habit of self-inflation and measuring oneself against others.

**The 4 factors of the hatred-rooted cittas (*dosamūlesu cattāro*, Abhidh-s 124): found in the two cittas associated with aversion (§24):**
8. **[Dosa](/glossary/dosa/)** - Hatred / Aversion: The destructive force of anger, rage, and irritation.
9. **[Issā](/glossary/issa/)** - Envy / Jealousy: Resenting the success, beauty, or wealth of others.
10. **[Macchariya](/glossary/macchariya/)** - Stinginess: The intense, contracting meanness that refuses to share gains or knowledge.
11. **[Kukkucca](/glossary/kukkucca/)** - Remorse / Worry: Lingering anxiety and regret over good deeds left undone or bad deeds committed.

**The 3 remaining factors: thinamiddha (the pair, *sasaṅkhāre dvayaṃ*) in the five prompted (*sasaṅkhārika*) cittas (§25), and vicikicchā only in the doubt-associated citta (§26) (Abhidh-s 125–126):**
12. **Thīna** - Sloth: Sluggishness and lack of adaptability within the mind itself.
13. **Middha** - Torpor: Morbid heavy drowsiness within the associated mental factors, causing sleepiness.
14. **[Vicikicchā](/glossary/vicikiccha/)** - Doubt: Skeptical, paralyzing indecision regarding the Buddha, [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/), and [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/).

### Quote
> **Moho ahirikaṃ anottappaṃ uddhaccaṃ lobho diṭṭhi māno doso issā macchariyaṃ kukkuccaṃ thinaṃ middhaṃ vicikicchā ceti cuddasime cetasikā akusalā nāma.**  
> 5. Delusion, moral shamelessness, lack of fear of wrongdoing, restlessness, greed, view, conceit, hatred, envy, avarice, regret, mental inertia, drowsiness, and doubt—these fourteen mental factors are called unwholesome.  
>
