### Overview
This is the very first triad in the *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) Mātikā* (the matrix of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/)). It categorizes every single phenomenon (*[dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/)*) in the universe based on its ethical quality and its ability to produce kammic results. 

### The List
1. **Kusalā dhammā** - Wholesome states: Mental states rooted in non-greed, non-hatred, and non-delusion (wisdom). They are morally blameless and produce pleasant kammic results.
2. **Akusalā dhammā** - Unwholesome states: Mental states rooted in greed, hatred, and delusion. They are morally blameworthy and produce painful kammic results.
3. **Abyākatā dhammā** - Indeterminate (Undeclared) states: States not declared as wholesome or unwholesome (As 846:13); their characteristic is having no result (*avipāka*, As 846:14). This includes the resultant states of wholesome and unwholesome states, functional (*kiriya*) states that are neither wholesome, unwholesome nor kamma-resultant, all materiality (*[rūpa](/glossary/rupa/)*), and the unconditioned element, [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Dhs 987).

### Quote
> **(Ka) kusalā dhammā. (Kha) akusalā dhammā. (Ga) abyākatā dhammā.**  
> 1.(Ka) Wholesome mental phenomena. (Kha) Unwholesome mental phenomena. (Ga) Indeterminate mental phenomena.  
>

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *Dhammasaṅgaṇī* – The entire first book of the Abhidhamma is primarily dedicated to expanding and explaining this specific triad in exhaustive detail.
* **Commentary**: *Aṭṭhasālinī* – Buddhaghosa's commentary, which explains *abyākata* as "not declared as wholesome or unwholesome" and as having the characteristic of no result (As 846:13-14).
