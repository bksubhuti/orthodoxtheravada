### Overview
The Ten Fetters (*[Saṃyojana](/glossary/samyojana/)*) are the five lower fetters (*orambhāgiya*) and the five higher fetters (*uddhambhāgiya*) taught in AN 10.13 (A-x 71:1-72:3). The stages of awakening are described by which of them are ended. The stream-enterer has ended the three fetters (*tiṇṇaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ parikkhayā sotāpanno*, A-iii 896:6); the once-returner has ended the three fetters and thinned lust, hatred and delusion (*rāgadosamohānaṃ tanuttā sakadāgāmī*, A-iii 897:6); the non-returner has ended the five lower fetters, is reborn spontaneously and attains final Nibbāna there (A-iii 898:6); and the Arahant, through the destruction of the taints, realises the taintless liberation of mind and liberation by wisdom here and now (A-iii 899:6). In the Dhammapada commentary, the monk who breaks the five lower and five higher fetters is established in Arahatship (Dhp-a 3930:1).

### The List
**The 5 Lower Fetters (Orambhāgiya):**
1. **[Sakkāyadiṭṭhi](/glossary/sakkayaditthi/)** - Identity View (ended with the three fetters at Stream-entry, A-iii 896:6).
2. **[Vicikicchā](/glossary/vicikiccha/)** - Doubt (ended at Stream-entry).
3. **[Sīlabbataparāmāsa](/glossary/silabbataparamasa/)** - Clinging to rules and observances (ended at Stream-entry).
4. **[Kāmacchanda](/glossary/kamachanda/)** - Sensual desire (ended at Non-returning, A-iii 898:6).
5. **[Byāpāda](/glossary/byapada/)** - Ill-will (ended at Non-returning). Lust, hatred and delusion are thinned at Once-returning (A-iii 897:6).

**The 5 Higher Fetters (Uddhambhāgiya):**
6. **[Rūparāga](/glossary/ruparaga/)** - Lust for the form realm.
7. **[Arūparāga](/glossary/aruparaga/)** - Lust for the formless realm.
8. **[Māna](/glossary/mana/)** - Conceit.
9. **[Uddhacca](/glossary/uddhacca/)** - Restlessness.
10. **[Avijjā](/glossary/avijja/)** - Ignorance (not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the way to its cessation, Dhs 1839:2).

The five higher fetters are called "higher" because they bring about rebirth in the form and formless realms (Vibh-a 2183:7-8). They are ended at Arahatship.


### Quote
> **“Dasayimāni, bhikkhave, saṃyojanāni. Katamāni dasa? Pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni, pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. Katamāni pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso, kāmacchando, byāpādo – imāni pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. “Katamāni pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni? Rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṃ, avijjā – imāni pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, dasa saṃyojanānī” ti.**  
> Monks, there are these ten fetters. What are the ten? The five lower fetters and the five higher fetters. What are the five lower fetters? Embodiment view, doubt, wrong grasp of behavior and observances, sensual desire, and ill will. These are the five lower fetters. What are the five higher fetters? Lust for form, lust for the formless, conceit, restlessness, and ignorance. These are the five higher fetters. Indeed, monks, these are the ten fetters.”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 10.13, Saṃyojana Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_10&page_number=269&name=dasakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTMu4oCY4oCYIERhc2F5aW3EgW5pLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIHNh4bmDeW9qYW7EgW5pLg%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Saṃyojana [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 10.13, A-x 70:1 "3. Saṃyojanasuttaṃ", para 13: A-x 71:1-72:3); the five lower fetters at S-v 398:3 and the five higher at S-v 400:3, D-iii 1476:1 and 1477:1 (Saṅgīti Sutta). The ten-fetter list of the Suttanta is not the Abhidhamma's different list of ten (*kāmarāga, paṭigha, māna, diṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāsa, bhavarāga, issā, macchariya, avijjā*, Dhs 1897:2; Nidd-ii 2614:1).
* **Commentary**: *Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā* (Vibh-a 2183:5-8); *Dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā* (Dhp-a 3930:1).
