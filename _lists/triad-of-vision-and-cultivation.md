### Overview
The eighth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena based on exactly *when* they are eradicated on the path to awakening. It divides the destruction of defilements into two phases: the initial breakthrough of "Vision" (Stream-entry) and the subsequent "Cultivation" of the higher paths.

### The List
1. **Dassanena pahātabbā dhammā** - States to be abandoned by vision: The defilements eradicated by the first Path of Stream-entry (*Sotāpattimagga*). This is the three fetters (identity view, uncertainty, adherence to rites and rituals), together with the defilements unified with them, the associated aggregates, and the bodily, verbal and mental actions arising from them (Dhs 1006-1010).
2. **Bhāvanāya pahātabbā dhammā** - States to be abandoned by cultivation: The remaining defilements eradicated by the higher three paths (Once-returning, Non-returning, Arahatship). This is the remaining greed, hatred and delusion, with the defilements unified with them, the associated aggregates, and the bodily, verbal and mental actions arising from them (Dhs 1011).
3. **Neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbā dhammā** - States to be abandoned by neither vision nor cultivation: The wholesome and indeterminate states of the sensuous, form, formless and unincluded planes, the aggregates, all materiality, and the unconditioned element, [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Dhs 1012).

### Quote
> **(Ka) dassanena pahātabbā dhammā. (Kha) bhāvanāya pahātabbā dhammā. (Ga) neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbā dhammā.**  
> 8.(Ka) Mental phenomena eradicated by the First Path. (Kha) Mental phenomena eradicated by the Higher Three Paths. (Ga) Mental phenomena to be eradicated neither by the First Path nor by the Higher Three Paths.  
>
