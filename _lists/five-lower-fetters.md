### Overview
These are the five mental chains that bind beings specifically to the sensual realm (*kāma-loka*), ensuring rebirth in the human or lower worlds. The Stream-enterer breaks the first three. The Once-returner weakens the remaining two. The Non-returner (*[Anāgāmī](/glossary/anagami/)*) shatters all five completely.

### The List
1. **[Sakkāyadiṭṭhi](/glossary/sakkayaditthi/)** - Identity View: The deeply held wrong view that one of the five aggregates is a permanent "self," "soul," or "I". (Eradicated at Stream-entry).
2. **[Vicikicchā](/glossary/vicikiccha/)** - Doubt: Doubt (*vicikicchā*). (Eradicated at Stream-entry).
3. **[Sīlabbataparāmāsa](/glossary/silabbataparamasa/)** - Clinging to Rules and Rituals: Wrongly grasping at rules and observances (*sīlabbataparāmāso*). (Eradicated at Stream-entry).
4. **[Kāmacchanda](/glossary/kamachanda/)** - Sensual Desire: The craving for physical pleasures of the five senses. (Completely eradicated at Non-returning).
5. **[Byāpāda](/glossary/byapada/)** - Ill-will: All forms of anger, resentment, and hatred. (Completely eradicated at Non-returning).

### Quote
> **“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, orambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. Katamāni pañca? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso, kāmacchando, byāpādo – imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni.**  
> “Monks, there are these five lower fetters. What are the five? Clinging to a doctrine of self, doubt, wrong grasp of behavior and observances, sensual desire, and ill will—these indeed, monks, are the five lower fetters.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 9.67, Orambhāgiya Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_09&page_number=251&name=navakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Njcu4oCY4oCYIFBhw7FjaW3EgW5pLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIG9yYW1iaMSBZ2l5xIFuaSBzYeG5g3lvamFuxIFuaS4%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Orambhāgiya Sutta* (AN 9.67, A-ix 452 "5. Orambhāgiyasuttaṃ") – The five in one list, as quoted above.
* **Canonical**: *Mahāmālukya [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (MN 64, M-ii 356 "4. Mahāmālukyasuttaṃ") – The Buddha asks the monks whether they remember the five lower fetters (M-ii 357:6) and then takes them up one by one, beginning with identity view (M-ii 360:1-3); he also says the path is needed to know, see and abandon them (M-ii 362-363).
