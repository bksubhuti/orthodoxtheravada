### Overview
The seventeenth triad of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) Mātikā categorizes phenomena based on their existential status in relation to arising. The Aṭṭhakathā explains the terms at As 867:1-3.

### The List
1. **Uppannā dhammā** - Arisen states: those that have been born, become, produced (*jātā bhūtā sañjātā nibbattā ...*), the aggregates of form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness (Dhs 1809:2). The Aṭṭhakathā: from their arising until their dissolution (As 867:1).
2. **Anuppannā dhammā** - Not arisen states: those that have not been born, not become (*ajātā abhūtā asañjātā ...*), again the five aggregates (Dhs 1810:2). The Aṭṭhakathā: those that have not arisen (As 867:2).
3. **Uppādino dhammā** - States bound to arise: the results of wholesome and unwholesome states whose results have not yet ripened, and the matter that will arise because of kamma done (Dhs 1811:2-4). The Aṭṭhakathā: because a part of their cause is completed, they will inevitably arise (As 867:3).

### Quote
> **(Ka) uppannā dhammā. (Kha) anuppannā dhammā. (Ga) uppādino dhammā.**  
> 17.(Ka) Arisen mental phenomena. (Kha) Unarisen mental phenomena. (Ga) Inevitably determined mental phenomena.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Tikamātikā 17, Uppannattika; Dhs 54-56) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=3&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTcuKEthKSB1cHBhbm7EgSBkaGFtbcSBLg%3D%3D)
