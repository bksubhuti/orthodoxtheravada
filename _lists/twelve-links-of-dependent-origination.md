### Overview
Dependent Origination (*[Paṭiccasamuppāda](/glossary/paticcasamuppada/)*) is the Buddha's teaching of how "this whole mass of suffering" arises: "With ignorance as condition, volitional formations come to be", and so on to birth, and with birth as condition, ageing-and-death with sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure and despair (S-ii 8:2-13). The Aṭṭhakathā calls it the twelve-factored dependent origination (*dvādasaṅge paṭiccasamuppāde*, As 894:9). Its cessation follows by the "remainderless fading away and cessation" of ignorance, from which come the cessation of formations, of consciousness, and so on (S-ii 9:1-3).

### The List
The Buddha himself analyses each link in the Vibhaṅga Sutta (S-ii 10:1 "2. Vibhaṅgasuttaṃ", 13-24):
1. **[Avijjā](/glossary/avijja/)** - Ignorance: not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the way leading to its cessation (S-ii 24:2), which conditions...
2. **Saṅkhārā** - Volitional Formations: the bodily, verbal and mental formations (*kāyasaṅkhāro, vacīsaṅkhāro, cittasaṅkhāro*, S-ii 23:2), which condition...
3. **[Viññāṇa](/glossary/vinnana/)** - Consciousness: the six classes of consciousness, eye-, ear-, nose-, tongue-, body- and mind-consciousness (S-ii 22:2), which conditions...
4. **[Nāmarūpa](/glossary/namarupa/)** - Mentality and Materiality: *nāma* is feeling, perception, volition, contact and attention; *rūpa* is the four great elements and the form derived from them (S-ii 21:2-4), which condition...
5. **[Saḷāyatana](/glossary/salayatana/)** - The Six Sense Bases: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body and mind (S-ii 20:2), which condition...
6. **[Phassa](/glossary/phassa/)** - Contact: the six classes of contact, from eye-contact to mind-contact (S-ii 19:2), which conditions...
7. **[Vedanā](/glossary/vedana/)** - Feeling: the six classes of feeling born of eye-contact and so on (S-ii 18:2), which conditions...
8. **[Taṇhā](/glossary/tanha/)** - Craving: the six classes of craving for forms, sounds, smells, tastes, tangibles and mental objects (S-ii 17:2), which conditions...
9. **[Upādāna](/glossary/upadana/)** - Clinging: the four clingings, to sensual pleasures, to views, to rules and observances, and to a doctrine of self (S-ii 16:2), which conditions...
10. **[Bhava](/glossary/bhava/)** - Becoming: the three kinds of becoming, sense-sphere, fine-material and immaterial (S-ii 15:2), which conditions...
11. **[Jāti](/glossary/jati/)** - Birth: the birth, arising, descent, production and appearance of the aggregates and the acquisition of the sense bases, in each class of beings (S-ii 14:2), which conditions...
12. **[Jarāmaraṇa](/glossary/jaramarana/)** - Ageing and Death: ageing, greying, wrinkling, decline of the life-span and of the faculties; death, passing away, the breaking up of the aggregates and the laying down of the body (S-ii 13:2-4). Sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure and despair accompany it (S-ii 8:12).


### Quote
> **“Katamo ca, bhikkhave, paṭiccasamuppādo? Avijjāpaccayā, bhikkhave, saṅkhārā; saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ; viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ; nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ; saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso; phassapaccayā vedanā; vedanāpaccayā taṇhā; taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ; upādānapaccayā bhavo; bhavapaccayā jāti; jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti. Ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, paṭiccasamuppādo.**  
> And what, monks, is dependent origination? With ignorance as condition, volitional formations come to be; with volitional formations as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, name-and-form; with name-and-form as condition, the six sense bases; with the six sense bases as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, craving; with craving as condition, clinging; with clinging as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging-and-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair come to be. Such is the origin of this whole mass of suffering. This, monks, is called dependent origination.  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 12.1, Paṭiccasamuppāda Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_02&page_number=243&name=nid%C4%81navaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIEthdGFtbyBjYSwgYmhpa2toYXZlLCBwYeG5rWljY2FzYW11cHDEgWRvPw%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Paṭiccasamuppāda [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (SN 12.1, S-ii 6:1 "1. Paṭiccasamuppādasuttaṃ", para 1; S-ii 7:1-8:14) gives the forward order of the twelve links and S-ii 9:1-3 the cessation; *Vibhaṅga Sutta* (SN 12.2, S-ii 10:1-24:3) defines each link.
* **Commentary**: *Aṭṭhasālinī* (As 894:9, "dvādasaṅge paṭiccasamuppāde").
