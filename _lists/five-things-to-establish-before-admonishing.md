### Overview
Venerable Sāriputta teaches the monks (AN 5.167, A-v 1125:1-2), and the Buddha teaches the same five to Venerable Upāli in the Vinaya (Vin-iv 2368:2), that a monk who wishes to accuse another should first establish five qualities within himself (*ajjhattaṃ upaṭṭhāpetvā*) and then accuse.

### The List
1. **Kālena vakkhāmi, no akālena** - I will speak at the right time, not at the wrong time. The Aṭṭhakathā: the time is that of the accused, not of the accuser; one should not accuse in the midst of an assembly, at the Uposatha or Pavāraṇā, or in the refectory, but after asking leave when he sits in his day-resting place (Mp-v 346:2-3).
2. **Bhūtena vakkhāmi, no abhūtena** - I will speak truthfully, not falsely (*bhūtena* = with what is real and factual, Mp-v 346:5).
3. **Saṇhena vakkhāmi, no pharusena** - I will speak gently, not harshly (*saṇhena* = smooth, mild, Mp-v 346:6).
4. **Atthasaṃhitena vakkhāmi, no anatthasaṃhitena** - I will speak beneficially, not harmfully (*atthasaṃhitena* = with a wish for his good and welfare, Mp-v 346:7).
5. **Mettacitto vakkhāmi, no dosantaro** - I will speak with a loving heart, not with inner hatred.

### Quote
> **Tatra kho āyasmā sāriputto bhikkhū āmantesi – “codakena, āvuso, bhikkhunā paraṃ codetukāmena pañca dhamme ajjhattaṃ upaṭṭhāpetvā paro codetabbo”. “Katame pañca? Kālena vakkhāmi, no akālena; bhūtena vakkhāmi, no abhūtena; saṇhena vakkhāmi, no pharusena; atthasaṃhitena vakkhāmi, no anatthasaṃhitena; mettacitto vakkhāmi, no dosantaro. Codakena, āvuso, bhikkhunā paraṃ codetukāmena ime pañca dhamme ajjhattaṃ upaṭṭhāpetvā paro codetabbo.**  
> 167. Then the venerable Sāriputta addressed the bhikkhus: “A bhikkhu who wishes to accuse another, friends, should first establish five qualities within himself and then accuse another.” “What five? I will speak at the right time, not at the wrong time; I will speak truthfully, not falsely; I will speak gently, not harshly; I will speak beneficially, not harmfully; I will speak with a loving heart, not with inner hatred. A bhikkhu who wishes to accuse another, friends, should establish these five qualities within himself and then accuse another.
