### Overview
The Floods (*[Ogha](/glossary/ogha/)*) are the overwhelming currents of defilement (sensuality, existence, views, ignorance) that drown beings in [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/). The Dyad of Floods isolates the specific mental factors responsible for this drowning.

### The List
1. **Oghā dhammā** - States that are floods: Like the Taints (*Āsavas*), the four floods are structurally composed of only three mental factors: Greed (*[lobha](/glossary/lobha/)* - covering the floods of sensuality and existence), Wrong View (*[diṭṭhi](/glossary/ditthi/)* - covering the flood of views), and Delusion (*[moha](/glossary/moha/)* - covering the flood of ignorance).
2. **No oghā dhammā** - States that are not floods: All other mental factors, consciousness, matter, and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). 

### Quote
> **“Cattāro oghā – kāmogho, bhavogho, diṭṭhogho, avijjogho.**  
> “The four floods: the flood of sensual desire, the flood of becoming, the flood of wrong view, the flood of ignorance.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 33, Saṅgīti Sutta, Catukka) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_03&page_number=192&name=p%C4%81thikavaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIENhdHTEgXJvIG9naMSBIOKAkyBrxIFtb2dobywgYmhhdm9naG8s)
