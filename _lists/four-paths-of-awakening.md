### Overview
In Theravāda [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), "Path" (*[Magga](/glossary/magga/)*) does not just refer to the gradual practice, but to a specific, instantaneous supramundane mind-moment. When insight matures, the mind permanently severs specific fetters binding the being to [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/). There are four distinct breakthrough moments.

### The List
1. **Sotāpattimagga** - The Path of Stream-Entry: The first glimpse of [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/). This single mind-moment permanently eradicates the fetters of identity view, doubt, and clinging to rites and rituals. The stream-enterer is bound for awakening (A-iii 896:6) and is reborn at most seven times (*sattakkhattuparamo*, A-iii 903:6).
2. **Sakadāgāmimagga** - The Path of Once-Returning: The second breakthrough. It does not eradicate further fetters but thins lust, hatred and delusion (*rāgadosamohānaṃ tanuttā*, A-iii 897:6); the once-returner returns to this world only once.
3. **Anāgāmimagga** - The Path of Non-Returning: The third breakthrough. It completely and permanently eradicates the fetters of sensual desire and ill-will. The practitioner is reborn spontaneously (*opapātika*), attains parinibbāna there and does not return from that world (A-iii 898:6).
4. **Arahattamagga** - The Path of Arahatship: The final, supreme breakthrough. It permanently obliterates the remaining five higher fetters (desire for form/formless existence, conceit, restlessness, and ignorance).

### Quote
> **Sotāpattimaggacittaṃ, sakadāgāmimaggacittaṃ, anāgāmimaggacittaṃ, arahattamaggacittañceti imāni cattāripi lokuttarakusalacittāni nāma.**  
> 31. Stream-entry path-consciousness, once-return path-consciousness, non-return path-consciousness, and arahantship path-consciousness—these four are called supramundane wholesome consciousnesses.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Aṅguttara Nikāya* (A-viii 244:5; A-ix 84:3) – The eight individuals: for each of the four stages the one practising for the realization of its fruit (*sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno*, …) and the one who has attained it.
* **Later handbook**: *[Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)* (§31, Abhidh-s 53:1) – Names the four path-consciousnesses as supramundane wholesome (*lokuttarakusala*).
