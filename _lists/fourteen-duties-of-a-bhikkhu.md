### Overview
The Vattakkhandhaka of the Cūḷavagga lays down many duties (*vatta*) for monks. The Vinaya Ṭīkā (Sāratthadīpanī) says that fourteen of them are the "great duties" (*cuddasa mahāvattāni*), and that the others, to be practised only at times such as after a censure-act, are the eighty chapter-duties (*asīti khandhakavattāni*). The Mūla gives each duty separately (Vin-iv 1986 ff.); it does not itself give the number fourteen.

### The List
1. **Āgantukavatta:** Duties of an incoming traveling monk.
2. **Āvāsikavatta:** Duties of a resident monk toward visitors.
3. **Gamikavatta:** Duties of an outgoing monk planning a journey.
4. **Anumodanavatta:** Etiquette for giving a [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) blessing after a meal.
5. **Bhattaggavatta:** Duties in the refectory (*bhattagga*) (Vin-iv 2004 ff.).
6. **Piṇḍacārikavatta:** Duties of monks on the alms round (Vin-iv 2017).
7. **Āraññikavatta:** Duties of forest dwellers (Vin-iv 2023).
8. **Senāsanavatta:** Duties regarding the lodging (Vin-iv 2029).
9. **Jantāgharavatta:** Duties in the hot-room (*jantāghara*, Vin-iv 2037).
10. **Vaccakuṭivatta:** Duties at the latrine (Vin-iv 2043).
11. **Upajjhāyavatta:** The duties of a pupil (*saddhivihārika*) toward his preceptor (Vin-iv 2047).
12. **Saddhivihārikavatta:** The duties of a preceptor toward his pupil (Vin-iv 2064).
13. **Ācariyavatta:** The duties of a pupil (*antevāsika*) toward his teacher (Vin-iv 2079).
14. **Antevāsikavatta:** The duties of a teacher toward his pupil (Vin-iv 2096).

### Quote
> **Imasmiṃ vattakkhandhake āgatāni āgantukāvāsikagamiyānumodanabhattaggapiṇḍacārikāraññika senāsana jantāghara vaccakuṭi upajjhācariya saddhivihārika antevāsikavattāni cuddasa mahāvattāni nāma, ito aññāni pana kadāci tajjanīyakammakatādikāleyeva caritabbāni asīti khandhakavattānīti veditabbāni.**  
> In this chapter on duties, the fourteen great duties, such as the duties of the newcomer, the resident, the departing monk, the thanksgiving, the meal-ticket, the alms-collector, the forest-dweller, the monastery, the sauna, the privy, the preceptor, the teacher, the pupil, and the resident pupil, are recorded; but others, like those to be practiced only at times of censure-acts and so on, should be known as the eighty chapter-duties.  
>
