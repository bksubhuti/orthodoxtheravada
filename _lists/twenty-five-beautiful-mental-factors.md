### Overview
The Twenty-Five Beautiful Mental Factors (*Sobhana Cetasikā*) are the mental factors that, according to the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha, are beautiful: nineteen common to all beautiful consciousnesses, three abstinences, two immeasurables and the wisdom faculty (Abhidh-s 90:1-92:1). The Saṅgaha says that the nineteen common ones are found in all the fifty-nine beautiful consciousnesses (*sabbesupi ekūnasaṭṭhisobhanacittesu saṃvijjanti*, Abhidh-s 129:1). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines each of them in its analysis of the first wholesome consciousness (Dhs 415-441).

### The List
**The 19 Beautiful Universals (Sobhanasādhāraṇa):**  
1. **[Saddhā](/glossary/saddha/)** - Faith: trusting, confidence, serene confidence (*saddahanā okappanā abhippasādo*, Dhs 415:2).  
2. **[Sati](/glossary/sati/)** - Mindfulness: recollection, remembrance, bearing in mind, non-forgetting (*anussati paṭissati … asammussanatā*, Dhs 442:2).  
3. **[Hiri](/glossary/hiri/)** - Moral Shame: being ashamed of what one should be ashamed of, ashamed of entering upon evil unwholesome states (Dhs 428:2).  
4. **[Ottappa](/glossary/ottappa/)** - Moral Dread: being afraid of what one should be afraid of, afraid of entering upon evil unwholesome states (Dhs 429:2).  
5. **[Alobha](/glossary/alobha/)** - Non-greed: absence of greed, the wholesome root (*alobho kusalamūlaṃ*, Dhs 422:2).  
6. **[Adosa](/glossary/adosa/)** - Non-hatred: absence of hatred, the wholesome root (Dhs 423:2).  
7. **Tatramajjhattatā** - Equanimity (mental balance): even-mindedness regarding those states; it has the characteristic of looking on with equanimity at consciousness and mental factors occurring evenly, like a charioteer with steady horses (As 1266:1; Abhidh-s-t 1053:8).  
8. **Kāyapassaddhi** - Tranquility of the mental body: of the feeling, perception and formations aggregates (Dhs 430:2).  
9. **Cittapassaddhi** - Tranquility of consciousness: of the consciousness aggregate (Dhs 431:2).  
10. **Kāyalahutā** - Lightness of the mental body (Dhs 432:2).  
11. **Cittalahutā** - Lightness of consciousness (Dhs 433:2).  
12. **Kāyamudutā** - Pliancy of the mental body: softness, non-harshness, non-stiffness (Dhs 434:2).  
13. **Cittamudutā** - Pliancy of consciousness (Dhs 435:2).  
14. **Kāyakammaññatā** - Wieldiness of the mental body (Dhs 436:2).  
15. **Cittakammaññatā** - Wieldiness of consciousness (Dhs 437:2).  
16. **Kāyapāguññatā** - Proficiency of the mental body (Dhs 438:2).  
17. **Cittapāguññatā** - Proficiency of consciousness (Dhs 439:2).  
18. **Kāyujukatā** - Uprightness of the mental body: straightness, non-crookedness (Dhs 440:2).  
19. **Cittujukatā** - Uprightness of consciousness (Dhs 441:2).  

("Mental body" (*kāya*) here is the aggregates of feeling, perception and formations; "consciousness" (*citta*) is the consciousness aggregate: Dhs 430:2-431:2.)

**The 3 Abstinences (Viratiyo):**  
20. **[Sammāvācā](/glossary/sammavaca/)** - Right Speech  
21. **[Sammākammanta](/glossary/sammakammanta/)** - Right Action  
22. **Sammāājīva** - Right Livelihood  
(Abhidh-s 91:1: *sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo*.)

**The 2 Immeasurables ([Appamaññā](/glossary/appamanna/)):**  
23. **[Karuṇā](/glossary/karuna/)** - Compassion  
24. **[Muditā](/glossary/mudita/)** - Sympathetic Joy  
(Abhidh-s 92:1.)

**The 1 Wisdom Faculty:**  
25. **Paññindriya** - Wisdom / Non-delusion (*[amoha](/glossary/amoha/)*): knowing, investigation, discernment of states, and so on; the wisdom faculty, wisdom power, non-delusion (Dhs 424:2).


### Quote
> **Sobhanacetasikaṃ 6. Saddhā sati hirī ottappaṃ alobho adoso tatramajjhattatā kāyapassaddhi cittapassaddhi kāyalahutā cittalahutā kāyamudutā cittamudutā kāyakammaññatā cittakammaññatā kāyapāguññatā cittapāguññatā kāyujukatā cittujukatā ceti ekūnavīsatime cetasikā sobhanasādhāraṇā nāma. 7. Sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo ceti tisso viratiyo nāma. 8. Karuṇā muditā appamaññāyo nāmāti sabbathāpi paññindriyena saddhiṃ pañcavīsatime cetasikā sobhanāti veditabbā.**  
> Beautiful Mental Factors 6. Faith, mindfulness, moral shame, fear of wrongdoing, non-greed, non-hatred, equanimity, bodily tranquility, mental tranquility, lightness of body, lightness of mind, pliancy of body, pliancy of mind, wieldiness of body, wieldiness of mind, proficiency of body, proficiency of mind, straightforwardness of body, and straightforwardness of mind—these nineteen mental factors are called beautiful universals. 7. Right speech, right action, and right livelihood—these three are called abstinences. 8. Compassion and altruistic joy are called illimitables; and so together with the faculty of wisdom, these twenty-five mental factors in all should be understood as beautiful.  
>  
> — *[Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)* (Chapter II, §6-8, Sobhanacetasika) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=8&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=U29iaGFuYWNldGFzaWth4bmD)
