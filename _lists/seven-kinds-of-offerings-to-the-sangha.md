### Overview
When Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī offered a new pair of garments to the Buddha, he told her to give them to the Saṅgha (M-iii 1204). In the Dakkhiṇāvibhaṅga Sutta he tells Ānanda that he nowhere declares a gift to an individual more fruitful than an offering based in the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) (*saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā*, M-iii 1213:4), and sets out seven kinds of it.

### The List
1. An offering made to both Saṅghas (monks and nuns) headed by the Buddha. *(Only possible during the Buddha's lifetime).*
2. An offering made to both Saṅghas (monks and nuns) after the Buddha has attained [Parinibbāna](/glossary/parinibbana/).
3. An offering made exclusively to the [Bhikkhu](/glossary/bhikkhu/) Saṅgha (the community of monks).
4. An offering made exclusively to the [Bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/) Saṅgha (the community of nuns).
5. An offering made by requesting: "Let a specified number of monks and nuns be detailed to me from the Saṅgha."
6. An offering made by requesting: "Let a specified number of monks be detailed to me from the Saṅgha."
7. An offering made by requesting: "Let a specified number of nuns be detailed to me from the Saṅgha."

### Quote
> **“Satta kho panimānanda, saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. Katamā satta? Buddhappamukhe ubhatosaṅghe dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ paṭhamā saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. Tathāgate parinibbute ubhatosaṅghe dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ dutiyā saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. Bhikkhusaṅghe dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ tatiyā saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. Bhikkhunisaṅghe dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ catutthī saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. ‘ Ettakā me bhikkhū ca bhikkhuniyo ca saṅghato uddissathā ’ ti dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ pañcamī saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. ‘ Ettakā me bhikkhū saṅghato uddissathā ’ ti dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ chaṭṭhī saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā. ‘ Ettakā me bhikkhuniyo saṅghato uddissathā ’ ti dānaṃ deti – ayaṃ sattamī saṅghagatā dakkhiṇā.**  
> Ānanda, there are these seven religious donations bestowed on a Saṅgha. What seven? One gives a gift to both Saṅghas headed by the Buddha—this is the first religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift to both Saṅghas when the Tathāgata has entered parinibbāna—this is the second religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift to the Saṅgha of monks—this is the third religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift to the Saṅgha of nuns—this is the fourth religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift, thinking: ‘Assign this many monks and nuns to me from the Saṅgha’—this is the fifth religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift, thinking: ‘Assign this many monks to me from the Saṅgha’—this is the sixth religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha. One gives a gift, thinking: ‘Assign this many nuns to me from the Saṅgha’—this is the seventh religious donation bestowed on a Saṅgha.  
>  
> — [Majjhima Nikāya](/glossary/majjhima-nikaya/) (MN 142, Dakkhiṇāvibhaṅga Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ma_03&page_number=298&name=uparipa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MzgwLuKAmOKAmCBTYXR0YSBraG8gcGFuaW3EgW5hbmRhLCBzYeG5hWdoYWdhdMSBIGRha2toaeG5h8SBLg%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Dakkhiṇāvibhaṅga [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (MN 142) – The occasion is Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī's offering of garments (M-iii 1204); the seven are spoken to Ānanda (M-iii 1212:1). The Buddha adds that even in the future, when unethical "saffron-necked" ones exist, a gift made in the name of the Saṅgha is "asaṅkheyyaṃ appameyyaṃ" (M-iii 1213:1-3).
