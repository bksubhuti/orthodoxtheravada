### Overview
In the Theravāda classification of persons (*puggala*), ordinary unenlightened beings are called *[puthujjana](/glossary/puthujjana/)*. Once a person attains the first glimpse of awakening (Stream-entry), they enter the ranks of the Noble Ones (*Ariya*), who are then divided into two broad categories based on whether their training is finished.

### The List
1. **[Sekha](/glossary/sekha/)** - The Trainee: An individual who has reached the supramundane path but has not yet eradicated all fetters. This includes the Stream-enterer (*[Sotāpanna](/glossary/sotapanna/)*), Once-returner (*[Sakadāgāmī](/glossary/sakadagami/)*), and Non-returner (*[Anāgāmī](/glossary/anagami/)*), as well as one on the path to Arahatship (the Aṭṭhakathā counts seven trainees, Mp-ii 137:2). They are still "training" in higher virtue, mind, and wisdom.
2. **[Asekha](/glossary/asekha/)** - The Non-Trainee (The Adept): An individual who has realized the fruit of Arahatship. They have completely destroyed all taints and fetters. They require no further training because they have perfected the path.

### Quote
> **“Dve kho, gahapati, loke dakkhiṇeyyā – sekho ca asekho ca. Ime kho, gahapati, dve loke dakkhiṇeyyā, ettha ca dānaṃ dātabban” ti.**  
> “Householder, there are two worthy of offerings in the world: a trainee and one beyond training. These two, householder, are worthy of offerings in the world, and to them a gift should be given.”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 2.36) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_02&page_number=64&name=dukanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIER2ZSBraG8sIGdhaGFwYXRpLCBsb2tlIGRha2toaeG5h2V5ecSB)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Sekha [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (MN 53) – Details the practices and mindset of the trainee.
* **Canonical**: *Dukanipāta* (AN 2.36, A-ii 80:1-6, CST para 36) – Anāthapiṇḍika asks how many are worthy of offerings; the Buddha answers: the trainee (*sekho*) and the one beyond training (*asekho*).
* **Commentary**: *Manorathapūraṇī* (Mp-ii 137:2) – “Sekhoti iminā satta sekkhe dasseti”: by *sekho* he reveals the seven trainees.
