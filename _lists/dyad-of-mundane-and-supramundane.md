### Overview
This Dyad categorizes reality based on whether a phenomenon is bound within the cycle of rebirth ([saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/)) or whether it actively transcends and escapes that cycle. It is the dividing line between the world and liberation.

### The List
1. **Lokiyā dhammā** - Mundane states: Phenomena belonging to the "world" (*loka*). This includes all physical matter, and all consciousnesses/mental factors that operate within the sense-sphere, the fine-material sphere, and the immaterial sphere. Even the highest, most blissful formless jhānas are strictly mundane.
2. **Lokuttarā dhammā** - Supramundane states: Phenomena that transcend the world. This includes only nine things: the 4 Paths (*[Magga](/glossary/magga/)*), the 4 Fruits (*Phala*), and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/).

### Quote
> **Katame dhammā lokiyā? Sāsavā kusalākusalābyākatā dhammā kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā, rūpakkhandho … pe … viññāṇakkhandho – ime dhammā lokiyā.**  
> 1099. What states are mundane states? Wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate states with cankers; states which belong to the sensuous plane, states of the form sphere, states of the formless sphere; the form aggregate … (and so on) … the consciousness aggregate—these states are mundane states.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs, Dukanikkhepa, Cūḷantaradukaṃ) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=220&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTA5OS4gS2F0YW1lIGRoYW1txIEgbG9raXnEgT8%3D)
