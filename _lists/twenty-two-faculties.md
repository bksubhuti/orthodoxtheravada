### Overview
In the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), an "[Indriya](/glossary/indriya/)" (Faculty) is a controlling principle—a phenomenon that exercises supreme dominance over its specific domain. The ancient masters categorized all of physical biology, mental affective states, and supramundane spiritual attainments into a master list of 22 controlling faculties.

### The List
**The 6 Sensory Faculties:**
1. Eye faculty, 2. Ear faculty, 3. Nose faculty, 4. Tongue faculty, 5. Body faculty, 6. Mind faculty.
**The 3 Physical/Biological Faculties:**
7. Femininity faculty (*itthindriya*).
8. Masculinity faculty (*purisindriya*).
9. Life faculty (*jīvitindriya* - the vital force sustaining the body).
**The 5 Feeling Faculties:**
10. Pleasure faculty (physical).
11. Pain faculty (physical).
12. Joy faculty (mental).
13. Grief faculty (mental).
14. Equanimity faculty (neutral feeling).
**The 5 Spiritual Faculties:**
15. Faith, 16. Energy, 17. Mindfulness, 18. Concentration, 19. Wisdom.
**The 3 Supramundane Faculties:**
20. "I shall come to know the unknown" faculty (*anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ*; it goes to one stage only, the path of stream-entry, Paṭis 1112:4).
21. Highest knowledge faculty (*aññindriyaṃ*; it goes to six stages, from the fruit of stream-entry to the path of arahantship, Paṭis 1112:5).
22. "He who knows" faculty (*aññātāvindriyaṃ*; it goes to one stage only, the fruit of arahantship, Paṭis 1112:6).

### Quote
> **Bāvīsatindriyāni – cakkhundriyaṃ, sotindriyaṃ, ghānindriyaṃ, jivhindriyaṃ, kāyindriyaṃ, manindriyaṃ, itthindriyaṃ, purisindriyaṃ, jīvitindriyaṃ, sukhindriyaṃ, dukkhindriyaṃ, somanassindriyaṃ, domanassindriyaṃ, upekkhindriyaṃ, saddhindriyaṃ, vīriyindriyaṃ, satindriyaṃ, samādhindriyaṃ, paññindriyaṃ, anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, aññindriyaṃ, aññātāvindriyaṃ.**  
> 219. The twenty-two faculties (bāvīsatindriyāni) are: the eye faculty (cakkhundriyaṃ), the ear faculty (sotindriyaṃ), the nose faculty (ghānindriyaṃ), the tongue faculty (jivhindriyaṃ), the body faculty (kāyindriyaṃ), the mind faculty (manindriyaṃ), the feminine faculty (itthindriyaṃ), the masculine faculty (purisindriyaṃ), the life faculty (jīvitindriyaṃ), the faculty of pleasure (sukhindriyaṃ), the faculty of pain (dukkhindriyaṃ), the faculty of mental pleasure (somanassindriyaṃ), the faculty of displeasure (domanassindriyaṃ), the faculty of equanimity (upekkhindriyaṃ), the faculty of faith (saddhindriyaṃ), the faculty of energy (vīriyindriyaṃ), the faculty of mindfulness (satindriyaṃ), the faculty of concentration (samādhindriyaṃ), the faculty of wisdom (paññindriyaṃ), the faculty of the assurance ‘I shall come to finally know what is as yet not finally known’ (anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ), the faculty of final knowledge (aññindriyaṃ), and the faculty of one who has finally known (aññātāvindriyaṃ).
