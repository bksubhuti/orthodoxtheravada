### Overview
In the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), an "[Indriya](/glossary/indriya/)" (Faculty) is a controlling principle—a phenomenon that exercises supreme dominance over its specific domain. The ancient masters categorized all of physical biology, mental affective states, and supramundane spiritual attainments into a master list of 22 controlling faculties.

### The List
**The 6 Sensory Faculties:**
1. Eye faculty, 2. Ear faculty, 3. Nose faculty, 4. Tongue faculty, 5. Body faculty, 6. Mind faculty.
**The 3 Physical/Biological Faculties:**
7. Femininity faculty (*itthindriya*).
8. Masculinity faculty (*purisindriya*).
9. Life faculty (*jīvitindriya* - the vital force sustaining the body).
**The 5 Feeling Faculties:**
10. Pleasure faculty (physical).
11. Pain faculty (physical).
12. Joy faculty (mental).
13. Grief faculty (mental).
14. Equanimity faculty (neutral feeling).
**The 5 Spiritual Faculties:**
15. Faith, 16. Energy, 17. Mindfulness, 18. Concentration, 19. Wisdom.
**The 3 Supramundane Faculties:**
20. "I shall come to know the unknown" faculty (arises only at the Path of Stream-entry).
21. Highest knowledge faculty (possessed by Stream-enterers up to the Path of Arahatship).
22. "He who knows" faculty (possessed exclusively by Arahats).

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)* (Indriyavibhaṅga) – The canonical analysis of how these 22 control the existence and liberation of beings.
