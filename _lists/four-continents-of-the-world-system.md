### Overview
The Mūla names four continents: Jambudīpa, Aparagoyāna, Uttarakuru and Pubbavideha, each with its Sineru and moon and sun, a thousand of each in a thousandfold world system (AN 3.81, A-iii 861:2). The Aṭṭhakathā says that in one *cakkavāḷa*, one *lokadhātu*, each of the great islands (*mahādīpa*) is surrounded by five hundred small islands (Sp-i 576:5-6; Pj-ii 1902:2-4).

### The List
1. **Jambudīpa** - Ten thousand yojanas in size (Sp-i 576:2); chariot-shaped (*sakaṭasaṇṭhāno*, Vism-mht-i 834:6, Ṭīkā). Its humans are heroes, are mindful, and the spiritual life is lived here (A-ix 179:3). Buddhas do not arise in the other three islands but only in Jambudīpa (Ja-a-i 669:1).
2. **Aparagoyāna** - Seven thousand yojanas in size (Sp-i 576:3); mirror-shaped (*ādāsasaṇṭhāno*, Sp-t-i-a 1098:6, Ṭīkā), and the faces of its people are said to match the shape of the island (Sp-t-i-a 1098:7).
3. **Uttarakuru** - Eight thousand yojanas in size (Sp-i 576:5); seat-shaped (*pīṭhasaṇṭhāno*, Sp-t-i-a 1098:6, Ṭīkā). Its humans are *amamā apariggahā niyatāyukā* (selfless, not possessive, of fixed lifespan; A-ix 177:3), and by these they surpass the humans of Jambudīpa, but Jambudīpa's humans surpass them in courage, mindfulness and the spiritual life (A-ix 179:3).
4. **Pubbavideha** - Seven thousand yojanas in size (Sp-i 576:4); half-moon-shaped (*aḍḍhacandasaṇṭhāno*, Sp-t-i-a 1098:6, Ṭīkā).

### Quote
> **“Yāvatā, ānanda, candimasūriyā pariharanti, disā bhanti virocanā, tāva sahassadhā loko. Tasmiṃ sahassadhā loke sahassaṃ candānaṃ, sahassaṃ sūriyānaṃ, sahassaṃ sinerupabbatarājānaṃ, sahassaṃ jambudīpānaṃ, sahassaṃ aparagoyānānaṃ, sahassaṃ uttarakurūnaṃ, sahassaṃ pubbavidehānaṃ, cattāri mahāsamuddasahassāni, cattāri mahārājasahassāni, sahassaṃ cātumahārājikānaṃ, sahassaṃ tāvatiṃsānaṃ, sahassaṃ yāmānaṃ, sahassaṃ tusitānaṃ, sahassaṃ nimmānaratīnaṃ, sahassaṃ paranimmitavasavattīnaṃ, sahassaṃ brahmalokānaṃ – ayaṃ vuccatānanda, sahassī cūḷanikā lokadhātu.**  
> “Ānanda, as far as the moon and sun revolve and the shining ones light up the directions, so far is a thousandfold world system. In that thousandfold world system there are a thousand moons, a thousand suns, a thousand Sinerus, kings of mountains, a thousand Jambudīpas, a thousand Aparagoyānas, a thousand Uttarakurus, a thousand Pubbavidehas, four thousand great oceans, four thousand great kings, a thousand realms of the Four Great Kings, a thousand Tāvatiṁsa heavens, a thousand Yāma heavens, a thousand Tusita heavens, a thousand heavens of devas who delight in creation, a thousand heavens of devas who control what is created by others, and a thousand brahmā worlds—this is called, Ānanda, a thousandfold lesser world system.  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Cūḷanika [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* (AN 3.81, A-iii 861:1-2) – names the four continents in the order Jambudīpa, Aparagoyāna, Uttarakuru, Pubbavideha. *Tiṭhāna Sutta* (AN 9.21, A-ix 177:1-179:3, heading A-ix 176:1 "1. Tiṭhānasuttaṃ") compares Uttarakuru and Jambudīpa. Commentaries: Sp-i 576:1-6, Pj-ii 1902:1-4 (sizes), Ja-a-i 669:1 (Buddhas only in Jambudīpa); Ṭīkā: Sp-t-i-a 1097:1, 1098:6-7, Vism-mht-i 834:6 (shapes).
