### What Is Classical Theravāda?

**Classical Theravāda** refers to the authentic, traditional form of Buddhism as preserved in the Pāḷi Canon — the unaltered teachings of the Buddha as transmitted through the Theravāda monastic lineage.

This means:

- **Reliance on the Pāḷi Canon** as the primary authority for the Dhamma and Vinaya.
- **Strict adherence to the Vinaya** (monastic code) as the foundation of the Saṅgha.
- **Emphasis on Abhidhamma** as a systematic exposition of the Buddha's teachings on mind and reality.
- **Rejecting innovations** that were not taught by the Buddha and are not found in the early texts.

The term *Orthodox* reflects a commitment to the original teachings — not a cultural or nationalistic form of Buddhism, but the *sasana* (dispensation) as taught, not as adapted.
