### Grammatical Analysis
**Anāgāmī**: [m.; f.] Non-Returner. Formed by negative prefix *an* (not) + *āgāmī* (one who returns). Signifies an individual who will never again return to the sensual sphere of existence.

### Orthodox Definition
The *Anāgāmī* is the third of the four noble disciples (*ariya-puggala*) in the Theravāda hierarchy. This sublime stage marks the definitive, absolute destruction of the five lower fetters (*orambhāgiya-[saṃyojana](/glossary/samyojana/)*) that bind beings to the lower sensuous realms.

The five lower fetters are sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāsa, kāmacchanda and byāpāda (A-ix 453:3; Vibh 2908:2); the commentaries and the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha call the last two *kāmarāga* and *byāpāda* (Abhidh-s 849:1):
1. *Sakkāya-diṭṭhi* (Identity view - broken at stream-entry)
2. *[Vicikicchā](/glossary/vicikiccha/)* (Skeptical doubt - broken at stream-entry)
3. *[Sīlabbataparāmāsa](/glossary/silabbataparamasa/)* (Clinging to rituals - broken at stream-entry)
4. *[Kāmarāga](/glossary/kamaraga/)* (Sensual lust/craving) - **Eradicated completely here**
5. *Byāpāda* / *[Paṭigha](/glossary/patigha/)* (Ill-will/Aversion) - **Eradicated completely here** (the third path abandons *kāmarāga* and *byāpāda*, Abhidh-s 849:1)

The Puggalapaññatti defines him: "pañcannaṃ orambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ parikkhayā opapātiko hoti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā" – by the complete destruction of the five lower fetters he arises spontaneously (in another world), attains parinibbāna there, and is not subject to return from that world (Pp 225:2). The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha says he becomes an *anāgāmī* by the complete abandoning (*anavasesappahāna*) of sensual desire and ill will through the non-return path, and does not come back to this state of existence (*anāgantvā itthattaṃ*, Abhidh-s 849:1; Nidd-a-ii 547:2 says the same of the third path). The Atthasālinī says that by the non-return path the subtle (*aṇusahagata*) sensual desire and ill will are abandoned (As 1714:2), and that the path is a settling of the two sorts of resentful mind (As 1762:3). The Puggalapaññatti then gives the kinds of non-returner, such as the *antarāparinibbāyī* (Pp 226:1-3) and the *uddhaṃsoto akaniṭṭhagāmī* (Pp 230:1-4); the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha lists the five Pure Abodes (*suddhāvāsabhūmi*: Avihā, Atappā, Sudassā, Sudassī, Akaniṭṭhā, Abhidh-s 390:1).

### Quote
> **Katamo ca puggalo anāgāmī? Idhekacco puggalo pañcannaṃ orambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ parikkhayā opapātiko hoti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā – ayaṃ vuccati puggalo “anāgāmī”.**  
> 35. What sort of person is a “non-returner”? Here a person, having completely destroyed the five fetters causing re-birth in the lower worlds, becomes a “being of apparitional re-birth”; attaining the final release in the self-same state, he is not liable to return from that world. Such a man is said to be a non-returner.  
>  
> — [Puggalapaññatti](/glossary/puggalapannatti/) (Catukkaniddesa, Anāgāmī) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_04&page_number=119&name=puggalapa%C3%B1%C3%B1attip%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MzUuIEthdGFtbyBjYSBwdWdnYWxvIGFuxIFnxIFtxKs%2F)

### Quote
> **Anāgāmimaggaṃ bhāvetvā kāmarāgabyāpādānamanavasesappahānena anāgāmī nāma hoti anāgantvā itthattaṃ.**  
> 70. Having developed the path of non-returning, by the residue-less abandoning of sensual lust and ill will, one becomes a non-returner (anāgāmī), not returning to this state of existence (itthattaṃ).  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (Ch. 9, §70) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=67&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=NzAuIEFuxIFnxIFtaW1hZ2dh4bmDIGJoxIF2ZXR2xIEga8SBbWFyxIFnYWJ5xIFwxIFkxIFuYW1hbmF2YXNlc2FwcGFoxIFuZW5hIGFuxIFnxIFtxKsgbsSBbWE%3D)

### Quote
> **Tato vuttanayena vipassitvā kāmarāgabyāpādānaṃ anavasesappahānaṃ karonto tatiyamaggaṃ pāpuṇāti, vuttanayena phalañca.**  
> Then, having practiced insight in the manner stated, destroying lust and ill-will without remainder, one attains the third path, and in the manner stated, the fruition.  
>

### Quote
> **Anāgāmimaggena aṇusahagatakāmarāgabyāpādavasena tāni eva cha cittāni pahīyanti.**  
> By the non-returner path (anāgāmimaggena), those very six minds (tāni eva cha cittāni) are abandoned (pahīyanti) by way of subtle sensual passion and ill will (aṇusahagatakāmarāgabyāpādavasena).
