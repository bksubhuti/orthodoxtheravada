### Grammatical Analysis
**Gotrabhuñāṇa**: [nt.] change-of-lineage knowledge. Formed by *gotra* (lineage) + *bhū* (becoming, overcoming) + *ñāṇa* (knowledge). TPR spells it *gotrabhuñāṇa* (Paṭis 15:1); the same state is called *gotrabhū*. The Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā explains it by three phrases: "it is *gotrabhū* because it overcomes (*abhibhuyyati*) the lineage of the worldling; it is *gotrabhū* because it enters (*pakkhandati*) the noble lineage; and *abhibhuyyitvā pakkhandatīti gotrabhū* combines both meanings" (Paṭis-a 1110:4-6).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Mūla the Paṭisambhidāmagga's Mātikā gives it as "understanding of emergence and turning away from the external is change-of-lineage knowledge" (*bahiddhā vuṭṭhānavivaṭṭane paññā gotrabhuñāṇaṃ*, Paṭis 15:1), and the Gotrabhuñāṇaniddesa (Paṭis 360-378) shows how: it overcomes arising, occurrence, the sign, accumulation and so on (Paṭis 361:1-2; 369:2). The *gotrabhū* states are eight by way of serenity (each overcoming the hindrances, applied thought, rapture, and so on for the sake of obtaining a jhāna or attainment, Paṭis 368:1-10) and ten by way of insight (for the sake of obtaining the paths, fruits and attainments, Paṭis 369:1-6). The Puggalapaññatti defines the person: "What sort of person is a *gotrabhū*? One endowed with those states immediately after which there is entry into the noble state (*ariyadhammassa avakkanti*)" (Pp 200:1-2).

The Aṭṭhakathā says that after the adaptation knowledge (*anulomañāṇa*), which is the end of insight with formations as its object, "beyond that arises the change-of-lineage knowledge, making Nibbāna its object, transcending the lineage of the worldling, descending into the noble lineage, the first advertence to the object Nibbāna, irreversible"; when it has ceased the path of stream-entry arises, destroying the three fetters (Nidd-a-ii 546:2-3, 546:7). Although it sees Nibbāna before the path, it is said not to be "seeing" because it does not do the work of abandoning defilements (Spk-iv 516:3). The Visuddhimagga, using the simile of the snake and a man who is rid of it, says "the change-of-lineage knowledge is like the releasing of the snake; the knowledge of the path is like standing and looking back at the way he came; the knowledge of fruition like standing in a place of safety" (Vism-ii 1826:2).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭisambhidāmagga)
> **Gotrabhuñāṇaniddeso 59. Kathaṃ bahiddhā vuṭṭhānavivaṭṭane paññā gotrabhuñāṇaṃ? Uppādaṃ abhibhuyyatīti – gotrabhu.**  
> 10. Exposition of change-of-lineage knowledge 59. How is it that understanding of emergence and turning away from the external is change-of-lineage knowledge? It overcomes arising, thus it is change of lineage.  
>  
> — [Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/) (Gotrabhuñāṇaniddesa, Paṭis 360-361) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_18&page_number=63&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTAuIEdvdHJhYmh1w7HEgeG5h2FuaWRkZXNv)


### Quote (Mūla: Puggalapaññatti)
> **Katamo ca puggalo gotrabhū? Yesaṃ dhammānaṃ samanantarā ariyadhammassa avakkanti hoti tehi dhammehi samannāgato – ayaṃ vuccati puggalo “gotrabhū”.**  
> 10. What sort of person is one become of the Ariya family? The person who is endowed with those conditions immediately after which there is the advent of the Ariyan nature is said to be one become of the Ariya family.  
>  
> — [Puggalapaññatti](/glossary/puggalapannatti/) (Pp 200, "10. Katamo ca puggalo gotrabhū?") · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_04&page_number=115&name=puggalapa%C3%B1%C3%B1attip%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTAuIEthdGFtbyBjYSBwdWdnYWxvIGdvdHJhYmjFqz8%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Abhibhuyyatīti gotrabhūti ca puthujjanagottābhibhavanato gotrabhubhāvo vutto. Pakkhandatīti gotrabhūti ariyagottabhāvanato gotrabhubhāvo vutto. Abhibhuyyitvā pakkhandatīti gotrabhūti ubho atthe samāsetvā vuttaṃ.**  
> It is called 'change-of-lineage' (gotrabhū) because it overcomes the run-of-the-mill lineage. It is called 'change-of-lineage' because it enters into the Noble lineage. 'Having overcome, it enters into... change-of-lineage'—the two meanings are summarized.  
>  
> — Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā (Saddhammappakāsinī, Gotrabhuñāṇaniddesavaṇṇanā, Paṭis-a 1109-1110) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_18_01&page_number=253&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=QWJoaWJodXl5YXTEq3RpIGdvdHJhYmjFq3RpIGNhIHB1dGh1amphbmFnb3R0xIFiaGliaGF2YW5hdG8gZ290cmFiaHViaMSBdm8gdnV0dG8u)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Cūḷaniddesa-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Idaṃ pana anulomañāṇaṃ saṅkhārārammaṇāya vuṭṭhānagāminiyā vipassanāya pariyosānaṃ hoti, nippariyāyena pana gotrabhuñāṇameva vipassanāya pariyosānanti vuccati. Tato paraṃ nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ kurumānaṃ puthujjanagottaṃ atikkamamānaṃ ariyagottaṃ okkamamānaṃ nibbānārammaṇe paṭhamasamannāhārabhūtaṃ apunarāvaṭṭakaṃ gotrabhuñāṇaṃ uppajjati.**  
> But this adaptation-knowledge is the end of the insight leading to emergence which has activities as its object; however, the change-of-lineage knowledge alone is called the end of insight. Beyond that, the change-of-lineage knowledge arises, which makes Nibbāna the object, transcends the lineage of worldlings, descends into the noble lineage, is the first occurrence in the Nibbāna-object, and is irreversible.  
>  
> — Saddhammapajjotikā (Cūḷaniddesa-aṭṭhakathā, Nidd-a-ii 546) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_17&page_number=100&name=c%C5%AB%E1%B8%B7aniddesaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=SWRh4bmDIHBhbmEgYW51bG9tYcOxxIHhuYdh4bmDIHNh4bmFa2jEgXLEgXJhbW1h4bmHxIF5YSB2deG5reG5rWjEgW5hZ8SBbWluaXnEgSB2aXBhc3NhbsSBeWE%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Kaṇhasappupamā paṭisaṅkhāñāṇe vuttāva. Upamāsaṃsandane panettha sappavissajjanaṃ viya gotrabhuñāṇaṃ, muñcitvā āgatamaggaṃ olokentassa ṭhānaṃ viya maggañāṇaṃ, gantvā abhayaṭṭhāne ṭhānaṃ viya phalañāṇanti ayaṃ viseso.**  
> 791. The simile of the black snake (kaṇhasappupamā) was already stated under the knowledge of reflection. But in the comparison of the similes here, the change-of-lineage knowledge is like the releasing of the snake; knowledge of the path is like the position of one who, after releasing it, looks back at the way he came; and knowledge of the fruition is like the standing in a place of safety after going there—this is the distinction.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Chapter XXI, Paṭipadāñāṇadassanavisuddhiniddesa, Vism-ii 1826) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=302&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NzkxLiBLYeG5h2hhc2FwcHVwYW3EgSBwYeG5rWlzYeG5hWtoxIHDscSB4bmHZSB2dXR0xIF2YS4%3D)
