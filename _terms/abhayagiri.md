### Grammatical Analysis
**Abhayagiri**: [m.] The Fearless Mountain. Formed by *abhaya* (fearless, without danger) + *giri* (mountain). In the texts the residents are called *abhayagirivāsī* and *abhayagirika* (Sp-i 2333:1; Mhv 7856:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Abhayagiri* was a great monastery in ancient Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka, and the name of the fraternity that lived there, rival to the orthodox [Mahāvihāra](/glossary/maha-vihara/). The Mahāvaṃsa says the king (who had killed the Tamil Dāṭhika) had it built on the site of a Nigaṇṭha monastery he had destroyed (Mhv 7792-7802); the Sāsanavaṃsappadīpikā says King Vaṭṭagāmaṇi had it built and that the fraternity split from the Mahāvihāra fraternity at Abhayagiri after it was built, and describes it as one that altered the text of the Parivāra (Sas 174:3). The Mahāvaṃsa says its monks "departed from the Theravāda" (Mhv 7856:1).

The Samantapāsādikā, the Vinaya commentary of the [Mahāvihāra](/glossary/maha-vihara/), records disputes between the Mahāvihāra residents and the Abhayagiri residents over how a Vinaya passage should be read (Sp-i 2333), and the Dīghanikāya commentary names the Abhayagiri among the great monasteries (Sv-ii 271:2). The Mahāvaṃsa also mentions Abhayagiri monks of the Vetulla school (Mhv 8899:1).

The Sāsanavaṃsappadīpikā records that, relying on the kings Vijayabāhu and Parakkamabāhu, the Mahāvihāra monks purified the Dispensation and only the Mahāvihāra group remained (Sas 192:1).

### Quote
> **Bhātiyarājakālepi mahāvihāravāsīnañca abhayagirivāsīnañca therānaṃ imasmiṃyeva pade vivādo ahosi. Abhayagirivāsinopi attano suttaṃ vatvā “tumhākaṃ vāde thero kārako hotī” ti vadanti.**

### Quote
> **Tato pacchā vi vijayabāhurājānaṃ parakkamabāhurājānañca nissāya mahāvihāra vāsino bhikkhū sāsanaṃ parisuddhaṃ akaṃsu, adhammavādino sabbepi uppabbājetvā mahāvihāravāsigaṇoyeva eko patiṭṭhahi, yathā abbhādiupakkilesamalehi vimutto nisānāthoti.**  
> Later on, relying on King Vijayabāhu and King Parakkamabāhu, the bhikkhus of the Mahāvihāra made the Dispensation pure; having exiled all those who speak against the Dhamma, only the Mahāvihāra group was established, just as the moon (freed) from stains of clouds and other impurities.  
>  
> — Sāsanavaṃsappadīpikā (Añña)
### Textual References
* **Aṭṭhakathā**: *Samantapāsādikā* (Sp-i 2333) – the Abhayagiri and Mahāvihāra elders dispute a Vinaya reading in the time of King Bhātiya; *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī* (Sv-ii 271:2) – the Abhayagiri among the great monasteries.
* **Historical**: *Mahāvaṃsa* (Mhv 7795-7802, 7852-7856, 8899) – founding, the departure of the Abhayagirians from the Theravāda, and the Vetulla-school monks.
* **Historical**: *Sāsanavaṃsappadīpikā* (Sas 174:3, 192:1) – the split, and the later purification under the [Mahāvihāra](/glossary/maha-vihara/).
