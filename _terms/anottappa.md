### Grammatical Analysis
**Anottappa**: [nt.] moral recklessness; fearlessness of wrongdoing; lack of dread. Formed by negative prefix *an* (not) + *[ottappa](/glossary/ottappa/)* (fear of consequences).

### Orthodox Definition
*Anottappa* is the twin of *[ahirika](/glossary/ahirika/)*; the Aṅguttara names the two together as the two dark qualities (*ahirikañca anottappañca*, A-ii 24:3). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines it: "Yaṃ na ottappati ottappitabbena, na ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati anottappaṃ", "that which does not fear what one should fear, does not fear the acquisition of evil unwholesome states" (Dhs 2038:2, §1245; at the unwholesome citta, Dhs 896:2). The Atthasālinī analyses the word (*na ottappaṃ anottappaṃ*, As 1773:4) and gives its characteristic: not being afraid of those same acts of misconduct (*teheva asārajjanalakkhaṇaṃ*), or not dreading them (*anuttāsanalakkhaṇaṃ*) (As 1773:6; Vism-ii 462:5). The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha lists it with moha, ahirika and uddhacca as one of the four universal unwholesome factors (*sabbākusalasādhāraṇa*), found in all twelve unwholesome cittas (Abhidh-s 115:1).

The sub-commentary on the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha (Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā) illustrates it with a moth that has no fear of fire: *aggito salabho viya tato anuttāsalakkhaṇaṃ anottappaṃ* (Abhidh-s-t 1031:8).

### Quote
> **“Dveme, bhikkhave, dhammā kaṇhā. Katame dve? Ahirikañca anottappañca. Ime kho, bhikkhave, dve dhammā kaṇhā” ti.**  
> “Monks, there are these two dark qualities. What are the two? Shamelessness and lack of moral dread. These, monks, are the two dark qualities.”  
>

### Quote
> **Tattha katamaṃ anottappaṃ? Yaṃ na ottappati ottappitabbena, na ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati anottappaṃ.**  
> 1245. What is lack of fear of wrongdoing? Whatever is not concerned about what should be feared, not concerned about the acquisition of bad, unwholesome states — this is called lack of fear of wrongdoing.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (§1245) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=246&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTI0NS4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtYeG5gyBhbm90dGFwcGHhuYM%2F)

### Quote
> **Itaresu pana – na hiriyatīti ahiriko. Ahirikassa bhāvo ahirikaṃ. Na ottappaṃ anottappaṃ. Tesu ahirikaṃ kāyaduccaritādīhi ajigucchanalakkhaṇaṃ, alajjālakkhaṇaṃ vā. Anottappaṃ teheva asārajjanalakkhaṇaṃ anuttāsanalakkhaṇaṃ vā.**  
> But (pana) regarding the other (terms) (itaresu), one who is not ashamed (na hiriyatī) is ‘morally shameless’ (ahiriko). The state (bhāva) of the morally shameless (ahirika) is ‘moral shamelessness’ (ahirikaṃ). Not fear of wrongdoing (na ottappaṃ) is ‘lack of fear of wrongdoing’ (anottappaṃ). Among them (tesu), moral shamelessness (ahirikaṃ) is characterized by not despising (ajigucchana) bodily misconduct (kāyaduccarita) and other (factors) (ādīhi), or (vā) it is characterized by not being ashamed (alajjā). Lack of fear of wrongdoing (anottappaṃ) is characterized by not being afraid (asārajjana) of these very (actions) (teheva), or (vā) it is characterized by not dreading (anuttāsana).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (on ahirika and anottappa) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=291&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=SXRhcmVzdSBwYW5hIOKAkyBuYSBoaXJpeWF0xKt0aSBhaGlyaWtvLg%3D%3D)

### Quote
> **Akusalesu pana moho ahirikaṃ anottappaṃ uddhaccañcāti cattārome cetasikā sabbākusalasādhāraṇā nāma, sabbesupi dvādasā kusalesu labbhanti.**  
> 20. Among the unwholesome (akusala), however, these four mental factors—delusion (moha), moral shamelessness (ahirika), lack of fear of wrongdoing (anottappa), and restlessness (uddhacca)—are called universal to all unwholesome states; they are found in all twelve unwholesome types of consciousness.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (Ch. 2, §20) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=10&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MjAuIEFrdXNhbGVzdSBwYW5hIG1vaG8gYWhpcmlrYeG5gyBhbm90dGFwcGHhuYM%3D)

### Quote
> **Tattha gūthato gāmasūkaro viya kāyaduccaritādito ajigucchanalakkhaṇaṃ ahirikaṃ, aggito salabho viya tato anuttāsalakkhaṇaṃ anottappaṃ.**  
> Therein, moral shamelessness has the characteristic of not being disgusted by bodily misconduct, etc., like a village pig is not disgusted by dung; lack of fear of wrongdoing has the characteristic of not fearing that misconduct, like a moth does not fear fire.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā (the village pig and the moth) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=107&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGfFq3RoYXRvIGfEgW1hc8Wra2FybyB2aXlhIGvEgXlhZHVjY2FyaXTEgWRpdG8gYWppZ3VjY2hhbmFsYWtraGHhuYdh4bmD)
