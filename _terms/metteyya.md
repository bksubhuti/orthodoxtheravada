### Grammatical Analysis
**Metteyya**: [m.] The Future Buddha. Derived from *mettā* (loving-kindness, benevolence). (Sanskrit: *Maitreya*).

### Orthodox Definition
*Metteyya* is the prophesied next *[Sammā-sambuddha](/glossary/samma-sambuddha/)* who will arise in this world. According to Theravāda cosmology, we currently live in a "Fortunate Aeon" (*Bhadda-kappa*), which will host a total of five Buddhas. Gotama was the fourth, and Metteyya will be the fifth and final Buddha of this cycle.

The canonical source for his time is the Cakkavatti Sutta (DN 26): after human lifespan and the ten wholesome courses of action have first declined and then recovered, Metteyya will arise "among humans living 80,000 years" (D-iii 247:1). In that age Jambudīpa is prosperous and crowded, and there are only three ailments: desire, hunger and old age (D-iii 245:1-3). He will teach the Dhamma and look after a Saṅgha of many thousands (D-iii 247:4-6). The later chronicle Mahāvaṃsa says the Bodhisatta Metteyya dwells in the Tusita city (Mhv 7509:1; also Thūpavaṃsa, Thup 678:2); this is not stated in the Mūla.

### Quote
> **“Asītivassasahassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu metteyyo nāma bhagavā loke uppajjissati arahaṃ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṃ buddho bhagavā.**  
> 107. ‘Among humans living 80,000 years, monks, there will arise in the world an Exalted One named Metteyya, Arahant, Fully Awakened, abounding in wisdom and goodness, happy, with knowledge of the worlds, unsurpassed as a guide to mortals willing to be led, a teacher for gods and men, an Exalted One, a Buddha, even as I am now.  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Cakkavatti-Sīhanāda Sutta* (DN 26; CST heading "3. Cakkavattisuttaṃ" D-iii 191:1, section "Metteyyabuddhuppādo" D-iii 246:1) – The Buddha prophesies the arising of Metteyya, who will look after a [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) of many thousands of monks (D-iii 247:6).
* **Canonical**: *[Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)* (Bv 3220:1) – "Ahametarahi sambuddho, metteyyo cāpi hessati" ("I am now the Perfectly Enlightened One, and Metteyya will also be").
* **Commentary**: *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī* (Sv-ii 16:14) – "Imasmiṃ bhaddakappe kakusandho, koṇāgamano, kassapo, gotamo … cattāro buddhā uppannā, metteyyo uppajjissati": four Buddhas have arisen in this Fortunate Aeon and Metteyya will arise.
* **Commentary**: *Anāgatavaṃsa* – A post-canonical text providing elaborate details on Metteyya's future life, family, and the utopia of his era.
