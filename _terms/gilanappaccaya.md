### Grammatical Analysis
**Gilānappaccaya**: [m.] requisite for the sick; medicinal requisite. Formed by *gilāna* (sick) + *paccaya* (requisite, support). TPR also spells it *gilānapaccaya* (Sv-i 758:7); in the Mūla it is a member of the compound *gilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhāra* (D-i 265:2).

### Orthodox Definition
*Gilānappaccaya* is the fourth of the four requisites: robes, almsfood, lodging and the medicinal requisite for the sick (*cīvara-piṇḍapāta-senāsana-gilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhāra*, D-i 265:2). The Aṭṭhakathā glosses the compound as "any medicine suitable for a sick person" (*yaṃ kiñci gilānassa sappāyaṃ osadhaṃ*, Sv-i 758:7), and for the "choice" medicines as "excellent medicines like ghee, oil, honey and molasses" (Ps-i 772:3).

The Vinaya's Gilānavatthukathā tells of a monk ill with dysentery, lying fallen in his own excrement, whom no monk tended because he was "of no use to the monks"; the Buddha had Ānanda bring water, Ānanda washed him, and they laid him on a couch; then the Buddha told the monks: "You have no mother, you have no father who might tend you. If you do not tend one another, who will tend you? Whoever would tend me, let him tend the sick" (Vin-iii 2208:1-4, 13-18, 29-31). The same passage goes on: if he has a preceptor, the preceptor is to tend him as long as he lives and wait for his recovery (Vin-iii 2208:32-33).

### Quote (Mūla: Dīgha Nikāya)
> **Atha kho naṃ mayameva abhivādeyyāmapi, paccuṭṭheyyāmapi, āsanenapi nimanteyyāma, abhinimanteyyāmapi naṃ cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārehi, dhammikampissa rakkhāvaraṇaguttiṃ saṃvidaheyyāmā” ti.**  
> Rather, we would pay homage to him, rise up out of respect for him, invite him to a seat, and invite him to accept from us robes, almsfood, dwelling, and medicinal requirements. And we would provide him righteous protection, defence, and security.”  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 2, Sāmaññaphala Sutta, D-i 265) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_01&page_number=57&name=s%C4%ABlakkhandhavaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=QXRoYSBraG8gbmHhuYMgbWF5YW1ldmEgYWJoaXbEgWRleXnEgW1hcGksIHBhY2N14bmt4bmtaGV5ecSBbWFwaSw%3D)


### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya Piṭaka (Mahāvagga))
> **“Natthi vo, bhikkhave, mātā, natthi pitā, ye vo upaṭṭhaheyyuṃ. Tumhe ce, bhikkhave, aññamaññaṃ na upaṭṭhahissatha, atha ko carahi upaṭṭhahissati? Yo, bhikkhave, maṃ upaṭṭhaheyya so gilānaṃ upaṭṭhaheyya. Sace upajjhāyo hoti, upajjhāyena yāvajīvaṃ upaṭṭhātabbo; vuṭṭhānamassa āgametabbaṃ.**  
> 'Monks, you have not a mother, you have not a father who might tend you.' 'If you, monks, do not tend one another, then who is there who will tend you?' 'Whoever, monks, would tend me, he should tend the sick.' 'If he has a preceptor he should be tended for life by the preceptor,' 'who should wait for his recovery.'  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Mahāvagga, Cīvarakkhandhaka, Gilānavatthukathā, Vin-iii 2207, para 365) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_03&page_number=417&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIE5hdHRoaSB2bywgYmhpa2toYXZlLCBtxIF0xIEsIG5hdHRoaQ%3D%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Sumaṅgalavilāsinī)
> **Ettha ca gilānapaccayabhesajjaparikkhāroti yaṃ kiñci gilānassa sappāyaṃ osadhaṃ.**  
> Here, “medicinal requirements” (gilānapaccayabhesajjaparikkhāro) refers to any medicine suitable for a sick person.  
>  
> — [Sumaṅgalavilāsinī](/glossary/sumangalavilasini/) (Sv-i 758, on the Sāmaññaphala Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_di_01&page_number=153&name=s%C4%ABlakkhandhavagga%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=RXR0aGEgY2EgZ2lsxIFuYXBhY2NheWFiaGVzYWpqYXBhcmlra2jEgXJvdGkgeWHhuYMga2nDsWNpIGdpbMSBbmFzc2E%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Papañcasūdanī)
> **Gilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārānanti sappitelamadhuphāṇitādīnaṃ uttamabhesajjānaṃ.**  
> ‘Choice medicines and supplies for the sick’ means excellent medicines like ghee, oil, honey, and molasses.  
>  
> — Papañcasūdanī (Ps-i 772, Majjhimanikāya-aṭṭhakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ma_01_01&page_number=153&name=m%C5%ABlapa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81saa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=R2lsxIFuYXBwYWNjYXlhYmhlc2FqamFwYXJpa2toxIFyxIFuYW50aSBzYXBwaXRlbGFtYWRodXBoxIHhuYdpdMSBZMSrbmHhuYMgdXR0YW1hYmhlc2FqasSBbmHhuYMu)
