### Grammatical Analysis
**Garudhamma**: [m.] weighty principle; principle of respect. Formed by *garu* (weighty, to be respected) + *dhamma* (principle, rule). Each of the eight ends with the same refrain, that this principle too is "to be honored, respected, esteemed, and worshiped, and not to be transgressed for life" (*sakkatvā garukatvā mānetvā pūjetvā yāvajīvaṃ anatikkamanīyo*, Vin-iv 2428:2).

### Orthodox Definition
The *aṭṭha garudhammā* are the eight important principles which the Buddha laid down for the bhikkhunīs, in the Bhikkhunikkhandhaka (Cv 10, Vin-iv 2419). When Ānanda had asked on behalf of Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī that women be allowed to go forth, the Buddha said: "If, Ānanda, Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī accepts the eight important principles, let that be her higher ordination" (*sace, ānanda, mahāpajāpatī gotamī aṭṭha garudhamme paṭiggaṇhāti, sāvassā hotu upasampadā*, Vin-iv 2427:1-2; 2436:1; A-viii 686:1).

The eight are, in the order of the text:
1. A bhikkhunī ordained even for a hundred years must pay respect (salutation, rising, *añjali*, proper courtesy) to a bhikkhu ordained that very day (Vin-iv 2428:1).
2. A bhikkhunī must not spend the rains in a dwelling where there is no bhikkhu (Vin-iv 2429:1).
3. Every fortnight she must expect two things from the bhikkhu Saṅgha: asking about the Uposatha and approaching for exhortation (Vin-iv 2430:1).
4. After the rains she must invite admonition (*pavāraṇā*) in both Saṅghas, on what was seen, heard or suspected (Vin-iv 2431:1).
5. A bhikkhunī who has committed a grave offence (*garudhammaṃ ajjhāpannā*) must undergo the *mānatta* for a fortnight in both Saṅghas (Vin-iv 2432:1).
6. A probationer (*sikkhamānā*) who has trained in the six rules for two years must seek higher ordination in both Saṅghas (Vin-iv 2433:1).
7. A bhikkhunī must not abuse or revile a bhikkhu in any way (Vin-iv 2434:1).
8. From today the path of speech of bhikkhunīs towards bhikkhus is closed, that of bhikkhus towards bhikkhunīs is not closed (Vin-iv 2435:1).

The Buddha compares their laying down to a man who builds an embankment around a great reservoir beforehand, solely to stop the water from overflowing: "even so, Ānanda, have I laid down beforehand the eight important principles for bhikkhunīs, not to be transgressed for life" (Vin-iv 2453:1-2). The Aṅguttara-aṭṭhakathā (Manorathapūraṇī) explains that they were prescribed beforehand in order that they should not be transgressed, so that the Dispensation might stand for the longer time (Mp-viii 181:1).

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya Piṭaka (Cūḷavagga))
> **“Sace, ānanda, mahāpajāpatī gotamī aṭṭha garudhamme paṭiggaṇhāti, sāvassā. Hotu upasampadā. “Vassasatūpasampannāya bhikkhuniyā tadahupasampannassa bhikkhuno abhivādanaṃ paccuṭṭhānaṃ añjalikammaṃ sāmīcikammaṃ kātabbaṃ. “Na bhikkhuniyā abhikkhuke āvāse vassaṃ vasitabbaṃ. “Anvaddhamāsaṃ bhikkhuniyā bhikkhusaṅghato dve dhammā paccāsīsitabbā – uposathapucchakañca, ovādūpasaṅkamanañca. “Vassaṃvuṭṭhāya bhikkhuniyā ubhatosaṅghe tīhi ṭhānehi pavāretabbaṃ – diṭṭhena vā, sutena vā, parisaṅkāya vā. “Garudhammaṃ ajjhāpannāya bhikkhuniyā ubhatosaṅghe pakkhamānattaṃ caritabbaṃ. “Dve vassāni chasu dhammesu sikkhitasikkhāya sikkhamānāya ubhatosaṅghe upasampadā pariyesitabbā. “Na bhikkhuniyā kenaci pariyāyena bhikkhu akkositabbo paribhāsitabbo. “Ajjatagge ovaṭo bhikkhunīnaṃ bhikkhūsu vacanapatho, anovaṭo bhikkhūnaṃ bhikkhunīsu vacanapatho. Ayampi dhammo sakkatvā garukatvā mānetvā pūjetvā yāvajīvaṃ anatikkamanīyo. “Sace, ānanda, mahāpajāpati gotamī ime aṭṭha garudhamme paṭiggaṇhāti, sāvassā hotu upasampadā” ti.**  
> 403. “If, Ānanda, Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī accepts the eight important principles, let that be her higher ordination.”  “A bhikkhunī ordained even for a hundred years must perform salutation, rising to welcome, acts of respectful service, and acts of propriety towards a bhikkhu ordained that very day. “A bhikkhunī must not spend the rains retreat in a dwelling where there are no bhikkhus. “Every fortnight, a bhikkhunī must expect two things from the Saṅgha of bhikkhus: asking about the Observance day, and approaching for exhortation. “Having completed the rains retreat, a bhikkhunī must invite admonition in both Saṅghas on three grounds: what is seen, what is heard, or what is suspected. “A bhikkhunī who has committed a grave offense must practice the mānatta discipline for a fortnight in both Saṅghas. “A probationer who has trained in the six points for two years must seek higher ordination in both Saṅghas. “A bhikkhunī must not abuse or revile a bhikkhu in any way. “From this day forth, the path of speech from bhikkhunīs to bhikkhus is closed, but the path of speech from bhikkhus to bhikkhunīs is open. This principle too is to be honored, respected, esteemed, and worshiped, and is not to be transgressed for life. “If, Ānanda, Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī accepts these eight important principles, let that be her higher ordination.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Cūḷavagga, Bhikkhunikkhandhaka, Aṭṭhagarudhammā, Vin-iv 2426-2436, para 403) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_04&page_number=444&name=c%C5%AB%E1%B8%B7avaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NDAzLuKAmOKAmCBTYWNlLCDEgW5hbmRhLCBtYWjEgXBhasSBcGF0xKsgZ290YW3EqyBh4bmt4bmtaGE%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya Piṭaka (the simile of the embankment))
> **“Seyyathāpi, ānanda, puriso mahato taḷākassa paṭikacceva āḷiṃ bandheyya yāvadeva udakassa anatikkamanāya; evameva kho, ānanda, mayā paṭikacceva bhikkhunīnaṃ aṭṭha garudhammā paññattā yāvajīvaṃ anatikkamanīyā” ti.**  
> “Just as, Ānanda, a man would build an embankment for a great reservoir beforehand, solely to prevent the water from overflowing; even so, Ānanda, have I laid down beforehand these eight important principles for bhikkhunīs, not to be transgressed for life.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Cūḷavagga, Bhikkhunikkhandhaka, Vin-iv 2453) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_04&page_number=447&name=c%C5%AB%E1%B8%B7avaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFNleXlhdGjEgXBpLCDEgW5hbmRhLCBwdXJpc28gbWFoYXRvIHRh4bi3xIFrYXNzYQ%3D%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Manorathapūraṇī (Aṅguttara-aṭṭhakathā))
> **Mahato taḷākassa paṭikacceva āḷinti iminā pana etamatthaṃ dasseti – yathā mahato taḷākassa pāḷiyā abaddhāyapi kiñci udakaṃ tiṭṭhateva, paṭhamameva baddhāya pana yaṃ abaddhapaccayā na tiṭṭheyya, tampi tiṭṭheyya, evameva ye ime anuppanne vatthusmiṃ paṭikacceva anatikkamanatthāya garudhammā paññattā, tesu apaññattesu mātugāmassa pabbajitattā pañca vassasatāni saddhammo tiṭṭheyya.**  
> Regarding ‘a dike around a great lake, beforehand’: by this, he shows this meaning – just as, even if the dike of a great lake is not built, some water indeed remains, but if it is built first, that too would remain which would not remain due to the condition of not being built; just so, these principles of respect were prescribed beforehand, before the occurrence of what is improper, for the purpose of not transgressing. If they were not prescribed, because of women’s going forth, the true Dhamma would remain for five hundred years.  
>  
> — [Manorathapūraṇī](/glossary/manorathapurani/) (Aṭṭhakanipāta-aṭṭhakathā, Mp-viii 181, on the Gotamī Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_an_04&page_number=238&name=a%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhak%C4%81dinip%C4%81taa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TWFoYXRvIHRh4bi3xIFrYXNzYSBwYeG5rWlrYWNjZXZhIMSB4bi3aW50aSBpbWluxIEgcGFuYQ%3D%3D)
