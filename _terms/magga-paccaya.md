### Grammatical Analysis
**Maggapaccaya**: [m.] path condition. A compound of *[magga](/glossary/magga/)* (path) + *paccaya* (condition). The commentary defines it by the sense of outlet (*niyyānaṭṭhena*): the path factors that help in the sense of leading out are the path condition (Vism-ii 940:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Magga-paccaya* is the eighteenth of the 24 conditions in the Paṭṭhāna: "the path factors are related to the states associated with the path and to the materiality produced by them, by path condition" (Paṭṭh-i 52:1).

The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha lists twelve path factors: eight with the right ones (right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration) and four wrong ones: wrong view, wrong intention, wrong effort and wrong concentration (Abhidh-s 628:1). The Visuddhimagga says the twelve path factors, classified as wholesome and so on, help in the sense of outlet (*niyyānaṭṭhena*) and are the path condition (Vism-ii 940:1-3). It adds from the Question Section that at the moment of rebirth-linking, resultant indeterminate path factors are a condition for the associated aggregates and kamma-produced matter by path condition (Vism-ii 940:4).

The Ṭīkā says: right view and the others help, in the sense of outlet, from a good destination by way of merit, or from a bad destination by way of evil (*sugatito puññato, duggahito pāpato vā niyyānaṭṭhena upakārakā sammādiṭṭhādayo maggapaccayo*, Abhidh-s-t 1670:2).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Maggapaccayoti – maggaṅgāni maggasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ maggapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 18. Path condition: The path factors are related to the states associated with the path and to the materiality produced by them, by path condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Yato tato vā niyyānaṭṭhena upakārakāni kusalādibhedāni dvādasa maggaṅgāni maggapaccayo. Yathāha – “maggaṅgāni maggasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ maggapaccayena paccayo” ti (paṭṭhā.1.1.18).**  
> 611. The twelve path factors (maggaṅgāni) classified as wholesome and so on, which assist in whatever way (yato tato vā) in the sense of outlet (niyyānaṭṭhena), are the path condition (maggapaccayo). As it is said: “Path factors are a condition for states associated with the path and for material phenomena originated thereby by way of path condition” (Paṭṭh. 1.1.18).  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Ch. XVII, §611) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=171&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NjExLiBZYXRvIHRhdG8gdsSBIG5peXnEgW5h4bmt4bmtaGVuYSB1cGFrxIFyYWvEgW5p)

### Quote (Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha: the twelve path factors)
> **Dvādasa maggaṅgāni – sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi micchādiṭṭhi micchāsaṅkappo micchāvāyāmo micchāsamādhi.**  
> 17. Twelve path factors: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration, wrong view, wrong intention, wrong effort, and wrong concentration.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (Missakasaṅgaha) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=48&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MTcuIER2xIFkYXNhIG1hZ2dh4bmFZ8SBbmkg4oCTIHNhbW3EgWRp4bmt4bmtaGkgc2FtbcSBc2HhuYVrYXBwbw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Sugatito puññato, duggahito pāpato vā niyyānaṭṭhena upakārakā sammādiṭṭhādayo maggapaccayo.**  
> Right view and so on, which are helpful in the sense of outlet from a good destination or merit, and from a bad destination or evil, are the path condition (maggapaccayo).  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=244&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U3VnYXRpdG8gcHXDscOxYXRvLCBkdWdnYWhpdG8gcMSBcGF0byB2xIEgbml5ecSBbmHhua3hua1oZW5h)
