### Grammatical Analysis
**Saddhammappakāsinī**: [f.] The Clarifier of the True Dhamma. Formed by *saddhamma* (true doctrine) + *pakāsinī* (clarifying, making known).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Saddhammappakāsinī* is the commentary on the *[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)*; its colophon calls it "the commentary on the Paṭisambhidāmagga treatise" (Paṭis-a 2525:1-2526:1). Its concluding verses say it was completed by "the Elder of great name" (*therena mahābhidhānena*, traditionally Mahānāma) living in the pariveṇa that the Uttaramantī had built in the Mahāvihāra, "in the third year after the passing of King Moggallāna" (Paṭis-a 2509:1-2512:1), and that it comes to fifty-eight bhāṇavāras, about fourteen thousand five hundred verses (Paṭis-a 2518:1, 2520:1).

The *[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)* is a canonical text on insight knowledge, in a dense Abhidhamma-style format. Its mātikā ends with the words "these are the seventy-three knowledges" (*imāni tesattati ñāṇāni*, Paṭis 79:1), and the commentary explains these knowledges, among them the knowledge of reviewing (e.g. Paṭis-a 171:3-11) and the treatment of mindfulness of breathing (*[ānāpānasati](/glossary/anapanasati/)*).

### Quote
> **Saddhammappakāsinī nāma Paṭisambhidāmaggappakaraṇassa aṭṭhakathā niṭṭhitā.**  
> The Saddhammappakāsinī by name The commentary on the Paṭisambhidāmagga treatise is finished.
