### Grammatical Analysis
**Bodhimaṇḍala**: [nt.] The seat of enlightenment; the circle of Awakening. Formed by *bodhi* (awakening, supreme knowledge) + *maṇḍala* (circle, specific ground, sacred area).

### Orthodox Definition
*Bodhimaṇḍala* refers to the exact geographical location where a [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/) sits to attain supreme Buddhahood. For Gotama Buddha, this is the area under the Assattha tree (the [Bodhi](/glossary/bodhi/) tree) at modern-day Bodh Gaya. 

The usual canonical and commentarial form is *bodhimaṇḍa*, e.g. Buddhavaṃsa "whatever fully enlightened ones awakened at the seat of enlightenment (*bodhimaṇḍamhi bujjhare*)" (Bv 809:1).

The Jātaka commentary (Nidānakathā) tells that when the Bodhisatta tried to take his stand on the south, west and north of the Bodhi tree, the world-system on that side subsided or rose. On the east he found "the seat of all the Buddhas, which neither shakes nor trembles" (*sabbabuddhānaṃ pallaṅkaṭṭhānaṃ*), and, knowing it as "the unshakeable place never abandoned by any of the Buddhas, where the cage of defilements is destroyed" (*sabbabuddhānaṃ avijahitaṃ acalaṭṭhānaṃ*), he spread the grass and a fourteen-cubit seat appeared (Ja-a-i 727:5-11). He then sat down on the "unconquered seat" (*aparājitapallaṅka*, Ja-a-i 730:1) and defeated Māra's army (Ja-a-i 738).

### Quote
> **“Yathā ye keci sambuddhā, bodhimaṇḍamhi bujjhare; Tatheva tvaṃ mahāvīra, bujjhassu jinabodhiyaṃ.**  
> 182. “Just as whatever fully enlightened ones awakened at the seat of enlightenment, just so, O heroic soul, may you realize the Bodhi of the Victors.”  
>

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)* – The Buddhas who awakened "at the seat of enlightenment" (Bv 809:1); the Buddhavaṃsa also names the Bodhi tree of each previous Buddha.
* **Commentary**: *Jātakaṭṭhakathā* (Nidānakathā) (Ja-a-i 727-738) – The [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/) finds the unshakeable place of all Buddhas, sits on the *aparājitapallaṅka* and defeats Māra's army.
