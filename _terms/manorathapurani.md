### Grammatical Analysis
**Manorathapūraṇī**: [f.] The Fulfiller of Desires. Formed by *manoratha* (desire, wish of the mind) + *pūraṇī* (fulfilling, completing).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Manorathapūraṇī* is the commentary on the *Aṅguttara Nikāya* (Collection of Numerical Discourses), compiled by Venerable Buddhaghosa; its colophon names it "Manorathapūraṇī nāma aṅguttaranikāyaṭṭhakathā" and its author as the elder known as Buddhaghosa (Mp-xi 790:1, 795:1). The verses above give the reason for the name: the author's wish to comment on all the Āgamas (*sabbāgamasaṃvaṇṇanamanoratha*) was fulfilled by it.

Its most famous and historically vital section is its commentary on the *Etadaggavagga* (the chapter on the foremost disciples). While the canonical [sutta](/glossary/sutta/) merely lists a single sentence for each disciple (e.g., "Sāriputta is foremost in wisdom"), the *Manorathapūraṇī* provides massive, epic biographical accounts of these individuals. For each disciple the commentary asks: under which Buddha he made his former aspiration (*pubbapatthanā abhinīhāra*), when he went forth, when he first realised the Dhamma, and when he was set in his foremost position (Mp-i 558:1). It "fulfills the desire" of practitioners wanting to know the history of the [Sāvaka-saṅgha](/glossary/savaka-sangha/).

### Quote
> **Sabbāgamasaṃvaṇṇanamanoratho pūrito ca me yasmā; Etāya manorathapūraṇīti nāmaṃ tato assā.**  
> And because my aspiration to comment upon all the Āgamas was fulfilled by this; therefore its name would be Manorathapūraṇī.
