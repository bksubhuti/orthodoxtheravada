### Grammatical Analysis
**Saddhammapajjotikā**: [f.] The Illuminator of the True Dhamma. Formed by *saddhamma* (true doctrine) + *pajjotikā* (illuminating, lighting up).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Saddhammapajjotikā* is the commentary on the *[Niddesa](/glossary/niddesa/)* (both the Mahāniddesa and Cūḷaniddesa): its colophons read "In the Saddhammapajjotikā, the commentary on the Mahāniddesa" (Nidd-a-i 1069:1) and "In the Saddhammappajjotikā, the commentary on the Cūḷaniddesa" (Nidd-a-ii 719:1). Its concluding account (Nidd-a-ii 726:1-737:1) places it in Anurādhapura, where the Mahāvihāra stood in the southern part; to the west of the Mahāthūpa the scribe Kittisena had a pariveṇa made and gave it to the great Elder Upasena, who lived there and composed the work. It was finished in the twenty-sixth year of King Sirinivāsa, the Sirisaṅghabodhi (Nidd-a-ii 738:1-739:1).

The Niddesa itself is, according to the commentary, the Mahāniddesa that "the Elder Sāriputta, of great wisdom, analyzed the Dhamma-wheel and spoke" (Nidd-a-i 13:1-14:1, 57:1); the commentator says he follows "the method of the ancient commentary" (*pubbaṭṭhakathānayaṃ*, Nidd-a-ii 724:1).

### Quote
> **Upaseno mahāthero, mahāpariveṇavāsiyo; Tassādāsi pariveṇaṃ, lekho kusalakammiko. Vasantenettha therena, thirasīlena tādinā; Upasenavhayena sā, katā saddhammajotikā.**  
> The great Elder Upasena, living in the great pariveṇa, Was given the pariveṇa by the scribe and skilled worker. Living there, the Elder, firm in virtue and unaffected, Named Upasena, composed that Saddhammajotikā.
