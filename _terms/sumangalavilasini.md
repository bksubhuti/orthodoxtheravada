### Grammatical Analysis
**Sumaṅgalavilāsinī**: [f.] The Illuminator of Supreme Auspiciousness. Formed by *su* (good, supreme) + *maṅgala* (auspicious, blessing) + *vilāsinī* (shining, illuminating).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī* is the commentary (*aṭṭhakathā*) on the *Dīgha Nikāya* (Collection of Long Discourses). Its closing verses say that it was begun at the request of the Elder Dāṭhānāga and that it was made by an elder named Buddhaghosa, taking the essence of the original commentary (*mūlaṭṭhakathā*) and of the Great Commentary (Sv-iii 1111-1125).

The Dīgha Nikāya is "the thirty-four discourses, such as the Brahmajāla, comprised in three divisions" (Sv-i 173), and the commentary has one part for each division: *Sīlakkhandhavagga-aṭṭhakathā* (Sv-i), *Mahāvagga-aṭṭhakathā* (Sv-ii) and *Pāthikavagga-aṭṭhakathā* (Sv-iii). In the first discourse, the *Brahmajāla Sutta*, it explains the sixty-two views as "the range of the Tathāgata alone" to divide and explain (Sv-i 528), and states when they have all been told (Sv-i 594).

### Quote
> **Āyācito sumaṅgala , pariveṇa nivāsinā thiraguṇena; Dāṭhānāgasaṅghattherena , theravaṃsanvayena. Dīghāgamavarassa dasabala, guṇagaṇaparidīpanassa aṭṭhakathaṃ; Yaṃ ārabhiṃ sumaṅgala , vilāsiniṃ nāma nāmena.**  
> Requested by the Venerable Sumaṅgala, who dwells in the monastery, possessing steady virtues; By the Elder Dāṭhānāga, of the lineage of the Elder’s tradition. The commentary on the excellent Dīgha Nikāya, which illuminates the group of qualities of the One with Ten Powers; Which I began, called by the name ‘Sumaṅgalavilāsinī’.

### Textual References
* **Subject Matter**: Comments on the thirty-four discourses of the Dīgha Nikāya in three parts: Sīlakkhandhavagga-aṭṭhakathā (Sv-i 3), Mahāvagga-aṭṭhakathā (Sv-ii 3) and Pāthikavagga-aṭṭhakathā (Sv-iii 3).
* **Key Features**: The commentary on the *Brahmajāla Sutta* (Sv-i 199-636), on the *Mahāparinibbāna Sutta* (Sv-ii 420-816) and the long commentary on the *Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna Sutta* (The Great Discourse on the Foundations of Mindfulness; Sv-ii 1373-1665).
