### Grammatical Analysis
**Sāmaṇera**: [m.] Novice monk. Derived from *samaṇa* (ascetic, striver) + suffix *era* (indicating a son or pupil). Literally translates to "son of an ascetic."

### Orthodox Definition
A *Sāmaṇera* is a male monastic who has taken the lower ordination (*[pabbajjā](/glossary/pabbajja/)*) but has not yet received the higher ordination (*[upasampadā](/glossary/upasampada/)*). 

Unlike fully ordained Bhikkhus who must observe the whole [Pātimokkha](/glossary/patimokkha/), a *sāmaṇera* is bound by the **Ten Precepts** (*Dasa Sikkhāpada*), which include abstaining from killing, stealing, unchastity, lying, intoxicants, eating after noon, entertainment, adornments, high beds, and handling money.

A sāmaṇera is taken in and looked after by a bhikkhu (*sāmaṇero upaṭṭhāpetabbo*, Vin-iii 475:1); Sāriputta let the boy Rāhula go forth by the three refuges (Vin-iii 598:18-23). A boy under fifteen who is able to scare crows (*kākuḍḍepaka*) may be let go forth (Vin-iii 565:13), but a person under twenty may not receive the full ordination (Vin-ii 1413:4).

### Quote
> **Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ … pe … anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sāmaṇerānaṃ dasa sikkhāpadāni, tesu ca sāmaṇerehi sikkhituṃ – pāṇātipātā veramaṇī, adinnādānā veramaṇī, abrahmacariyā veramaṇī, musāvādā veramaṇī, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇī, vikālabhojanā veramaṇī, naccagītavāditavisūkadassanā veramaṇī, mālāgandhavilepanadhāraṇamaṇḍanavibhūsanaṭṭhānā veramaṇī, uccāsayanamahāsayanā veramaṇī, jātarūparajatapaṭiggahaṇā veramaṇī. Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sāmaṇerānaṃ imāni dasa sikkhāpadāni, imesu ca sāmaṇerehi sikkhitunti.**  
> They told this matter to the Blessed One … and he said: “I allow, bhikkhus, ten training rules for novices, and novices are to train in these: refraining from killing living creatures, refraining from taking what has not been given, refraining from uncelibacy, refraining from false speech, refraining from taking distilled and fermented drinks that cause heedlessness, refraining from eating at the wrong time, refraining from dancing, singing, music, and watching shows, refraining from wearing garlands, using perfumes, and beautifying the body with cosmetics, refraining from high and luxurious beds, refraining from accepting gold and silver.” “I allow, bhikkhus, these ten training rules for novices, and novices are to train in these.”  
>

### Textual References
* **[Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: *[Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/)* – The story of Rāhula (Vin-iii 597, 41. Rāhulavatthu), where the going forth of a novice by the three refuges is allowed (Vin-iii 598:18), and the Sikkhāpadakathā (Vin-iii 602, 42), where the ten training rules for novices are allowed (Vin-iii 603:3).
* **Canonical**: *[Khuddakapāṭha](/glossary/khuddakapatha/)* – The Dasasikkhāpadaṃ (Khp 15:1, the second section), the ten training rules.
* **Commentary**: *Paramatthajotikā* (Khp-aṭṭhakathā, Pj-i 106:2) – says the ten training rules were spoken at Sāvatthī to establish the rules for novices after Rāhula had been let go forth; *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* (Sp-iii 85:1-2) cites the Mahāvagga passage on the ten rules.
