### Grammatical Analysis
**Paṭisambhidāmagga**: [m.] The Path of Discrimination (Analytical Knowledges). Formed by *paṭisambhidā* (analytical knowledge) + *magga* (path). The Aṭṭhakathā: "the path to these four analytical knowledges is the Paṭisambhidāmagga; the Paṭisambhidāmagga itself is the treatise (*pakaraṇa*)" (Paṭis-a 50). The four are *atthapaṭisambhidā, dhammapaṭisambhidā, niruttipaṭisambhidā, paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā* (Vibh 718).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Paṭisambhidāmagga* is a book of the Khuddaka Nikāya (TPR file "Paṭisambhidāmaggapāḷi", book id mula_ku_18). Its commentary says it was spoken by the Elder Sāriputta, the Commander of the Dhamma, and that the Elder Ānanda recited it as he had heard it at the first great council (Paṭis-a 51). The Aṭṭhakathā names it "the path" to the four analytical knowledges because its teaching, expounded by detailed distinctions, brings about the knowledge of the paṭisambhidās for noble listeners and is a condition for it in the future for ordinary people (Paṭis-a 30).

The Dīghanikāya-aṭṭhakathā lists it among the books of the Khuddakagantha, which the Dīghabhāṇakas say the reciters included in the Abhidhammapiṭaka (Sv-i 108). Its contents are the seventy-three kinds of knowledge (Paṭis 79:1) in the Ñāṇakathā, and, among others, the Ānāpānassatikathā. The Visuddhimagga cites it often as "paṭi. ma." (123 sentences in Vism-i and Vism-ii, counted with TPR), for example for the comprehension knowledge quoted on the Sammasanañāṇa page.

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: why it is called Paṭisambhidāmagga)
> **Tattha paṭisambhidānaṃ maggoti tannāmavisesito cāti vuttattā paṭisambhidāmaggassa paṭisambhidāmaggatā tāva vattabbā.**  
> “Here, since it is stated, 'It is called the Path of Paṭisambhidā, thus specifically named,' the fact that the Paṭisambhidāmagga is the Paṭisambhidāmagga must first be explained.”  
> 
> **Catasso hi paṭisambhidā – atthapaṭisambhidā, dhammapaṭisambhidā, niruttipaṭisambhidā, paṭibhānapaṭisambhidāti.**  
> “For there are four paṭisambhidās: atthapaṭisambhidā, dhammapaṭisambhidā, niruttipaṭisambhidā, and paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā.”  
> 
> **Tāsaṃ paṭisambhidānaṃ maggo adhigamūpāyoti paṭisambhidāmaggo, paṭisambhidāpaṭilābhahetūti vuttaṃ hoti.**  
> “The path to these paṭisambhidās, the means of attainment, is the Paṭisambhidāmagga; it is said to be the cause for the acquisition of the paṭisambhidās.”  
> 
> **Kathamayaṃ tāsaṃ maggo hotīti ce?**  
> “If it is asked, 'How is this the path to them?'”  
> 
> **Pabhedato desitāya desanāya paṭisambhidāñāṇāvahattā.**  
> “It is because the teaching, expounded in detail, brings forth the knowledge of the paṭisambhidās.”  
> 
> **Nānābhedabhinnānañhi dhammānaṃ nānābhedabhinnā desanā sotūnaṃ ariyapuggalānaṃ paṭisambhidāñāṇappabhedañca sañjaneti, puthujjanānaṃ āyatiṃ paṭisambhidāñāṇappabhedāya ca paccayo hoti.**  
> “For a teaching differentiated into various distinctions generates the distinctions of paṭisambhidā-knowledge for noble individuals who listen, and for ordinary people, it is a condition for the distinctions of paṭisambhidā-knowledge in the future.”  
> 
> **Vuttañca – ‘‘pabhedato hi desanā ghanavinibbhogapaṭisambhidāñāṇāvahā hotī’’ti (dha. sa. aṭṭha. 1.kāmāvacarakusalapadabhājanīya).**  
> “And it has been said: 'Indeed, a teaching given in detail brings forth paṭisambhidā-knowledge that penetrates the compact' (Dhammasaṅgaṇī-aṭṭhakathā 1, Kāmāvacarakusalapadabhājanīya).”  
> 
> **Ayañca nānābhedabhinnā desanā, tenassā paṭisambhidānaṃ maggattasiddhi.**  
> “And this teaching is differentiated into various distinctions; hence, its success as the path to the paṭisambhidās.”  
> 
> — *Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā* ([Saddhammappakāsinī](/glossary/saddhammappakasini/)) (pa)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: the name explained)
> **Imāsaṃ catassannaṃ paṭisambhidānaṃ maggoti paṭisambhidāmaggo, paṭisambhidāmaggo eva pakaraṇaṃ paṭisambhidāmaggappakaraṇaṃ, pakārena karīyante vuccante ettha nānābhedabhinnā gambhīrā atthā iti pakaraṇaṃ.**  
> The path to these four analytical knowledges is the 'Path of Analytical Knowledges'; the path of analytical knowledges itself is the 'Analytical Knowledges Path Treatise'; it is a treatise because deep meanings, divided by various divisions, are spoken of here by methods.  
>  
> — [Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā](/glossary/saddhammappakasini/) (Saddhammappakāsinī, Paṭis-a 50) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_18_01&page_number=7&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=SW3EgXNh4bmDIGNhdGFzc2FubmHhuYMgcGHhua1pc2FtYmhpZMSBbmHhuYMgbWFnZ290aSBwYeG5rWlzYW1iaGlkxIFtYWdnbywgcGHhua1pc2FtYmhpZMSBbWFnZ28%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: who spoke it)
> **Tadetaṃ paṭisambhidāmaggappakaraṇaṃ atthasampannaṃ byañjanasampannaṃ gambhīraṃ gambhīratthaṃ lokuttarappakāsanaṃ suññatāpaṭisaññuttaṃ paṭipattiphalavisesasādhanaṃ paṭipattipaṭipakkhapaṭisedhanaṃ yogāvacarānaṃ ñāṇavararatanākarabhūtaṃ dhammakathikānaṃ dhammakathāvilāsavisesahetubhūtaṃ saṃsārabhīrukānaṃ dukkhanissaraṇaṃ tadupāyadassanena assāsajananatthaṃ tappaṭipakkhanāsanatthañca gambhīratthānañca anekesaṃ suttantapadānaṃ atthavivaraṇena sujanahadayaparitosajananatthaṃ tathāgatena arahatā sammāsambuddhena sabbattha appaṭihatasabbaññutaññāṇamahāpadīpāvabhāsena sakalajanavitthatamahākaruṇāsinehasiniddhahadayena veneyyajanahadayagatakilesandhakāravidhamanatthamujjalitassa saddhammamahāpadīpassa tadadhippāyavikāsanasinehaparisekena pañcavassasahassamaviratamujjalanamicchatā lokānukampakena satthukappena dhammarājassa dhammasenāpatinā āyasmatā sāriputtattherena bhāsitaṃ sutvā āyasmatā ānandena paṭhamamahāsaṅgītikāle yathāsutameva saṅgītiṃ āropitaṃ.**  
> This Paṭisambhidāmagga treatise is accomplished in meaning, accomplished in phrasing, deep, possessing deep meaning, illuminating the supramundane, associated with emptiness, an achiever of the fruit of practice, a preventer of the obstacles to practice, a treasury of the jewel of knowledge for yoga-practitioners, a cause for the special charm of the Dhamma-talk of Dhamma-speakers, the escape from suffering for those afraid of the cycle, a producer of relief by showing the means to that, a destroyer of that which is opposed to it, and a producer of delight in the hearts of good people by explaining the meaning of many discourses that have deep meanings. It was spoken by the Commander of the Dhamma, the venerable Elder Sāriputta, who was comparable to the Teacher, a King of Dhamma who desired the long-standing of the Great Lamp of the True Dhamma—which was lit by the Realized One, the Perfected One, the Fully Self-Awakened One, who possessed the Great Lamp of Unobstructed Omniscient Knowledge, whose heart was tender with the affection of Great Compassion spread to all creatures, and who lit to dispel the darkness of defilements in the hearts of creatures to be taught—and by him by the pouring of the oil of the meanings of. Having heard this, it was raised into the recitation by the venerable Ānanda during the first Great Recitation, just as he had heard it.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: the Khuddaka books at the first council)
> **Tato paraṃ jātakaṃ, niddeso, paṭisambhidāmaggo, apadānaṃ, suttanipāto, khuddakapāṭho, dhammapadaṃ, udānaṃ, itivuttakaṃ, vimānavatthu, petavatthu, theragāthā, therīgāthāti imaṃ tantiṃ saṅgāyitvā “khuddakagantho nāmāyan” ti ca vatvā “abhidhammapiṭakasmiṃyeva saṅgahaṃ āropayiṃsū” ti dīghabhāṇakā vadanti.**  
> Immediately after that, having recited this text—the Jātaka, Niddesa, Paṭisambhidāmagga, Apadāna, Suttanipāta, Khuddakapāṭha, Dhammapada, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Vimānavatthu, Petavatthu, Theragāthā, and Therīgāthā—and having said, “This is called the Khuddakagantha,” the Dīghabhāṇaka reciters say that they included it within the Abhidhammapiṭaka itself.  
>  
> — Dīghanikāya-aṭṭhakathā (Sumaṅgalavilāsinī, on the first council) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_di_01&page_number=15&name=s%C4%ABlakkhandhavagga%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGF0byBwYXJh4bmDIGrEgXRha2HhuYMsIG5pZGRlc28sIHBh4bmtaXNhbWJoaWTEgW1hZ2dvLCBhcGFkxIFuYeG5gyw%3D)
