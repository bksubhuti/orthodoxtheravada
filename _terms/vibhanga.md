### Grammatical Analysis
**Vibhaṅga**: [m.] analysis; the second book of the Abhidhamma Piṭaka. The word is the same as in the chapter names, such as *Khandhavibhaṅga* ("Analysis of the Aggregates"). The Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā is called *Sammohavinodanī*.

### Orthodox Definition
The *Vibhaṅga* (Vibhaṅgapāḷi, in the Abhidhamma Piṭaka) comes straight after the *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)*. The Sammohavinodanī says that the Buddha, having expounded the Dhammasaṅgaṇī in four ways, taught the Vibhaṅga "by way of the eighteen vibhaṅgas beginning with the aggregates" (*aṭṭhārasannaṃ khandhādi-vibhaṅgānaṃ vasena*).

The eighteen chapters are, in the Pāḷi: *Khandha-, Āyatana-, Dhātu-, Sacca-, Indriya-, Paṭiccasamuppāda-, Satipaṭṭhāna-, Sammappadhāna-, Iddhipāda-, Bojjhaṅga-, Maggaṅga-, Jhāna-, Appamaññā-, Sikkhāpada-, Paṭisambhidā-, Ñāṇa-, Khuddakavatthu-* and *Dhammahadayavibhaṅga*.

The first chapter, for example, has three expositions: **Suttantabhājanīya** (the exposition by the method of the suttas, which begins "the five aggregates: the form aggregate, ..."), **Abhidhammabhājanīya** (the Abhidhamma exposition) and **Pañhāpucchaka** (the set of questions).

### Textual References
* **Abhidhamma**: *[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)* (Vibh 3–3426) – the eighteen chapters, from *Khandhavibhaṅga* (Vibh 4) to *Dhammahadayavibhaṅga*; in the first chapter the three expositions are at Vibh 5 (*Suttantabhājanīya*), 65 (*Abhidhammabhājanīya*) and 514 (*Pañhāpucchaka*). The Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga is the sixth chapter (Vibh 903).
* **Commentary**: *[Sammohavinodanī](/glossary/sammohavinodani/)* (Vibh-a), the Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā.
