### Grammatical Analysis
**Diṭṭhi**: [f.] view; belief; theory; standpoint. From the root *dis* (to see). The Aṭṭhasālinī explains *micchādiṭṭhi* as "a view not as it really is" (*ayāthāvadiṭṭhi*), or a false view (*vitathā diṭṭhi*) because of grasping after having erred, or a view scorned by the wise because it brings harm; and also because people see wrongly by means of it, or it itself sees wrongly, or it is merely wrong seeing (*micchādassanamattameva*) (As 1772:1-4). In the Dīgha it appears in the plural as *diṭṭhiṭṭhānā*, "standpoints of view" (D-i 76:2).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Dhammasaṅgaṇī the wrong view that arises with the greed-rooted consciousness is defined with a series of synonyms: "whatever at that time is *diṭṭhi*, *diṭṭhigata*, *diṭṭhigahana*, *diṭṭhikantāra*, *diṭṭhivisūkāyika*, *diṭṭhivipphandita*, *diṭṭhisaṃyojana*, grasping, establishment, adherence (*abhiniveso*), misapprehension (*parāmāso*), the wrong path, the wrong way, wrongness, the sectarian fold, the grasping of perversion: this is wrong view at that time" (Dhs 883:2).

The Aṭṭhasālinī gives the characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause: wrong view is characterized by unwise adherence (*ayoniso abhinivesa-lakkhaṇā*), has misapprehension as its function (*parāmāsa-rasā*), is manifested as wrong adherence (*micchābhinivesa-paccupaṭṭhānā*), and has as its proximate cause the desire not to see the noble ones and the like (*ariyānaṃ adassanakāmatādi-padaṭṭhānā*); "it should be considered the ultimate fault" (*paramaṃ vajjanti daṭṭhabbā*, As 1772:5-6).

The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha lists *diṭṭhi* among the fourteen unwholesome mental factors (Abhidh-s 88:1) and says it is found in the four types of consciousness associated with view (*diṭṭhigatasampayutta*, Abhidh-s 117:1), which are four of the eight greed-rooted consciousnesses (Abhidh-s 13:2). It is the second of the four clingings (*diṭṭhupādāna*, Dhs 2009:2) and the fourth of the seven latent tendencies (*diṭṭhānusaya*, Paṭis 637:2); *sakkāyadiṭṭhi* is the first of the three fetters in the Dhammasaṅgaṇī (Dhs 1774:2).

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Katamā tasmiṃ samaye micchādiṭṭhi hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ diṭṭhigahanaṃ diṭṭhikantāro diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ gāho patiṭṭhāho abhiniveso parāmāso kummaggo micchāpatho micchattaṃ titthāyatanaṃ vipariyāsaggāho– ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye micchādiṭṭhi hoti.**  
> 381. Which is wrong view at that time? Whatever at that time is the view, the view-point, the thicket of views, the wilderness of views, the perplexity of views, the agitation of views, the fetter of views, the grasping, the establishment, the misinterpretation, the misinterpretation, the wrong path, the wrong way, the wrongness, the sectarian fold, the grasping of perversion—this is wrong view at that time.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs, Micchādiṭṭhi in the first akusala citta, §381) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=93&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MzgxLiBLYXRhbcSBIHRhc21p4bmDIHNhbWF5ZSBtaWNjaMSBZGnhua3hua1oaSBob3RpPw%3D%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Aṭṭhasālinī)
> **Micchādiṭṭhīti ayāthāvadiṭṭhi, virajjhitvā gahaṇato vā vitathā diṭṭhi micchādiṭṭhi. Anatthāvahattā paṇḍitehi jigucchitā diṭṭhītipi micchādiṭṭhi. Micchāsaṅkappādīsu pi eseva nayo. Apica micchā passanti tāya, sayaṃ vā micchā passati, micchādassanamattameva vā esāti micchādiṭṭhi. Sā ayoniso abhinivesalakkhaṇā, parāmāsarasā, micchābhinivesapaccupaṭṭhānā, ariyānaṃ adassanakāmatādipadaṭṭhānā; paramaṃ vajjanti daṭṭhabbā.**  
> ‘Wrong view’ (micchādiṭṭhi) is a view not as it really is (ayāthāvadiṭṭhi); or (vā) it is a false view (vitathā diṭṭhi) due to grasping (gahaṇato) having erred (virajjhitvā)—this is wrong view (micchādiṭṭhi). Also (itīpi), a view (diṭṭhi) that is scorned (jigucchitā) by the astute (paṇḍitehi) because it brings harm (anatthāvahattā) is ‘wrong view’ (micchādiṭṭhi). This same method (eseva nayo) applies also (pi) to ‘wrong intention’ (micchāsaṅkappa) and so on (ādīsu). Furthermore (apica), it is ‘wrong view’ (micchādiṭṭhi) because (1) people see wrongly (micchā passanti) by means of it (tāya), or (vā) (2) it (wrong view) itself (sayaṃ) sees wrongly (micchā passati), or (vā) (3) this (view) is merely wrong perception (micchādassanamattameva). That (wrong view) (sā) is characterized by unwise adherence (ayoniso abhinivesa), has the essence of clinging (parāmāsarasā), manifests as wrong adherence (micchābhinivesapaccupaṭṭhānā), and is rooted in the desire not to see the Noble Ones etc. (ariyānaṃ adassanakāmatādipadaṭṭhānā). It should be considered (daṭṭhabbā) the ultimate fault (paramaṃ vajja).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1772, on the micchādiṭṭhi of the first unwholesome consciousness, Dhs §381) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=290&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TWljY2jEgWRp4bmt4bmtaMSrdGkgYXnEgXRoxIF2YWRp4bmt4bmtaGksIHZpcmFqamhpdHbEgSBnYWhh4bmHYXRvIHbEgSB2aXRhdGjEgQ%3D%3D)
