### Grammatical Analysis
**Aniyata**: [adj.] Undetermined; indefinite; uncertain. Formed by negative prefix *a* (not) + *niyata* (fixed, certain, determined).

### Orthodox Definition
*Aniyata* is the third section of the monks' [Pātimokkha](/glossary/patimokkha/) (heading "3. Aniyatakaṇḍaṃ", Vin-i 1849:1), consisting of **two rules** ("Ime kho panāyasmanto dve aniyatā dhammā", Vin-i 1851:1). The Vibhaṅga explains the word: "Aniyatoti na niyato, pārājikaṃ vā saṅghādiseso vā pācittiyaṃ vā" – undetermined, because it may be a pārājika, a saṅghādisesa or a pācittiya (Vin-i 1874:1; for the second rule, a saṅghādisesa or a pācittiya, Vin-i 1903:1). The first rule deals with a monk sitting alone with a woman in a secluded seat suitable for intercourse; the second with a seat that is not so suitable but is suitable for addressing her with lewd words (Vin-i 1881:1-2).

The origin story of both rules is that the Elder Udāyī, at Sāvatthī, sat alone with a young woman of a family he visited; Visākhā Migāramātā, invited to that house, saw him, told him to leave, and when he would not listen, reported the matter to the bhikkhus (Vin-i 1853:1-1854:9; the second case, Vin-i 1880:4-10). The Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī glosses the "trustworthy" woman (*saddheyyavacasā upāsikā*) as one whose word is to be trusted, a noble female disciple (*ariyasāvikā*); yet even so, the monk is to be dealt with only if he admits the sitting, and for whichever of the three offences he admits (Kkh 1437:13-15), because what was seen may be so or otherwise (Kkh 1437:16-17).

### Quote
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu mātugāmena saddhiṃ eko ekāya raho paṭicchanne āsane alaṃkammaniye nisajjaṃ kappeyya , tamenaṃ saddheyyavacasā upāsikā disvā tiṇṇaṃ dhammānaṃ aññatarena vadeyya – pārājikena vā saṅghādisesena vā pācittiyena vā , nisajjaṃ bhikkhu paṭijānamāno tiṇṇaṃ dhammānaṃ aññatarena kāretabbo – pārājikena vā saṅghādisesena vā pācittiyena vā yena vā sā saddheyyavacasā upāsikā vadeyya , tena so bhikkhu kāretabbo . Ayaṃ dhammo aniyato”ti.**  
> “Whatever monk sits down in private alone with a woman on a concealed seat suitable for the deed, and a trustworthy female lay follower sees him and accuses him of an offense entailing expulsion, an offense entailing suspension, or an offense entailing confession, then, if he admits to the sitting, he is to be dealt with according to one of these three or according to what that trustworthy female lay follower has said. This Dhamma is undetermined.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Bhikkhupātimokkha, Aniyata 1; heading "1. Yo pana bhikkhu tathārūpe āsane mātugāmena saddhiṃ eko ekāya raho nisajjaṃ kappeyya , tamenaṃ saddheyyavacasā upāsikā disvā dvinnaṃ dhammānaṃ aññatarena vadeyya – saṅghādisesena vā pācittiyena vā . Nisajjaṃ bhikkhu paṭijānamāno dvinnaṃ dhammānaṃ aññatarena kāretabbo – saṅghādisesena vā pācittiyena vā . Yena vā sā saddheyyavacasā upāsikā vadeyya tena so bhikkhu kāretabbo . Ayampi dhammo aniyato”ti.**  
> “A seat is not concealed, nor suitable for the deed; it may be suitable for speaking indecently to a woman. If a monk sits down on such a seat in private alone with a woman, and a trustworthy female lay follower sees him and accuses him of an offense entailing suspension or an offense entailing confession. If the monk admits to the sitting, he is to be dealt with according to one of these two: an offense entailing suspension or an offense entailing confession. He is to be dealt with according to what that trustworthy female lay follower has said. This rule too is undetermined.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Bhikkhupātimokkha, Aniyata 2; heading "2. ### Quote
> **Saddheyyavacasāti saddhātabbavacanā, ariyasāvikāti attho. Nisajjaṃ bhikkhu paṭijānamānoti kiñcāpi evarūpā upāsikā disvā vadati, atha kho bhikkhu nisajjaṃ paṭijānamānova tiṇṇaṃ dhammānaṃ aññatarena kāretabbo, na appaṭijānamānoti attho. Yena vā sāti nisajjādīsu ākāresu yena vā ākārena saddhiṃ methunādīni āropetvā sā upāsikā vadeyya, paṭijānamānova tena so bhikkhu kāretabbo, evarūpāyapi hi upāsikāya vacanamattena ākārena na kāretabboti attho. Kasmā? Yasmā diṭṭhaṃ nāma tathāpi hoti, aññathāpīti. Ayaṃ dhammo aniyatoti tiṇṇaṃ āpattīnaṃ yaṃ āpattiṃ vā vatthuṃ vā paṭijānāti, tassa vasena kāretabbatāya aniyato.**  
> 'Trustworthy' means one whose word is to be believed, an Ariyan lay follower—this is the meaning. 'If a monk admits to the sitting' means even if such an Ariyan lay follower has seen him and charges him, the monk should be dealt with according to one of the three offenses only if he admits to the sitting, not if he does not admit it. 'Or according to what' means with whichever mode—sitting or otherwise—that Ariyan lay follower charges him, having connected it to sexual intercourse or the like, the monk should be dealt with according to that mode if he admits it; he should not be dealt with by the mere word of such an Ariyan lay follower. Why? Because what is seen is indeed so in that way, and it is also otherwise. 'This rule is undetermined' means it is undetermined because it should be dealt with according to whichever offense or matter he admits.  
>  
> — Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī (Pātimokkha-aṭṭhakathā, on Aniyata 1) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_vi_01&page_number=150&name=dvem%C4%81tik%C4%81+ka%E1%B9%85kh%C4%81vitara%E1%B9%87%C4%AB&note=&selected_text=U2FkZGhleXlhdmFjYXPEgXRpIHNhZGRoxIF0YWJiYXZhY2FuxIEsIGFyaXlhc8SBdmlrxIF0aSBhdHRoby4%3D)

### Textual References
* **Vinaya**: *[Suttavibhaṅga](/glossary/suttavibhanga/)* (Aniyatakaṇḍa; Vin-i 1849:1-1911:1) – the origin story: the Elder Udāyī sitting privately with a young woman, and Visākhā Migāramātā reporting it to the bhikkhus (Vin-i 1853:1-1854:9; the second case at Vin-i 1880:4-10); the word-analysis at Vin-i 1874:1 and Vin-i 1903:1; the closing recital at Vin-i 1907:1.
* **Commentary**: *Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī* (Kkh 1437:12-18) – the sense of the trustworthy woman, the admission of the sitting, and why the rule is "undetermined"; the *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* has its own Aniyatakaṇḍa commentary (heading Sp-i 2512:1).
