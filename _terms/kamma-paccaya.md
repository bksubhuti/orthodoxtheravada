### Grammatical Analysis
**Kammapaccaya**: [m.] kamma condition; action condition. A compound of *kamma* (volitional action) + *paccaya* (condition). The Visuddhimagga and the Paṭṭhāna commentary define it: "a phenomenon that assists by way of the state of action known as mental application (*cittapayogasaṅkhātena kiriyabhāvena*) is a kamma condition (*kammapaccayo*)" (Vism-ii 935:1; Paṭṭh-a 1933:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Kamma-paccaya* is the thirteenth of the 24 conditions in the Paṭṭhāna (Paṭṭh-i 46:1). The Paṭṭhāna states it in two ways: wholesome and unwholesome kamma is a condition for the resultant aggregates and for kamma-produced materiality; and volition (*[cetanā](/glossary/cetana/)*) is a condition for its associated states and the materiality produced by them (Paṭṭh-i 46:1-2).

The commentaries say it is of two kinds: by way of wholesome and unwholesome volition at a different time (*nānakkhaṇikā*), and by way of all conascent volition (*sahajātā*) (Vism-ii 935:2; Paṭṭh-a 1933:2).
1. **Conascent kamma condition** (*sahajāta*): volition that arises together with its associated states, even that conascent with the five sense-consciousnesses, acts as kamma by way of "coordinating" (*saṃvidahana*) the conascent states (PmtDīp 1374:8-9).
2. **Asynchronous kamma condition** (*nānākkhaṇika*): wholesome and unwholesome volition established in a past time, separate from the moment at which its result occurs (PmtDīp 1374:12).

The Paramatthadīpanī explains why the asynchronous kind works later by the simile of a seed: the depositing of the seed of the result does not at once accomplish the function of a seed; like the seeds of mangoes and so on, it accomplishes it in a later moment after reaching a mature state (*pariṇatabhāvaṃ patvā khaṇantare*) (PmtDīp 1374:10-11).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Kammapaccayoti – kusalākusalaṃ kammaṃ vipākānaṃ khandhānaṃ kaṭattā ca rūpānaṃ kammapaccayena paccayo. Cetanā sampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ kammapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 13. Kamma condition: Wholesome and unwholesome kamma is related to resultant aggregates and to kamma-produced materiality by kamma condition. Volition is related to its associated states and to the materiality produced by them, by kamma condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Cittapayogasaṅkhātena kiriyabhāvena upakārako dhammo kammapaccayo. So nānakkhaṇikāya ceva kusalākusalacetanāya sahajātāya ca sabbāyapi cetanāya vasena duvidho hoti.**  
> 606. A phenomenon that assists by way of the state of action known as mental application (cittapayogasaṅkhātena) is a kamma condition (kammapaccayo). It is of two kinds: by way of wholesome and unwholesome volition at a different time, and by way of all conascent volition.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Ch. ### Quote (Añña: Paramatthadīpanī)
> **Tattha sahajātācetanāti antamaso pañcaviññāṇa sahajātāpi cetanā. Sāpi hi sahajātānaṃ saṃvidahanasaṅkhātena kammabhāvena pavattattā kammaṃevanāma hotīti. Yasmā attano pavattākārasaṅkhātassa vipākabījassa nidahanaṃnāma, na tāvadeva bījakiccaṃ sampādeti. Ambabījādīni viya pariṇatabhāvaṃ patvā khaṇantareeva sampādetīti vuttaṃ nānākkhaṇikā cetanātiādi. Tattha nānākkhaṇikācetanāti attano paccayuppannadhamma pavattikkhaṇato visuṃ bhūte atītakāle siddhā kusalā kusalacetanā. Kusalākusalacetanātivā pāṭho siyā.**  
> In that context, 'co-arisen volition' (sahajātācetanā) even the volition that is co-arisen with the five sense-consciousness. For that too, by way of the state of acting as kamma—termed the 'coordination' of co-arisen—is called kamma indeed. Since the 'depositing' of the seed of the result—termed the mode of its own occurrence—is depositing, it does not fulfill the duty of the seed at that very moment. Like mango seeds and so on, it fulfills only after reaching a state of maturity in another moment; this is why it is stated with 'asynchronous volition' (nānākkhaṇikā cetanā) and so on. In that context, 'asynchronous volition' (nānākkhaṇikā cetanā) wholesome unwholesome volition established in the past, separate from the moment of occurrence of the conditioned phenomenon that is its result. Or there could be a reading: 'wholesome and unwholesome volition' (kusalākusalacetanāti).  
>
