### Grammatical Analysis
**Vinaya**: [m.] discipline; training; monastic discipline. From the prefix *vi* + the verb *vineti* (leads away, trains). The Samantapāsādikā explains the word as *vinayanato ... kāyavācānaṃ* ("because it trains body and speech") and as *vividhavisesanayattā* ("because of its various and special methods", *vividha* + *visesa* + *naya*); see the second Quote.

### Orthodox Definition
In the Theravāda transmission, *Vinaya* is the monastic discipline laid down by the Buddha for monks (*bhikkhus*) and nuns (*bhikkhunīs*). It is the first division of the [Tipiṭaka](/glossary/tipitaka/) (*Vinaya Piṭaka*). The Atthasālinī lists its contents: both Pātimokkhas, the two Vibhaṅgas, the twenty-two Khandhakas and the sixteen Parivāras (As 151:4).

The Samantapāsādikā (Sp-i 134-136) gives the meaning of the word: there are "various" methods (*vividhā nayā*), namely the fivefold Pātimokkha recitation, the seven groups of offences beginning with the Pārājikas, the mātikās and the Vibhaṅgas; there are "special" methods (*visesabhūtā*), the *anupaññatti* rules, which serve to make firm or to relax; and it trains (*vineti*) body and speech by prohibiting bodily and verbal transgression. Therefore it is called *vinaya*.

The Atthasālinī also characterises the three Piṭakas by the way the Buddha taught them: the Vinaya Piṭaka is *āṇādesanā* (teaching by command), taught by the Blessed One who is entitled to command (*āṇārahena*) mostly in the manner of command (*āṇābāhullato*); the Suttanta is *vohāradesanā* and the Abhidhamma is *paramatthadesanā* (As 181:1-3).

The orthodox tradition holds that the Vinaya is the life of the Buddha's Dispensation: *vinayo nāma buddhasāsanassa āyu, vinaye ṭhite sāsanaṃ ṭhitaṃ hoti* (Sp-i 104:4, third Quote). When the Vinaya stands, the Sāsana stands.

### Quote (Mūla: Cūḷavagga, Dhamma and Vinaya recited)
> **“Suṇātu me, āvuso, saṅgho. Yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, saṅgho imāni pañca bhikkhusatāni sammanneyya – rājagahe vassaṃ vasantāni dhammañca vinayañca saṅgāyituṃ, na aññehi bhikkhūhi rājagahe vassaṃ vasitabbanti.**  
> 438. “Let the Saṅgha listen to me, friends. If it seems right to the Saṅgha, the Saṅgha should appoint these five hundred bhikkhus to spend the rains in Rājagaha to recite the Dhamma and the Vinaya, and no other bhikkhus should spend the rains in Rājagaha.

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā, the word vinaya)
> **Vividhavisesanayattā, vinayanato ceva kāyavācānaṃ; Vinayatthavidūhi ayaṃ, vinayo vinayoti akkhāto. Vividhā hi ettha pañcavidha pātimokkhuddesa pārājikādi sattaāpattikkhandhamātikā vibhaṅgādippabhedā nayā, visesabhūtā ca daḷhīkammasithilakaraṇappayojanā anupaññattinayā, kāyikavācasikaajjhācāranisedhanato cesa kāyaṃ vācañca vineti, tasmā vividhanayattā visesanayattā kāyavācānañca vinayanato “vinayo” ti akkhāto.**  
> Because it possesses various and special methods, and because it trains body and speech; this is declared to be the monastic discipline (vinayo) by those who know the meaning of the word discipline (vinayatthavidūhi). For here, there are various (vividhā) methods, such as the divisions of the fivefold proclamation of the monastic code (pātimokkhuddesa), the seven groups of offenses (āpattikkhandha) beginning with the Pārājikas, the schedules (mātikā), and the Vibhaṅgas. There are also special (visesabhūtā) methods, namely the supplementary regulations (anupaññatti), which serve to strengthen or relax the rules. Furthermore, it trains (vineti) both body and speech by prohibiting physical and verbal misconduct. Therefore, it is declared to be the monastic discipline (vinayo) because of its various methods, its special methods, and its training of body and speech.  
>  
> — *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* (Bāhiranidānakathā; also As 152-153, Sv-i 121-122) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_01_01&page_number=15&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikaka%E1%B9%87%E1%B8%8Daa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=Vml2aWRoYXZpc2VzYW5heWF0dMSBLCB2aW5heWFuYXRvIGNldmEga8SBeWF2xIFjxIFuYeG5gzs%3D)

### Textual References
* **Vinaya**: *Pārājikapāḷi* and *Pācittiyapāḷi* ([Suttavibhaṅga](/glossary/suttavibhanga/)) – Containing the specific conditions under which each moral training rule was legislated.
* **Vinaya**: *[Khandhaka](/glossary/khandhaka/)* (*[Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/)* and *[Cūḷavagga](/glossary/culavagga/)*) – Dealing with monastic procedures, ordinations, and institutional organization.
* **Commentary**: *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* – Ācariya Buddhaghosa’s immense commentary managing the legal interpretations of the Vinaya code.
