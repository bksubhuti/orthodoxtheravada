### Grammatical Analysis
**Madhuratthavilāsinī**: [f.] The Illuminator of Sweet Meaning. Formed by *madhura* (sweet, pleasant) + *attha* (meaning) + *vilāsinī* (illuminating).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Madhuratthavilāsinī* is the commentary on the *[Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)* (The Chronicle of Buddhas). It was authored by **Venerable Buddhadatta**, a contemporary of Buddhaghosa who famously met Buddhaghosa on a ship while traveling between India and Sri Lanka.

Because the canonical *[Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/)* reads like a rigid, poetic ledger (simply listing the lifespan, tree, and disciples of the 24 previous Buddhas), Buddhadatta's commentary is required to breathe life into the text. He heavily expands the narrative, providing dramatic descriptions of the [Bodhisatta](/glossary/bodhisatta/)'s interactions with these ancient Buddhas. It is a foundational text for the study of Buddhology (the nature and attributes of a Buddha) in the Theravāda tradition.

### Textual References
* **Subject Matter**: Commentary on the [Buddhavaṃsa](/glossary/buddhavamsa/).
* **Author Note**: Written by Buddhadatta while residing at Kāvīrapaṭṭana (Kaveripattinam) in South India, in a vihāra built by Kaṇhadāsa (Bv-a 4113:1-4120:1; author named at Bv-a 4129:1).
* **Later tradition**: The meeting with Buddhaghosa on a ship in mid-ocean is told in the Sāsanavaṃsa (Sas 208:2-3, an Añña-layer text); the Visuddhimagga-nidāna-ṭīkā says Buddhadatta, author of the Buddhavaṃsa commentary, was a contemporary of Buddhaghosa (Vism-nid 293:3).
