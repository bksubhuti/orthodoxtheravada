### Grammatical Analysis
**Sīlavanta**: [adj./m.] Virtuous; possessing moral habit; a person of good character. Formed by *[sīla](/glossary/sila/)* (morality, virtue) + possessive suffix *vanta* (possessing, endowed with).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sīlavanta* (also *sīlavā*, *sīlavato sīlasampanno*) is one endowed with *[sīla](/glossary/sila/)*, virtue; its opposite is *dussīla*, which the Aṭṭhakathā glosses as *nissīlo*, "without virtue" (Dhp-a 1978:1). In the Dhammapada the Buddha says one day of life is better for the virtuous one absorbed in meditation (*sīlavantassa jhāyino*) than a hundred years immoral and unconcentrated (Dhp 361:1-362:1); the commentary says the virtuous one who meditates is "superior" (*seyyo*, *uttama*) and adds that he is absorbed in two kinds of jhāna (Dhp-a 1978:2).

In the Aṅguttara the Buddha shows what follows for one who is *sīlavā sīlasampanno*: no volition need be exerted "let non-regret arise in me", for it is the rule (*dhammatā*) that non-regret (*avippaṭisāra*) arises in him (A-x 19:1-3); with non-regret present joy (*pāmojja*) has its proximate cause, with joy rapture (*pīti*) and so on (A-x 23:1-3, Paṭhamaupanisa Sutta, AN 10.3).

In the Sīlavanta Sutta (SN 22.122), Sāriputta tells Mahākoṭṭhita that a virtuous bhikkhu (*sīlavatā bhikkhunā*) should wisely consider the five aggregates subject to clinging as impermanent, suffering, a disease, a cancer, an arrow, a misery, an affliction, alien, a disintegration, empty and not-self, and that it is possible (*ṭhānaṃ kho panetaṃ vijjati*) that such a virtuous bhikkhu will realise the fruit of stream-entry (S-iii 764:3-8). The commentary explains these terms (Spk-iii 379:1-2).

### Quote (Mūla: Cetanākaraṇīya Sutta)
> **“Sīlavato, bhikkhave, sīlasampannassa na cetanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘ avippaṭisāro me uppajjatū ’ ti. Dhammatā esā, bhikkhave, yaṃ sīlavato sīlasampannassa avippaṭisāro uppajjati.**  
> “Monks, for one who is virtuous, one whose behavior is virtuous, no volition need be exerted: ‘Let non-regret arise in me.’ Monks, it is the rule that non-regret arises in one who is virtuous, one whose behavior is virtuous.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Sīlavanta Sutta)
> **“sīlavatāvuso, sāriputta, bhikkhunā katame dhammā yoniso manasikātabbā” ti? “Sīlavatāvuso, koṭṭhika, bhikkhunā pañcupādānakkhandhā aniccato dukkhato rogato gaṇḍato sallato aghato ābādhato parato palokato suññato anattato yoniso manasi kātabbā. Ṭhānaṃ kho panetaṃ, āvuso, vijjati yaṃ sīlavā bhikkhu ime pañcupādānakkhandhe aniccato … pe … anattato yoniso manasi karonto sotāpattiphalaṃ sacchikareyyā” ti.**  
> “Friend Sāriputta, what qualities should a virtuous bhikkhu wisely consider?” “Friend Koṭṭhika, a virtuous bhikkhu should wisely consider the five aggregates subject to clinging as impermanent, as suffering, as a disease, as a cancer, as an arrow, as a misery, as an affliction, as alien, as a disintegration, as empty, as not-self. It is possible, friend, that a virtuous bhikkhu, wisely considering these five aggregates subject to clinging as truly impermanent … as not-self, may realise the fruit of stream-entry.”  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 22.122, Sīlavanta Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_03&page_number=136&name=khandhavaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIHPEq2xhdmF0xIF2dXNvLCBzxIFyaXB1dHRhLCBiaGlra2h1bsSBIGthdGFtZSBkaGFtbcSB)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Tattha dussīloti nissīlo. Sīlavantassāti dussīlassa vassasataṃ jīvanato sīlavantassa dvīhi jhānehi jhāyino ekadivasampi ekamuhuttampi jīvitaṃ seyyo , uttamanti attho.**  
> Among these, immoral means without virtue. Virtuous means that for someone who is virtuous and absorbed in two types of meditation, a single day or even a single moment of living is better—that is, superior—to living for a hundred years as an immoral person.  
>  
> — Dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā (Dhp-a 1978:1-2, on Dhp 110) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_02_01&page_number=432&name=dhammapadaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGR1c3PEq2xvdGkgbmlzc8SrbG8u)
