### Grammatical Analysis
**Upasampadā**: [f.] Higher ordination; full ordination. Formed by *upa* + *sampadā* (attainment, accomplishment). The one ordained is *upasampanno* (Sp-i 1020:3).

### Orthodox Definition
*Upasampadā* is the formal act that makes a person a fully ordained bhikkhu or bhikkhunī. In the Mūla the Buddha first allowed it by the three refuges and then withdrew that: "Monks, the upasampadā that I allowed by the three goings for refuge, I abolish from this day forth. I allow you, monks, to ordain by a formal act consisting of a motion and a resolution put three times (*ñatticatutthena kammena*)" (Vin-iii 398:19-20). The Samantapāsādikā counts eight kinds of upasampadā in the texts and says: "ñatticatutthakammūpasampadā nāma bhikkhūnaṃ etarahi upasampadā", this is the upasampadā of bhikkhus at present (Sp-i 1020:1-3). It explains that the going forth (*pabbajjā*) of a novice, which the Buddha later re-allowed, is given by the three refuges and not by a formal act (Sp-iii 255:1-3).

The act needs a Saṅgha of a prescribed number: in the border regions a group of five including an expert in the Vinaya (*vinayadharapañcamena gaṇena*, Vin-iii 1538:3, §259); in the middle districts the fivefold Order cannot perform it (Vin-iii 2344:3), so ten are needed. The candidate is questioned about the "stumbling-blocks" (*antarāyike dhamme*): "Have you such diseases as leprosy, boils, eczema, consumption, epilepsy? Are you a human being, a man, a freeman, free of debt, not in the royal service? Have you your parents' consent? Are you twenty years old? Are you complete as to bowl and robes?" (Vin-iii 680:7). The age is counted "from conception": "Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, gabbhavīsaṃ upasampādetuṃ" (Vin-iii 667:8). A bhikkhu who knowingly ordains a person under twenty commits a pācittiya, and the person is not ordained (Pācittiya 65, Vin-ii 1242:1). For women, a sikkhamānā who has trained for two years in the six rules must seek upasampadā from both Saṅghas (Vin-iv 2433:1, the sixth garudhamma).

The Mahāvibhaṅga contains 227 rules (*sattavīsādhikāni dve sikkhāpadasatāni*, Sv-i 90:9) and the Bhikkhunīvibhaṅga 304 rules (*tīṇi sikkhāpadasatāni cattāri ca*, Sv-i 91:8), with the seven *adhikaraṇasamathā* set down after them (Sv-i 91:7); these are the training that binds a newly ordained monk or nun.

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, ordination by formal act)
> **Atha kho bhagavā etasmiṃ nidāne etasmiṃ pakaraṇe dhammiṃ kathaṃ katvā bhikkhū āmantesi – yā sā, bhikkhave, mayā tīhi saraṇagamanehi upasampadā anuññātā, taṃ ajjatagge paṭikkhipāmi. Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, ñatticatutthena kammena upasampādetuṃ.**  
> Then the Lord, on this occasion, in this connection, having given reasoned talk, addressed the monks, saying: ‘From this day forth, monks, I abolish that ordination by going to the three refuges which I allowed. I allow you, monks, to ordain by a (formal) act consisting of a motion and a resolution put three times.’ (tr. TPR; its next sentence, “through a group of ten or more”, belongs to the following line and is omitted.)

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, age from conception)
> **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, gabbhavīsaṃ upasampādetunti.**  
> I allow you, monks, to ordain one who is twenty years of age from his conception.

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, the border regions)
> **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sabbapaccantimesu janapadesu vinayadharapañcamena gaṇena upasampadaṃ.**  
> I allow, monks, in all border districts, ordination by a group with, as fifth, an expert on discipline.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Pācittiyapāḷi, under twenty)
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu jānaṃ ūnavīsativassaṃ puggalaṃ upasampādeyya , so ca puggalo anupasampanno, te ca bhikkhū gārayhā , idaṃ tasmiṃ pācittiyan”ti.**  
> “Whatever monk should knowingly confer the upasampadā ordination on an individual under twenty years of age, both that individual is not ordained and these monks are blameworthy; this is for him an offence of expiation.”  
>  
> — *Pācittiyapāḷi* (Pācittiya 65, §403) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_02&page_number=170&name=p%C4%81cittiyap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NDAzLuKAmOKAmCBZbyBwYW5hIGJoaWtraHUgasSBbmHhuYMgxatuYXbEq3NhdGl2YXNzYeG5gw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā)
> **Ñatticatutthakammūpasampadā nāma bhikkhūnaṃ etarahi upasampadā. Imāsu aṭṭhasu upasampadāsu “yā sā, bhikkhave, mayā tīhi saraṇagamanehi upasampadā anuññātā, taṃ ajjatagge paṭikkhipāmi. Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, ñatticatutthena kammena upasampādetun” ti (mahāva.69) evaṃ anuññātāya imāya upasampadāya upasampannoti vuttaṃ hoti.**  
> Higher ordination by a formal act consisting of a motion and a resolution put three times (ñatticatutthakammūpasampadā) is the higher ordination for bhikkhus at present (etarahi). Among these eight kinds of higher ordination, it was stated: “Monks, whatever higher ordination was allowed (anuññātā) by me by taking the three refuges, I abolish it from this day forth (ajjatagge). I allow you, monks, to ordain them (upasampādetuṃ) through a legal procedure consisting of one motion and three announcements” (Vin.i.22); it is said that one who has received full ordination (upasampanno) through this allowed higher ordination is meant.  
>
