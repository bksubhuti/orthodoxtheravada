### Grammatical Analysis
**Phalañāṇa**: [nt.] Fruition knowledge. Formed by *phala* (fruit, result, consequence) + *ñāṇa* (knowledge). 

### Orthodox Definition
*Phalañāṇa* is the knowledge of fruition, the fruit of the Path knowledge (*[maggañāṇa](/glossary/magganana/)*). The Paṭisambhidāmagga calls it "understanding of tranquillization of the task is knowledge of fruition" (*payogappaṭippassaddhi paññā phale ñāṇaṃ*, Paṭis 17:1) and, for each path factor, says "right view arises because of the tranquillizing of that task ... this is the fruition of the path" (*maggassetaṃ phalaṃ*, Paṭis 402:3, 403:3).

The *Paṭṭhāna* gives the path as the proximity condition of fruition: "*maggo phalassa*" (Paṭṭh-i 1417:2, among the proximity-condition cases). The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha says the four path-moments each last one consciousness-moment, after which two or three fruition consciousnesses arise as appropriate, then the fall into the life-continuum (Abhidh-s 346:1). Fruition consciousness takes Nibbāna as its object. The Visuddhimagga's "Discussion of Fruition Attainment" (*Phalasamāpattikathā*, Vism-ii 2067:1) explains that noble ones who wish to experience the supramundane happiness make a determination of the time and enter fruition attainment at will (Vism-ii 2075:2). A noble disciple can later re-enter this state for extended periods through the "Fruition Attainment" (*phala-samāpatti*).

### Quote
> **Kathaṃ payogappaṭippassaddhipaññā phale ñāṇaṃ? Sotāpattimaggakkhaṇe dassanaṭṭhena sammādiṭṭhi micchādiṭṭhiyā vuṭṭhāti, tadanuvattakakilesehi ca khandhehi ca vuṭṭhāti, bahiddhā ca sabbanimittehi vuṭṭhāti. Taṃpayogappaṭippassaddhattā uppajjati sammādiṭṭhi.**  
> 63. How is it that understanding of tranquillization of the tasks is knowledge of fruition? At the moment of the stream-entry path, right view in the sense of seeing emerges from wrong view, and it emerges from defilements and from aggregates that occur consequent upon them, and externally it emerges from all signs. Right view arises because of the tranquillizing of that task.  
>  
> — [Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/) (Ñāṇakathā, Phalañāṇaniddesa, Paṭis 401:1; mātikā 12 at Paṭis 17:1) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_18&page_number=68&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NjMuIEthdGhh4bmDIHBheW9nYXBwYeG5rWlwcGFzc2FkZGhpcGHDscOxxIEgcGhhbGUgw7HEgeG5h2HhuYM%2F)

* **[Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/)**: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)* – the supramundane resultant consciousnesses: "tasseva lokuttarassa kusalassa jhānassa katattā bhāvitattā vipākaṃ" (the result of that same supramundane wholesome jhāna, because it was done and developed, Dhs 1037:3); *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)* – the proximity condition (*[anantara-paccaya](/glossary/anantara-paccaya/)*) between path and fruit (Paṭṭh-i 1417:2).
* **Later handbook**: Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha (Abhidh-s 346:1, two or three fruition moments).
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XXIII, "23. Paññābhāvanānisaṃsaniddeso", Vism-ii 2056:1; Phalasamāpattikathā, Vism-ii 2067:1).
