### Grammatical Analysis
**Sammākammanta**: [m.] Right action. Formed by *sammā* (rightly) + *kammanta* (action, work). The Ṭīkā: "*kammameva kammanto*, action itself is 'work' ... work that has proceeded rightly is *sammākammanto*; it is the abstinence from bodily misconduct (*kāyaduccaritavirati*)" (Abh-s-t 1055). The Sammohavinodanī gives its characteristic: "*sammā samuṭṭhāpanalakkhaṇo sammākammanto*", right action has the characteristic of correctly initiating (Vibh-a 515).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sammākammanta* is the fourth factor of the noble eightfold path (DN 22). The Buddha defines it as refraining from killing living creatures, from taking what is not given, and from sexual misconduct (*pāṇātipātā veramaṇī, adinnādānā veramaṇī, kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī*, DN 22; Vibh 1578). In the Abhidhamma it is abstention from the three bodily misconducts (*tīhi kāyaduccaritehi ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī ... setughāto*), "a path factor, included in the path" (Dhs 300; Vibh 1592). It is the second of the three *viratiyo* of the handbook: *sammāvācā, sammākammanto, sammāājīvo* (Abhidh-s 7).

The Sammohavinodanī explains that, in the preliminary stage, the three abstinences are different, since one abstains from killing with one mind, from stealing with another, and from sexual misconduct with another. At the moment of the path, by cutting off the threefold unwholesome volition called wrong action and ensuring that it does not arise again, a single abstinence called right action arises, fulfilling the path factor (Vibh-a 534; see also 541).

### Quote (Mūla: Dīgha Nikāya)
> **“Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammākammanto? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī adinnādānā veramaṇī kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī, ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammākammanto.**  
> “Now what, monks, is right action? Refraining from killing living creatures, refraining from taking what has not been given, refraining from sexual misconduct. This, monks, is called right action.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 22, Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna Sutta, Maggasacca) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_02&page_number=250&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIEthdGFtbyBjYSwgYmhpa2toYXZlLCBzYW1txIFrYW1tYW50bz8%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Vibhaṅga, Suttantabhājanīya)
> **Tattha katamo sammākammanto? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī, adinnādānā veramaṇī, kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī – ayaṃ vuccati “sammākammanto”.**  
> Which among these is right action? Refraining from killing living creatures, refraining from taking what has not been given, refraining from sexual misconduct – this is called right action (sammākammanto).  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Maggaṅgavibhaṅga, Suttantabhājanīya) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=244&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGthdGFtbyBzYW1txIFrYW1tYW50bz8%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Vibhaṅga, Abhidhammabhājanīya)
> **Tattha katamo sammākammanto? Yā tīhi kāyaduccaritehi ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī akiriyā akaraṇaṃ anajjhāpatti velāanatikkamo setughāto sammākammanto maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati “sammākammanto”.**  
> Which among these is right action? That abstention, abstinence, refraining, abstention, non-effecting, no doing, non-transgression, non-transgression of limits, cutting off of the bridge from the three bodily misconducts, right action, a path factor, included in the path – this is called right action (sammākammanto).  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Maggaṅgavibhaṅga, Abhidhammabhājanīya) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=246&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGthdGFtbyBzYW1txIFrYW1tYW50bz8%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Katamo tasmiṃ samaye sammākammanto hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye tīhi kāyaduccaritehi ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī akiriyā akaraṇaṃ anajjhāpatti velāanatikkamo setughāto sammākammanto maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammākammanto hoti.**  
> 300. What is right action at that time? Whatever refraining, refraining, refraining, abstention, inoperative one, No doing, non-transgression, non-transgression of limits, cutting off of the bridge from the three bodily misconducts, being right action, being a path factor, included in the path exists at that time—this is right action at that time.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Cittuppādakaṇḍa, lokuttara, para. 300) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=76&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MzAwLiBLYXRhbW8gdGFzbWnhuYMgc2FtYXllIHNhbW3EgWthbW1hbnRvIGhvdGk%2F)

> Right action (sammākammanto) has the characteristic of correctly initiating.

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: preliminary and path moment)
> **Sammākammanta niddesepi yasmā aññeneva cittena pāṇātipātā viramati, aññena adinnādānā, aññena kāmesumicchācārā, tasmā tissopetā veramaṇiyo pubbabhāge nānā; maggakkhaṇe pana micchākammantasaṅkhātāya tividhāya akusaladussīlyacetanāya padacchedato anuppattisādhanavasena maggaṅgaṃ pūrayamānā ekāva sammākammantasaṅkhātā akusalaveramaṇī uppajjati. Ayaṃ sammākammanto nāma.**  
> In the exposition of right action (sammākammanta) too, because one abstains from killing living creatures with one mind, from taking what has not been given with another mind, and from sexual misconduct with another mind, these three abstinences, in the preliminary phase, are therefore diverse; at the path-moment, however, by cutting off the threefold unwholesome volition of bad character known as wrong action and by way of ensuring its non-occurrence, a single wholesome abstinence known as right action arises, fulfilling the path factor. This is called right action (sammākammanto).  
>  
> — Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā (Sammohavinodanī, Maggaṅgavibhaṅga) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_02&page_number=111&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U2FtbcSBa2FtbWFudGEgbmlkZGVzZXBpIHlhc23EgSBhw7HDsWVuZXZhIGNpdHRlbmEgcMSB4bmHxIF0aXDEgXTEgQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Handbook: Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha)
> **Sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo ceti tisso viratiyo nāma.**  
> 7. Right speech, right action, and right livelihood—these three are called abstinences.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (para. 7, the three viratis) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=8&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=Ny4gU2FtbcSBdsSBY8SBIHNhbW3EgWthbW1hbnRvIHNhbW3EgcSBasSrdm8gY2V0aSB0aXNzbw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Kammameva kammanto suttantavanantādayo viya. Sammā pavatto kammanto sammākammanto , kāyaduccaritavirati. Sā tividhā pāṇātipātā veramaṇi, adinnādānā veramaṇi, kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇīti.**  
> Action itself is 'work' (kammanto), just as in words like 'suttanta' and 'vananta'. Work that has proceeded rightly is right action (sammākammanto); it is the abstinence from bodily misconduct. It is of three kinds: abstinence from killing living beings, abstinence from taking what is not given, and abstinence from sexual misconduct.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinīṭīkā (para. 7) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=111&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=S2FtbWFtZXZhIGthbW1hbnRvIHN1dHRhbnRhdmFuYW50xIFkYXlvIHZpeWEu)
