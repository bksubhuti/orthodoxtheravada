### Grammatical Analysis
**Paramatthadīpanī**: [f.] The Elucidator of the Ultimate Meaning. Formed by *paramattha* (ultimate meaning) + *dīpanī* (elucidating). The Itivuttaka commentary gives the name: “Pakāsanā paramattha-dīpanī nāma nāmato”, the exposition of the ultimate meanings in the suttantas is called *Paramatthadīpanī* by name (It-a 1746:1-1747:1).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Paramatthadīpanī* is the name of the commentary of the Elder Dhammapāla, resident of the Badaratittha monastery (*badaratitthavihāravāsinā ācariyadhammapālena*), on several books of the Khuddaka Nikāya. The colophons name him for the Itivuttaka (It-a 1758:1), the Udāna (Ud-a 1687:1-1688:1), the Cariyāpiṭaka (Cp-a 2124:1), the Petavatthu (Pv-a 3234:1-3235:1), the Vimānavatthu (Vv-a 4013:1), the Theragāthā and the Therīgāthā (Thī-a 4545:1). The Aṭṭhakathā itself uses the title for these books: “Paramatthadīpaniyā cariyāpiṭakasaṃvaṇṇanāya” (Cp-a 224:1), “Paramatthadīpaniyā theragāthāsaṃvaṇṇanāya” (Th-a-i 236:1), “Iti paramatthadīpaniyā khuddaka-aṭṭhakathāya vimānavatthusmiṃ” (Vv-a 760:1), and refers to “paramatthadīpaniyā udānaṭṭhakathāya itivuttakaṭṭhakathāya” (Th-a-i 352:14). The books covered, as the colophons show, are the *[Udāna](/glossary/udana/)*, *[Itivuttaka](/glossary/itivuttaka/)*, *[Vimānavatthu](/glossary/vimanavatthu/)*, *[Petavatthu](/glossary/petavatthu/)*, *[Theragāthā](/glossary/theragatha/)*, *[Therīgāthā](/glossary/therigatha/)* and *[Cariyāpiṭaka](/glossary/cariyapitaka/)*.

On method, the author says that he follows the method of the old commentaries (*porāṇaṭṭhakathānayaṃ*, It-a 1744:1, Ud-a 24:1), relying on the five Nikāyas and not contradicting the tradition of the Mahāvihāra residents (*mahāvihāravāsīnaṃ samayaṃ avilomayaṃ*), avoiding repeated explanations (Ud-a 24:1-28:1). The Itivuttaka commentary was completed in thirty-eight recital sections (*aṭṭhattiṃsappamāṇāya pāḷiyā bhāṇavārato*, It-a 1749:1).

The title is shared with a later work on the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha, *Paramatthadīpanī* (TPR book PmtDīp, outside the Tipiṭaka commentaries), which is a different text and is not the one this page describes.

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Itivuttaka-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Tassa atthaṃ pakāsetuṃ, porāṇaṭṭhakathānayaṃ; Nissāya yā samāraddhā, atthasaṃvaṇṇanā mayā. Sā tattha paramatthānaṃ, suttantesu yathārahaṃ; Pakāsanā paramattha-dīpanī nāma nāmato. Sampattā pariniṭṭhānaṃ, anākulavinicchayā;**  
> Relying on the method of the ancient commentaries to make its meaning manifest, this explanation of the meaning was begun by me. There, regarding the ultimate truths in the discourses as appropriate, this is the Paramattha-dīpanī, (so named) by name, displaying them. It has reached completion, with settled and unconfused judgments,  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Udāna-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Nikāye upanissāya, porāṇaṭṭhakathānayaṃ. Suvisuddhaṃ asaṃkiṇṇaṃ, nipuṇatthavinicchayaṃ; Mahāvihāravāsīnaṃ , samayaṃ avilomayaṃ. Punappunāgataṃ atthaṃ, vajjayitvāna sādhukaṃ; Yathābalaṃ karissāmi, udānassatthavaṇṇanaṃ.**  
> Collections, in dependence on the method of the ancient commentaries, showing the very pure, unconfused, and subtle determination of meaning which does not conflict with the tradition of the dwellers of the Great Monastery (mahāvihāravāsī). Carefully avoiding any meanings that are repeated again and again, I shall, according to my ability, write this commentary on the meaning of the Udāna (udānassatthavaṇṇana).  
>
