### Grammatical Analysis
**Abhidhammapiṭaka**: [nt.] The Basket of Higher Doctrine; The Basket of Ultimate Realities. Formed by *abhi* (higher, surpassing, ultimate) + *dhamma* (doctrine, phenomenon) + *piṭaka* (basket).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Abhidhamma-piṭaka* is the third and most philosophically dense basket of the Pali Canon. It contains seven treatises beginning with the Dhammasaṅgaṇī (As 151:10).

It is classified as **Paramatthadesanā** (teaching of ultimate reality): the Aṭṭhakathā says the Abhidhamma Piṭaka was taught by the Buddha, "skilled in ultimate reality", mainly in terms of ultimate facts, as the Vinaya is the teaching of command and the Suttanta the teaching of convention (As 181:1-3). The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha states its topics as four ultimate realities: consciousness (*[citta](/glossary/citta/)*), mental factors (*cetasika*), materiality (*[rūpa](/glossary/rupa/)*) and [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/) (Abhidh-s 7:1-8:1).

According to Theravāda orthodoxy, the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) was not invented by later monks. The Atthasālinī says the Tathāgata, seated at the foot of the Pāricchattaka tree in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven in the midst of deities, made his mother the witness and taught "kusalā dhammā, akusalā dhammā, abyākatā dhammā" (As 122:5), and that the teaching went on without a break for three months (As 122:6). While teaching he created a duplicate Buddha, went for alms, and on returning gave Sāriputta the method ("Sāriputta, ettako dhammo mayā desito", As 125:5, 126:1-3). The Atthasālinī then traces the transmission from Sāriputta and others down to the Third Council and in Sri Lanka by the lineage of teachers (As 239:1-3).

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: Consists of seven books: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/), [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/), [Dhātukathā](/glossary/dhatukatha/), [Puggalapaññatti](/glossary/puggalapannatti/), [Kathāvatthu](/glossary/kathavatthu/), [Yamaka](/glossary/yamaka/),* and the massive *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)*.
* **Commentary**: *[Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/)* – "Dhammasaṅgaṇīādīni satta pakaraṇāni abhidhammapiṭakaṃ nāma" (As 151:10); the Tāvatiṃsa teaching (As 122:5-6, 236:1-3), and the question-and-answer on where and by whom the Abhidhamma was taught (As 238:1-9).
* **Milindapañha**: Mil 55:1 – lists the seven books by name.
* **Textual**: the Abhidhamma Piṭaka is the third of the three baskets (As 151:3).
