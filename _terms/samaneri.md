### Grammatical Analysis
**Sāmaṇerī**: [f.] Novice nun. The feminine form of *[sāmaṇera](/glossary/samanera/)*.

### Orthodox Definition
A *Sāmaṇerī* is a female monastic who has gone forth from the household life but has not yet taken higher ordination. Like her male counterpart, she shaves her head, wears the monastic robes, and is bound by the exact same **Ten Precepts** (*Dasa Sikkhāpada*).

In the structural path to becoming a fully ordained [Bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/), the *Sāmaṇerī* stage is the foundational step. Before a *sāmaṇerī* can receive higher ordination (*[upasampadā](/glossary/upasampada/)*), she must transition into the status of a *[Sikkhāmānā](/glossary/sikkhamana/)* (probationary nun) for exactly two years to thoroughly test her resolve and physical suitability for the ascetic life.

### Quote
> **1212. Yā panāti yā yādisā…pe… bhikkhunīti…pe… ayaṃ imasmiṃ atthe adhippetā bhikkhunīti.**  
> 1212. “Any” means whoever, whatever kind…pe… “bhikkhuni”…pe… a bhikkhuni referred to in this context.  
> 
> **Sikkhamānā nāma dve vassāni chasu dhammesu sikkhitasikkhā.**  
> A sikkhamānā is one who has trained for two years in the six dhammas.  
> 
> **Sāmaṇerī nāma dasasikkhāpadikā.**  
> A sāmaṇerī is one who observes the ten training rules.  
> 
> **Gihinī nāma agārinī vuccati.**  
> A laywoman is said to be a householder.  
> 
> — *Pācittiyapāḷi*
