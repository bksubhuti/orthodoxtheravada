### Grammatical Analysis
**Sāmaṇerī**: [f.] Novice nun. The feminine form of *[sāmaṇera](/glossary/samanera/)*.

### Orthodox Definition
The Vinaya defines it in one line: "*Sāmaṇerī nāma dasasikkhāpadikā*", "a female novice means one who keeps the ten training rules" (Vin-ii 1164). She is one who has gone forth: "having had her hair and beard shaved and the yellow robes put on" she takes the three refuges (Vin-iii 186). The ten rules are those the Buddha allowed for novices in the Mahāvagga: refraining from killing, taking what is not given, unchastity, false speech, intoxicants, eating at the wrong time, dancing, singing, music and shows, garlands, scents and adornment, high and luxurious beds, and accepting gold and silver (Vin-iii 603).

She is not a *[bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/)* (one ordained by both Saṅghas, Vin-ii 1161) and not yet a *[sikkhamānā](/glossary/sikkhamana/)* (one who has trained for two years in the six rules, Vin-ii 1162). The sixth *gurudhamma* lays down that a probationer "who has trained for two years in the six rules should seek ordination from both Saṅghas" (AN 8.51). The Samantapāsādikā adds that the Saṅgha gives the agreement to the training (*sikkhāsammuti*): to a married girl (*gihigatā*) at ten years with ordination at twelve, and to others given at twelve to eighteen, ordination at twenty (Sp-ii-b 1375). The Vinaya's own formulas for the *sikkhāsammuti* are asked by a married girl or a maiden (*gihigatā, kumāribhūtā*) as well as by one already a probationer (Vin-ii-b 3293, 3332, 3395), so the Vinaya does not make the *sāmaṇerī* stage a required step before the two years.

### Quote (Mūla: Suttavibhaṅga, Vikappanasikkhāpada)
> **Bhikkhunī nāma ubhatosaṅghe upasampannā. Sikkhamānā nāma dve vassāni chasu dhammesu sikkhitasikkhā. Sāmaṇero nāma dasasikkhāpadiko. Sāmaṇerī nāma dasasikkhāpadikā.**  
> ‘A nun’ means: one ordained by both Orders. ‘A female probationer’ means: one training in the six rules for two years. ‘A male novice’ means: one conforming to the ten rules of training. ‘A female novice’ means: one conforming to the ten rules of training.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, the ten training rules)
> **Atha kho sāmaṇerānaṃ etadahosi – “kati nu kho amhākaṃ sikkhāpadāni, kattha ca amhehi sikkhitabban” ti? Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ … pe … anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sāmaṇerānaṃ dasa sikkhāpadāni, tesu ca sāmaṇerehi sikkhituṃ – pāṇātipātā veramaṇī, adinnādānā veramaṇī, abrahmacariyā veramaṇī, musāvādā veramaṇī, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇī, vikālabhojanā veramaṇī, naccagītavāditavisūkadassanā veramaṇī, mālāgandhavilepanadhāraṇamaṇḍanavibhūsanaṭṭhānā veramaṇī, uccāsayanamahāsayanā veramaṇī, jātarūparajatapaṭiggahaṇā veramaṇī.**  
> 106. Then it occurred to the novices – “How many training rules are there for us, and in which should we train?” They told this matter to the Blessed One … and he said: “I allow, bhikkhus, ten training rules for novices, and novices are to train in these: refraining from killing living creatures, refraining from taking what has not been given, refraining from uncelibacy, refraining from false speech, refraining from taking distilled and fermented drinks that cause heedlessness, refraining from eating at the wrong time, refraining from dancing, singing, music, and watching shows, refraining from wearing garlands, using perfumes, and beautifying the body with cosmetics, refraining from high and luxurious beds, refraining from accepting gold and silver.”  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Aṅguttara Nikāya, the sixth gurudhamma)
> **“Dve vassāni chasu dhammesu sikkhitasikkhāya sikkhamānāya ubhatosaṅghe upasampadā pariyesitabbā.**  
> “A trainee nun, after training in the six rules for two years, should seek full ordination from the communities of both bhikkhus and bhikkhunis.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 8.51, Gotamī Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_08&page_number=104&name=a%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIER2ZSB2YXNzxIFuaSBjaGFzdSBkaGFtbWVzdSBzaWtraGl0YXNpa2toxIF5YQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā)
> **Gihigatāya dasavassakāle sikkhāsammutiṃ datvā dvādasavassakāle upasampadā kātabbā. Mahāsikkhamānā pana gihigatātipi vattuṃ na vaṭṭati, kumāribhūtātipi vattuṃ na vaṭṭati, sikkhāsammutidānavasena pana tissopi sikkhamānāti vattuṃ vaṭṭati.**  
> For a married girl (gihigatā), the agreement as to training (sikkhāsammuti) should be given (datvā) at ten years of age, and ordination (upasampadā) should be carried out (kātabbā) at twelve years of age. However (pana), it is not proper (na vaṭṭati) to say (vattuṃ) that a great probationer (mahāsikkhamānā) is a married girl (gihigatā), nor is it proper (vaṭṭati) to say (vattuṃ) that she is a maiden (kumāribhūtā). But (pana), by means of giving the agreement as to training (sikkhāsammuti), it is proper (vaṭṭati) to say (vattuṃ) that all three (tissopi) are 'probationer (sikkhamānā).'  
>  
> — Samantapāsādikā (Bhikkhunīvibhaṅgavaṇṇanā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_02&page_number=222&name=p%C4%81cittiyaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=R2loaWdhdMSBeWEgZGFzYXZhc3Nha8SBbGUgc2lra2jEgXNhbW11dGnhuYMgZGF0dsSBIGR2xIFkYXNhdmFzc2FrxIFsZSB1cGFzYW1wYWTEgQ%3D%3D)
