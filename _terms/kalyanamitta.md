### Grammatical Analysis
**Kalyāṇamitta**: [m.] good friend; admirable friend. Formed by *kalyāṇa* (good, admirable) + *mitta* (friend). The Mūla uses it in the stock triad *kalyāṇamittatā kalyāṇasahāyatā kalyāṇasampavaṅkatā* (good friendship, good companionship, good comradeship), and of the person, *kalyāṇamitto kalyāṇasahāyo kalyāṇasampavaṅko*.

### Orthodox Definition
In SN 45.2 the Buddha corrects Ānanda: good friendship is not half of the spiritual life but the whole of it (*sakalamevidaṃ brahmacariyaṃ*), because a monk with a good friend, good companion and good comrade may be expected to develop and cultivate the Noble Eightfold Path. In AN 9.3 (Meghiya Sutta) the good friend is the first of the five things that bring unripe release of the heart to ripeness.

AN 7.37 gives seven qualities of a friend to be resorted to, associated with and attended upon: he is dear (*piyo*), agreeable (*manāpo*), respected (*garu*), esteemed (*bhāvanīyo*), an admonisher (*vattā*), one who accepts admonishment (*vacanakkhamo*), one who speaks on deep matters (*gambhīrañca kathaṃ kattā*), and he does not urge one to what is improper (*no ca aṭṭhāne niyojeti*).

The *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (III) quotes these qualities when it explains the *kalyāṇamitta* from whom a meditation subject is to be taken, and states that "it is only the Fully Enlightened One who is the good friend perfect in every way" (*sammāsambuddhoyeva sabbākārasampanno kalyāṇamitto*); after his passing it is proper to take the subject from a living great disciple, and in the absence of one, from an Arahant who has reached the destruction of the taints (Vism-i 716:1-4). In the discussion of resort (*gocara*), the same text describes the *upanissayagocara* as the *kalyāṇamitta* endowed with the qualities of the ten subjects of talk, relying on whom one grows in faith, virtue, learning, generosity and wisdom (Vism-i 151:3-4).

### Quote (Mūla: Saṃyutta Nikāya, Upaḍḍha Sutta)
> **“Mā hevaṃ, ānanda, mā hevaṃ, ānanda! Sakalamevidaṃ, ānanda, brahmacariyaṃ, yadidaṃ – kalyāṇamittatā kalyāṇasahāyatā kalyāṇasampavaṅkatā. Kalyāṇamittassetaṃ, ānanda, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṃ kalyāṇasahāyassa kalyāṇasampavaṅkassa – ariyaṃ aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ bhāvessati, ariyaṃ aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ bahulīkarissati.**  
> “Not so, Ānanda! Not so, Ānanda! This is the entire spiritual life, Ānanda, that is, good friendship, good companionship, good comradeship. When a bhikkhu has a good friend, a good companion, a good comrade, it is to be expected that he will develop and cultivate the Noble Eightfold Path.”  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 45.2, Upaḍḍha Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_05&page_number=2&name=mah%C4%81vaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIE3EgSBoZXZh4bmDLCDEgW5hbmRhLCBtxIEgaGV2YeG5gyw%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Aṅguttara Nikāya, Dutiyamitta Sutta)
> **“Sattahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu mitto sevitabbo bhajitabbo payirupāsitabbo api panujjamānenapi. Katamehi sattahi? Piyo ca hoti manāpo ca garu ca bhāvanīyo ca vattā ca vacanakkhamo ca gambhīrañca kathaṃ kattā hoti, no ca aṭṭhāne niyojeti.**  
> “Mendicants, when a bhikkhu is possessed of seven qualities, he is a friend to be resorted to, associated with, and attended upon, even if he drives you away. Which seven? He is likable and agreeable, respected and admired. He is an admonisher and accepts admonishment. He speaks on deep matters, and does not urge one to do things without a reason.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 7.37, Dutiyamitta Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_07&page_number=422&name=sattakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Mzcu4oCY4oCYIFNhdHRhaGksIGJoaWtraGF2ZSwgZGhhbW1laGkgc2FtYW5uxIFnYXRvIGJoaWtraHU%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Kalyāṇamittanti – Piyo garu bhāvanīyo, vattā ca vacanakkhamo; Gambhīrañca kathaṃ kattā, no caṭṭhāne niyojakoti.(a. ni.7.37);**  
> who is a 'good friend' (kalyāṇamitta) — Lovable, respected, and esteemed, an admonisher who accepts admonishment; He speaks on deep matters and does not urge one to what is improper.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Ch. III, Kammaṭṭhānaggahaṇaniddesa) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_01&page_number=94&name=visuddhimaggo+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=S2FsecSB4bmHYW1pdHRhbnRpIOKAkw%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Upaḍḍha Sutta* (SN 45.2, S-v 10-12) – Ānanda says that good friendship is half of the spiritual life; the Buddha replies "Mā hevaṃ, ānanda": it is the whole of it (S-v 12:1-3).
* **Canonical**: *Meghiya Sutta* (AN 9.3, A-ix 24-41) – "Kalyāṇamittassetaṃ, meghiya, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṃ …" (A-ix 38:1); the good friend is the first of the five things that conduce to the ripening of unripe release of the heart (A-ix 33:3-4). *Dutiyamitta Sutta* (AN 7.37, A-vii 242-243) – the seven qualities.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter III, *Kammaṭṭhānaggahaṇaniddesa*, Vism-i 649ff.; the *kalyāṇamitta* at Vism-i 712-716, and at Vism-i 151:3-4).
