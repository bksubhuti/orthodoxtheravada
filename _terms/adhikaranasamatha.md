### Grammatical Analysis
**Adhikaraṇasamatha**: [nt.] Settlement of legal issues; resolution of disputes. Formed by *adhikaraṇa* (legal issue, dispute, case) + *samatha* (calming, settling, resolution).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Adhikaraṇasamatha* are the **seven legal principles** used to settle disputes within the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/). They come last in the [Pātimokkha](/glossary/patimokkha/) recitation (Vin-ii 2153:1-2156:1). The Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī explains the name: they are called *adhikaraṇasamathā* "because they settle or appease legal issues" (*adhikaraṇāni samenti vūpasamentīti*), and the legal issues (*adhikaraṇāni*) are four: litigation because of disputes, because of accusation, because of an offence, and because of a business matter of the Saṅgha (Kkh 2146:2-10).

The seven principles are:
1. **Sammukhā-[vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: Proceeding in the presence (of the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/), the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/), the [Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/), and the disputing parties).
2. **Sati-[vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: Verdict of mindfulness (recollection).
3. **Amūḷha-[vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: Verdict of unbewilderment (amūḷha), given to a monk who acted while out of his mind.
4. **Paṭiññāya**: Acting on the acknowledgment/confession of the accused.
5. **Yebhuyyasikā**: Decision by majority vote using voting tickets (*salākā*).
6. **Tassapāpiyasikā**: Specific penalization for a monk who lies, evades, or acts stubbornly during an interrogation.
7. **Tiṇavatthāraka**: "Covering over with grass." The procedure for settling a dispute by covering it over, as grass covers excrement.

### Quote
> **“Sattime, bhikkhave, adhikaraṇasamathā dhammā uppannuppannānaṃ adhikaraṇānaṃ samathāya vūpasamāya. Katame satta? Sammukhāvinayo dātabbo, sativinayo dātabbo, amūḷhavinayo dātabbo, paṭiññātakaraṇaṃ dātabbaṃ, yebhuyyasikā dātabbā, tassapāpiyasikā dātabbā, tiṇavatthārako dātabbo. Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta adhikaraṇasamathā dhammā uppannuppannānaṃ adhikaraṇānaṃ samathāya vūpasamāyā” ti.**  
> “Mendicants, there are these seven principles for the settlement of any disciplinary issues that might arise. What seven? Resolution face-to-face to be applied, resolution through recollection to be granted, resolution because of past insanity to be granted, effecting of acknowledgement of an offence to be applied, majority decision to be granted, pronouncement of bad character to be granted, covering over as with grass to be applied. These are the seven principles for the settlement of any disciplinary issues that might arise.”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 7, Adhikaraṇasamatha Sutta, the tenth of the vagga; CST §84) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_07&page_number=507&name=sattakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=ODQu4oCY4oCYIFNhdHRpbWUsIGJoaWtraGF2ZSwgYWRoaWthcmHhuYdhc2FtYXRoxIEgZGhhbW3EgSB1cHBhbm51cHBhbm7EgW5h4bmD)

### Quote
> **Ime kho panāyasmanto satta adhikaraṇasamathā 655. Uppannuppannānaṃ adhikaraṇānaṃ samathāya vūpasamāya sammukhāvinayo dātabbo, sativinayo dātabbo, amūḷhavinayo dātabbo, paṭiññāya kāretabbaṃ, yebhuyyasikā, tassapāpiyasikā, tiṇavatthārakoti.**  
> These, venerable sirs, are the seven rules for the settlement of disputes For the settling and pacifying of disputes that arise from time to time: a verdict in the presence of should be given, a verdict based on past conduct should be given, a verdict of past sanity should be given, it should be done by admission, a decision by the majority, a decision of specific depravity, and covering over as with grass.  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Bhikkhupātimokkha, Adhikaraṇasamathā, Pācittiyapāḷi) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_02&page_number=272&name=p%C4%81cittiyap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=SW1lIGtobyBwYW7EgXlhc21hbnRvIHNhdHRhIGFkaGlrYXJh4bmHYXNhbWF0aMSB)

### Quote
> **Adhikaraṇāni samenti vūpasamentīti adhikaraṇasamathā . Adhikaraṇānanti vivādādhikaraṇaṃ anuvādādhikaraṇaṃ āpattādhikaraṇaṃ kiccādhikaraṇanti imesaṃ catunnaṃ.**  
> They are called ‘settlements of legal issues’ because they settle or appease legal issues. ‘Legal issues’ refers to these four: litigation because of disputes, litigation because of an accusation, litigation because of an offense, and litigation because of a business matter.  
>  
> — Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī (Pātimokkha-aṭṭhakathā), Adhikaraṇasamathavaṇṇanā · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_vi_01&page_number=289&name=dvem%C4%81tik%C4%81+ka%E1%B9%85kh%C4%81vitara%E1%B9%87%C4%AB&note=&selected_text=QWRoaWthcmHhuYfEgW5pIHNhbWVudGkgdsWrcGFzYW1lbnTEq3RpIGFkaGlrYXJh4bmHYXNhbWF0aMSBIC4%3D)

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Adhikaraṇasamatha Sutta* (AN 7; heading "10. Adhikaraṇasamathasuttaṃ", A-vii 726:1) – the seven in the Buddha's own words (A-vii 727:1-4).
* **[Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: *Bhikkhupātimokkha* (Vin-ii 2153:1-2156:1, "8. Adhikaraṇasamathā") – the seven recited at the end of the Pātimokkha; the bhikkhunī version at Vin-ii-b 3682:1.
* **[Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: *[Cūḷavagga](/glossary/culavagga/)* (Vin-iv 951:1, "4. Samathakkhandhakaṃ"; it begins with "1. Sammukhāvinayo", Vin-iv 952:1) – the detailed procedure for each of the seven.
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Sāmagāma Sutta* (MN 104; heading "4. Sāmagāmasuttaṃ", M-iii 83:1) – "Satta kho panime, ānanda, adhikaraṇasamathā …" (M-iii 90:4).
* **Commentary**: *Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī* (Kkh 2145:1-2156:1) – the Pātimokkha commentary on the seven; and the *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* on the Cūḷavagga, whose Samathakkhandhakavaṇṇanā closes at Sp-iv 225:1.
