### Grammatical Analysis
**Paramatthajotikā**: [f.] The Illuminator of Ultimate Meaning. Formed by *paramattha* (ultimate reality/meaning) + *jotikā* (illuminating, shedding light).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Paramatthajotikā* is the title given to two commentaries on texts of the Khuddaka Nikāya. Both closing colophons name the author as the Elder Buddhaghosa of the Mahāvihāra (Pj-i 1229:1; Pj-ii 2684:1).

1. **Paramatthajotikā I** (Pj-i): "the explanation of the Khuddakapāṭha" (*khuddakapāṭhavaṇṇanā*, Pj-i 1229:1). It analyses the word *buddho* and the going for refuge at length (e.g. Pj-i 55:1, 59:3, 73:4).
2. **Paramatthajotikā II** (Pj-ii): the commentary on the *[Suttanipāta](/glossary/suttanipata/)*, "the explanation of the meaning of the Suttanipāta, which is comprised of five chapters such as the Uraga Vagga and divided into seventy suttas" (Pj-ii 2678:1). Its closing colophon calls it "this Paramatthajotikā, the Suttanipāta commentary" (*paramatthajotikā nāma suttanipāta-aṭṭhakathā*, Pj-ii 2684:1); an earlier closing line reads "Iti paramatthajotikāya khuddaka-aṭṭhakathāya" (Pj-ii 2668:1).

### Quote
> **Paramavisuddhasaddhābuddhivīriyaguṇappaṭimaṇḍitena sīlācārajjavamaddavādiguṇasamudayasamuditena sakasamayasamayantaragahanajjhogāhaṇasamatthena paññāveyyattiyasamannāgatena tipiṭakapariyattidhammappabhede sāṭṭhakathe satthusāsane appaṭihatañāṇappabhāvena chamahāveyyākaraṇenachamahāveyyākaraṇena karaṇasampattijanitasukhaviniggatamadhurodāravacanalāvaṇṇayuttena yuttamuttavādinā vādīvarena mahākavinā chaḷabhiññāpaṭisambhidādippabhedaguṇappaṭimaṇḍite uttarimanussadhamme suppatiṭṭhitabuddhīnaṃ theravaṃsappadīpānaṃ therānaṃ mahāvihāravāsīnaṃ vaṃsālaṅkārabhūtena vipulavisuddhabuddhinā buddhaghosoti garūhi gahitanāmadheyyena therena katā ayaṃ paramatthajotikā nāma khuddakapāṭhavaṇṇanā –**  
> This paramatthajotikā (Paramatthajotikā), which is the explanation of the Khuddakapāṭha, was composed by the Elder known by the name buddhaghoso (Buddhaghosa)—given by the teachers, whose intellect is widely purified, who is an ornament to the lineage of the Great Monastery residents (Mahāvihāravāsī), an Elder who is a lamp for the lineage of the Elders, whose intellect is firmly established in the supramundane states, who is adorned with virtues such as the six direct knowledges and the four analytical knowledges, who is a great poet, a master of debate, who speaks suitable and well-articulated words, who is endowed with the beauty of sweet and noble speech that flows easily from the perfection of his practice, who is a great grammarian, whose knowledge is unobstructed in the Teacher’s message which consists of the three baskets of the scriptures along with their commentaries, who is endowed with skilled wisdom, who is capable of delving into one’s own tradition and the traditions of others, who is moved by the collection of virtues such as morality, conduct, uprightness, and gentleness, and who is adorned with the qualities of perfectly pure faith, wisdom, and energy.  
>
