### Grammatical Analysis
**Āyusaṅkhāra**: [m.] vital formation; life-span; the karmic energy sustaining life. Formed by *āyu* (life, lifespan, age) + *saṅkhāra* (formation, constructed energy). 

### Orthodox Definition
*Āyusaṅkhāra* is the "life-process" (also rendered "vital formations") that sustains a being's life. The term appears most famously in the *[Mahāparinibbāna](/glossary/mahaparinibbana/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)*, in the section headed *Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana* ("Relinquishing the Life-Process"). At the Cāpāla shrine the Buddha, mindful and clearly comprehending, relinquished it (*sato sampajāno āyusaṅkhāraṃ ossaji*), and there was a great earthquake (D-ii 422). He had announced that the Tathāgata would attain final Nibbāna "after three months" (D-ii 420:3).

The Sumaṅgalavilāsinī explains that the Blessed One did not let go of the life-process like dropping a clod from the hand. Rather, he entered an attainment (*samāpatti*) for three months, and then formed the thought "I will not enter it after this"; this is what "he relinquished" refers to (Sv-ii 569:1-2).

### Quote
> **Atha kho bhagavā cāpāle cetiye sato sampajāno āyusaṅkhāraṃ ossaji. Ossaṭṭhe ca bhagavatā āyusaṅkhāre mahābhūmicālo ahosi bhiṃsanako salomahaṃso, devadundubhiyo ca phaliṃsu.**  
> 169. Then at the Cāpāla shrine the Gracious One, mindfully, with full awareness, relinquished the life-process. With the relinquishment of the life-process by the Gracious One there was a great earthquake, and a fearful, horrifying crash of the Divinities’ thunder drum.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 16, Mahāparinibbāna Sutta, Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_02&page_number=89&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTY5LiBBdGhhIGtobyBiaGFnYXbEgSBjxIFwxIFsZSBjZXRpeWU%3D)

### Textual References
* **Sutta**: *Mahāparinibbāna Sutta* (DN 16, D-ii 421-422) – The Tathāgata relinquishes the life-process at the Cāpāla shrine; the same event is repeated at S-v 1585:5 and A-viii 859:1.
* **Commentary**: *[Sumaṅgalavilāsinī](/glossary/sumangalavilasini/)* (Sv-ii 569:1-2) – Explains that he entered an attainment for three months and then resolved not to enter it again.
