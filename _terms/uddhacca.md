### Grammatical Analysis
**Uddhacca**: [nt.] Restlessness; agitation; distraction; mental flurry. The commentaries explain it as *uddhatassa bhāvo uddhaccaṃ*, the state of one who is agitated (As 1780:3; Vism-ii 466:1; Nidd-a-i 221:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Uddhacca* operates in Theravāda doctrine on two levels: as a hindrance (*[nīvaraṇa](/glossary/nivarana/)*, paired with kukkucca as *uddhaccakukkucca*, A-ix 444:3) and as a fetter (*[saṃyojana](/glossary/samyojana/)*), one of the five higher fetters (*pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni*): *rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṃ, avijjā* (A-ix 462:3). In the Sutta-list of ten fetters it is the ninth (Abhidh-s 615:1).

The Abhidhamma defines it: *yaṃ cittassa uddhaccaṃ avūpasamo cetaso vikkhepo bhantattaṃ cittassa*, "the mind's restlessness, lack of calming, scattered mind, agitation of the mind" (Dhs 1950:2). It is one of the four unwholesome cetasikas present in all unwholesome consciousness (*akusalasādhāraṇā cattāro*: moha, ahirika, anottappa, uddhacca; Abhidh-s 88:1, 165:1).

The commentary gives its characteristic: the mind's non-calm, like water struck by the wind; function, instability, like a flag struck by the wind; manifestation, bewilderment, like ashes scattered by a blow of a stone; proximate cause, unwise attention to the non-calm of mind; it should be seen as distraction of mind (As 1780:3-5; Vism-ii 466:2; Nidd-a-i 389:7 "restlessness is characterized by lack of peace").

As a fetter it is among the last to be abandoned: the arahatta-magga abandons lust for form, lust for the formless, conceit, restlessness and ignorance (*arahattamaggena … uddhaccaṃ … pahīyanti*, Paṭis 1376:8), and the Aṭṭhakathā lists these five as the higher fetters (Vism-ii 1948:3).

### Quote (Mūla: Uddhambhāgiya Sutta)
> **“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, uddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. Katamāni pañca? Rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṃ, avijjā – imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni.**  
> “Mendicants, there are five higher fetters. What five? Lust for form, lust for the formless, conceit, restlessness, and ignorance—these indeed are the five higher fetters.  
>

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamaṃ uddhaccaṃ? Yaṃ cittassa uddhaccaṃ avūpasamo cetaso vikkhepo bhantattaṃ cittassa – idaṃ vuccati uddhaccaṃ.**  
> 1165. Which is restlessness? Whatever is the mind's restlessness, lack of calming, a scattered mind, the agitation of the mind — this is called restlessness (uddhaccaṃ).  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs 1165, Uddhacca) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=233&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTE2NS4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtYeG5gyB1ZGRoYWNjYeG5gz8%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Itaraṃ pana uddhatassa bhāvo ‘ uddhaccaṃ ’. Taṃ cetaso avūpasamalakkhaṇaṃ vātābhighātacalajalaṃ viya, anavaṭṭhānarasaṃ vātābhighātacaladhajapaṭākā viya, bhantattapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ pāsāṇābhighātasamuddhatabhasmā viya, cetaso avūpasame ayonisomanasikārapadaṭṭhānaṃ. Cittavikkhepoti daṭṭhabbaṃ.**  
> But (pana) another (factor) (itaraṃ), the state (bhāva) of one given to restlessness (uddhatassa), is ‘restlessness’ (uddhaccaṃ). That (restlessness (taṃ)) is characterized by the mind’s (cetaso) agitation (avūpasama), like wind-struck (vātābhighāta) moving water (calajalaṃ viya); it has the essence of instability (anavaṭṭhāna), like wind-struck (vātābhighāta) moving flags and banners (caladhajapaṭākā viya); it manifests as bewilderment (bhantatta), like stone-struck (pāsāṇābhighāta) scattered ashes (samuddhatabhasmā viya); and it has unwise attention (ayoniso manasikāra) as its proximate cause (padaṭṭhāna) for mental agitation (cetaso avūpasame). It should be understood (daṭṭhabbaṃ) as the distraction of mind (cittavikkhepa).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1780:3-5, Uddhacca) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=292&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=SXRhcmHhuYMgcGFuYSB1ZGRoYXRhc3NhIGJoxIF2byDigJggdWRkaGFjY2HhuYM%3D)
