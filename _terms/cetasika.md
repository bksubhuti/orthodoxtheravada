### Grammatical Analysis
**Cetasika**: [nt.; adj.] mental factor; mental concomitant; associated state. Formed from *cetas* (mind) + suffix *ika* (belonging to). The commentaries give the derivation: "*cetasi bhavā cetasikā, cittasampayuttāti attho*" ("they exist in the mind; the meaning is, associated with consciousness", Paṭis-a 1154:11), and the Mūlaṭīkā: "*cetasi niyuttā, cetasā saṃsaṭṭhā vā cetasikā*" ("engaged in the mind, or associated with the mind", As-mt 150:3). The Aṭṭhasālinī says it is a *cetasika* "because it depends on the mind" (*cittanissitattā cetasikaṃ*, As 1296:9).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Dhammasaṅgaṇī's pairs of dhammas the Mūla has *cetasikā dhammā* ("mental factors are mental phenomena") alongside *acetasikā dhammā* (Dhs 205:1 and 206:1), followed by *cittasampayuttā dhammā* ("associated with consciousness", Dhs 207:1).

The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha lists the topics of the Abhidhamma as fourfold from the standpoint of ultimate reality (*paramatthato*, Abhidh-s 7:1): *cittaṃ cetasikaṃ rūpaṃ nibbānaṃ* (Abhidh-s 8:1). Its second chapter, the Cetasikaparicchedo (Abhidh-s 79), opens with the characteristics of association: "they arise and cease together, and they have the same object and base" (*ekuppādanirodhā ca, ekālambaṇavatthukā*), and "these fifty-two mental phenomena associated with the mind are known as mental factors" (*cetoyuttā dvipaññāsa, dhammā cetasikā matā*, Abhidh-s 81:1, 82:1).

The fifty-two are divided into three groups. The *aññasamāna* are thirteen: the seven universals (contact, feeling, perception, volition, one-pointedness, life faculty, attention; Abhidh-s 84:2) and the six occasionals (applied thought, sustained thought, decision, energy, zest, zeal; Abhidh-s 85:1), "these thirteen mental factors should be understood as ethically variable" (Abhidh-s 86:1). The unwholesome are fourteen (Abhidh-s 88:1: delusion, shamelessness, lack of fear of wrongdoing, restlessness, greed, view, conceit, hatred, envy, avarice, regret, sloth, torpor, doubt). The beautiful (*sobhana*) are twenty-five (Abhidh-s 90:1, 91:1, 92:1).

### Quote (Mūla: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **(Ka) cetasikā dhammā. (Kha) acetasikā dhammā. 58.(Ka) cittasampayuttā dhammā.**  
> 57.(Ka) Mental factors are mental phenomena. (Kha) Not mental factors are mental phenomena. 58.(Ka) Mental phenomena associated with consciousness.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs, Mahantaradukaṃ, para 57-58) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=9&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NTcuKEthKSBjZXRhc2lrxIEgZGhhbW3EgS4%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Cetasikāti cetasi bhavā cetasikā, cittasampayuttāti attho.**  
> “Mental” means existing in the mind; mental dhammas are those conjoined with mind; this is the meaning.  
>  
> — Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā (Saddhammappakāsinī, Paṭis-a 1154) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_18_01&page_number=270&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=Q2V0YXNpa8SBdGkgY2V0YXNpIGJoYXbEgSBjZXRhc2lrxIEsIGNpdHRhc2FtcGF5dXR0xIF0aSBhdHRoby4%3D)
