### Grammatical Analysis
**Sammāvācā**: [f.] Right Speech; Correct Verbal Conduct. Formed by *sammā* (correctly) + *vācā* (speech, talk, word).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sammāvācā* is the third limb of the Noble Eightfold Path and the first component of the moral discipline training division (*[sīla](/glossary/sila/)-sikkhā*). Doctrinally, it is the mental factor of abstinence from wrong speech (*[virati](/glossary/virati/)*, abstinence from the four *vacīduccaritas*).

The training requires a practitioner to systematically abstain from four distinct modes of harmful verbal action:
1. *Musāvādā*: Abstaining from deliberate lying or deceitful statements.
2. *Pisuṇāya vācāya*: Abstaining from malicious, divisive, or backbiting speech designed to fracture friendships.
3. *Pharusāya vācāya*: Abstaining from harsh, abrasive, abusive, or insulting language.
4. *Samphappalāpā*: Abstaining from frivolous chatter, useless gossip, or loose talk that scatters mental concentration.

The Aṭṭhakathā gives its specific characteristic as *sammāpariggahaṇa* ("taking up correctly"; It-a 637, *sammāpariggahaṇalakkhaṇā sammāvācā*). The Abhidhammic definition (Dhs §299, Vibh) calls it abstinence (*virati*) from the four verbal misconducts, a path factor "cutting the bridge" (*setughāto*). The Aṭṭhakathā explains that this abstinence belongs to the supramundane path as the *virati* that uproots the conditions of verbal misconduct, but the same abstinence, called *sammāvācā*, is also found in the preliminary stage in various minds, since one mind abstains from false speech and another from malicious speech and the rest (As 1661).

### Quote
> **“Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāvācā? Musāvādā veramaṇī pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇī pharusāya vācāya veramaṇī samphappalāpā veramaṇī, ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāvācā.**  
> “Now what, monks, is right speech? Refraining from false speech, refraining from malicious speech, refraining from rough speech, refraining from frivolous talk. This, monks, is called right speech.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 22, Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_02&page_number=250&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIEthdGFtxIEgY2EsIGJoaWtraGF2ZSwgc2FtbcSBdsSBY8SBPw%3D%3D)

> **Katamā tasmiṃ samaye sammāvācā hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye catūhi vacīduccaritehi ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī akiriyā akaraṇaṃ anajjhāpatti velāanatikkamo setughāto sammāvācā maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāvācā hoti.**  
> 299. What is right speech at that time? Whatever refraining, refraining, refraining, abstention, inoperative one, No doing, non-transgression, non-transgression of limits, cutting off of the bridge from the four verbal misconducts, being right speech, being a path factor, included in the path exists at that time—this is right speech at that time.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (§299, Abhidhamma) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=76&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Mjk5LiBLYXRhbcSBIHRhc21p4bmDIHNhbWF5ZSBzYW1txIF2xIFjxIEgaG90aT8%3D)

> **“Sammādassanalakkhaṇā sammādiṭṭhi, sammāabhiniropanalakkhaṇo sammāsaṅkappo, sammāpariggahaṇalakkhaṇā sammāvācā, sammāsamuṭṭhāpanalakkhaṇo sammākammanto, sammāvodānalakkhaṇo sammāājīvo, sammāpaggahalakkhaṇo sammāvāyāmo, sammāupaṭṭhānalakkhaṇā sammāsati, sammāavikkhepalakkhaṇo sammāsamādhī” ti evaṃ visesalakkhaṇato.**  
> ‘Right view has the characteristic of seeing correctly; right intention has the characteristic of applying the mind correctly; right speech has the characteristic of taking up correctly; right action has the characteristic of establishing correctly; right livelihood has the characteristic of purifying correctly; right effort has the characteristic of exerting correctly; right mindfulness has the characteristic of establishing correctly; right concentration has the characteristic of non-distraction;’ thus, by the specific characteristic (visesalakkhaṇato).  
>  
> — Itivuttaka-aṭṭhakathā (Aṭṭhakathā, on the eightfold path) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_04&page_number=125&name=itivuttakaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFNhbW3EgWRhc3NhbmFsYWtraGHhuYfEgSBzYW1txIFkaeG5reG5rWhpLCBzYW1txIFhYmhpbmlyb3BhbmFsYWtraGHhuYdvIHNhbW3EgXNh4bmFa2FwcG8sIHNhbW3EgXBhcmlnZ2FoYeG5h2FsYWtraGHhuYfEgQ%3D%3D)

> **Setussa ghāto setughāto. Vacīduccaritapaccayasamugghāṭikāya viratiyā etaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Ayaṃ pana sammāvācāsaṅkhātā virati pubbabhāge nānācittesu labbhati. Aññeneva hi cittena musāvādā viramati, aññena pesuññādīhi.**  
> The destruction (ghāto) of the bridge (setussa) is bridge-breaking (setughāto). This (etaṃ) is a designation (adhivacanaṃ) for the abstinence (viratiyā) that completely uproots (samugghāṭikā) the conditions (paccaya) for verbal misconduct (vacīduccarita). But this abstinence (ayaṃ virati) called right speech (sammāvācāsaṅkhātā) is obtained (labbhati) in various minds (nānācittesu) in the preliminary phase (pubbabhāge). Indeed (hi), by one way (aññeneva) of mind (cittena), one abstains (viramati) from false speech (musāvādā), and another (aññena) (mind) from malicious speech (pesuñña) and so on (ādīhi).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (Aṭṭhakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=264&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U2V0dXNzYSBnaMSBdG8gc2V0dWdoxIF0by4%3D)

> **Tathā passato vitakkayato ca taṃsampayuttāva vacīduccaritasamugghātikā micchāvācāya virati sammāvācā nāma.**  
> For one who thus sees and thinks, the abstinence from wrong speech associated with that, which uproots verbal misconduct, is called right speech (sammāvācā).  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVI, Magganiddesakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=141&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=VGF0aMSBIHBhc3NhdG8gdml0YWtrYXlhdG8gY2EgdGHhuYNzYW1wYXl1dHTEgXZhIHZhY8SrZHVjY2FyaXRhc2FtdWdnaMSBdGlrxIE%3D)

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Abhayarājakumāra Sutta* (MN 58, M-ii 248-260) – The Buddha sorts speech by whether it is true and accurate (*bhūtaṃ tacchaṃ*), connected with the goal (*atthasaṃhitaṃ*), and pleasant or unpleasant to others; he speaks only what is true and beneficial, and knows the right time (*kālaññū*) for it (M-ii 252:8-13).
* **[Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/)**: *[Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)* §299 (Dhs 781), and *Vibhaṅga*, Maggavibhaṅga (Vibh 744) and Vibh 760 (abstinence factors).
* **Commentary**: *[Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/)* (As 1661); *Itivuttaka-aṭṭhakathā* (It-a 637, the characteristics of the eight path factors); *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* XVI (Vism-ii 786, right speech as the abstinence that uproots verbal misconduct).
