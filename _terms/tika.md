### Grammatical Analysis
**Ṭīkā**: [f.] Sub-commentary: a commentary (*vaṇṇanā*) on an *aṭṭhakathā*. The Ṭīkā-authors use the word in this sense of their own works: "Ṭīkā katā aṭṭhakathāya yena" ("by whom a Ṭīkā was made on the commentary", Vin-vn-t 31:1), and the closing verses of the Sāratthadīpanī call their work "vaṇṇanā yā samāraddhā vinayaṭṭhakathāya", the explanation undertaken on the Vinaya commentary (Sp-t-v 2380:1).

### Orthodox Definition
If the *[Aṭṭhakathā](/glossary/atthakatha/)* explains the [Tipiṭaka](/glossary/tipitaka/), the *Ṭīkā* explains the *[Aṭṭhakathā](/glossary/atthakatha/)*. There is no Mūla passage for the word in this sense, since the Ṭīkās are later works of the commentarial tradition; their own verses state their purpose. The Sāratthadīpanī, the Ṭīkā on the Vinaya commentary Samantapāsādikā, says it was written "for skill in the Vinaya and for the growth of the teaching" (*vinaye pāṭavatthāya, sāsanassa ca vuḍḍhiyā*, Sp-t-v 2379:1), at the request of King Parakkamabāhu (Sp-t-v 2383:1).

The Ṭīkās are scholarly works, often citing earlier Ṭīkā-authors by name and disagreeing with them. The Sīlakkhandhavagga-abhinavaṭīkā, for example, quotes "the Elder Ānanda" as "Abhidhammaṭīkākārena", the Abhidhamma Ṭīkā-author (Sv-nt-ii 287:1), and sets his explanation beside that of the Elder Dhammapāla (Sv-nt-ii 299:28). Together with the *[Aṭṭhakathā](/glossary/atthakatha/)* they are the layer through which the orthodox Theravāda tradition reads the [Tipiṭaka](/glossary/tipitaka/) and the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/).

Notable authors named in the texts themselves: Ācariya Ānanda (his explanations are cited from the Vibhaṅga-mūlaṭīkā, "vibha. mūlaṭī.", at Sv-nt-ii 244:6 and 281:5) and Ācariya Dhammapāla of the Badaratittha monastery, whose colophon names his *Paramatthamañjūsā* as the great Ṭīkā on the *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Vism-mht-ii 1849-1850).

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Sāratthadīpanī)
> **Vinaye pāṭavatthāya, sāsanassa ca vuḍḍhiyā; Vaṇṇanā yā samāraddhā, vinayaṭṭhakathāya sā. Sāratthadīpanī nāma, sabbaso pariniṭṭhitā; Tiṃsasahassamattehi, ganthehi parimāṇato.**  
> For skill in the discipline, and for the growth of the teaching; That explanation which was undertaken, of the Vinaya commentary. The Sāratthadīpanī by name, is completely finished in all respects; In the amount of thirty thousand, in measure by text-units.  
>

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Paramatthamañjūsā)
> **Badaratitthavihāravāsinā ācariyadhammapālena katā Paramatthamañjūsā nāma visuddhimaggamahāṭīkā samattā.**  
> Made by the Acariya Dhammapāla, resident of the Badaratittha Monastery. The Great Commentary on the Path of Purification, named the Paramatthamañjūsā, is finished.  
>
