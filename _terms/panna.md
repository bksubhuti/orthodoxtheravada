### Grammatical Analysis
**Paññā**: [f.] wisdom; spiritual understanding; non-delusion; discernment. Formed by the prefix *pa* (intensely, thoroughly) + root *ñā* (to know). Literally means "knowing thoroughly or penetratively."

### Orthodox Definition
In the Theravāda [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), *paññā* is classified as the mental factor of non-delusion (*[amoha](/glossary/amoha/)-cetasika*). It is the faculty that understands the true nature of ultimate phenomena (*paramattha-[dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/)*), penetrating through the conceptual overlays (*paññatti*) of the world to directly perceive the characteristics of impermanence, suffering, and non-self.

The *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* contrasts *paññā* with perception (*[saññā](/glossary/sanna/)*) and consciousness (*[viññāṇa](/glossary/vinnana/)*): all three know, but *paññā* is "knowing in various ways, distinguished from the modes of perceiving and cognizing" (*sañjānanavijānanākāravisiṭṭhaṃ nānappakārato jānanaṃ*, Vism-ii 279:3). *Saññā* only apprehends the object as blue, yellow and so on, like a child seeing a coin; *viññāṇa* also reaches the penetration of characteristics, like a villager seeing it; *paññā* goes further and brings about the manifestation of the path, like a money-changer (*heraññika*) seeing it (Vism-ii 281:1-3).

The texts (D-iii 1385:1; Vibh 2153:1) list three kinds of *paññā*:
1. *Cintāmayā paññā*: Wisdom consisting in reflection.
2. *Sutamayā paññā*: Wisdom consisting in hearing, acquired from another (Vibh 2350:2).
3. *Bhāvanāmayā paññā*: Wisdom consisting in development, i.e. through meditation (*[vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/)*).

### Quote
> **Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye paññindriyaṃ hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye paññā pajānanā vicayo pavicayo dhammavicayo sallakkhaṇā upalakkhaṇā paccupalakkhaṇā paṇḍiccaṃ kosallaṃ nepuññaṃ vebhabyā cintā upaparikkhā bhūrī medhā pariṇāyikā vipassanā sampajaññaṃ patodo paññā paññindriyaṃ paññābalaṃ paññāsatthaṃ paññāpāsādo paññāāloko paññāobhāso paññāpajjoto paññāratanaṃ amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye paññindriyaṃ hoti.**  
> 16. What is the faculty of wisdom at that time? Whatever wisdom, understanding, investigation, thorough investigation, analysis of phenomena, discernment, observation, scrutiny, sagacity, skill, cleverness, elucidation, thought, scrutiny, breadth, wit, guidance, insight, awareness, goad, wisdom, faculty of wisdom, the power of wisdom, weapon of wisdom, palace of wisdom, light of wisdom, illumination of wisdom, brightness of wisdom, jewel of wisdom, non-delusion, analysis of phenomena, or right view exists at that time—this is the faculty of wisdom at that time.  
>
