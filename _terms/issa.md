### Grammatical Analysis
**Issā**: [f.] envy; jealousy. The Aṭṭhasālinī: "it is called *issā* because it envies (*issatīti issā*)" (As 1815:4); the Visuddhimagga: "*issāyanā issā*", the act of envying is envy (Vism-ii 473:1).

### Orthodox Definition
The Mūla defines it by its object: "whatever envy (*issā*), envying, state of having envied, jealousy (*usūyā*), act of being jealous, state of having been jealous, regarding the gain, honour, respect, reverence, salutation and worship paid to others: this is said to be *issā*" (Pp 261:3; Vibh 2651:2). In the Dhammasaṅgaṇī the same words, regarding what is given to others, define the envy fetter (*issāsaṃyojana*, Dhs 1905:2).

The Aṭṭhasālinī and the Visuddhimagga give the characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause: *issā* has the characteristic of grudging others' success (*parasampattīnaṃ usūyana-lakkhaṇā*); its function is aversion to just that success (*tattheva anabhirati-rasā*); it is manifested as turning away from it (*vimukhabhāva-paccupaṭṭhānā*); its proximate cause is others' success (*parasampatti-padaṭṭhānā*); and it should be seen as a fetter (*saṃyojananti daṭṭhabbā*) (As 1815:4-6; Vism-ii 473:1-3).

The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha says that envy, with hatred, avarice and regret, is one of the factors that enter the unprompted consciousness associated with resentment (*paṭighasampayutta*), and that envy, avarice and regret should be combined separately, one at a time (Abhidh-s 169:1); it is one of the fourteen unwholesome mental factors (Abhidh-s 88:1). The Papañcasūdanī says that "disparagement (*makkha*), contempt (*paḷāsa*), envy (*issā*), avarice (*macchariya*), deceit (*māyā*) and fraudulence (*sāṭheyya*): these six are abandoned by the path of stream-entry" (Ps-i 855:7).

### Quote (Mūla: Puggalapaññatti)
> **Tattha katamā issā? Yā paralābhasakkāragarukāramānanavandanapūjanāsu issā issāyanā issāyitattaṃ usūyā usūyanā usūyitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati issā.**  
> What then is envy? That which is envying, enviousness, jealousy, regarding with jealousy, the state of being jealous with regard to the gain, honour, respect, reverence, salutation and worship paid to others—this is said to be envy.  
>  
> — [Puggalapaññatti](/glossary/puggalapannatti/) (Pp 261, "69. Katamo ca puggalo anissukī?") · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_04&page_number=126&name=puggalapa%C3%B1%C3%B1attip%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGthdGFtxIEgaXNzxIE%2F)


### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamaṃ issāsaṃyojanaṃ? Yā paralābhasakkāragarukāramānanavandanapūjanāsu issā issāyanā issāyitattaṃ usūyā usūyanā usūyitattaṃ– idaṃ vuccati issāsaṃyojanaṃ.**  
> 1126. Which among these is the fetter of jealousy? Whatever envy, act of envying, state of having envied, detraction, act of detracting, state of having detracted there is regarding the gains, respect, deference, veneration, salutation, and adoration of others — this is called the fetter of jealousy.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs §1126, Issāsaṃyojana) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=226&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTEyNi4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtYeG5gyBpc3PEgXNh4bmDeW9qYW5h4bmDPw%3D%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Aṭṭhasālinī)
> **Tesu issatīti ‘ issā ’. Sā parasampattīnaṃ usūyanalakkhaṇā, tattheva anabhiratirasā, tato vimukhabhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, parasampattipadaṭṭhānā. Saṃyojananti daṭṭhabbā.**  
> Among them (tesu), it is called ‘envy’ (issā) because it grudges (issatīti). That (sā) (envy) has the characteristic of grudging others’ success (parasampattīnaṃ usūyanalakkhaṇā), its function is aversion (anabhiratirasā) to those very things, its manifestation is turning away (vimukhabhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā) from that (tato), and its proximate cause is others’ success (parasampattipadaṭṭhānā). It should be understood as a fetter (saṃyojananti daṭṭhabbā).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1815) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=299&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGVzdSBpc3NhdMSrdGkg4oCYIGlzc8SBIOKAmS4%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Issāyanā issā. Sā parasampattīnaṃ usūyanalakkhaṇā. Tattheva anabhiratirasā, tato vimukhabhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, parasampattipadaṭṭhānā, saṃyojananti daṭṭhabbā.**  
> 486. The act of envying is envy (issā). It has the characteristic of being envious of the success of others. It has the function of not being pleased with that very success, is manifested as turning away from it, has the success of others as its proximate cause, and should be seen as a fetter.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Vism-ii 473, para 486) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=100&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NDg2LiBJc3PEgXlhbsSBIGlzc8SBLg%3D%3D)
