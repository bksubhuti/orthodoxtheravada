### Grammatical Analysis
**Bhavaṅga**: [nt.] life-continuum, a compound of *bhava* (existence) + *aṅga* (factor, cause). The word is used in the Mūla in the Paṭṭhāna: "*bhavaṅgaṃ āvajjanāya anantarapaccayena paccayo*", "the life-continuum is related to adverting by contiguity condition" (Paṭṭh-i 3463:2). The texts consulted give no analysis of the compound itself, so only the senses below are stated.

### Orthodox Definition
The *bhavaṅga* is the consciousness that continues the stream of a being between rebirth-linking and death. The Visuddhimagga: "after death there is rebirth-linking again, after rebirth-linking the life-continuum again; thus the continuous stream of consciousness of beings who wander through existences, destinies, stations of consciousness and abodes of beings proceeds without a break" (Vism-ii 409:1). The Aṭṭhakathā says that when rebirth-linking has passed, the resultant becomes life-continuum for the lifespan, whether sixty or eighty years or countless, and at the time of death becomes decease (As 1844:4, 1849:5). The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha says that the nine sublime resultants perform three functions, rebirth-linking, life-continuum and decease (*paṭisandhibhavaṅgacutivasena*) (Abhidh-s 231:1).

In the cognitive process the life-continuum is the proximate condition for adverting (Paṭṭh-i 3463:2). The Ṭīkā adds that the life-continuum consciousness which is the contiguity condition for adverting is the mind-door, because it is the opening for the arising of the process-consciousnesses (Abhidh-s-t 948:3); the five-door adverting does not let the stream of consciousness continue as life-continuum, but turns it toward becoming process-consciousness (Abhidh-s-t 948:2). The Aṭṭhakathā adds that when an object strikes the sense-base, no one says "you be the life-continuum, you be adverting, you be seeing"; each arises by its own nature (As 1873:1, 1883:1).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Vipāko dhammo nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo. Bhavaṅgaṃ āvajjanāya anantarapaccayena paccayo. Vipākamanoviññāṇadhātu kiriyamanoviññāṇadhātuyā anantarapaccayena paccayo.(2)**  
> A resultant state is related to a state which is neither resultant nor producing resultant states by contiguity condition. The life-continuum (bhavaṅga) is related to adverting by contiguity condition. The resultant mind-consciousness-element is related to the functional mind-consciousness-element by contiguity condition. (2)  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Cutito pana puna paṭisandhi, paṭisandhito puna bhavaṅganti evaṃ bhavagatiṭhitinivāsesu saṃsaramānānaṃ sattānaṃ avicchinnaṃ cittasantānaṃ pavattatiyeva.**  
> After death, there is rebirth-linking again; after rebirth-linking, the life-continuum again. Thus, the continuous stream of consciousness of beings who wander through existences, destinies, stations of consciousness, and abodes of beings, proceeds without break.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Vism-ii 409:1) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=90&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=Q3V0aXRvIHBhbmEgcHVuYSBwYeG5rWlzYW5kaGksIHBh4bmtaXNhbmRoaXRvIHB1bmE%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Paṭisandhiyā vītivattāya pavatte saṭṭhipi asītipi vassāni asaṅkhyeyyampi āyukālaṃ bhavaṅgaṃ hutvā, balavārammaṇe chasu dvāresu tadārammaṇaṃ hutvā, maraṇakāle cuti hutvāti.**  
> When rebirth-linking has elapsed in the course of existence, they become life-continuum for a lifespan of sixty, eighty, or even countless years; they become registration in the six doors when there is a strong object; and they become decease at the hour of death.  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1849) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=307&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=UGHhua1pc2FuZGhpecSBIHbEq3RpdmF0dMSBeWEgcGF2YXR0ZSBzYeG5reG5rWhpcGkgYXPEq3RpcGkgdmFzc8SBbmk%3D)

> **Mahaggatavipākāni nava paṭisandhibhavaṅgacutivasena tikiccāni nāma.**  
> 29. The nine sublime resultants perform three functions: rebirth-linking, life-continuum, and death.  
>

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Cakkhādipañcadvāre ghaṭṭitamārammaṇaṃ āvajjeti tattha ābhogaṃ karoti, cittasantānaṃ vā bhavaṅgavasena pavattituṃ adatvā vīthicittabhāvāya pariṇāmetīti pañcadvārāvajjanaṃ , kiriyāhetukamanodhātucittaṃ. Āvajjanassa anantarapaccayabhūtaṃ bhavaṅgacittaṃ manodvāraṃ vīthicittānaṃ pavattimukhabhāvato.**  
> It adverts to the object that impinges on one of the five doors, such as the eye-door; it performs attention there, or, without allowing the mental continuum to proceed by way of the bhavaṅga, it turns it toward being a process-consciousness; thus it is five-door adverting (pañcadvārāvajjanaṃ), which is the functional rootless mind-element. The bhavaṅga mind that has become the proximity-condition for adverting is the mind-door, because it is the opening for the arising of process-consciousnesses.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)-ṭīkā (Abhidh-s-t 948) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=86&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=Q2Fra2jEgWRpcGHDsWNhZHbEgXJlIGdoYeG5reG5rWl0YW3EgXJhbW1h4bmHYeG5gyDEgXZhampldGkgdGF0dGhhIMSBYmhvZ2HhuYMga2Fyb3RpLA%3D%3D)
