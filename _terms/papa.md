### Grammatical Analysis
**Pāpa**: [nt.; adj.] evil; demerit; sin; wicked deed; bad. The commentaries explain it as "low" or "contemptible" (*lāmakaṭṭhena pāpaṃ*, "in the sense of lowness it is evil"; It-a 756:3, Ps-i 466:5, Sp-i 634:4), paired with *akusala*, "not born of skill" (*akosallasambhūtaṭṭhena akusalaṃ*).

### Orthodox Definition
*Pāpa* refers to demerit or evil deeds performed through body, speech, or mind that degrade the character and inevitably mature into painful karmic results (*[akusala](/glossary/akusala/)-vipāka*). It is the structural counterpart to *[puñña](/glossary/punna/)*.

The Itivuttaka commentary lists its dangers: it is defiling (*saṃkilesika*), leads to renewed existence (*ponobbhavika*), is accompanied by distress and fever (*sadaratha*, *sapariḷāha*), has painful result (*dukkhavipāka*) and leads to future birth, ageing and death (It-a 756:3).

The Buddha warned that practitioners must never treat minor moral lapses lightly, for just as a water jar is filled drop by drop, an unwise person fills themselves with pāpa little by little, ensuring an oppressive, painful journey through the lower realms of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).

### Quote
> **Māvamaññetha pāpassa, na mantaṃ āgamissati; Udabindunipātena, udakumbhopi pūrati; Bālo pūrati pāpassa, thokaṃ thokampi ācinaṃ.**  
> Do not disregard evil, thinking, 'It will not come to me'; by the falling of drops of water, a water-pot is filled; the fool becomes full of evil, accumulating it little by little.  
>  
> — [Dhammapada](/glossary/dhammapada/) (Dhp 121, Pāpavagga) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_02&page_number=31&name=dhammapadap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTIxLiBNxIF2YW1hw7HDsWV0aGEgW23EgXBwYW1hw7HDsWV0aGEgKHPEqy4gc3nEgS4gcMSrLild)
