### Grammatical Analysis
**Satthā**: [m.] Teacher; Master. Derived from root *sās* (to teach, instruct, rule). The commentary explains it as *anusāsatīti satthā*: one who instructs (Vism-i 1276:1; Bv-a 400:9).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Pali Canon, *Satthā* is an epithet of the Buddha as guide. In the standard formula of the Buddha's qualities, one of the qualities is *satthā devamanussānaṃ*, "the Teacher of devas and humans" (A-vi 38:2).

The Visuddhimagga explains it: he instructs beings appropriately by way of benefits in this life, in the next life and the highest benefit (*diṭṭhadhammikasamparāyikaparamatthehi yathārahaṃ anusāsatīti satthā*). Further, he is like a caravan-leader (*satthavāho*) who leads beings across the wilderness, the wilderness of birth and so on. "Of devas and humans" is said by way of the most excellent beings and of those capable of progress, but the Blessed One is a Teacher even to animals, by giving them instruction (Vism-i 1276:1-6).

Shortly before his passing the Buddha said that the Teaching and Discipline he had taught and laid down (*dhammo ca vinayo ca*) would be the Teacher of the monks after his passing (*so vo mamaccayena satthā*, D-ii 586:5). Ānanda likewise tells the brahmin Gopaka Moggallāna that no single bhikkhu was appointed as refuge after the Buddha, and that the monks have the Dhamma as their refuge (*dhammappaṭisaraṇā*, M-iii 168:1-5). See [Dhamma-vinaya](/glossary/dhamma-vinaya/).

### Quote (Mūla: Buddha's qualities)
> **‘ itipi so bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṃ buddho bhagavā ’ ti.**  
> ‘The Blessed One is an Arahant, a perfectly self-awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, sublime, knower of worlds, unsurpassed leader of persons to be tamed, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 6.10, Mahānāma Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_06&page_number=252&name=chakkanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCYIGl0aXBpIHNvIGJoYWdhdsSBIGFyYWhh4bmDIHNhbW3EgXNhbWJ1ZGRobw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāparinibbāna)
> **‘ atītasatthukaṃ pāvacanaṃ, natthi no satthā ’ ti. Na kho panetaṃ, ānanda, evaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ. Yo vo, ānanda, mayā dhammo ca vinayo ca desito paññatto, so vo mamaccayena satthā.**  
> ‘Past is the Teacher’s word, there is now no Teacher for us.’ But it should not be seen like that, Ānanda. Whatever Teaching and Discipline has been taught by me or laid down, Ānanda, that is your Teacher after my passing away.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 16, Mahāparinibbāna Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_02&page_number=126&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCYIGF0xKt0YXNhdHRodWth4bmDIHDEgXZhY2FuYeG5gywgbmF0dGhpIG5vIHNhdHRoxIE%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Diṭṭhadhammikasamparāyikaparamatthehi yathārahaṃ anusāsatīti satthā. Apica satthā viyāti satthā , bhagavā satthavāho. Yathā satthavāho satthe kantāraṃ tāreti corakantāraṃ tāreti vāḷakantāraṃ tāreti dubbhikkhakantāraṃ tāreti nirudakakantāraṃ tāreti uttāreti nittāreti patāreti khemantabhūmiṃ sampāpeti, evameva bhagavā satthā satthavāho satte kantāraṃ tāreti, jātikantāraṃ tāretītiādinā niddesanayenapettha attho veditabbo.**  
> 140. He teaches, as appropriate, in the highest goals of this life and the next; thus he is the Teacher (satthā). Furthermore, he is the Teacher (satthā) because he is like a caravan leader; the Blessed One is a caravan chief. Just as a caravan chief gets his caravan across a wilderness—gets them across a wilderness inhabited by robbers, gets them across a wilderness inhabited by fierce animals, gets them across a famine-wilderness, gets them across a waterless wilderness, gets them across, gets them quite across, gets them right across, gets them properly across, and makes them reach a place of sanctuary—just so, the Blessed One, as the Teacher and caravan chief, gets beings across the wilderness, gets them across the wilderness of birth; in this way, the meaning here should be understood according to the Niddesa exposition.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Vism-i 1276, Buddhānussati) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_01&page_number=202&name=visuddhimaggo+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=MTQwLiBEaeG5reG5rWhhZGhhbW1pa2FzYW1wYXLEgXlpa2FwYXJhbWF0dGhlaGkgeWF0aMSBcmFoYeG5gyBhbnVzxIFzYXTEq3RpIHNhdHRoxIEu)
