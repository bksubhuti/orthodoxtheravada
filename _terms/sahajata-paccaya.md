### Grammatical Analysis
**Sahajātapaccaya**: [m.] Co-nascence condition; arising-together condition. Formed by *saha* (together, with) + *jāta* (born, arisen) + *paccaya* (condition).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sahajāta-paccaya* is the sixth of the 24 Conditions (Paṭṭh-i 33). The Paṭṭhāna's first statement is: "the four formless aggregates are a condition for one another by way of the co-nascence condition". The Aṭṭhakathā defines it as the phenomenon that "is helpful by arising together with what arises" (*uppajjamāno saha uppajjamānabhāvena upakārako*), "like a lamp for light" (*pakāsassa padīpo viya*), and says it is of six kinds (Paṭṭh-a 1924):
* the four formless aggregates for one another;
* the four great elements for one another;
* at the moment of descent into the womb (*okkantikkhaṇe*), name-and-form for one another;
* consciousness and its concomitants for the matter produced by consciousness;
* the great elements for derived matter;
* material states, at some time, for the immaterial ones.

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Sahajātapaccayoti – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññaṃ sahajātapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 6. Co-nascence condition: The four immaterial aggregates are mutually related to one another by co-nascence condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭṭhāna-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Uppajjamāno saha uppajjamānabhāvena upakārako dhammo sahajātapaccayo , pakāsassa padīpo viya. So arūpakkhandhādivasena chabbidho hoti. Yathāha – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññaṃ sahajātapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā aññamaññaṃ … pe … okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññaṃ … pe … cittacetasikā dhammā cittasamuṭṭhānānaṃ rūpānaṃ … pe … mahābhūtā upādārūpānaṃ … pe … rūpino dhammā arūpīnaṃ dhammānaṃ kiñcikāle sahajātapaccayena paccayo;**  
> The phenomenon that is helpful when it arises simultaneously, by way of being simultaneously arisen, is the co-nascence condition, like a lamp for light. That is of six kinds by way of formless aggregates and so on. As it was said: 'The four formless aggregates are a condition for each other by way of co-nascence condition'. The four great elements (are a condition) for each other ... at the moment of descent into the womb, name-and-form (are a condition) for each other ... consciousness and its concomitants are a condition for materiality rooted in thought ... the great elements are a condition for derived materiality ... material phenomena are a condition for non-material phenomena at some time by way of co-nascence condition;  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā (Paṭṭh-a 1924) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=346&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VXBwYWpqYW3EgW5vIHNhaGEgdXBwYWpqYW3EgW5hYmjEgXZlbmEgdXBha8SBcmFrbyBkaGFtbW8gc2FoYWrEgXRhcGFjY2F5bw%3D%3D)
