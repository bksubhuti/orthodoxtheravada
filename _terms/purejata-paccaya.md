### Grammatical Analysis
**Purejātapaccaya**: [m.] Pre-nascence condition; Condition of arising beforehand. Formed by *pure* (before, previously) + *jāta* (born, arisen) + *paccaya* (condition).

### Orthodox Definition
*Purejāta-paccaya* is the tenth of the 24 Conditions (Paṭṭh-i 41). The Aṭṭhakathā defines it: "a phenomenon that helps by having arisen first and by way of being present (*vattamānabhāvena*)", and says it is of eleven kinds in the five doors, by way of base (*vatthu*), object (*ārammaṇa*) and heart-base (*hadayavatthu*) (Vism-ii 932; Paṭṭh-a 1930).

The Paṭṭhānaṭṭhakathā adds that this condition is "pure matter only" (*suddharūpameva*): the eighteen kinds of produced matter that, having passed the moment of arising, have reached the moment of presence. It stands in two ways, as base-pre-nascent (*vatthupurejāta*) and object-pre-nascent (*ārammaṇapurejāta*) (Paṭṭh-a 2018). The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha gives the reason in terms of time: seventeen mind-moments are the life-span of material phenomena (para. 9), so the sense base is already present when the consciousness arises. The eye-base is thus a pre-nascent condition for eye-consciousness and its associated states (Paṭṭh-i 41), and the visible object as well (Vism-ii 932; Paṭṭh-i 1496-1497).


### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Purejātapaccayoti – cakkhāyatanaṃ cakkhuviññāṇadhātuyā taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ purejātapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 10. Pre-nascence condition: Eye sense base is related to eye-consciousness-element and its associated states by pre-nascence condition.

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna, the two kinds)
> **Abyākato dhammo abyākatassa dhammassa purejātapaccayena paccayo – ārammaṇapurejātaṃ, vatthupurejātaṃ.**  
> An indeterminate state is related to an indeterminate state by pre-nascence condition: as object pre-nascence and base pre-nascence.  
>  
> — [Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/) (para. 424) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_07_01&page_number=152&name=pa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADh%C4%81nap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=NDI0LiBBYnnEgWthdG8gZGhhbW1vIGFiecSBa2F0YXNzYSBkaGFtbWFzc2EgcHVyZWrEgXRhcGFjY2F5ZW5h)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭṭhāna-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Ayaṃ pana purejātapaccayo suddharūpameva hoti. Tañca kho uppādakkhaṇaṃ atikkamitvā ṭhitippattaṃ aṭṭhārasavidhaṃ rūparūpameva. Taṃ sabbampi vatthupurejātaṃ ārammaṇapurejātanti dvidhā ṭhitaṃ.**  
> However, this pre-nascence condition is only pure form. But it is only the eighteen kinds of produced form that, having passed the moment of arising, have reached the static moment. All of that stands in two ways: as base-pre-nascence and object-pre-nascence.  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā (Paṭṭh-a 2018) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=371&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QXlh4bmDIHBhbmEgcHVyZWrEgXRhcGFjY2F5byBzdWRkaGFyxatwYW1ldmEgaG90aS4%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga XVII)
> **Paṭhamataraṃ uppajjitvā vattamānabhāvena upakārako dhammo purejātapaccayo. So pañcadvāre vatthārammaṇahadayavatthuvasena ekādasavidho hoti. Yathāha – “cakkhāyatanaṃ cakkhuviññāṇadhātuyā taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ purejātapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 603. A phenomenon that assists by way of present actual manifestation (vattamānabhāvena), having arisen first, is a prenascence condition (purejātapaccayo). In the five doors, it is of eleven kinds by way of basis, object, and heart-basis. As it is said: “The eye sense base (cakkhāyatanaṃ) is a condition for the eye-consciousness element and for the states associated therewith by way of prenascence condition.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, para. 603) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=169&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NjAzLiBQYeG5rWhhbWF0YXJh4bmDIHVwcGFqaml0dsSBIHZhdHRhbcSBbmFiaMSBdmVuYSB1cGFrxIFyYWtvIGRoYW1tbw%3D%3D)

> 9. Seventeen such mind-moments are the lifespan of material phenomena.
