### Grammatical Analysis
**Pārājika**: [adj./nt.] Defeat; involving defeat. The Aṭṭhakathā: "*pārājikoti parājito, parājayaṃ āpanno*", one who is defeated, who has fallen into defeat; the word applies to the training rule, to the offence and to the person (Sp-i 1097).

### Orthodox Definition
*Pārājika* is the gravest class of offence of the Vinaya. The Pātimokkha recital says that a bhikkhu who commits any one of the four "does not obtain communion with the bhikkhus as before; as before, so after, he is *pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso*" (Vin-i 792). The Mūla explains it with a simile: "just as a man whose head is cut off is unable to live by that bodily connection, so a bhikkhu who has had sexual intercourse is no longer an ascetic, no Sakyan son" (Vin-i 93).

There are four *pārājikas* for bhikkhus, in the order of the summary verse "*methunādinnādānañca, manussaviggahuttari*" (Vin-i 795):
1. Sexual intercourse (*methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭiseveyya*, Vin-i 58).
2. Taking what is not given, "the sort of stealing for which kings, having caught a thief, would strike, imprison or banish him" (Vin-i 193).
3. Intentionally depriving a human being of life, seeking an instrument of death for him, praising death or inciting him to die (Vin-i 387).
4. Claiming, without knowing, a superhuman quality (*uttarimanussadhamma*): "*jhānaṃ, vimokkho, samādhi, samāpatti, ñāṇadassanaṃ, maggabhāvanā, phalasacchikiriyā, kilesappahānaṃ, vinīvaraṇatā cittassa, suññāgāre abhirati*" (Vin-i 553, 556), unless it was said through overestimation (*adhimāna*).

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, the four pārājikas recited)
> **Uddiṭṭhā kho āyasmanto cattāro pārājikā dhammā, yesaṃ bhikkhu aññataraṃ vā aññataraṃ vā āpajjitvā na labhati bhikkhūhi saddhiṃ saṃvāsaṃ, yathā pure tathā pacchā, pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso.**  
> Venerable sirs, the four offenses entailing expulsion have been recited. If a monk commits any one of them, he is no longer part of the community of monks; as before, so after, he is expelled and excluded from the community.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, first pārājika)
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭiseveyya , pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso”ti.**  
> “If a monk indulges in sexual intercourse, he is an expulsion-offender, excluded from the community.”  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, the head-cut-off simile)
> **Pārājiko hotīti seyyathāpi nāma puriso sīsacchinno abhabbo tena sarīrabandhanena jīvituṃ, evameva bhikkhu methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevitvā assamaṇo hoti asakyaputtiyo.**  
> “Pārājiko hoti: Just as a man whose head is cut off is unable to continue living by reconnecting it to the body, so too a monk who has had sexual intercourse is not an ascetic, not a Sakyan monk.  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa, 1. ### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, second pārājika)
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu adinnaṃ theyyasaṅkhātaṃ ādiyeyya , yathārūpe adinnādāne rājāno coraṃ gahetvā haneyyuṃ vā bandheyyuṃ vā pabbājeyyuṃ vā – ‘ corosi bālosi mūḷhosi thenosī ’ ti , tathārūpaṃ bhikkhu adinnaṃ ādiyamāno ayampi pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso”ti.**  
> 89. “Whatever monk, whether in a village or in the wilderness, takes what has not been given, the sort of stealing for which kings, having caught a thief, would strike, imprison, or banish him, saying, ‘You’re a bandit, you’re a fool, you’ve gone astray, you’re a thief’— if such a monk takes what has not been given, he too is expelled and excluded from the community.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa, 2. Dutiyapārājikaṃ, para. 89) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_01&page_number=55&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=ODku4oCY4oCYIFlvIHBhbmEgYmhpa2todSBhZGlubmHhuYMgdGhleXlhc2HhuYVraMSBdGHhuYM%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, third pārājika)
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu sañcicca manussaviggahaṃ jīvitā voropeyya satthahārakaṃ vāssa pariyeseyya maraṇavaṇṇaṃ vā saṃvaṇṇeyya maraṇāya vā samādapeyya – ‘ ambho purisa , kiṃ tuyhiminā pāpakena dujjīvitena , mataṃ te jīvitā seyyo ’ ti , iti cittamano cittasaṅkappo anekapariyāyena maraṇavaṇṇaṃ vā saṃvaṇṇeyya , maraṇāya vā samādapeyya , ayampi pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso” ti.**  
> 171. “Whoever, monks, intentionally kills a human being, or seeks an instrument of death for them, or praises death, or incites someone to die— ‘My friend, what is the point of this miserable and difficult life? Death is better for you than life!’—thinking and intending thus, if he praises death in many ways or incites someone to die—he too is expelled and excluded from the community.”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa, 3. Tatiyapārājikaṃ, para. 171) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_01&page_number=92&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTcxLuKAmOKAmCBZbyBwYW5hIGJoaWtraHUgc2HDsWNpY2NhIG1hbnVzc2F2aWdnYWhh4bmD)

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, fourth pārājika)
> **“Yo pana bhikkhu anabhijānaṃ uttarimanussadhammaṃ attupanāyikaṃ alamariyañāṇadassanaṃ samudācareyya – ‘ iti jānāmi iti passāmī ’ ti , tato aparena samayena samanuggāhīyamāno vā asamanuggāhīyamāno vā āpanno visuddhāpekkho evaṃ vadeyya – ‘ ajānamevaṃ , āvuso , avacaṃ jānāmi , apassaṃ passāmi . Tucchaṃ musā vilapin ’ ti , aññatra adhimānā , ayampi pārājiko hoti asaṃvāso” ti .**  
> “Whoever, monks, not knowing, claims a superhuman quality for himself, a knowledge and vision worthy of the noble ones, saying: ‘This I know, this I see,’ but later, whether questioned or not, having committed the offense, seeking purification, should say: ‘Not knowing I said that I know, not seeing that I see. What I said was empty and false,’ except if it is due to overestimation, he too is expelled and excluded from the community.’”  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa, 4. Catutthapārājikaṃ, para. 197) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_01&page_number=117&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTk3LuKAmOKAmCBZbyBwYW5hIGJoaWtraHUgYW5hYmhpasSBbmHhuYMgdXR0YXJpbWFudXNzYWRoYW1tYeG5gw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Vinaya, the superhuman qualities)
> ** Uttarimanussadhammo nāma jhānaṃ vimokkho samādhi samāpatti ñāṇadassanaṃ maggabhāvanā phalasacchikiriyā kilesappahānaṃ vinīvaraṇatā cittassa suññāgāre abhirati.**  
> A superhuman quality is: absorption, release, concentration, attainment, knowledge and vision, cultivation of the path, realization of the fruits, abandoning of defilements, a mind without hindrances, delighting in solitude.  
>  
> — [Vinaya Piṭaka](/glossary/vinaya-pitaka/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa, 4. Catutthapārājikaṃ, padabhājanīya, Vin-i 556) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_01&page_number=117&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=W3DEgWNpLjcwXSBVdHRhcmltYW51c3NhZGhhbW1vIG7EgW1hIGpoxIFuYeG5gyB2aW1va2tobyBbdmltb2traGHhuYM%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā)
> **Pārājikoti parājito, parājayaṃ āpanno. Ayañhi pārājikasaddo sikkhāpadāpattipuggalesu vattati. Tattha “aṭṭhānametaṃ, ānanda, anavakāso yaṃ tathāgato vajjīnaṃ vā vajjiputtakānaṃ vā kāraṇā sāvakānaṃ pārājikaṃ sikkhāpadaṃ paññattaṃ samūhaneyyā” ti (pārā.43) evaṃ sikkhāpade vattamāno veditabbo. “Āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājikan” ti (pārā.67) evaṃ āpattiyaṃ. “Na mayaṃ pārājikā, yo avahaṭo so pārājiko” ti (pārā.155) evaṃ puggale vattamāno veditabbo.**  
> Expelled (pārājiko) means defeated, one who has fallen into defeat. Indeed, this word “pārājika” applies to training rules, offenses, and persons. Among these, it should be understood as applying to a training rule in such passages as: “It is impossible, Ānanda, it cannot happen that the Tathāgata would abolish the pārājika training rule he has laid down for his disciples because of the Vajjians or the sons of the Vajjians.” It applies to an offense in such passages as: “Bhikkhu, you have committed a pārājika offense.” It should be understood as applying to a person in such passages as: “We are not expelled; the one who plundered is the one who is expelled.”  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa-aṭṭhakathā, Sp-i 1097) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_01_01&page_number=224&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikaka%E1%B9%87%E1%B8%8Daa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=UMSBcsSBamlrb3RpIHBhcsSBaml0bywgcGFyxIFqYXlh4bmDIMSBcGFubm8u)
