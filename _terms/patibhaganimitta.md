### Grammatical Analysis
**Paṭibhāganimitta**: [nt.] counterpart sign; conceptualized image; reflex image. Formed by prefix *paṭi* (against, counter, matching) + *bhāga* (portion, part) + *[nimitta](/glossary/nimitta/)* (sign). The Ṭīkā glosses *tappaṭibhāgaṃ* as "the counterpart of that" (the *uggahanimitta*), "free from the faults of the kasiṇa such as colour". The word does not occur in the Mūla Piṭakas (0 sentences in the Mūla); it is found from the Aṭṭhakathā on.

### Orthodox Definition
The *Paṭibhāganimitta* is the third of the three signs (*parikammanimitta*, *uggahanimitta*, *paṭibhāganimitta*) and the object of access and absorption concentration (*upacārappanānaṃ ārammaṇattā*).

As the meditator continues to focus on the flawed learning sign (*[uggahanimitta](/glossary/uggahanimitta/)*), the mind's concentration becomes so intense and purified that the image suddenly shifts. The *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* describes it as the sign appearing "as if breaking out, having pierced through the learning sign", a hundred or a thousand times more purified: in the learning sign the fault of the kasiṇa is apparent, in the counterpart sign it is not. It is likened to a mirror disk drawn from its bag, a well-washed mother-of-pearl dish, or the moon's disk emerging from behind a cloud. It has neither colour nor shape; it is merely the mode of appearance, born of perception (*saññaja*), for one who has attained concentration.

From the time the sign arises the hindrances (*[nīvaraṇa](/glossary/nivarana/)*) are suppressed and the mind is concentrated in access concentration (*upacārasamādhi*) (Vism-i 848). The Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha adds that the counterpart sign is obtained only in the kasiṇas, the foulnesses, the parts of the body and mindfulness of breathing (para. 27).

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Tatrāyaṃ purimassa ca uggahanimittassa imassa ca viseso, uggahanimitte kasiṇadoso paññāyati, paṭibhāganimittaṃ thavikato nihatādāsamaṇḍalaṃ viya sudhotasaṅkhathālaṃ viya valāhakantarā nikkhantacandamaṇḍalaṃ viya meghamukhe balākā viya uggahanimittaṃ padāletvā nikkhantamiva tato sataguṇaṃ sahassaguṇaṃ suparisuddhaṃ hutvā upaṭṭhāti. Tañca kho neva vaṇṇavantaṃ, na saṇṭhānavantaṃ.**  
> Herein, this is the difference between the earlier learning sign and this counterpart sign: in the learning sign, any fault in the kasiṇa is apparent; but the counterpart sign appears as if breaking out, having pierced through the learning sign, a hundred times, a thousand times more purified than that—like a mirror disk drawn from its bag, like a well-washed mother-of-pearl dish, like the moon's disk emerging from behind a cloud, or like cranes against a thunder cloud. But indeed, it has neither color nor shape.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Vism IV, Pathavīkasiṇaniddesa, para. 57) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_01&page_number=122&name=visuddhimaggo+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=VGF0csSBeWHhuYMgcHVyaW1hc3NhIGNhIHVnZ2FoYW5pbWl0dGFzc2EgaW1hc3NhIGNh)

### Quote (Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha)
> **Tathā samāhitassa panetassa tato paraṃ tasmiṃ uggahanimitte parikammasamādhinā bhāvanamanuyuñjantassa yadā tappaṭibhāgaṃ vatthudhammavimuccitaṃ paññattisaṅkhātaṃ bhāvanāmayamārammaṇaṃ citte sannisannaṃ samappitaṃ hoti, tadā taṃ paṭibhāganimittaṃ samuppannanti pavuccati.**  
> 30. When one is thus concentrated and beyond that applies oneself to the development of that learning sign by means of preliminary concentration, an object which is the counterpart of that—freed from the flaws of the physical object, reckoned as a concept, and born of development—becomes well settled and fully engaged (samappita) in the mind; then it is said that the counterpart sign (paṭibhāganimitta) has arisen.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (para. 30) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=61&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MzAuIFRhdGjEgSBzYW3EgWhpdGFzc2EgcGFuZXRhc3NhIHRhdG8gcGFyYeG5gw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Parikammassa nimittaṃ ārammaṇattāti parikammanimittaṃ , kasiṇamaṇḍalādi. Tadeva cakkhunā diṭṭhaṃ viya manasā uggahetabbaṃ nimittaṃ, uggaṇhantassa vā nimittanti uggahanimittaṃ. Tappaṭibhāgaṃ vaṇṇādikasiṇadosarahitaṃ nimittaṃ upacārappanānaṃ ārammaṇattāti paṭibhāganimittaṃ.**  
> 5. The preliminary sign (parikammanimittaṃ) is the sign of preliminary work, such as the kasiṇa-disk and so on, because it is the object. That very sign, which should be grasped by the mind just as if it were seen with the eye, or the sign for one who is grasping it mentally, is the learning sign (uggahanimittaṃ). The sign that is the counterpart of that, free from the defects of the kasiṇa such as color and so on, is the counterpart sign (paṭibhāganimittaṃ), because it is the object of access and absorption meditations.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinīṭīkā (Nimittabhedavaṇṇanā, para. 5) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=258&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=NS4gUGFyaWthbW1hc3NhIG5pbWl0dGHhuYMgxIFyYW1tYeG5h2F0dMSBdGkgcGFyaWthbW1hbmltaXR0YeG5gyAs)

### Textual References
* **Commentary**: *Visuddhimagga* (Chapter IV, Pathavīkasiṇaniddesa, Vism-i 846–851) – The counterpart sign arising with access concentration; if absorption is not reached in that sitting, he must guard the sign "like the embryo of a Wheel-turning Monarch" (*cakkavattigabbho viya*).
* **Abhidhamma handbook**: *Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha* (para. 5, 27, 30) – the three signs, where the counterpart sign is found, and when it is said to have arisen.
* **Ṭīkā**: *Abhidhammatthavibhāvinīṭīkā* (Nimittabhedavaṇṇanā, para. 5) – the definition of each sign.
