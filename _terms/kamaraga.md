### Grammatical Analysis
**Kāmarāga**: [m.] sensual lust; lust for sense pleasures. A compound of *kāma* (sensuality) + *rāga* (lust). The Atthasālinī (As 2284:3) explains it: "due to desiring and due to being stained, sensuality itself is lust (*kāmanavasena rajjanavasena ca kāmoyeva rāgo kāmarāgo*)", with *kāmesu* meaning "in the five strands of sensual pleasure" (*pañcasu kāmaguṇesu*, As 2284:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Kāmarāga* is the fetter of sensual lust (*kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ*). In the Abhidhamma it is the first of the ten fetters (Dhs 2286:2), and the Dhammasaṅgaṇī says it arises in the eight consciousnesses accompanied by greed (*aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati*, Dhs 2286:3). Its Abhidhamma definition (Dhs 1898:2) is a string of synonyms for greed directed at sense pleasures: *kāmacchando, kāmarāgo, kāmanandī, kāmataṇhā, kāmasineho, kāmapariḷāho, kāmamucchā, kāmajjhosānaṃ*.

In the discourses, the Mahāmālukya Sutta (MN 64) shows it as a lower fetter (*orambhāgiyaṃ saṃyojanaṃ*): when sensual lust has become habitual and is not removed, it is a lower fetter (M-ii 360:12). The five lower fetters are given in AN 9.67 (A-ix 453:3), where the sutta wording for it is *kāmacchando*, in fourth place. The five strands of sensual pleasure are forms, sounds, smells, tastes and tangibles (S-v 392:3).

The Anāgāmī is the one who, with the destruction of the five lower fetters, is reborn spontaneously and attains parinibbāna there, not returning from that world (*pañcannaṃ orambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ parikkhayā opapātiko hoti … anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā*, A-iii 898:6). The Sakadāgāmī, with the destruction of three fetters and the weakening of lust, hatred and delusion (*rāgadosamohānaṃ tanuttā*), returns to this world only once (A-iii 897:6).

### Quote (Mūla: Majjhima Nikāya, Mahāmālukya Sutta)
> **Kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhitena cetasā viharati kāmarāgaparetena; uppannassa ca kāmarāgassa nissaraṇaṃ yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti. Tassa so kāmarāgo thāmagato appaṭivinīto orambhāgiyaṃ saṃyojanaṃ.**  
> He abides with a mind obsessed and enslaved by sensual lust; and he does not understand as it really is the escape from the arisen sensual lust. That sensual lust has become habitual and is uneradicated in him; it is a lower fetter.  
>  
> — [Majjhima Nikāya](/glossary/majjhima-nikaya/) (MN 64, Mahāmālukya Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ma_02&page_number=97&name=majjhimapa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=S8SBbWFyxIFnYXBhcml5deG5reG5rWhpdGVuYSBjZXRhc8SBIHZpaGFyYXRpIGvEgW1hcsSBZ2FwYXJldGVuYTs%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Aṅguttara Nikāya, Orambhāgiya Sutta)
> **“Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, orambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni. Katamāni pañca? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso, kāmacchando, byāpādo – imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni.**  
> “Monks, there are these five lower fetters. What are the five? Clinging to a doctrine of self, doubt, wrong grasp of behavior and observances, sensual desire, and ill will—these indeed, monks, are the five lower fetters.  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (AN 9.67, Orambhāgiya Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_09&page_number=251&name=navakanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Njcu4oCY4oCYIFBhw7FjaW3EgW5pLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIG9yYW1iaMSBZ2l5xIFuaSBzYeG5g3lvamFuxIFuaS4%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamaṃ kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ? Yo kāmesu kāmacchando kāmarāgo kāmanandī kāmataṇhā kāmasineho kāmapariḷāho kāmamucchā kāmajjhosānaṃ – idaṃ vuccati kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ.**  
> 1119. Which among these is the fetter of sensual passion? Whatever sensual desire, lust for sense-objects, sensual delight, craving in regard to sense pleasures, sensual affection, sensual passion, sensual infatuation, and sensual attachment there is in sense pleasures — this is called the fetter of sensual passion.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Saṃyojanagocchaka, §1119) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=224&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTExOS4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtYeG5gyBrxIFtYXLEgWdhc2HhuYN5b2phbmHhuYM%2F)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī, the ten fetters)
> **Katame dhammā saṃyojanā? Dasa saṃyojanāni – kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ, mānasaṃyojanaṃ, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ, vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ, sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ, bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, issāsaṃyojanaṃ, macchariyasaṃyojanaṃ, avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ. Kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati.**  
> 1477. What mental phenomena are fetters? The ten fetters: the fetter of sensual passion, the fetter of aversion, the fetter of conceit, the fetter of views, the fetter of doubt, the fetter of adherence to rites and rituals, the fetter of desire for continued existence, the fetter of jealousy, the fetter of stinginess, and the fetter of ignorance. The fetter of sensual passion (kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ) arises in the eight consciousness-arisings accompanied by greed.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Saṃyojanagocchaka, §1477) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=280&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTQ3Ny4gS2F0YW1lIGRoYW1txIEgc2HhuYN5b2phbsSBPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Kāmesūti pañcasu kāmaguṇesu. Kāmacchandoti kāmasaṅkhāto chando, na kattukamyatāchando, na dhammacchando. Kāmanavasena rajjanavasena ca kāmoyeva rāgo kāmarāgo.**  
> 1103. The term ‘in sense pleasures’ (kāmesu) means ‘in the five strands of sensual pleasure’. The term ‘sensual desire’ (kāmacchando) is the desire reckoned as sensuality; it is not the desire to do, nor the desire for wholesome qualities. Due to desiring and due to being stained, sensuality itself is lust, and thus is lust for sense-objects (kāmarāgo).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (Dhammasaṅgaṇī-aṭṭhakathā, §1103) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=402&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=MTEwMy4gS8SBbWVzxat0aSBwYcOxY2FzdSBrxIFtYWd14bmHZXN1Lg%3D%3D)
