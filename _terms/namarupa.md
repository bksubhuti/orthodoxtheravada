### Grammatical Analysis
**Nāmarūpa**: [nt.] mentality-materiality; name and form; mind and body. A dvandva compound of *nāma* (mentality) + *rūpa* (materiality). The commentaries explain the two members by characteristic: *namanalakkhaṇaṃ nāmaṃ* ("mentality has the characteristic of bending") and *ruppanalakkhaṇaṃ rūpaṃ* ("materiality has the characteristic of being deformed") (Vibh-a 617:4-5; Ps-i on MN 9).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta (MN 9) the Mūla defines it: feeling, perception, volition, contact and attention are *nāma*; the four great elements and the materiality derived from them are *rūpa*; "this mentality and this materiality is called name-and-form". The Vibhaṅga (Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga) states *nāma* as the three aggregates of feeling, perception and formations, and *rūpa* as the growth of the sense bases and the thought-born materiality; so consciousness (*viññāṇa*) is not counted inside *nāma* where it is the preceding link.

In dependent origination (*[paṭiccasamuppāda](/glossary/paticcasamuppada/)*) it is the fourth member in the stock formula, conditioned by consciousness (*viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ*) and the condition for the six sense bases (*nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ*). In the Naḷakalāpī Sutta (SN 12.67), the elder Sāriputta gives the simile of two sheaves of reeds leaning against each other: consciousness and name-and-form condition each other. In the Paṭṭhāna, at the moment of descent into the womb, name and form are mutually related by dependence condition (Paṭṭh-i 35:3).

The Sammohavinodanī gives the detailed definition of *nāma*: it has the characteristic of bending (*namanalakkhaṇa*), the function of association (*sampayogarasa*), the manifestation of inseparability (*avinibbhogapaccupaṭṭhāna*), and its proximate cause is consciousness (*viññāṇapadaṭṭhāna*). *Rūpa* has the characteristic of being deformed, the function of being dispersed (*vikiraṇarasa*), the manifestation of the undetermined (*abyākatapaccupaṭṭhāna*), and consciousness as its proximate cause.

### Quote (Mūla: Majjhima Nikāya)
> **Katamaṃ panāvuso, nāmarūpaṃ, katamo nāmarūpasamudayo, katamo nāmarūpanirodho, katamā nāmarūpanirodhagāminī paṭipadā? Vedanā, saññā, cetanā, phasso, manasikāro – idaṃ vuccatāvuso, nāmaṃ; cattāri ca mahābhūtāni, catunnañca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāyarūpaṃ – idaṃ vuccatāvuso, rūpaṃ. Iti idañca nāmaṃ idañca rūpaṃ – idaṃ vuccatāvuso, nāmarūpaṃ.**  
> And what, friends, is name-and-form, what is the origin of name-and-form, what is the cessation of name-and-form, what is the path leading to the cessation of name-and-form? Feeling, perception, volition, contact, and attention—this, friends, is called mentality. The four great elements and the derived materiality of the four great elements—this, friends, is called materiality. So this mentality and this materiality—this, friends, is called name-and-form.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Samyutta Nikāya)
> **Seyyathāpi, āvuso, dve naḷakalāpiyo aññamaññaṃ nissāya tiṭṭheyyuṃ. Evameva kho, āvuso, nāmarūpapaccayā viññāṇaṃ; viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ;**  
> Just as, friend, two sheaves of reeds might stand leaning against each other. Even so, friend, with name-and-form as condition, consciousness comes to be; with consciousness as condition, name-and-form comes to be;  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 12.67, Naḷakalāpī Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_02&page_number=335&name=nid%C4%81navaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=U2V5eWF0aMSBcGksIMSBdnVzbywgZHZlIG5h4bi3YWthbMSBcGl5byBhw7HDsWFtYcOxw7Fh4bmDIG5pc3PEgXlh)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Vibhaṅga)
> **Tattha katamaṃ viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ? Atthi nāmaṃ, atthi rūpaṃ. Tattha katamaṃ nāmaṃ? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho – idaṃ vuccati “nāmaṃ”. Tattha katamaṃ rūpaṃ? Cakkhāyatanassa upacayo, sotāyatanassa upacayo, ghānāyatanassa upacayo, jivhāyatanassa upacayo, kāyāyatanassa upacayo, yaṃ vā panaññampi atthi rūpaṃ cittajaṃ cittahetukaṃ cittasamuṭṭhānaṃ – idaṃ vuccati “rūpaṃ”.**  
> Therein (Tattha), what is (katamaṃ) ‘with consciousness as condition, name-and-form (viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ)’? There is mentality (atthi nāmaṃ), there is materiality (atthi rūpaṃ). Therein (Tattha), what is mentality (katamaṃ nāmaṃ)? The feeling aggregate (vedanākkhandho), the perception aggregate (saññākkhandho), the formations aggregate (saṅkhārakkhandho)—this (idaṃ) is called (vuccati) ‘mentality (nāmaṃ)’. Therein (Tattha), what is materiality (katamaṃ rūpaṃ)? Growth (upacayo) of the eye sense base (cakkhāyatanassa), growth (upacayo) of the ear sense base (sotāyatanassa), growth (upacayo) of the nose sense base (ghānāyatanassa), growth (upacayo) of the tongue sense base (jivhāyatanassa), growth (upacayo) of the body sense base (kāyāyatanassa), or whatever other (yaṃ vā panaññampi) materiality (rūpaṃ) there is (atthi) that is thought-born (cittajaṃ), thought-caused (cittahetukaṃ), rooted in thought (cittasamuṭṭhānaṃ)—this (idaṃ) is called (vuccati) ‘materiality (rūpaṃ)’.  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=153&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGthdGFtYeG5gyB2acOxw7HEgeG5h2FwYWNjYXnEgSBuxIFtYXLFq3Bh4bmDPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Sammohavinodanī)
> **Namanalakkhaṇaṃ nāmaṃ, sampayogarasaṃ, avinibbhogapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, viññāṇapadaṭṭhānaṃ. Ruppanalakkhaṇaṃ rūpaṃ, vikiraṇarasaṃ, abyākatapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, viññāṇapadaṭṭhānaṃ.**  
> Mentality has the characteristic of bending, its function is to associate, it is manifested as inseparability, and its proximate cause is consciousness. Materiality has the characteristic of being deformed, its function is to be dispersed, it is manifested as the undetermined, and its proximate cause is consciousness.  
>  
> — Sammohavinodanī (Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā, on the Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_02&page_number=129&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TmFtYW5hbGFra2hh4bmHYeG5gyBuxIFtYeG5gywgc2FtcGF5b2dhcmFzYeG5gywgYXZpbmliYmhvZ2FwYWNjdXBh4bmt4bmtaMSBbmHhuYMsIHZpw7HDscSB4bmHYXBhZGHhua3hua1oxIFuYeG5gy4%3D)
