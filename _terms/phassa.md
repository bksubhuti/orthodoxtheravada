### Grammatical Analysis
**Phassa**: [m.] contact; sensory impression; collision. From root *phus* (to touch, strike, contact). Meaning: the critical psychological collision between a sense organ, an object, and awareness.

### Orthodox Definition
In the Theravāda [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), *phassa* is classified as a universal mental factor (*sabbacittasādhāraṇa-[cetasika](/glossary/cetasika/)*), arising in every single mind-moment without exception. Within Dependent Origination, it forms the sixth link (*[saḷāyatana](/glossary/salayatana/)-paccayā phasso*).

The Mūla defines it as "the meeting of the three" (*tiṇṇaṃ saṅgati phasso*): eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and forms, and the coming together of the three is contact; conditioned by contact, feeling arises (MN 18). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines it as "touching, thorough touching, the state of having thoroughly touched" (*phusanā saṃphusanā saṃphusitattaṃ*). The Aṭṭhakathā stresses that although *phassa* is a formless state, it occurs "in the manner of touching" the object (*phusanākāreneva*), and that it makes the mind and the object impinge, like the eye and a form, or the ear and a sound (As 1156).

The Aṭṭhakathā gives its operational signature (*lakkhaṇa, rasa, paccupaṭṭhāna, padaṭṭhāna*) in two forms:
* In the Atthasālinī and in Visuddhimagga XIV (where it is treated as a mental factor): **Lakkhaṇa**: touching (*phusanalakkhaṇo*); **Rasa**: impingement (*saṅghaṭṭanaraso*); **Paccupaṭṭhāna**: concurrence (*sannipātapaccupaṭṭhāno*, "known by way of its cause, the concurrence of the three"); **Padaṭṭhāna**: an object that has come into range (*āpāthagatavisayapadaṭṭhāno*).
* In Visuddhimagga XVII (the dependent-origination chapter, where contact follows the six sense-bases): **Lakkhaṇa**: touching; **Rasa**: impingement; **Paccupaṭṭhāna**: coincidence (*saṅgatipaccupaṭṭhāno*); **Padaṭṭhāna**: the six sense-bases (*saḷāyatanapadaṭṭhāno*).

The Atthasālinī quotes the Milindapañha similes: two rams fighting, or two cymbals or two hands struck together; as the meeting of the two, so contact should be seen (As 1157).

### Quote (Mūla: Majjhima Nikāya)
> **“Cakkhuñcāvuso, paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati cakkhuviññāṇaṃ, tiṇṇaṃ saṅgati phasso, phassapaccayā vedanā, yaṃ vedeti taṃ sañjānāti, yaṃ sañjānāti taṃ vitakketi, yaṃ vitakketi taṃ papañceti, yaṃ papañceti tatonidānaṃ purisaṃ papañcasaññāsaṅkhā samudācaranti atītānāgatapaccuppannesu cakkhuviññeyyesu rūpesu.**  
> “Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and forms. The meeting of the three is contact. Conditioned by contact, feeling arises. What one experiences, that one perceives. What one perceives, that one reflects on. What one reflects on, that one proliferates. On account of what one proliferates, judgments driven by proliferating perceptions beset a man regarding forms known by eye-consciousness in the past, future, and present.  
>  
> — [Majjhima Nikāya](/glossary/majjhima-nikaya/) (MN 18, Madhupiṇḍika Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ma_01&page_number=158&name=m%C5%ABlapa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIENha2todcOxY8SBdnVzbywgcGHhua1pY2NhIHLFq3BlIGNhIHVwcGFqamF0aQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Katamo tasmiṃ samaye phasso hoti? Yo tasmiṃ samaye phasso phusanā saṃphusanā saṃphusitattaṃ – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye phasso hoti.**  
> 2. What is contact at that time? Whatever contact, touching (phusanā), thorough touching, or the state of having thoroughly touched exists at that time—this is contact at that time.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Cittuppādakaṇḍa, para. 2) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=18&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Mi4gS2F0YW1vIHRhc21p4bmDIHNhbWF5ZSBwaGFzc28gaG90aT8%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Seyyathidaṃ – phusatīti phasso. Svāyaṃ phusanalakkhaṇo, saṅghaṭṭanaraso, sannipātapaccupaṭṭhāno, āpāthagatavisayapadaṭṭhāno. “yathā, mahārāja, dve meṇḍā yujjheyyuṃ, tesu yathā eko meṇḍo evaṃ cakkhu daṭṭhabbaṃ, yathā dutiyo meṇḍo evaṃ rūpaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ; yathā tesaṃ sannipāto evaṃ phasso daṭṭhabbo”.**  
> That is to say—it touches, thus it is contact (phasso). This has the characteristic of touching (phusanalakkhaṇo), the function of impingement (saṅghaṭṭanaraso), the manifestation of a meeting (sannipātapaccupaṭṭhāno), and its proximate cause is an object that has come into range (āpāthagatavisayapadaṭṭhāno). ‘‘Great King, just as two rams might fight—there, as is the first ram, so the eye should be seen; as is the second ram, so the visual object should be seen; as is their meeting, so contact should be seen.’’  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (Phassa, As 1155-1157) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=151&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U2V5eWF0aGlkYeG5gyDigJMgcGh1c2F0xKt0aSBwaGFzc28u)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga XIV)
> **Tattha phusatīti phasso. Svāyaṃ phusanalakkhaṇo. Saṅghaṭṭanaraso, sannipātapaccupaṭṭhāno, āpāthagatavisayapadaṭṭhāno.**  
> 459. Therein, it touches, thus it is contact (phassa). It has the characteristic of touching. Its function is impingement, it is manifested as concurrence, and its proximate cause is an object that has come into range.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XIV, para. 459) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=93&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NDU5LiBUYXR0aGEgcGh1c2F0xKt0aSBwaGFzc28u)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga XVII)
> **Phusanalakkhaṇo phasso , saṅghaṭṭanaraso, saṅgatipaccupaṭṭhāno, saḷāyatanapadaṭṭhāno.**  
> Contact (phasso) has the characteristic of touching (phusanalakkhaṇo); its function is impingement (saṅghaṭṭanaraso); it is manifested as coincidence (saṅgatipaccupaṭṭhāno); and its proximate cause is the six sense bases (saḷāyatanapadaṭṭhāno).  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, para. 588) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=159&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=UGh1c2FuYWxha2toYeG5h28gcGhhc3NvICwgc2HhuYVnaGHhua3hua1hbmFyYXNvLCBzYeG5hWdhdGlwYWNjdXBh4bmt4bmtaMSBbm8sIHNh4bi3xIF5YXRhbmFwYWRh4bmt4bmtaMSBbm8u)
