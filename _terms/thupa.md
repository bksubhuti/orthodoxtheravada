### Grammatical Analysis
**Thūpa**: [m.] Stupa; mound; reliquary monument. The commentary glosses *thūpārahā* as "suitable and appropriate for a stupa" (*thūpassa yuttā anucchavikā*, Mp-ii 198:1).

### Orthodox Definition
A *Thūpa* is a monument raised over the remains of the great. In the *[Mahāparinibbāna](/glossary/mahaparinibbana/) [Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)* the Buddha tells Ānanda that four are worthy of a *thūpa* (*thūpārahā*): a Tathāgata, Arahant, Perfectly Self-Awakened One; a Paccekasambuddha; a disciple of the Tathāgata; and a Universal Monarch (*rājā cakkavattī*) (D-ii 555:1-3; the same four at A-iv 1840:3, "Cattārome, bhikkhave, thūpārahā").

The reason given for each is that many people purify their minds, thinking "this is the stupa of ...", and after death are reborn in a fortunate destination, a heavenly world (D-ii 556:2-4, 557:2-4, 558:2, 559:2). He also instructs that, as they act in regard to a Universal Monarch's body, wrapping it, burning it and building a stupa for him at the crossroads (*cātumahāpathe … thūpaṃ karonti*), so should they act in regard to the Tathāgata's body: *cātumahāpathe tathāgatassa thūpo kātabbo* (D-ii 553:4-9).

The Aṭṭhakathā adds the merit of looking after such a monument: having attended to the relic mound (*cetiya*) of a Universal Monarch two kinds of attainments are obtained, of the Buddhas three (Mp-ii 198:2). For the Vinaya status of a stupa the commentary says *thūpo nāma asenāsanaṃ*, "a stupa is not a lodging" (Sp-iv 342:1).

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāparinibbāna Sutta)
> **“Cattārome, ānanda, thūpārahā. Katame cattāro? Tathāgato arahaṃ sammāsambuddho thūpāraho, paccekasambuddho thūpāraho, tathāgatassa sāvako thūpāraho, rājā cakkavattī thūpārahoti.**  
> “These four persons, Ānanda, are worthy of a Shrine. Which four? A Realised One, a Worthy One, a Perfect Sambuddha is worthy of a Shrine; an Individual Sambuddha is worthy of a Shrine; a Realised One’s disciple is worthy of a Shrine; and a Universal Monarch is worthy of a Shrine.  
>  
> — [Dīgha Nikāya](/glossary/digha-nikaya/) (DN 16, Mahāparinibbāna Sutta, Thūpārahapuggalo) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_di_02&page_number=117&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MjA2LuKAmOKAmCBDYXR0xIFyb21lLCDEgW5hbmRhLCB0aMWrcMSBcmFoxIEu)

### Quote (Mūla: Thūpāraha Sutta)
> **“Cattārome, bhikkhave, thūpārahā. Tathāgato arahaṃ sammāsambuddho thūpāraho, paccekabuddho thūpāraho, tathāgatasāvako thūpāraho, rājā cakkavattī thūpāraho – ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro thūpārahā” ti.**  
> “Monks, these four are worthy of a stupa (thūpārahā). A Tathāgata, an Arahant, a fully enlightened Buddha is worthy of a stupa; a private Buddha is worthy of a stupa; a disciple of a Tathāgata is worthy of a stupa; and a Wheel-turning Monarch is worthy of a stupa. These indeed, monks, are the four worthy of a stupa.”  
>  
> — [Aṅguttara Nikāya](/glossary/anguttara-nikaya/) (Thūpāraha Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_an_04&page_number=567&name=catukkanip%C4%81tap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MjQ3LuKAmOKAmCBDYXR0xIFyb21lLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIHRoxatwxIFyYWjEgS4%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Manorathapūraṇī)
> **Catutthe thūpārahāti thūpassa yuttā anucchavikā. Cakkavattino hi cetiyaṃ paṭijaggitvā dve sampattiyo labhanti, buddhānaṃ cetiyaṃ paṭijaggitvā tissopi.**  
> 56. In the fourth, worthy of a stupa (thūpārahā) means 'suitable and appropriate for a stupa'. Indeed, having attended to a relic mound of a Wheel-turning Monarch, two kinds of attainments are obtained; having attended to a relic mound of the Buddhas, three kinds are obtained.  
>  
> — [Manorathapūraṇī](/glossary/manorathapurani/) (Mp-ii 198:1-2, on the Thūpāraha Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_an_02&page_number=51&name=duk%C4%81dinip%C4%81taa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=NTYuIENhdHV0dGhlIHRoxatwxIFyYWjEgXRpIHRoxatwYXNzYSB5dXR0xIEgYW51Y2NoYXZpa8SBLg%3D%3D)

### Textual References
* **Sutta**: *[Mahāparinibbāna](/glossary/mahaparinibbana/) Sutta* (DN 16, CST "3. Mahāparinibbānasuttaṃ", D-ii 287:1; "Thūpārahapuggalo" D-ii 554:1; the crossroads stupa D-ii 553:6-9, the four worthy of a stupa D-ii 555:1-3) – the Buddha's instructions to Ānanda on his remains.
* **Sutta**: *Thūpāraha Sutta* (A-iv 1839:1, "5. Thūpārahasuttaṃ"; the four at A-iv 1840:1-3).
* **Commentary**: *[Manorathapūraṇī](/glossary/manorathapurani/)* (Mp-ii 198:1-2); *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* (Sp-iv 342:1). The Mahāvaṃsa (Mhv) and Thūpavaṃsa (Thup) tell of later stupas; both contain the word *mahāthūpa* (48 and 17 sentences respectively in TPR).
