### Grammatical Analysis
**Aññamaññapaccaya**: [m.] Mutuality condition; Reciprocity condition. Formed by *aññamañña* (one another, mutual, reciprocal) + *paccaya* (condition).

### Orthodox Definition
*Aññamañña-paccaya* is the seventh of the 24 Conditions. The Paṭṭhāna states it: "cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññapaccayena paccayo" (Paṭṭh-i 34:1). The Visuddhimagga defines it: "a phenomenon that is helpful by way of mutual production and support is the mutuality condition, like a tripod, which supports one another" (*aññamaññaṃ uppādanupatthambhanabhāvena upakārako dhammo aññamaññapaccayo aññamaññūpatthambhakaṃ tidaṇḍakaṃ viya*, Vism-ii 926:1; Paṭṭh-a 1925:1), and gives its three kinds: the four immaterial aggregates, the four great elements, and at the moment of rebirth-linking mind and matter (Vism-ii 926:2-5; Paṭṭh-i 34:1-3; Abhidh-s 727:1).

The sub-commentary on the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha distinguishes it from the conascence condition: it is a condition by way of mutual support, not merely by arising together (*aññamaññatāvaseneva ca upakārakatā aññamaññapaccayatā, na sahajātamattena*, Abhidh-s-t 1664:2), so a conascent condition is not always a mutuality condition.

### Quote
> **Aññamaññapaccayoti – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 7. Mutuality condition: The four immaterial aggregates are related to one another by mutuality condition. The four great elements are related to one another by mutuality condition. At the moment of descent into the womb, name-and-form are related to each other by mutuality condition.  
>

### Quote
> **Aññamaññaṃ uppādanupatthambhanabhāvena upakārako dhammo aññamaññapaccayo. Aññamaññupatthambhakaṃ tidaṇḍaṃ viya. So arūpakkhandhādivasena tividho hoti. Yathāha – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā … pe … okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññapaccayena paccayoti.**  
> The phenomenon that is helpful by way of producing and supporting one another is the reciprocal condition. Like a three-legged stand supporting one another. That is threefold by way of formless aggregates and so on. As it was said: 'The four formless aggregates are a condition for one another by way of reciprocal condition'. The four great elements ... at the moment of descent into the womb, name-and-form (are a condition) for one another by way of reciprocal condition.  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=347&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QcOxw7FhbWHDscOxYeG5gyB1cHDEgWRhbnVwYXR0aGFtYmhhbmFiaMSBdmVuYSB1cGFrxIFyYWtvIGRoYW1tbyBhw7HDsWFtYcOxw7FhcGFjY2F5by4%3D)

### Quote
> **Aññamaññaṃ uppādanupatthambhanabhāvena upakārako dhammo aññamaññapaccayo aññamaññūpatthambhakaṃ tidaṇḍakaṃ viya. So arūpakkhandhādivasena tividho hoti. Yathāha – “cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññapaccayena paccayo” ti (paṭṭhā.1.1.7).**  
> 600. A phenomenon that is helpful by way of producing and supporting each other is a mutuality condition (aññamaññapaccayo), like a tripod supporting itself. It is of three kinds by way of formless aggregates and so on. As he said— “The four formless aggregates are conditions by way of mutuality condition. The four great elements and name-and-form at the moment of descent into the womb are conditions by way of mutuality condition.”  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (§600) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=167&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NjAwLiBBw7HDsWFtYcOxw7Fh4bmDIHVwcMSBZGFudXBhdHRoYW1iaGFuYWJoxIF2ZW5hIHVwYWvEgXJha28gZGhhbW1vIGHDscOxYW1hw7HDsWFwYWNjYXlv)

### Quote
> **Aññamaññaṃ upatthambhayamānaṃ tidaṇḍaṃ viya attano upakārakadhammānaṃ upatthambhakabhāvena paccayo aññamaññapaccayo. Aññamaññatāvaseneva ca upakārakatā aññamaññapaccayatā, na sahajātamattenāti ayametesaṃ dvinnaṃ viseso.**  
> A mutuality condition (aññamaññapaccayo) is a condition by way of supporting those phenomena that are helpful to it, like a three-legged stand supporting each other. And helpfulness only by way of mutuality is the state of mutuality condition, and not by mere co-nascence; this is the distinction between these two.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā (aññamañña and sahajāta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=243&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QcOxw7FhbWHDscOxYeG5gyB1cGF0dGhhbWJoYXlhbcSBbmHhuYMgdGlkYeG5h%2BG4jWHhuYMgdml5YSBhdHRhbm8gdXBha8SBcmFrYWRoYW1txIFuYeG5gw%3D%3D)

* **Commentary**: *Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā* (Paṭṭh-a 1925:1-5) and *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Vism-ii 926:1-5, §600) – the definition with the tripod simile (*tidaṇḍakaṃ viya*) and the three kinds.
* **Handbook and sub-commentary**: *[Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)* (Abhidh-s 727:1) – the three kinds; *Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā* (Abhidh-s-t 1664:1-4) – how it differs from the conascence condition.
