### Grammatical Analysis
**Adhiṭṭhāna**: [nt.] determination; resolution; resolve; foundation; steadying (of mind). Formed by prefix *adhi* (over, intensely) + root *ṭhā* (to stand, stay). Literally means "standing firm upon a resolution" or "absolute fixedness of purpose."

### Orthodox Definition
*Adhiṭṭhāna* is the eighth of the ten perfections (*[pāramī](/glossary/parami/)*) in the order of Sv-ii 978:1. It represents the unshakeable resolution necessary to fulfill all other perfections across countless lifetimes without wavering, collapsing under adversity, or straying due to worldly temptation.

The Cariyāpiṭaka-aṭṭhakathā defines it: *adhiṭṭhāna* in the requisites of awakening is its characteristic (*bodhisambhāresu adhiṭṭhānalakkhaṇā*), overcoming their opponents is its function (*paṭipakkhābhibhavanarasā*), unwavering steadfastness in them is its manifestation (*acalatāpaccupaṭṭhānā*), and the requisites of awakening are its proximate cause (*bodhisambhārapadaṭṭhānā*) (Cp-a 1910:9). Elsewhere the same commentary describes it as "unshaken and firm determination everywhere" (*sabbattha acalasamādānādhiṭṭhānena*, Cp-a 205:6). In the Temiya conduct the Bodhisatta says "Adhiṭṭhānena me samo natthi, esā me adhiṭṭhānapāramī" (Cp 981:1), and the commentary says that in it his perfection of determination "attained its culmination" (Cp-a 1560:1).

### Quote
> **“Adhiṭṭhitamadhiṭṭhānaṃ, tajjento vividhakāraṇā; Na bhindiṃ tamadhiṭṭhānaṃ, bodhiyāyeva kāraṇā. 66. “Ete aṅge adhiṭṭhāya, vassāni soḷasaṃ vasiṃ; Adhiṭṭhānena me samo natthi, esā me adhiṭṭhānapāramī” ti.**  
> Though being tortured by various reasons for the sake of the resolve I had undertaken; I did not break that resolve, for the sake of awakening alone. Resolving on these factors, I lived for sixteen years; there is no equal to my resolve: this is my perfection of resolve.”

### Quote
> **Bodhisambhāresu adhiṭṭhānalakkhaṇā adhiṭṭhānapāramī , tesaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanarasā, tattha acalatāpaccupaṭṭhānā, bodhisambhārapadaṭṭhānā.**

>  
> — Cariyāpiṭaka-aṭṭhakathā (adhiṭṭhānapāramī: lakkhaṇa, rasa, paccupaṭṭhāna, padaṭṭhāna) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_13&page_number=273&name=cariy%C4%81pi%E1%B9%ADakaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=Qm9kaGlzYW1iaMSBcmVzdSBhZGhp4bmt4bmtaMSBbmFsYWtraGHhuYfEgSBhZGhp4bmt4bmtaMSBbmFwxIFyYW3EqyAsIHRlc2HhuYMgcGHhua1pcGFra2jEgWJoaWJoYXZhbmFyYXPEgSw%3D)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Cariyāpiṭaka](/glossary/cariyapitaka/)* (Temiyacariya, Cp 974:1-981:1) – the Bodhisatta Temiya, who after being anointed king (Cp 965:1) kept his resolve for sixteen years (*vassāni soḷasaṃ vasiṃ*, Cp 980:1); "this is my perfection of resolve" (Cp 981:1).
* **Commentary**: *Cariyāpiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā* – the characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause of adhiṭṭhānapāramī (Cp-a 1910:9); the culmination of the perfection in the Temiya conduct, including the resolve of muteness (Cp-a 1560:1-2); "adhiṭṭhānapāramī paramatthapāramī nāma jātā" at Cp-a 1867:1.
* **Commentary**: *[Sumaṅgalavilāsinī](/glossary/sumangalavilasini/)* (Mahāvagga-aṭṭhakathā, Aṭṭhayathābhuccavaṇṇanā, Sv-ii 978:1) – the ten perfections in order (adhiṭṭhāna is the eighth), fulfilled over four incalculable aeons and a hundred thousand aeons for the welfare of many.
