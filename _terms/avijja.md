### Grammatical Analysis
**Avijjā**: [f.] ignorance. The Aṭṭhakathā gives the derivation: "*vijjāya paṭipakkhabhāvato na vijjāti avijjā*", "it is *avijjā* because, being the opposite of knowledge (*vijjā*), it is not knowledge" (As 1798:7). It is one of the many terms by which the Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines delusion (*moho*): "*moho pamoho sammoho avijjā avijjogho avijjāyogo avijjānusayo avijjāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ*" (Dhs 1839:2).

### Orthodox Definition
*Avijjā* is the first link of dependent origination: "conditioned by ignorance, formations" (S-ii 12:2). The Buddha defines it in the Vibhaṅga Sutta: "ignorance regarding suffering, ignorance regarding the origin of suffering, ignorance regarding the cessation of suffering, ignorance regarding the path leading to the cessation of suffering; this is called ignorance" (S-ii 24:1-3). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī, defining delusion, lists eight: not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the path, and also not knowing the past, the future, the past and future, and the conditionality (*idappaccayatā*) of dependently arisen states (Dhs 1839:2). In the Avijjā Sutta the Buddha says ignorance is the forerunner in the entry upon unwholesome states, with shamelessness and fearlessness of wrongdoing following (S-v 8:1).

The Aṭṭhakathā explains the sequence of dependent origination with the simile of the blind man: ignorance is like a blind man, because of not seeing phenomena by their individual and common characteristics; formations conditioned by ignorance are like his stumbling, and so on to ageing and death (Vibh-a 985:1). The Ṭīkā compares ignorance to a cataract (*paṭala*): one whose eyes are covered by a cataract does not see forms, or sees them wrongly; in the same way one overcome by ignorance does not practise or see suffering and the rest, or practises wrongly (Vibh-mt 507:1).

### Quote (Mūla: Saṃyutta Nikāya)
> **“Katamā ca, bhikkhave, avijjā? Yaṃ kho, bhikkhave, dukkhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhasamudaye aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṃ. Ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, avijjā.**  
> “And what, monks, is ignorance? Monks, that ignorance regarding suffering, ignorance regarding the origin of suffering, ignorance regarding the cessation of suffering, ignorance regarding the path leading to the cessation of suffering. This, monks, is called ignorance.  
>

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamo moho? Dukkhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhasamudaye aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṃ, pubbante aññāṇaṃ, aparante aññāṇaṃ, pubbantāparante aññāṇaṃ, idappaccayatā paṭiccasamuppannesu dhammesu aññāṇaṃ, yaṃ evarūpaṃ aññāṇaṃ adassanaṃ anabhisamayo ananubodho asambodho appaṭivedho asaṃgāhanā apariyogāhanā asamapekkhanā apaccavekkhaṇā apaccakkhakammaṃ dummejjhaṃ bālyaṃ asampajaññaṃ moho pamoho sammoho avijjā avijjogho avijjāyogo avijjānusayo avijjāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati moho.**  
> 1067. Among these, what is delusion? Not knowing about suffering, not knowing about the origin of suffering, not knowing about the cessation of suffering, not knowing about the path leading to the cessation of suffering, not knowing about the past, not knowing about the future, not knowing about the past and future, not knowing about the conditionality of this (idappaccayatā) and of dependently arisen phenomena (paṭiccasamuppannesu dhammesu), whatever such ignorance, non-seeing, non-penetration, non-awakening, non-realization, non-discernment, non-grasping, inability to fathom, unequal observation, non-reflecting, dullness, witlessness, folly, unawareness, delusion, confusion, confusion, ignorance, flood of ignorance, bond of ignorance, the underlying tendency of ignorance, obsession of ignorance, lock of ignorance, delusion, a root of the unwholesome — this is called delusion.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs 1839, §1067) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=216&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTA2Ny4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtbyBtb2hvPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Muyhatīti moho. Balavataro moho pamoho. Samantato muyhatīti sammoho. Vijjāya paṭipakkhabhāvato na vijjāti avijjā .**  
> Delusion (moho) means it is deluded. Confusion (pamoho) means a mightier (balavataro) delusion (moho). Confusion (sammoho) means it is deluded all around (samantato). Ignorance (avijjā) means that, from being the opposite (paṭipakkhato) of true knowledge (vijjāya), it is not true knowledge.  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1798) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=296&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TXV5aGF0xKt0aSBtb2hvLg%3D%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Vibhaṅga-mūlaṭīkā)
> **Paṭalābhibhūtacakkhuko rūpāni na passati, kiñcipi passanto ca viparītaṃ passati, evaṃ avijjābhibhūto dukkhādīni na paṭipajjati na passati, micchā vā paṭipajjatīti paṭalaṃ viya avijjā, kiminā viya attanā katattā vaṭṭassa attanoyeva paribbhamanakāraṇattā ca kosappadesā viya saṅkhārā , saṅkhārapariggahaṃ vinā patiṭṭhaṃ alabhamānaṃ viññāṇaṃ pariṇāyakapariggahaṃ vinā patiṭṭhaṃ alabhamāno rājakumāro viyāti pariggahena vinā patiṭṭhālābho ettha sāmaññaṃ.**  
> Just as a blind man covered by a cataract does not see forms at all, or if he sees anything at all, he sees it incorrectly; in the same way, a fool crushed by ignorance does not understand or see the Noble Truths, or sees them incorrectly; therefore, ignorance is like a cataract. And because, like a silkworm wrapping itself, the fool, crushed by it, wraps himself in volitional formations,; and consciousness grasped by volitional formations finds no footing without that grasping, like a prince who finds no footing without the support of a regent—thus, being without support is the similarity here.  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga-mūlaṭīkā](/glossary/mulatika/) (Vibh-mt 507) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_bi_02_01&page_number=129&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gam%C5%ABla%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=UGHhua1hbMSBYmhpYmjFq3RhY2Fra2h1a28gcsWrcMSBbmkgbmEgcGFzc2F0aSwga2nDsWNpcGkgcGFzc2FudG8%3D)
