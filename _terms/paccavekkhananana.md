### Grammatical Analysis
**Paccavekkhaṇañāṇa**: [nt.] Reviewing knowledge; retrospective knowledge. Formed by *paccavekkhaṇa* (looking back, reviewing, reflecting) + *ñāṇa* (knowledge). 

### Orthodox Definition
Immediately after the supramundane cognitive sequence (Path and Fruition) concludes and the mind drops back into the mundane life-continuum (*[bhavaṅga](/glossary/bhavanga/)*), the practitioner naturally engages in *Paccavekkhaṇañāṇa*. 

This is a mundane cognitive process where the newly awakened disciple retrospectively evaluates their achievement. A noble disciple typically reviews five things:
1. The Path they just traversed.
2. The Fruition they just attained.
3. The Defilements that have been destroyed.
4. The Defilements that still remain (except for an [Arahat](/glossary/arahat/), who has none).
5. [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/), the object they just penetrated.

The Paṭisambhidāmagga gives the name in the list of knowledges: "understanding of insight into ideas then arrived at is knowledge of reviewing" (*tadā samudāgate dhamme passane paññā paccavekkhaṇe ñāṇaṃ*, Paṭis 19:1; explained at Paṭis 421:1ff, ending each path and fruition moment with "having emerged, he reviews", Paṭis 429:3). The commentary counts them: five reviews for each of the three trainees (stream-enterer, once-returner, non-returner) and four for the Arahant, "thus in total nineteen knowledges of reviewing" (Paṭis-a 171:3-11).

### Quote
> **Vuṭṭhahitvā paccavekkhati, ime dhammā tadā samudāgatā. Taṃ ñātaṭṭhena ñāṇaṃ, pajānanaṭṭhena paññā. Tena vuccati – “tadā samudāgate dhamme passane paññā paccavekkhaṇe ñāṇaṃ”.**  
> Having emerged, he reviews: these ideas are then arrived at. Knowledge is in the sense of that being known and understanding is in the sense of the act of understanding that. Hence it was said: “understanding of insight into ideas then arrived at is knowledge of reviewing”.  
>  
> — [Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/) (Mahāvagga, Ñāṇakathā, mātikā 14 at Paṭis 19:1) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_18&page_number=72&name=pa%E1%B9%ADisambhid%C4%81maggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VnXhua3hua1oYWhpdHbEgSBwYWNjYXZla2toYXRpLCBpbWUgZGhhbW3EgSB0YWTEgSBzYW11ZMSBZ2F0xIEu)

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *[Paṭisambhidāmagga](/glossary/patisambhidamagga/)* (Ñāṇakathā, Paṭis 19:1, 421:1, 429:3-6) – the knowledge of reviewing as the fourteenth knowledge. The Mūla text does not itself give the number nineteen or the five objects; those are in the commentary below.
* **Commentary**: *Paṭisambhidāmagga-aṭṭhakathā* (Paṭis-a 171:3-11: the five reviews and the total of nineteen) and *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter XXII, Vism-ii 1895:3-5: mind descends to bhavaṅga, mind-door adverting arises for reviewing the path, then the five reviews; Vism-ii 1902:3).
