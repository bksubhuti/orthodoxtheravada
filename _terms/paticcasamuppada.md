### Grammatical Analysis
**Paṭiccasamuppāda**: [m.] Dependent Origination; Conditioned Genesis. Formed by *paṭicca* (dependent upon, falling back on) + *samuppāda* (arising, origination). Meaning: the dependent co-arising of phenomena where nothing exists in isolation.

### Orthodox Definition
*Paṭiccasamuppāda* is the structural core of the Buddha's cognitive discovery at the foot of the [Bodhi](/glossary/bodhi/) tree. It maps out the exact causal mechanics of how suffering (*[dukkha](/glossary/dukkha/)*) is generated and sustained across the three lifetimes of [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/), without relying on a creator god or an eternal soul.

The standard structural formula consists of **twelve interlinked causal nodes**:
1. Ignorance (*[avijjā](/glossary/avijja/)*)
2. Volitional Formations (*[saṅkhāra](/glossary/sankhara/)*)
3. Consciousness (*[viññāṇa](/glossary/vinnana/)*)
4. Mentality-Materiality (*nāmārupa*)
5. Six Sense Bases (*[saḷāyatana](/glossary/salayatana/)*)
6. Contact (*[phassa](/glossary/phassa/)*)
7. Feeling (*[vedanā](/glossary/vedana/)*)
8. Craving (*[taṇhā](/glossary/tanha/)*)
9. Clinging (*[upādāna](/glossary/upadana/)*)
10. Becoming (*[bhava](/glossary/bhava/)*)
11. Birth (*[jāti](/glossary/jati/)*)
12. Aging and Death (*[jarāmaraṇa](/glossary/jaramarana/)*)

The [Mahāvihāra](/glossary/maha-vihara/) commentary emphasizes that this is a dynamic wheel, operating via specific modes of relation (*paccaya*). The forward chain (*anuloma*) shows how suffering accumulates, while the reverse chain (*paṭiloma*) charts how the complete cessation of ignorance causes the entire mass of suffering to break apart permanently.
