### Grammatical Analysis
**Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha**: [m.] Compendium of the Topics of the Higher Doctrine. Formed by *Abhidhamma* (higher/ultimate doctrine) + *attha* (topics, matters, meanings) + *saṅgaha* (compendium, summary, collection).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha* is the premier, authoritative manual of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/) philosophy in the Theravāda tradition. Composed by **Ācariya Anuruddha** (colophon: "Iti anuruddhācariyena racitaṃ", Abhidh-s 872:1) at the request of a lay devotee named Namma (Cūḷgv 56:3), it serves as an indispensable introductory gateway and structural summary of the complex *[Abhidhamma Piṭaka](/glossary/abhidhamma-pitaka/)*. 

Due to its absolute conciseness and systematic precision, it is traditionally studied as the first handbook before the canonical texts themselves.

The work is organized into **nine chapters** (*paricchedas*, Abhidh-s 9:1 to 753:1; each closes with a colophon "…saṅgahavibhāgo nāma", e.g. Abhidh-s 77:1):
1. **Citta-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Consciousness (classifying the 89 or 121 types of consciousness, Abhidh-s 63:1, 66:1).
2. **Cetasika-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Mental Factors (the 52 mental factors, Abhidh-s 82:1, and their combinations).
3. **Pakiṇṇaka-saṅgaha**: Miscellaneous Compendium (grouping states by feelings, roots, functions, doors, objects, and bases).
4. **Vīthi-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Cognitive Processes (outlining the active series of mind-moments).
5. **Vīthimutta-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Process-Freed Consciousness (dealing with death, rebirth-linking, [kamma](/glossary/kamma/), and cosmology).
6. **Rūpa-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Materiality (classifying the 28 types of materiality, Abhidh-s 524:1, and defining [Nibbāna](/glossary/nibbana/), Abhidh-s 594:1).
7. **Samuccaya-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Categories (fourfold: the unwholesome, the mixed, the bodhipakkhiya, and the all-inclusive compendium, Abhidh-s 606:1, with the 37 bodhipakkhiya factors, Abhidh-s 657:1).
8. **Paccaya-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Relations (summarizing dependent origination and the 24 conditional relations (Abhidh-s 740:1) of the *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)*).
9. **Kammaṭṭhāna-saṅgaha**: Compendium of Meditation Subjects (the 40 subjects of serenity/[samatha](/glossary/samatha/), Abhidh-s 770:1, and the stages of insight/[vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/), Abhidh-s 810:1).

### Quote
> **Sammāsambuddhamatulaṃ, sasaddhammagaṇuttamaṃ. Abhivādiya bhāsissaṃ, abhidhammatthasaṅgahaṃ. 2. Tattha vuttābhidhammatthā, catudhā paramatthato. Cittaṃ cetasikaṃ rūpaṃ, nibbānamiti sabbathā.**  
> 1. Having respectfully saluted the Peerless Fully Self-Awakened One, along with the Sublime Teaching and the Noble Order (sasaddhammagaṇuttamaṃ), I will speak the Compendium of Abhidhamma Topics, a manual of the things contained in the Abhidhamma. 2. The topics of the Abhidhamma mentioned therein are, fourfold from the standpoint of ultimate reality (paramatthato). Consciousness (cittaṃ), mental factors (cetasikaṃ), materiality (rūpaṃ), and Nibbāna—in every way.  
>

### Textual References
* **Colophon**: *Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha* (Abhidh-s 872:1-873:1) – "Iti anuruddhācariyena racitaṃ Abhidhammatthasaṅgahaṃ nāma pakaraṇaṃ."
* **Authorship**: *Cūḷagandhavaṃsa* (Cūḷgv 56:3) – written by Teacher Anuruddha at the request of the lay devotee Namma; and Cūḷgv 56:14 – the ṭīkā on it was written by Teacher Sumaṅgala.
* **Sub-commentaries**:
  * *Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā* (Abhidh-s-t) – its colophons read "Iti abhidhammatthavibhāviniyā nāma abhidhammatthasaṅgahavaṇṇanāya" (Abhidh-s-t 1016:1); the Sāsanavaṃsappadīpikā lists Sumaṅgalasāmi as author of the ṭīkā on the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha (Sas 230:1).
  * *Paramatthadīpanī* (PmtDīp) – a further commentary on the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha (PmtDīp 93:1).
