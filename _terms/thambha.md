### Grammatical Analysis
**Thambha**: [m.] obstinacy; stubbornness; mental rigidity; stiffness. The Vibhaṅga explains it by *thambho thambhanā thambhitattaṃ*, stiffening, stiffness (Vibh 2555:2).

### Orthodox Definition
*Thambha* is one of the sixteen imperfections that defile the mind (*cittassa upakkilesā*) of the Vatthasutta (M-i 191:2); the bhikkhu who knows "obstinacy is an imperfection of the mind" abandons it (M-i 192:11). It is paired with *sārambha* (rivalry) in the list of evil states: *thambho ca pāpako sārambho ca pāpako* (M-i 105:1).

The Abhidhamma defines it: *yo thambho thambhanā thambhitattaṃ kakkhaḷiyaṃ phārusiyaṃ ujucittatā amudutā* (obstinacy, stiffening, stiffness, harshness, roughness, rigidity of mind, inflexibility), "this is called obstinacy" (Vibh 2555:2, under "(30) Thambho", Vibh 2554:1).

The commentary (Papañcasūdanī) gives the characteristics: *cittassa uddhumātabhāvalakkhaṇo thambho, appatissayavuttiraso, amaddavatāpaccupaṭṭhāno*: the characteristic is the mind being puffed up, the function is not bowing down, the manifestation is unyieldingness (Ps-i 563:1). On the Vatthasutta it says obstinacy is like a leather bag filled with air, a hard state in which the head is held up and one acts without humility (Ps-i 852:1). It illustrates it with the monk who gets what he wishes and becomes stubborn and unyielding, unable to be instructed (*thaddho hoti amuducitto*, Ps-i 566:7). It is abandoned by the path of arahatship (Ps-i 855:5).

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Vibhaṅga)
> **Tattha katamo thambho? Yo thambho thambhanā thambhitattaṃ kakkhaḷiyaṃ phārusiyaṃ ujucittatā amudutā – ayaṃ vuccati “thambho”.**  
> 847. Which is obstinacy (thambho) there? Whatever obstinacy, stiffening, stiffness, harshness, roughness, rigidity of mind, or inflexibility there is—this is called obstinacy (thambho).  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Vibh 2554-2555, (30) Thambho) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=363&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=ODQ3LiBUYXR0aGEga2F0YW1vIHRoYW1iaG8%2F)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Papañcasūdanī)
> **Yathā cetaṃ, evaṃ vātabharitabhastasadisathaddhabhāvapaggahitasiraanivātavuttikārakaraṇo thambho.**  
> Just as this is, so too is obstinacy—a state of hardness like a leather bag filled with air, causing a puffed-up, unbending state.  
>
