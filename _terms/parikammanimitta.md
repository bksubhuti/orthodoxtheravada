### Grammatical Analysis
**Parikammanimitta**: [nt.] preparatory sign; preliminary object. Formed by *parikamma* (preparation, preliminary action) + *[nimitta](/glossary/nimitta/)* (sign, object). The Ṭīkā explains it: “parikammassa nimittaṃ ārammaṇattāti parikammanimittaṃ, kasiṇamaṇḍalādi”, the sign of the preliminary work, being its object, such as the kasiṇa disc (Abhidh-s-t 1723:1). The word is post-canonical: TPR has no occurrence in the Mūla and none in the Visuddhimagga; it is found in the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha and its ṭīkās.

### Orthodox Definition
The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha defines it: “Ādikammikassa hi pathavīmaṇḍalādīsu nimittaṃ uggaṇhantassa tamārammaṇaṃ parikammanimittanti pavuccati, sā ca bhāvanā parikammabhāvanā nāma”: for a beginner who is apprehending a sign in the earth disc and the like, that object is called the preliminary sign, and that development is called preliminary development (Abhidh-s 791:2). It is the first of three signs, followed by the learning sign (*uggahanimitta*) and the counterpart sign (*paṭibhāganimitta*) (Abhidh-s 763:1); the preliminary and learning signs are obtained in all the subjects, while the counterpart sign arises only in the kasiṇas, the foulness, the parts of the body and mindfulness of breathing (Abhidh-s 789:1, 790:1).

How it is used: in the earth kasiṇa the disc is made of clay (Vism IV, Vism-i 843:5-9), and the meditator sits at the right distance and, with the eyes opened to the right degree, repeats “pathavī pathavī”, opening and closing the eyes, “until the learning sign arises” (*yāva uggahanimittaṃ nuppajjati*, Vism-i 844-845). The Ṭīkā says that when the preliminary sign is well grasped by the mind, it comes into the range of the mind door like the disc even with the eyes closed (Abhidh-s-t 1747:5-6; Abhidh-s 792:1). The concentration at this stage is *parikammasamādhi*, and the learning sign is developed by means of it until the counterpart sign arises (Abhidh-s 793:1). The Visuddhimagga calls the first javana moments before absorption *parikamma* (Vism-i 925:4-6).

### Quote (Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha)
> **Kathaṃ? Ādikammikassa hi pathavīmaṇḍalādīsu nimittaṃ uggaṇhantassa tamārammaṇaṃ parikammanimittanti pavuccati, sā ca bhāvanā parikammabhāvanā nāma.**  
> 28. How? For when a beginner apprehends a sign from the earth disk or similar objects, that object is called the preliminary sign (parikammanimitta), and that development is called preliminary development (parikammabhāvanā).  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (IX, Nimittabhedo, 28) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=61&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MjguIEthdGhh4bmDPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha)
> **Tathā samāhitassa panetassa tato paraṃ tasmiṃ uggahanimitte parikammasamādhinā bhāvanamanuyuñjantassa yadā tappaṭibhāgaṃ vatthudhammavimuccitaṃ paññattisaṅkhātaṃ bhāvanāmayamārammaṇaṃ citte sannisannaṃ samappitaṃ hoti, tadā taṃ paṭibhāganimittaṃ samuppannanti pavuccati.**  
> 30. When one is thus concentrated and beyond that applies oneself to the development of that learning sign by means of preliminary concentration, an object which is the counterpart of that—freed from the flaws of the physical object, reckoned as a concept, and born of development—becomes well settled and fully engaged (samappita) in the mind; then it is said that the counterpart sign (paṭibhāganimitta) has arisen.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (IX, 30) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=61&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MzAuIFRhdGjEgSBzYW3EgWhpdGFzc2EgcGFuZXRhc3NhIHRhdG8gcGFyYeG5gw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Parikammassa nimittaṃ ārammaṇattāti parikammanimittaṃ , kasiṇamaṇḍalādi.**  
> 5. The preliminary sign (parikammanimittaṃ) is the sign of preliminary work, such as the kasiṇa-disk and so on, because it is the object.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā (Nimittabhedavaṇṇanā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=258&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=NS4gUGFyaWthbW1hc3NhIG5pbWl0dGHhuYMgxIFyYW1tYeG5h2F0dMSBdGkgcGFyaWthbW1hbmltaXR0YeG5gyAs)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Yadā pana taṃ nimittaṃ cittena samuggahitanti evaṃ pavattānupubbabhāvanāvasena yadā taṃ parikammanimittaṃ cittena sammā uggahitaṃ hoti. Manodvārassa āpāthamāgatanti cakkhuṃ nimmīletvā, aññattha gantvā vā manasi karontassa kasiṇamaṇḍalasadisameva hutvā manodvārikajavanānaṃ āpāthaṃ āgataṃ hoti.**  
> When that sign has been thoroughly apprehended by the mind means when that preliminary sign has been correctly apprehended by the mind by way of the development that proceeds in that sequence. Has come into range of the mind door means for one who is attending to it after closing the eyes or having gone elsewhere (aññattha), it has come into the range (āpāthaṃ) of the mind-door impetuses, becoming (hutvā) just like the kasiṇa disk.  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā (on IX, 29) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=263&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=WWFkxIEgcGFuYSB0YeG5gyBuaW1pdHRh4bmDIGNpdHRlbmEgc2FtdWdnYWhpdGFudGk%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Apica pathavīti etadeva nāmaṃ pākaṭaṃ, tasmā pākaṭavaseneva pathavī pathavīti bhāvetabbaṃ. Kālena ummīletvā kālena nimīletvā āvajjitabbaṃ. Yāva uggahanimittaṃ nuppajjati, tāva kālasatampi kālasahassampi tato bhiyyopi eteneva nayena bhāvetabbaṃ.**  
> Furthermore, the name pathavī is the most obvious; therefore, he should develop it using the obvious name, repeating: “earth, earth” (pathavī pathavī). He should review it, sometimes with eyes open, sometimes with eyes shut. Until the learning sign arises, he should go on developing it in this very manner, a hundred times, a thousand times, and even more than that.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (IV, 56, Pathavīkasiṇaniddesa) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_01&page_number=122&name=visuddhimaggo+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=QXBpY2EgcGF0aGF2xKt0aSBldGFkZXZhIG7EgW1h4bmDIHDEgWth4bmtYeG5gywgdGFzbcSB)
