### Grammatical Analysis
**Dosa**: [m.] hatred; hate. The Aṭṭhasālinī derives it: "*dussanti tena, sayaṃ vā dussati, dussanamattameva vā taṃ* – by it they are offended, or it offends itself, or it is merely an offending; therefore *doso*" (As 1813:2).

### Orthodox Definition
*Dosa* is one of the three unwholesome roots: greed, hatred and delusion (*lobho doso ca moho ca hetū akusalā tayo*, Abhidh-s 213:1-214:1). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines it by a series of synonyms: vexation (*āghāto*) of mind, hostility, resentment, opposition, anger, great anger, intense anger, hatred, malice, mental perversity, wrath, ferocity, ill-temper, displeasure of mind: "this is called *dosa*" (Dhs 1838:3); in the first consciousness accompanied by displeasure and resentment it is "whatever hatred, the act of corrupting, … ferocity, … disgruntlement of mind" (Dhs 924:2).

The Aṭṭhasālinī gives the characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause: *dosa* has the characteristic of ferocity (*caṇḍikka-lakkhaṇo*), like a struck snake; its function is to spread (*visappana-raso*), like a venomous attack, or to burn its own support (*attano nissayadahana-raso*), like a forest fire; it is manifested as offending (*dussana-paccupaṭṭhāno*), like an enemy who has gained an opportunity; its proximate cause is the grounds of resentment (*āghātavatthu-padaṭṭhāno*), and it should be seen like putrid urine mixed with poison (As 1813:2).

The Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha says that two types of consciousness are "accompanied by displeasure and resentment" (*domanassasahagataṃ paṭighasampayuttaṃ*), one unprompted and one prompted (Abhidh-s 14:1), and lists *doso* among the fourteen unwholesome mental factors (Abhidh-s 88:1). In the Paṭisambhidāmagga the resentment fetter (*paṭighasaṃyojana*) and the latent tendency of resentment (*paṭighānusaya*) are cut off in their gross form by the path of the once-returner and in their subtle form by the path of the non-returner (Paṭis 416:1, 417:1).

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamo doso? Yo evarūpo cittassa āghāto paṭighāto paṭighaṃ paṭivirodho kopo pakopo sampakopo doso padoso sampadoso cittassa byāpatti manopadoso kodho kujjhanā kujjhitattaṃ doso dussanā dussitattaṃ byāpatti byāpajjanā byāpajjitattaṃ virodho paṭivirodho caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati doso.**  
> 1066. Among these, what is hate? Whatever such is the mind's vexation (cittassa āghāto), hostility, resentment, recurrent hostility, anger (kopo), strong anger (pakopo), intense anger (sampakopo), hate, corruption (padoso), intense corruption (sampadoso), the mind's impairment (cittassa byāpatti), mental corruption (manopadoso), anger, the act of anger (kujjhanā), the state of being angry (kujjhitattaṃ), hate, the act of corrupting, the state of being corrupted, vexation, the act of vexing (byāpajjanā), the state of being vexed, hostility, recurrent hostility, fierceness, bad speech, disgruntlement of the mind — this is called hate.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs §1066, Hetugocchaka) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=216&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTA2Ni4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtbyBkb3NvPw%3D%3D)


### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī (first citta with displeasure))
> **Katamo tasmiṃ samaye doso hoti? Yo tasmiṃ samaye doso dussanā dussitattaṃ byāpatti byāpajjanā byāpajjitattaṃ virodho paṭivirodho caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye doso hoti … pe ….**  
> 418. What is hatred at that time? Whatever hatred, the act of corrupting, the state of being corrupted, vexation, the mode of vexing, the state of being vexed, hostility, recurrent hostility, fierceness, bad speech, disgruntlement of mind exists at that time—this is hatred at that time ...  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs §418, Dosamūla citta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=101&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NDE4LiBLYXRhbW8gdGFzbWnhuYMgc2FtYXllIGRvc28gaG90aT8%3D)


### Quote (Mūla: Itivuttaka)
> **“Tayome, bhikkhave, aggī. Katame tayo? Rāgaggi, dosaggi, mohaggi – ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo aggī” ti.**  
> “Monks, there are these three fires. What three? The fire of lust, the fire of hate, the fire of delusion—these, monks, are the three fires.”  
>  
> — [Itivuttaka](/glossary/itivuttaka/) (Iti 93, Aggisutta, It 929) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ku_04&page_number=257&name=itivuttakap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFRheW9tZSwgYmhpa2toYXZlLCBhZ2fEqy4%3D)


### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Aṭṭhasālinī)
> **Mūlakammapathesu yathā purimacittesu lobho hoti, abhijjhā hotīti āgataṃ, evaṃ doso hoti, byāpādo hotīti vuttaṃ. Tattha dussanti tena, sayaṃ vā dussati, dussanamattameva vā tanti doso so caṇḍikkalakkhaṇo pahaṭāsiviso viya, visappanaraso visanipāto viya, attano nissayadahanaraso vā dāvaggi viya, dussanapaccupaṭṭhāno laddhokāso viya sapatto, āghātavatthupadaṭṭhāno visasaṃsaṭṭhapūtimuttaṃ viya daṭṭhabbo.**  
> Just as (yathā) greed (lobho) and covetousness (abhijjhā) were mentioned in the earlier minds in the root courses of action (mūlakammapathesu), so (evaṃ) too, it was stated (vuttaṃ) that hatred (doso) and ill will (byāpādo) arise. There, hatred (doso) means that they (te) are offended (dussanti) by that (tena), or it offends (dussati) itself (sayaṃ), or it is simply an offending; and that (so) (hatred) has the characteristic of fierceness (caṇḍikkalakkhaṇo), like (viya) a struck snake, its function is to spread (visappanaraso), like (viya) a venomous attack (visanipāto), or its function is to burn its own support (attano nissayadahanaraso), like (viya) a forest fire (dāvaggi), its manifestation is defiling (dussanapaccupaṭṭhāno), like (viya) an enemy (sapatto) who has gained an opportunity, and its proximate cause is a basis for resentment (āghātavatthupadaṭṭhāno), and it should be understood (daṭṭhabbo) like (viya) putrid urine mixed with poison (visasaṃsaṭṭhapūtimuttaṃ).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1813, on the dosa of the first citta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=298&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TcWrbGFrYW1tYXBhdGhlc3UgeWF0aMSBIHB1cmltYWNpdHRlc3UgbG9iaG8gaG90aSwgYWJoaWpqaMSB)


### Quote (Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha: Hetusaṅgaha)
> **Lobho doso ca moho ca, Hetū akusalā tayo; Alobhādosāmoho ca, Kusalābyākatā tathā.**  
> 16. Greed, hatred, and delusion are the three unwholesome roots; and non-greed, non-hatred, and non-delusion are similarly wholesome and indeterminate.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (Abhidh-s 213-216) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=17&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MTYuIExvYmhvIGRvc28gY2EgbW9obyBjYSw%3D)
