### Grammatical Analysis
**Makkha**: [m.] contempt; denigration; belittling; smearing. Derived from root *makkh* (to smear, to rub out). Signifies the psychological erasing or covering up of others' goodness.

### Orthodox Definition
*Makkha* is a deeply corrosive minor defilement (*[upakkilesa](/glossary/upakkilesa/)*) rooted in aversion. It manifests as the deliberate minimization, denial, or smearing of the virtues, assistance, and good deeds done by others.

The Papañcasūdanī gives its characteristic as the denigration of others' virtues (*paraguṇamakkhanalakkhaṇo*), its function as destroying them and its manifestation as covering them over (Ps-i 560:1), and describes it as destroying the good done by another (*sukatakaraṇavināsano*): a monk who was supported and taught by his teacher or preceptor, once honoured by kings and ministers, says "What have you done for me?" and so destroys the good done to him (Ps-i 850:4-6). It acts like dirt smeared across a clean mirror, intentionally obscuring the reflection of another's virtue.

Because the Theravāda path relies heavily on honoring one's teachers, parents, and noble friends (*[kalyāṇamitta](/glossary/kalyanamitta/)*), *makkha* severs the practitioner's connection to the sources of spiritual guidance.

### Quote
> **(Ka) tattha katamo makkho? Yo makkho makkhāyanā makkhāyitattaṃ niṭṭhuriyaṃ niṭṭhuriyakammaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati “makkho”.**  
> 892.(A) What is contempt (makkho) there? Whatever is contempt, the act of detracting, the state of having detracted, harshness, the act of harshness—this is called contempt (makkho).  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Vatthasutta* (MN 7, the CST name of the "Vatthūpama" sutta) – *makkho cittassa upakkileso*, listed among the defilements of the mind (M-i 191:2).
* **Canonical**: *[Suttanipāta](/glossary/suttanipata/)* (Vasala Sutta - Sn 1.7, Sn 395:1) – "Kodhano upanāhī ca, pāpamakkhī ca yo naro": the man who is angry, harbours resentment and is a malicious denigrator (*pāpamakkhī*) is to be known as an outcast (*vasala*).
* **Commentary**: *[Papañcasūdanī](/glossary/papancasudani/)* on the Vatthasutta (Ps-i 560:1; 850:4-7; 855:7, *makkha* is abandoned by the stream-entry path).
