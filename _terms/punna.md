### Grammatical Analysis
**Puñña**: [nt.] merit; meritorious action; the good result of it. The Ṭīkā gives the derivation: "it purifies, cleanses (*punāti visodheti*) its doer or his continuum", and also "because it produces an auspicious state or fruit that is to be honoured" (*pujjabhavaphalanibbattanato*) (Sv-nt-i 114).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Suttas *puñña* is both the wholesome deed and its happy result: "this is a designation for happiness, for what is desirable, dear and agreeable, namely meritorious deeds" (It, para. 22). The Vibhaṅga defines the "formation of merit" (*puññābhisaṅkhāra*) as wholesome volition (*kusalā cetanā*) of the sense-sphere and the form-sphere, consisting of giving, morality and meditation.

The Mūla gives three **Puññakiriyavatthu** (Bases of Meritorious Action), in the Saṅgīti Sutta and in AN (Aṭṭhakanipāta, "6. Puññakiriyavatthusuttaṃ"):
1. *[Dāna](/glossary/dana/)* (*dānamaya*): giving.
2. *[Sīla](/glossary/sila/)* (*sīlamaya*): morality.
3. *[Bhāvanā](/glossary/bhavana/)* (*bhāvanāmaya*): meditative development.

The Atthasālinī explains each as volition (*cetanā*): the volition arising before, during and after giving is *dānamaya*, and so on for the others (As 1379-1380). It then says: "in the discourse only three bases are stated; the others are included in them. Reverence and service go into the one consisting of virtue; transference and rejoicing in merit into giving; teaching, listening and straightening of one's view into development. Thus they are three in summary and ten in detail" (As 1390). The Vibhaṅga places the *puññābhisaṅkhāra* among the formations conditioned by ignorance.

### Quote (Mūla: Dīgha Nikāya)
> **“Tīṇi puññakiriyavatthūni – dānamayaṃ puññakiriyavatthu, sīlamayaṃ puññakiriyavatthu, bhāvanāmayaṃ puññakiriyavatthu.**  
> “Three bases of meritorious activity: basis of meritorious activity consisting in giving, basis of meritorious activity consisting in virtue, basis of meritorious activity consisting in meditative development.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Itivuttaka)
> **“Mā, bhikkhave, puññānaṃ bhāyittha. Sukhassetaṃ, bhikkhave, adhivacanaṃ iṭṭhassa kantassa piyassa manāpassa yadidaṃ puññāni.**  
> “Monks, do not fear meritorious deeds. This is a designation for happiness, for what is desirable, wished for, dear, and agreeable—namely, meritorious deeds.  
>

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Vibhaṅga)
> **Tattha katamo puññābhisaṅkhāro? Kusalā cetanā kāmāvacarā rūpāvacarā dānamayā sīlamayā bhāvanāmayā – ayaṃ vuccati “puññābhisaṅkhāro”.**  
> Therein, what is the formation of merit (puññābhisaṅkhāro)? Wholesome volition (kusalā cetanā) that belongs to the sense-sphere, the form sphere, consisting of giving, consisting of morality, and consisting of meditation — this is called the formation of merit (puññābhisaṅkhāro).  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=142&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGthdGFtbyBwdcOxw7HEgWJoaXNh4bmFa2jEgXJvPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī, dānamaya)
> **Tattha cīvarādīsu catūsu paccayesu, rūpādīsu vā chasu ārammaṇesu, annādīsu vā dasasu dānavatthūsu, taṃ taṃ dentassa tesaṃ tesaṃ uppādanato paṭṭhāya pubbabhāge, pariccāgakāle, pacchā somanassacittena anussaraṇakāle cāti tīsu kālesu pavattā cetanā ‘ dānamayaṃ puññakiriyavatthu ’ nāma.**  
> Among these, the volition that arises in three times—starting from the production of such and such objects for the donor, in the preliminary phase, at the time of giving, and later, at the time of recalling with a mind accompanied by happiness—concerning the four requisites like robes and so on, or the six sense objects like forms and so on, or the ten objects of giving like food and so on, is called the basis of meritorious activity consisting in giving.  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (Puññakiriyavatthu, As 1379) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=201&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGPEq3ZhcsSBZMSrc3UgY2F0xatzdSBwYWNjYXllc3UsIHLFq3DEgWTEq3N1IHbEgQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī, three and ten)
> **Sutte pana tīṇiyeva puññakiriyavatthūni āgatāni. Tesu itaresampi saṅgaho veditabbo. Apacitiveyyāvaccāni hi sīlamaye saṅgahaṃ gacchanti. Pattānuppadānaabbhanumodanāni dānamaye. Desanāsavanadiṭṭhijukammāni bhāvanāmaye. Ye pana ‘ diṭṭhijukammaṃ sabbesaṃ niyamalakkhaṇan ’ ti vadanti tesaṃ taṃ tīsupi saṅgahaṃ gacchati. Evametāni saṅkhepato tīṇi hutvā vitthārato dasa honti.**  
> But in the discourse, only three bases of meritorious activity are stated. Among them, the inclusion of even the others should be understood. Indeed, reverence and service are included in that consisting in virtue. The transference of merit and rejoicing in merit in that consisting in giving. Teaching, listening, and straightening of view in that arising out of contemplation. But for those who say, ‘Straightening of view is the characteristic that determines the abundance of fruit for all’, that is included in all three. Thus, having been three in summary, these are ten in detail.  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (Puññakiriyavatthu, As 1390) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=203&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U3V0dGUgcGFuYSB0xKvhuYdpeWV2YSBwdcOxw7Fha2lyaXlhdmF0dGjFq25pIMSBZ2F0xIFuaS4%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Sīlakkhandhavagga-abhinavaṭīkā)
> **Pujjabhavaphalanibbattanato puññaṃ niruttinayena, attano kārakaṃ, santānaṃ vā punāti visodhetīti puññaṃ , sakammakattā dhātussa kāritavasena atthavivaraṇaṃ labbhati, saddanipphatti pana suddhavasenevāti saddavidū.**  
> By etymology, puñña ('merit') because it produces auspicious state or fruit that is to be honored; because it purifies or cleanses the continuum, the explanation by way of 'meaning' is obtained through the causative of the root, but the completion of the word is just by way of the root itself, say the grammarians.  
>  
> — Sīlakkhandhavaggaabhinavaṭīkā (Sv-nt-i 114) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_di_01_02&page_number=25&name=s%C4%ABlakkhandhavaggaabhinava%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=UHVqamFiaGF2YXBoYWxhbmliYmF0dGFuYXRvIHB1w7HDsWHhuYMgbmlydXR0aW5heWVuYSwgYXR0YW5vIGvEgXJha2HhuYMsIHNhbnTEgW5h4bmD)

* **Abhidhamma**: *[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)* (Vibh 908) – the *puññābhisaṅkhāra*.
* **Commentary**: *[Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/)* (As 1378-1390) – the ten bases and their inclusion in the three.
