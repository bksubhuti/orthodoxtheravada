### Grammatical Analysis
**Sakkāyadiṭṭhi**: [f.] Identity view; personality view. Formed by *sakkāya* + *diṭṭhi* (view). The Aṭṭhakathā: "the five aggregates, form and the rest, are *sakkāya* in the sense of existing (*vijjamānaṭṭhena*)" (Nidd-a-i 605); the Mūla: "*sakkāya* is the five aggregates of clinging" (MN 44; SN 22.105).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sakkāyadiṭṭhi* is the first of the five lower fetters (*orambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni*): *sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso, kāmacchanda, byāpāda* (DN 33; AN 10.13 and others, A-ix 453, A-x 71). The Mūla (MN 44) defines it: an untaught ordinary person "regards form as self, or self as possessing form, or form in self, or self in form", and likewise with feeling, perception, formations and consciousness. Five aggregates in four ways make twenty, which the Aṭṭhakathā calls "identity view with twenty grounds" (*vīsativatthukā sakkāyadiṭṭhi*), identical with clinging to a doctrine of self (*attavādupādāna*, Vism-ii 1203; Paṭis-a 1659). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī has the same definition (Dhs 1775).

It is abandoned "by seeing" (*dassanena pahātabba*): when a person attends wisely to the four truths, three fetters are abandoned, *sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāsa* (MN 2). The Visuddhimagga says these three, with the two (*kāmarāga* and *paṭigha*) of the lower fetters that lead to the lower realms, are removed by the first path (Vism-ii 1965).

### Quote (Mūla: Majjhima Nikāya, what sakkāya is)
> **‘“sakkāyo sakkāyo ’ ti, ayye, vuccati. Katamo nu kho, ayye, sakkāyo vutto bhagavatā” ti? “Pañca kho ime, āvuso visākha, upādānakkhandhā sakkāyo vutto bhagavatā, seyyathidaṃ – rūpupādānakkhandho, vedanupādānakkhandho, saññupādānakkhandho, saṅkhārupādānakkhandho, viññāṇupādānakkhandho. Ime kho, āvuso visākha, pañcupādānakkhandhā sakkāyo vutto bhagavatā” ti.**  
> “‘Embodiment, embodiment,’ is said, Noble Lady. What, Noble Lady, is said to be embodiment by the Blessed One?” “These five constituents (of mind and body) that provide fuel for attachment, friend Visākha, are said to be embodiment by the Blessed One, as follows: the form constituent that is fuel for attachment, the feelings constituent that is fuel for attachment, the perceptions constituent that is fuel for attachment, the (mental) processes constituent that is fuel for attachment, the consciousness constituent that is fuel for attachment. These are the five constituents (of mind and body) that provide fuel for attachment, friend Visākha, that are said to be embodiment by the Blessed One.”  
>  
> — [Majjhima Nikāya](/glossary/majjhima-nikaya/) (MN 44, Cūḷavedalla Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ma_01&page_number=373&name=m%C5%ABlapa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCY4oCYIHNha2vEgXlvIHNha2vEgXlvIOKAmSB0aSwgYXl5ZSw%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Majjhima Nikāya, how sakkāyadiṭṭhi arises)
> **“Kathaṃ panāyye, sakkāyadiṭṭhi hotī” ti? “Idhāvuso visākha, assutavā puthujjano, ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto, sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto, rūpaṃ attato samanupassati, rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā rūpaṃ, rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Vedanaṃ … pe … saññaṃ … saṅkhāre … viññāṇaṃ attato samanupassati, viññāṇavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā viññāṇaṃ, viññāṇasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Evaṃ kho, āvuso visākha, sakkāyadiṭṭhi hotī” ti.**  
> “But how, Noble Lady, does embodiment view arise?” “Here, friend Visākha, an untaught ordinary person, having no sight of the noble ones, unskilled in the noble Dhamma, undisciplined in their Dhamma, having no sight of good persons, unskilled in the Dhamma of good persons, undisciplined in their Dhamma, regards form as self, or self as possessing form, or form as within self, or self as within form. They regard feeling … perception … volitional formations … consciousness as self, or self as possessing consciousness, or consciousness as within self, or self as within consciousness. Thus, friend Visākha, embodiment view arises.”  
>  
> — [Majjhima Nikāya](/glossary/majjhima-nikaya/) (MN 44, Cūḷavedalla Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_ma_01&page_number=374&name=m%C5%ABlapa%E1%B9%87%E1%B9%87%C4%81sap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=NDYxLuKAmOKAmCBLYXRoYeG5gyBwYW7EgXl5ZSwgc2Fra8SBeWFkaeG5reG5rWhpIGhvdMSrIOKAmeKAmQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Mūla: Saṃyutta Nikāya)
> **Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sakkāyo? Pañcupādānakkhandhātissa vacanīyaṃ.**  
> And what, mendicants, is embodiment? It should be said: the five constituents that are fuel for attachment.  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (SN 22.105, Sakkāya Sutta) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_03&page_number=129&name=khandhavaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=S2F0YW1vIGNhLCBiaGlra2hhdmUsIHNha2vEgXlvPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Dhammasaṅgaṇī)
> **Tattha katamā sakkāyadiṭṭhi? Idha assutavā puthujjano ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto rūpaṃ attato samanupassati, rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā rūpaṃ, rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ.**  
> 1007. Which is identity view (sakkāyadiṭṭhi) there? Here, an untaught ordinary person, having no sight of the noble ones, unskilled in the Dhamma of the noble ones, undisciplined in their Dhamma, having no sight of good persons, unskilled in the Dhamma of good persons, undisciplined in their Dhamma, sees form as self, or himself as possessed of form, or form in self, or self in form.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (Dhs 1775, para. 1007) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=208&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTAwNy4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtxIEgc2Fra8SBeWFkaeG5reG5rWhpPw%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Mahāniddesa-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Rūpakkhandhādayo pañcakkhandhā vijjamānaṭṭhena sakkāyo.**  
> The five aggregates, such as the materiality aggregate, are the body in the sense of existing.  
>  
> — Mahāniddesa-aṭṭhakathā (Nidd-a-i 605) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_16&page_number=143&name=mah%C4%81niddesaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=UsWrcGFra2hhbmRoxIFkYXlvIHBhw7FjYWtraGFuZGjEgSB2aWpqYW3EgW5h4bmt4bmtaGVuYSBzYWtrxIF5by4%3D)
