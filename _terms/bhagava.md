### Grammatical Analysis
**Bhagavā**: [m.] The Blessed One; The Exalted One; The Lord. Derived from *bhaga* (fortune, wealth, glory) + possessive suffix *va*. Literally, "He who is endowed with supreme fortune/glory." 

### Orthodox Definition
*Bhagavā* is the most frequently used epithet for the Buddha throughout the Pali Canon, often used by the narrator of the suttas (e.g., "Thus have I heard. At one time the *Bhagavā* was staying at..."). 

In the orthodox commentarial tradition, the title is considered supreme and unassigned by human beings, devas, or Brahmas; it is a natural acquisition resulting directly from final enlightenment at the root of the [Bodhi](/glossary/bodhi/) tree. The *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* provides six profound etymological definitions to use as a meditation object. Among them:
1. **Bhaggavā**: He who has broken (*bhagga*) all defilements.
2. **Bhattavā**: He who has associated with (*bhatta*) supreme worldly and supramundane qualities.
3. He who has analyzed and classified (*vibhajja*) the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) into aggregates, elements, and truths.

### Quote
> **Bhagavāti netaṃ nāmaṃ mātarā kataṃ, na pitarā kataṃ, na bhātarā kataṃ, na bhaginiyā kataṃ, na mittāmaccehi kataṃ, na ñātisālohitehi kataṃ na samaṇabrāhmaṇehi kataṃ, na devatāhi kataṃ. Vimokkhantikametaṃ buddhānaṃ bhagavantānaṃ bodhiyā mūle saha sabbaññutaññāṇassa paṭilābhā sacchikā paññatti yadidaṃ bhagavāti – metteyyāti bhagavā.**  
> This name “Blessed One” was not given by his mother, father, brother, sister, friends, acquaintances, relatives, monastics, brahmins, or deities. It is a designation realized at the end of liberation by the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, together with the acquisition of omniscience at the foot of the Bodhi tree; this is the Blessed One—thus, “Metteyya, Blessed One.”  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Dhajagga Sutta* (SN 11.3) – The final quality listed in the standard recollection of the Buddha (Iti pi so Bhagavā...).
* **Canonical**: *Mahāniddesa* (Nidd-i 757:3-5) – Explains the title by many reasons (he has destroyed lust, hatred, delusion; he has divided and analyzed the Dhamma-jewel; and so on), and says that the name was not given by mother, father, relatives, ascetics or deities but is a designation realized by Buddhas at the foot of the Bodhi tree with omniscience.
* **Commentary**: *Visuddhimagga* (Chapter VII; the verse of six derivations is at Vism-i 1295-1296: *bhāgyavā bhaggavā yutto bhagehi ca vibhattavā, bhattavā vantagamano*) – Deconstructs the grammar and meaning of Bhagavā for the practice of *[Buddhānussati](/glossary/buddhanussati/)*.
