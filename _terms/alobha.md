### Grammatical Analysis
**Alobha**: [m.] Non-greed; unselfishness; detachment. Formed by negative prefix *a* (not, without) + *lobha* (greed, clinging, lust).

### Orthodox Definition
*Alobha* is the first of the three Wholesome Roots (*[kusala-mūla](/glossary/kusala-mula/)*). It is a beautiful mental factor (*sobhana-[cetasika](/glossary/cetasika/)*) present in every single wholesome state of consciousness. 

Although formulated as a negative word (non-greed), its psychological function is entirely positive. The Atthasālinī derives the word: "by this, associated states do not cling, or one does not cling oneself, or it is merely non-clinging" (*na lubbhanti etena, sayaṃ vā na lubbhati, alubbhanamattameva vā tanti alobho*, As 1233:1), and gives its characteristic, function and manifestation: the mind's not being greedy for the object, or non-adherence, like a drop of water on a lotus leaf (*kamaladale jalabindu viya*); non-grasping as its function, like a liberated monk; and non-adherence as its manifestation, like a man fallen into filth (As 1233:3-5; Vism-ii 439:1-440:1 is the same). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines it by its synonyms: "alobho alubbhanā alubbhitattaṃ asārāgo asārajjanā asārajjitattaṃ anabhijjhā alobho kusalamūlaṃ" (Dhs 1832:2).

### Quote
> **“Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, kusalamūlāni. Katamāni tīṇi? Alobho kusalamūlaṃ, adoso kusalamūlaṃ, amoho kusalamūlaṃ. “Yadapi, bhikkhave, alobho tadapi kusalamūlaṃ; yadapi aluddho abhisaṅkharoti kāyena vācāya manasā tadapi kusalaṃ; yadapi aluddho lobhena anabhibhūto apariyādinnacitto na parassa asatā dukkhaṃ uppādayati vadhena vā bandhanena vā jāniyā vā garahāya vā pabbājanāya vā balavamhi balattho itipi tadapi kusalaṃ.**  
> “Monks, there are these three roots of the wholesome. What are these three? Non-greed is a root of the wholesome, non-hatred is a root of the wholesome, non-delusion is a root of the wholesome. “Monks, non-greed itself is a root of the wholesome; whatever a person without greed generates by means of body, speech, or mind, that too is wholesome; whatever suffering a person without greed—his mind not overcome by greed, his mind not obsessed by it—does not wrongly inflict on another person through beating, imprisonment, confiscation, placing blame, or banishment, thinking, ‘I have power. I want power,’ that too is wholesome.  
>

### Quote
> **Tattha katame tayo kusalahetū? Alobho, adoso, amoho. 1061. Tattha katamo alobho? Yo alobho alubbhanā alubbhitattaṃ asārāgo asārajjanā asārajjitattaṃ anabhijjhā alobho kusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati alobho.**  
> 1060. Among these, what three wholesome roots are there? Non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion. 1061. Among these, what is non-greed? Whatever is non-greed, non-greediness, the state of not being greedy, non-attachment, non-clinging, the state of not clinging, non-covetousness, non-greed, a root of the wholesome — this is called non-greed.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/) (§1060-1061) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_01&page_number=214&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABp%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MTA2MC4gVGF0dGhhIGthdGFtZSB0YXlvIGt1c2FsYWhldMWrPw%3D%3D)

### Quote
> **Na lubbhanti etena, sayaṃ vā na lubbhati, alubbhanamattameva vā tanti alobho. Tesu alobho ārammaṇe cittassa agedhalakkhaṇo, alaggabhāvalakkhaṇo vā kamaladale jalabindu viya. Apariggahaṇaraso muttabhikkhu viya. Anallīnabhāvapaccupaṭṭhāno asucimhi patitapuriso viya.**  
> Non-greed (alobho) is defined as: by this (phenomenon), (associated mental states) do not cling; or one does not cling oneself; or it is merely non-clinging. Among these, non-greed (alobho) is characterized by the mind not being greedy towards the object (ārammaṇe), or it is characterized by non-adherence, like a drop of water on a lotus leaf (kamaladale jalabindu viya). Its function is non-grasping, like a liberated monk (muttabhikkhu viya). Its manifestation is non-adherence, like a person fallen (patitapuriso) into filth (asucimhi).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (on alobha) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=170&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=TmEgbHViYmhhbnRpIGV0ZW5hLCBzYXlh4bmDIHbEgSBuYQ%3D%3D)
