### Grammatical Analysis
**Sacchikiriyā**: [f.] Realization; making manifest. Formed by *sacchi* (before the eyes) + *kiriyā* (doing). The Mūla's verb is *sacchikaroti* with the gerundive *sacchikātabba* ("to be realized") and the past participle *sacchikata* ("realized"): "*Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ sacchikātabbaṃ*" (SN 56.11). The Visuddhimagga glosses the mundane kind, for jhāna, as "touching" by the contact of knowledge: *paccakkhato ñāṇaphassena phusanā*, "directly touching, 'this has been attained by me'" (Vism-ii 2041).

### Orthodox Definition
*Sacchikiriyā* is the function (*kicca*) of the path-knowledge with regard to the third noble truth. The cessation of suffering "is to be realized" (*sacchikātabbaṃ*), whereas suffering is to be fully understood, the origin abandoned, and the path developed (SN 56.11; Vibh 3347). The Paṭisambhidāmagga puts it as "understanding of the meaning of realizing is knowledge of cessation" (*sacchikiriyaṭṭhe paññā nirodhe ñāṇaṃ*, Paṭis 58).

The Visuddhimagga divides realization into mundane (*lokiya*), as of the first jhāna and so on, and supramundane (*lokuttara*), and the supramundane again by way of seeing and development: at the moment of the first path, seeing Nibbāna is *dassanasacchikiriyā*; at the moments of the remaining paths it is *bhāvanāsacchikiriyā*; both are intended as the function of path knowledge (Vism-ii 2041, 2044, XXII 852). The Ṭīkā adds that the stream-entry path is first "seeing" (*dassana*) because it sees Nibbāna first (Vism-mht-ii 1746).

### Quote (Mūla: Saṃyutta Nikāya)
> **‘ Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ sacchikātabban ’ ti me, bhikkhave, pubbe … pe … udapādi. ‘ Taṃ kho panidaṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ sacchikatan ’ ti me, bhikkhave, pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṃ udapādi, ñāṇaṃ udapādi, paññā udapādi, vijjā udapādi, āloko udapādi.**  
> “‘This noble truth of the cessation of suffering is to be realized’: thus, monks, in regard to teachings unheard before, there arose in me the eye … and light. “‘This noble truth of the cessation of suffering has been realized’: thus, monks, in regard to teachings unheard before, there arose in me the eye, knowledge, wisdom, true knowledge, and light.  
>

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Vibhaṅga)
> **Nirodhasaccaṃ abhiññeyyaṃ pariññeyyaṃ na pahātabbaṃ na bhāvetabbaṃ sacchikātabbaṃ.**  
> The truth of cessation (nirodhasaccaṃ) is to be directly known, to be fully understood, and to be realized; it is not to be abandoned and not to be developed.  
>  
> — [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) (Dhammahadayavibhaṅga, Vibh 3347) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_bi_02&page_number=442&name=vibha%E1%B9%85gap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=Tmlyb2RoYXNhY2Nh4bmDIGFiaGnDscOxZXl5YeG5gyBwYXJpw7HDsWV5eWHhuYMgbmEgcGFoxIF0YWJiYeG5gyBuYQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga, mundane and supramundane realization)
> **Sacchikiriyāpīti lokiyasacchikiriyā lokuttarasacchikiriyāti dvedhā bhinnāpi lokuttarāya dassanabhāvanāvasena bhedato tividhā hoti. Tattha “paṭhamassa jhānassa lābhīmhi, vasīmhi, paṭhamajjhānaṃ sacchikataṃ mayā” tiādinā (pārā.203-204) nayena āgatā paṭhamajjhānādīnaṃ phassanā lokiyasacchikiriyā nāma.**  
> 852. Realization (sacchikiriyā) also: although divided into two as mundane realization and supramundane realization, supramundane realization is of three kinds by way of differentiation between seeing and development. Therein, the touching (phassanā) of the first absorption and so on that occurs in the way stated as, “I am a recipient of the first absorption, I have mastery in it, the first absorption has been realized by me,” and so on, is called mundane realization.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XXII, Vism-ii 2041, para. 852) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=338&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=ODUyLiBTYWNjaGlraXJpecSBcMSrdGkgbG9raXlhc2FjY2hpa2lyaXnEgSBsb2t1dHRhcmFzYWNjaGlraXJpecSBdGkgZHZlZGjEgSBiaGlubsSBcGk%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga, realization by seeing and by development)
> **Paṭhamamaggakkhaṇe pana nibbānadassanaṃ dassanasacchikiriyā. Sesamaggakkhaṇesu bhāvanāsacchikiriyāti. Sā duvidhāpi idha adhippetā. Tasmā dassanabhāvanāvasena nibbānassa sacchikiriyā imassa ñāṇassa kiccanti veditabbaṃ.**  
> But seeing Nibbana at the moment of the first path is realization by seeing. In the moments of the remaining paths, it is realization by development. Both kinds are intended here. Therefore, the realization of Nibbana by way of seeing and development should be understood as the function of this knowledge.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XXII, Vism-ii 2044) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=338&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=UGHhua1oYW1hbWFnZ2Fra2hh4bmHZSBwYW5hIG5pYmLEgW5hZGFzc2FuYeG5gyBkYXNzYW5hc2FjY2hpa2lyaXnEgS4%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Visuddhimagga-mahāṭīkā)
> **Sotāpattimaggo paṭhamaṃ nibbānadassanato dassanaṃ, tena dassanena sacchikiriyā dassanasacchikiriyā.**  
> The path of stream-entry is first realization by seeing Nibbana, and realization by that seeing is realization by seeing (dassanasacchikiriyā).  
>  
> — Visuddhimagga-mahāṭīkā (Paramatthamañjūsā, on XXII) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_04&page_number=511&name=visuddhimaggamah%C4%81%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=U290xIFwYXR0aW1hZ2dvIHBh4bmtaGFtYeG5gyBuaWJixIFuYWRhc3NhbmF0byBkYXNzYW5h4bmDLCB0ZW5hIGRhc3NhbmVuYQ%3D%3D)
