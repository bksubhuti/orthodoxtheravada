### Grammatical Analysis
**Paccekabuddha**: [m.] Solitary Buddha; Silent Buddha. Formed by *pacceka* (separate, individual, singular) + *buddha* (awakened).

### Orthodox Definition
A *Paccekabuddha* is an individual who attains full enlightenment and the destruction of all defilements entirely on their own, in an era where the teachings of a *[Sammā-sambuddha](/glossary/samma-sambuddha/)* have completely vanished from the world. 

While their liberation from [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/) is identical to that of a Fully Enlightened Buddha, their scope of knowledge is limited. Crucially, a *Paccekabuddha* lacks the supreme pedagogical mastery required to teach the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) to others in a systematic, liberating way. They cannot establish a [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) or a dispensation. They often teach merely through their silent, perfect behavior or by giving brief moral exhortations. They arise in the world either singly or in large groups, often living secluded in the mountains.

### Quote
> **Tathāgataṁ jetavane vasantaṁ,**  
> While the Tathāgata was dwelling at Jetavana,  
> 
> **Apucchi vedehamunī nataṅgo;**  
> The sage of Videha, Ānanda, asked him respectfully:  
> 
> **"Paccekabuddhā kira nāma honti,**  
> "There are said to be those called Paccekabuddhas;  
> 
> **Bhavanti te hetubhi kehi vīra".**  
> Through what causes do they arise, O Hero?"  
> 
> **Tadāha sabbaññuvaro mahesi,**  
> Then the supreme omniscient one, the great seer, spoke,  
> 
> **Ānandabhaddaṁ madhurassarena;**  
> To good Ānanda with a sweet voice:  
> 
> **"Ye pubbabuddhesu katādhikārā,**  
> "Those who made their aspiration under former Buddhas,  
> 
> **Aladdhamokkhā jinasāsanesu.**  
> But did not attain liberation in the dispensations of the Victors;  
> 
> **Teneva saṁvegamukhena dhīrā,**  
> Wise ones, through that very doorway of spiritual urgency,  
> 
> **Vināpi buddhehi sutikkhapaññā;**  
> Having very sharp wisdom even without Buddhas present,  
> 
> **Ārammaṇenāpi parittakena,**  
> Even by means of a slight object,  
> 
> **Paccekabodhiṁ anupāpuṇanti.**  
> Attain the awakening of a Paccekabuddha.  
> 
> **Sabbamhi lokamhi mamaṁ ṭhapetvā,**  
> In the whole world, apart from me,  
> 
> **Paccekabuddhehi samova natthi;**  
> There is none equal to the Paccekabuddhas.  
> 
> **Tesaṁ imaṁ vaṇṇapadesamattaṁ,**  
> I shall speak this mere portion of praise  
> 
> **Vakkhāmahaṁ sādhu mahāmunīnaṁ.**  
> Of those good great sages.  
> 
> **Sayameva buddhānaṁ mahāisīnaṁ,**  
> Of the self-awakened great seers,  
> 
> **Sādhūni vākyāni madhūva khuddaṁ;**  
> Their good sayings are like fine honey;  
> 
> **Anuttaraṁ bhesajaṁ patthayantā,**  
> Seeking the unsurpassed medicine,  
> 
> **Suṇātha sabbesu pasannacittā".**  
> Listen, all of you, with confident minds."  
> 
> **Paccekabuddhānaṁ samāgatānaṁ,**  
> Of the Paccekabuddhas assembled together,  
> 
> **Paramparaṁ byākaraṇāni yāni;**  
> Their successive explanations,  
> 
> **Ādīnavo yañca virāgavatthuṁ,**  
> The danger, the basis for dispassion,  
> 
> **Yathā ca bodhiṁ anupāpuṇiṁsu.**  
> And how they attained awakening;  
> 
> **Sarāgavatthūsu virāgasaññī,**  
> Perceiving dispassion toward things bound up with passion,  
> 
> **Rattamhi lokamhi virattacittā;**  
> With minds detached in a world attached,  
> 
> **Hitvā papañce jiya phanditāni,**  
> Having abandoned mental proliferation and the quivering of the bowstring,  
> 
> **Tatheva bodhiṁ anupāpuṇiṁsu.**  
> In just that way, they attained awakening.  
> 
> — *Paccekabuddhaapadāna* (Therāpadāna, Buddhavagga, Tha-ap 2.2-8)

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Isigili Sutta* (MN 116) – The Buddha names hundreds of Paccekabuddhas who previously lived on Mount Isigili, praising their solitary purity.
* **Canonical**: *Khaggavisāṇa Sutta* (Sn 1.3) – The famous "Rhinoceros Horn" discourse, widely attributed to the inspired utterances (*[udāna](/glossary/udana/)*) of various Paccekabuddhas praising solitary asceticism.
* **Canonical**: *Paccekabuddhaapadāna* (Therāpadāna, Buddhavagga) – Explaining how beings who made aspirations under former Buddhas, but did not gain liberation then, later attain Paccekabodhi through sharp wisdom and spiritual urgency.
* **Commentary**: *[Paramatthadīpanī](/glossary/paramatthadipani/)* – Detailing the specific *pāramīs* (two incalculable aeons and 100,000 aeons) required to reach this state.
