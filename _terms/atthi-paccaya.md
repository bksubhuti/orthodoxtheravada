### Grammatical Analysis
**Atthipaccaya**: [m.] Presence condition, a compound of *atthi* (it is; the state of presence, *atthibhāva*) + *paccaya* (condition). The Aṭṭhakathā defines it as the state that is helpful "through the state of presence (*atthibhāvena*), with the characteristic of the present (*paccuppannalakkhaṇena*), by way of supporting (*upatthambhakattena*) a similar state" (Vism-ii 943:1; Paṭṭh-a 1941:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Atthi-paccaya* is the twenty-first of the twenty-four conditions of the *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)* (Paṭṭh-i 55:1). The Mūla gives it by examples: the four immaterial aggregates are a condition for one another by presence condition; the four great elements for one another; at the moment of conception name-and-form for one another; consciousness and mental factors for mind-born matter; the great elements for derived matter (Paṭṭh-i 55:1-5); then the sense bases for the corresponding consciousness elements (Paṭṭh-i 56:1-57:6), and the matter dependent on which mind-element and mind-consciousness-element occur (the base, *vatthu*) (Paṭṭh-i 58:1).

The Aṭṭhakathā defines the condition (Vism-ii 943:1) and says that its *mātikā* is laid down in seven ways, by way of the formless aggregates, the great elements, name-and-form, consciousness and mental factors, the great elements, the sense bases and the base (Vism-ii 943:2; Paṭṭh-a 1941:2). In the question section the exposition is made by way of co-nascent, pre-nascent and post-nascent states, nutriment and faculty (Vism-ii 944:1-4). The Ṭīkā explains that the activity of the presence conditions is outstanding in the stage of endurance, which is why their quality of supporting is taken (Abhidh-s-t 1672:2).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Atthipaccayoti – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññaṃ atthipaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā aññamaññaṃ atthipaccayena paccayo. Okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññaṃ atthipaccayena paccayo. Cittacetasikā dhammā cittasamuṭṭhānānaṃ rūpānaṃ atthipaccayena paccayo. Mahābhūtā upādārūpānaṃ atthipaccayena paccayo.**  
> 21. Presence condition: the four immaterial aggregates are mutually related to one another by presence condition. The four great elements are mutually related to one another by presence condition. At the moment of conception, name-and-form are mutually related to each other by presence condition. The phenomena consciousness and its mental factors are related to mind-born matter by presence condition. The great elements are related to derived matter by presence condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Paccuppannalakkhaṇena atthibhāvena tādisasseva dhammassa upatthambhakattena upakārako dhammo atthipaccayo. Tassa arūpakkhandhamahābhūtanāmarūpacittacetasikamahābhūtaāyatanavatthuvasena sattadhā mātikā nikkhittā.**  
> 614. A phenomenon that assists another similar phenomenon through its presence (atthibhāvena), characterized by its existence in the present (paccuppannalakkhaṇena), by way of consolidating it, is the presence condition (atthipaccayo). A sevenfold heading (mātikā) has been laid down for it by way of: formless aggregates, the great elements, name-and-form, consciousness and its concomitants, the great elements, the sense bases, and the heart-base.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Paccayākāra, Vism-ii 943) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=171&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NjE0LiBQYWNjdXBwYW5uYWxha2toYeG5h2VuYSBhdHRoaWJoxIF2ZW5hIHTEgWRpc2Fzc2V2YSBkaGFtbWFzc2EgdXBhdHRoYW1iaGFrYXR0ZW5h)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Paccuppannasabhāvasaṅkhātena atthibhāvena tādisasseva dhammassa upatthambhakattena upakārakā “sahajātaṃ purejātan” tyādinā vakkhamānadhammā atthipaccayo. Satipi hi janakatte ṭhitiyaṃyeva sātisayo atthipaccayānaṃ byāpāroti upatthambhakatāva tesaṃ gahitā.**  
> The phenomena that will be stated as "co-nascent, pre-nascent," and so on, which are helpful for a similar phenomenon by way of being a supporter through the state of presence known as a present nature, are the presence condition (atthipaccayo). For even though there is a generative nature, the activity of presence conditions is exceptional only during endurance; thus their state of being a supporter is taken.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/)-ṭīkā (Abhidh-s-t 1672) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=245&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=UGFjY3VwcGFubmFzYWJoxIF2YXNh4bmFa2jEgXRlbmEgYXR0aGliaMSBdmVuYSB0xIFkaXNhc3NldmEgZGhhbW1hc3NhIHVwYXR0aGFtYmhha2F0dGVuYSB1cGFrxIFyYWvEgQ%3D%3D)
