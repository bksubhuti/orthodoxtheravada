### Grammatical Analysis
**Samanantarapaccaya**: [m.] Immediate proximity condition; Contiguous condition. Formed by *sam* (well, thoroughly, exactly) + *anantara* (without gap) + *paccaya* (condition).

### Orthodox Definition
*Samanantara-paccaya* is the fifth of the 24 Conditions (Paṭṭh-i 24). The Aṭṭhakathā defines it: "a phenomenon helpful by way of proximity (*samanantarabhāvena upakārako dhammo*)", just as *anantarapaccaya* is the one helpful by way of *anantarabhāva* (Paṭṭh-a 1922).

The Aṭṭhakathā states plainly that the two are the same: "whichever is the *anantarapaccaya*, that very one is the *samanantarapaccaya*; the difference here is only in phrasing (*byañjanamattameva*), like *upacaya* and *santati*, or *adhivacana* and *nirutti*; in meaning there is no difference" (Paṭṭh-a 1923). It rejects the teachers' view that *anantara* means "no interval of entity" and *samanantara* "no temporal interval", because the Paṭṭhāna also calls the wholesome consciousness of the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception a proximity condition for the attainment of fruition after emerging from cessation; and there, it says, there is no temporal interval either (Paṭṭh-a 1923).

The Ṭīkā explains why two words were used: *anantarapaccaya* is the capacity to produce without a gap between the cessation of the earlier and the arising of the later state; *samanantarapaccaya* is the capacity to produce "thoroughly, with no interval", since, unlike material states, which have shape and position, the condition and the conditioned do not stand together and there is no division of "this is below, above, across", so it is brought "as if into one with itself" (*attanā ekattamiva upanetvā*, Paṭṭh-mt 1075).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Samanantarapaccayoti – cakkhuviññāṇadhātu taṃsampayuttakā ca dhammā manodhātuyā taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ samanantarapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 5. Immediacy condition: Eye-consciousness-element and its associated states are related to mind-element and its associated states by immediacy condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭṭhāna-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Anantarabhāvena upakārako dhammo anantarapaccayo. Samanantarabhāvena upakārako dhammo samanantarapaccayo.**  
> The phenomenon that is helpful by way of immediate contiguity is the immediate contiguity condition. The phenomenon that is helpful by way of proximity is the proximity condition.  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā (Paṭṭh-a 1922) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=346&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QW5hbnRhcmFiaMSBdmVuYSB1cGFrxIFyYWtvIGRoYW1tbyBhbmFudGFyYXBhY2NheW8u)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭṭhāna-aṭṭhakathā, identity of the two)
> **Yo anantarapaccayo, sveva samanantarapaccayo. Byañjanamattameva hettha nānaṃ, upacayasantatiādīsu viya, adhivacananiruttidukādīsu viya ca, atthato pana nānaṃ natthi. Yampi “addhānantaratāya anantarapaccayo, kālānantaratāya samanantarapaccayo” ti ācariyānaṃ mataṃ, taṃ “nirodhā vuṭṭhahantassa nevasaññānāsaññāyatanakusalaṃ phalasamāpattiyā samanantarapaccayena paccayo” tiādīhi virujjhati.**  
> Whichever is the immediate contiguity condition, that very one is the proximity condition. Here, the difference is only in phrasing, like in accumulation, continuity and so on, and in the pairs of designations and etymologies and so on; but there is no difference in meaning. And the view of the teachers that 'the immediate contiguity condition is by way of no interval of entity, and the proximity condition is by way of no temporal interval' is alienated by (statements) such as 'the wholesome consciousness of the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception for one emerging from cessation is a condition for the attainment of fruits by way of proximity condition'.  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā (Paṭṭh-a 1923) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=346&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=WW8gYW5hbnRhcmFwYWNjYXlvLCBzdmV2YSBzYW1hbmFudGFyYXBhY2NheW8u)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa-mūlaṭīkā)
> **Tattha purimapacchimānaṃ nirodhuppādantarābhāvato nirantaruppādanasamatthatā anantarapaccayabhāvo. Rūpadhammānaṃ viya saṇṭhānābhāvato paccayapaccayuppannānaṃ sahāvaṭṭhānābhāvato ca “idamito heṭṭhā uddhaṃ tiriyan” ti vibhāgābhāvā attanā ekattamiva upanetvā suṭṭhu anantarabhāvena uppādanasamatthatā samanantarapaccayatā.**  
> Therein, the immediate-contiguity condition is the capacity to produce without delay, due to the absence of gap between the cessation and the arising of the preceding and succeeding. And the immediate-contiguity condition is the capacity to produce by immediate-contiguity well-performed, having brought close as if it were one with itself, due to the absence of division 'this is below this' 'this is above across this', and due to the absence of form—like material phenomena—and due to the absence of simultaneous existence of the condition and the conditioned.  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa-mūlaṭīkā (Paṭṭh-mt 1075) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_bi_03&page_number=170&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87am%C5%ABla%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIHB1cmltYXBhY2NoaW3EgW5h4bmDIG5pcm9kaHVwcMSBZGFudGFyxIFiaMSBdmF0byBuaXJhbnRhcnVwcMSBZGFuYXNhbWF0dGhhdMSBIGFuYW50YXJhcGFjY2F5YWJoxIF2by4%3D)
