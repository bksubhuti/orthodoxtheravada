### Grammatical Analysis
**Peṭakopadesa**: [m.] Instructions on the Piṭakas; Disclosure of the Baskets. Formed by *peṭaka* (basket, referring to the [Tipiṭaka](/glossary/tipitaka/)) + *upadesa* (instruction, advice, teaching).

### Orthodox Definition
Like the *Netti*, the *Peṭakopadesa* is a technical manual of textual interpretation. Its opening verse names its contents: "Sixteen Conveyings (*soḷasahārā*) as a guide, five guide-lines (*pañcanayā*) for the Dispensation's search, and eighteen root-terms (*aṭṭhārasamūlapadā*), demonstrated by one of the Kaccāyana lineage (*kaccāyanagottaniddiṭṭhā*)" (Pet 14:1-15:1). The text thus attributes the teaching to Kaccāyana-gotta, which the tradition takes as the Elder Mahākaccāna. TPR places it in the Khuddaka Nikāya, as in the Burmese tradition (Pet 2:1), whereas the Khuddaka list given in the Paramatthajotikā does not include it (Pj-i 24:5); it is thus canonical in one tradition and not in the other.

It is closely related to the *Netti* in structure: both use the sixteen *hāras*, which the Peṭakopadesa lists at Pet 17:1: Desanā, Vicayo, Yutti, Padaṭṭhānaṃ, Lakkhaṇaṃ, Catubyūho, Āvaṭṭo, Vibhatti, Parivattano, Vevacano, Paññatti, Otaraṇo, Sodhano, Adhiṭṭhāno, Parikkhāro, Samāropano. It opens with the two conditions for the arising of a hearer's right view, another's utterance and reasoned attention (Pet 7:1).
