### Grammatical Analysis
**Sammohavinodanī**: [f.] The Dispeller of Delusion. Formed by *sammoha* (delusion, bewilderment, ignorance) + *vinodanī* (dispelling, driving away).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Sammohavinodanī* is the *Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā*, the commentary on the *[Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/)* (The Book of Analysis), the second book of the [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/). Its colophon says that it was composed by the Elder known by the name Buddhaghosa (Vibh-a 2341).

In its opening verses the commentator says that the Teacher taught the *Vibhaṅga* by way of eighteen *vibhaṅgas*, beginning with the aggregates (*khandhādi*), and that he will explain its meaning "having plunged into the method of the ancient commentaries" (*porāṇaṭṭhakathānayaṃ*; Vibh-a 7-14). The commentary follows these chapters in order, beginning with "1. Khandhavibhaṅgo" (Vibh-a 5), and reaches "6. Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgo" at Vibh-a 580. There the commentary on the dependent origination analysis quotes the verse that four matters are hard to see and extremely difficult to teach, among them the mode of conditions (*paccayākāra*; Vibh-a 583-584), and deals with the *paccayākāra* at length (e.g. Vibh-a 1004).

### Textual References
* **Subject Matter**: Commentary on the [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) in eighteen chapters (Vibh-a 5-14), with the Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga as the sixth (Vibh-a 580; the canonical text is Vibh 903).
* **Author**: Buddhaghosa (Vibh-a 2341, colophon).
