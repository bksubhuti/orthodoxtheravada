### Grammatical Analysis
**Anāthapiṇḍika**: [m.] Feeder of the destitute; Supporter of the helpless. Formed by *anātha* (helpless, destitute, without protection) + *piṇḍa* (lump of food) + *ika* (agent suffix). His given name was Sudatta.

### Orthodox Definition
Anāthapiṇḍika was the householder (*gahapati*) Sudatta, whom the Buddha declared foremost among givers (*dāyakānaṃ … sudatto gahapati anāthapiṇḍiko*, A-i 235:1) and the greatest lay patron of the Saṅgha. The commentaries explain that Sudatta was the name given by his parents, and that he became known as Anāthapiṇḍika, "feeder of the helpless", because, rich in every pleasure, free from the stain of meanness and endowed with compassion and other qualities, he constantly gave food to the helpless (Pj-i 526:1; Ps-i 365:6). The Aṅguttara commentary adds that he had aspired to this rank in the time of the Buddha Padumuttara and was born in Sāvatthī in the house of the merchant Sumana (Mp-i 1182:2-3).

In the Sudatta Sutta he came to Rājagaha on business, heard "a Buddha has arisen in the world", set out at night and went to the Buddha at Sītavana, who called him "Come, Sudatta" (S-i 3105:1-4, 3122:1-5). The Cūḷavagga says that at that very seat he gained the stainless Dhamma-eye, "whatever is subject to arising is subject to cessation" (Vin-iv 1591:3), the vision of the stream-enterer. His most famous act of devotion was buying Prince Jeta's grove: told it could not be given "even for a layer of coins" (*koṭisantharena*), he had gold brought and laid over the ground (Vin-iv 1599:4-10), and made it a monastery for the Saṅgha of the four quarters (Vin-iv 1628:14-15), the *[Jetavana](/glossary/jeta-vana/)*. The Dhammapada commentary says the Buddha spent nineteen rains-residences in it (*anāthapiṇḍikena kārite jetavanamahāvihāre ekūnavīsativassāni*, Dhp-a 46:2).

### Quote
> **Ekaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā rājagahe viharati sītavane. Tena kho pana samayena anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati rājagahaṃ anuppatto hoti kenacideva karaṇīyena. Assosi kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati – “buddho kira loke uppanno” ti.**  
> On one occasion the Blessed One was dwelling near Rājagaha in the Cool Grove. Now on that occasion Anāthapiṇḍika the householder had arrived at Rājagaha for some business. Anāthapiṇḍika the householder heard that a Buddha had indeed appeared in the world.  
>

### Quote
> **Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā rattiyā paccūsasamayaṃ paccuṭṭhāya abbhokāse caṅkamati. Addasā kho bhagavā anāthapiṇḍikaṃ gahapatiṃ dūratova āgacchantaṃ. Disvāna caṅkamā orohitvā paññatte āsane nisīdi. Nisajja kho bhagavā anāthapiṇḍikaṃ gahapatiṃ etadavoca – “ehi sudattā” ti. Atha kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati, nāmena maṃ bhagavā ālapatīti, haṭṭho udaggo tattheva bhagavato pādesu sirasā nipatitvā bhagavantaṃ etadavoca – “kacci, bhante, bhagavā sukhamasayitthā” ti?**  
> Now on that occasion, the Blessed One, having risen early in the morning, was walking up and down in the open air. And the Blessed One saw Anāthapiṇḍika the householder coming in the distance. Having seen him, he descended from the walking path and sat down on a prepared seat. Seated, the Blessed One said to Anāthapiṇḍika the householder: “Come, Sudatta.” Then Anāthapiṇḍika the householder, thinking, “The Blessed One calls me by name!”, joyful and elated, prostrated himself with his head at the Blessed One’s feet and said: “Venerable sir, did the Blessed One sleep well?”  
>

### Quote
> **Addasā kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati jetassa kumārassa uyyānaṃ – gāmato neva atidūre na accāsanne, gamanāgamanasampannaṃ, atthikānaṃ atthikānaṃ manussānaṃ abhikkamanīyaṃ, divā appākiṇṇaṃ, rattiṃ appasaddaṃ, appanigghosaṃ, vijanavātaṃ, manussarāhasseyyakaṃ, paṭisallānasāruppaṃ. Disvāna yena jeto kumāro tenupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā jetaṃ kumāraṃ etadavoca – “dehi me, ayyaputta, uyyānaṃ ārāmaṃ kātun” ti. “Adeyyo, gahapati, ārāmo api koṭisantharenā” ti. “Gahito, ayyaputta, ārāmo” ti. “Na, gahapati, gahito ārāmo” ti. Gahito na gahitoti vohārike mahāmatte pucchiṃsu. Mahāmattā evamāhaṃsu – “yato tayā, ayyaputta, aggho kato, gahito ārāmo” ti. Atha kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati sakaṭehi hiraññaṃ nibbāhāpetvā jetavanaṃ koṭisantharaṃ santharāpesi.**  
> The householder Anāthapiṇḍika saw the park of Prince Jeta, which was neither too far from the village nor too near, possessed of a means for going and returning, accessible to all people who wished to visit, during the day not disturbed by people, at night quiet, still, free from disturbances, suitable for human seclusion, and suitable for seclusion. Having seen it, he approached where Prince Jeta was, and after approaching, he said to Prince Jeta: “My lord, give me the park to make a monastery.” “The park is not to be given away, householder, even for a layer of ten million coins.” “It is bought, my lord!” “No, householder, it is not bought.” They asked the judicial chief ministers whether it was bought or not. The chief ministers said this: “Since a price was set by you, my lord, the park is bought.” Then the householder Anāthapiṇḍika, having had gold carted out, had Jeta's Grove covered with coins spread out, aligning their edges.  
>  
> — [Cūḷavagga](/glossary/culavagga/) (Senāsanakkhandhaka, Anāthapiṇḍikavatthu; heading Vin-iv 1571:1) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_04&page_number=306&name=c%C5%AB%E1%B8%B7avaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=QWRkYXPEgSBraG8gYW7EgXRoYXBp4bmH4biNaWtvIGdhaGFwYXRpIGpldGFzc2Ega3VtxIFyYXNzYQ%3D%3D)

### Quote
> **Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāmeti ettha sudatto nāma so gahapati mātāpitūhi katanāmavasena, sabbakāmasamiddhitāya tu vigatamalamaccheratāya karuṇādiguṇasamaṅgitāya ca niccakālaṃ anāthānaṃ piṇḍaṃ adāsi, tena anāthapiṇḍikoti saṅkhyaṃ gato.**  
> In the phrase Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme, the meaning should be understood as follows: that householder was named Sudatta by the name given by his mother and father, but because he was always giving alms to the helpless, he attained the count of Anāthapiṇḍika.  
>  
> — Paramatthajotikā (Khuddakapāṭha-aṭṭhakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_01&page_number=94&name=khuddakap%C4%81%E1%B9%ADhaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QW7EgXRoYXBp4bmH4biNaWthc3NhIMSBcsSBbWV0aSBldHRoYSBzdWRhdHRvIG7EgW1hIHNv)
