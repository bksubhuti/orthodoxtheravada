### Grammatical Analysis
**Bojjhaṅga**: [m.] factor of enlightenment. The Aṭṭhakathā, quoting the Paṭisambhidāmagga, gives the senses: "*bodhāya saṃvattantīti bojjhaṅgā, bujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, anubujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, paṭibujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, sambujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā*", they are *bojjhaṅgā* because they lead to awakening, because they awaken, awaken in accordance, awaken to, and fully awaken (As 1657:2). Another commentary says: "*bojjhaṅgāti bodhiyā, bodhissa vā aṅgāti bojjhaṅgā*", "factors of *bodhi*, or factors of the one who awakens" (Paṭis-a 2116:2).

### Orthodox Definition
The *bojjhaṅgas* are seven: "*satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo*" (D-iii 1541:1; Abhidh-s 650:1), that is, mindfulness, investigation of states, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration and equanimity. The Buddha says that these seven, developed and pursued, do not arise unarisen apart from the appearance of a Tathāgata (S-v 470:1-3), and that they lead from the near shore to the far shore (S-v 502:1).

In the Aggi Sutta the Buddha says when to develop which factors: when the mind is sluggish it is the time to develop investigation of states, energy and rapture, "because a sluggish mind is easy to arouse by these things"; when the mind is excited it is the time to develop tranquillity, concentration and equanimity (S-v 673:1-3, 679:1). The simile is of a man wanting to make a small fire flare up, who feeds it dry grass, dry cow dung and dry wood and blows on it (S-v 674:1-3). The Aṭṭhakathā quotes the Buddha as saying that mindfulness is useful everywhere (*sabbatthika*) (It-a 846:5).

### Quote (Mūla: Dīgha Nikāya)
> **“Satta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.**  
> “Seven factors of enlightenment: the factor of mindfulness, the factor of investigation of principles, the factor of energy, the factor of rapture, the factor of tranquility, the factor of concentration, and the factor of equanimity.”  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Saṃyutta Nikāya)
> **“Yasmiñca kho, bhikkhave, samaye līnaṃ cittaṃ hoti, kālo tasmiṃ samaye dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya, kālo vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya, kālo pītisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya. Taṃ kissa hetu? Līnaṃ, bhikkhave, cittaṃ taṃ etehi dhammehi susamuṭṭhāpayaṃ hoti.**  
> “But, bhikkhus, on an occasion when the mind becomes sluggish, it is timely to develop the enlightenment factor of discrimination of states, the enlightenment factor of energy, and the enlightenment factor of rapture. For what reason? Because the mind is sluggish, bhikkhus, and it is easy to arouse it with those things.  
>  
> — [Saṃyutta Nikāya](/glossary/samyutta-nikaya/) (Aggi Sutta, S-v 666 "3. Aggisuttaṃ") · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_sa_05&page_number=99&name=mah%C4%81vaggasa%E1%B9%83yuttap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=4oCY4oCYIFlhc21pw7FjYSBraG8sIGJoaWtraGF2ZSwgc2FtYXllIGzEq25h4bmD)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Atthasālinī)
> **Apica “bojjhaṅgāti kenaṭṭhena bojjhaṅgā? Bodhāya saṃvattantīti bojjhaṅgā, bujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, anubujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, paṭibujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā, sambujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā” ti (paṭi. ma.2.17) iminā paṭisambhidānayenāpi bojjhaṅgattho veditabbo.**  
> Furthermore (apica), “In what sense (kenaṭṭhena) are they factors of enlightenment (bojjhaṅga) called factors of enlightenment (bojjhaṅga)? “They contribute to (saṃvattanti) enlightenment (bodhāya), hence factors of enlightenment (bojjhaṅga). They awaken (bujjhati), hence factors of enlightenment. They awaken in accordance with (anubujjhati), hence factors of enlightenment. They perceive (paṭibujjhati), hence factors of enlightenment. They fully awaken (sambujjhati), hence factors of enlightenment.” Even by this (iminā) method (naya) of analytical knowledge (Paṭisambhidā), the meaning of factors of enlightenment (bojjhaṅga) should be known (veditabbo).  
>  
> — [Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/) (As 1657) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=262&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QXBpY2Eg4oCY4oCYIGJvampoYeG5hWfEgXRpIGtlbmHhua3hua1oZW5hIGJvampoYeG5hWfEgT8%3D)
