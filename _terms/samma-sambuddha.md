### Grammatical Analysis
**Sammā-sambuddha**: [m.] Fully Enlightened Buddha; Perfectly Awakened One. Formed by *sammā* (perfectly, fully, rightly) + *sam* (by oneself) + *buddha* (awakened).

### Orthodox Definition
A *Sammā-sambuddha* is the supreme class of awakened being. This is an individual who himself (*sāmaṃ*) awakens to the truths in teachings unheard before (*pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu*), and attains omniscience and mastery over the powers (Pp §22).

Unlike a *[Paccekabuddha](/glossary/paccekabuddha/)* (who also awakens to the truths himself, but attains neither omniscience nor mastery over the powers, Pp §23), a *Sammā-sambuddha* attains omniscience (*sabbaññutā*) and mastery over the powers (*balesu vasībhāva*). He is the arouser of the unarisen path, the declarer of the undeclared path (M-iii 165:13), and the commentary explains the word as one who has perfectly and by himself awakened to all phenomena (Mp-i 442:2). The Buddhas fulfil the ten perfections (*[Pāramī](/glossary/parami/)*) (Bv 806:1).

### Quote
> **Katamo ca puggalo sammāsambuddho? Idhekacco puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṃ saccāni abhisambujjhati; tattha ca sabbaññutaṃ pāpuṇāti, balesu ca vasībhāvaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati puggalo “sammāsambuddho”.**  
> 22. What sort of person is a Perfectly Enlightened One? Here a certain person who himself thoroughly understands the truths in regard to doctrines unheard of before, and attains omniscience therein, as well as mastery over the powers—this sort of person is said to be a Perfectly Enlightened One.  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Mahāpadāna Sutta* (DN 14, D-ii 4:1, "1. Mahāpadānasuttaṃ") – The biography of Vipassī and the earlier Buddhas, each called *bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho* (e.g. D-ii 12:1).
* **Sutta**: *Gopakamoggallāna Sutta* (MN 108, M-iii 163:1) – Ānanda says no bhikkhu has all the qualities of the Blessed One, *arahaṃ sammāsambuddho*, the arouser of the unarisen path (M-iii 165:12-13). *Sammāsambuddha Sutta* (SN 22.58, S-iii 316:1, 317:2) – the Tathāgata is liberated by non-clinging and is called *sammāsambuddha*.
* **Commentary**: *Manorathapūraṇī* (Aṅguttara commentary, Mp-i 442:2) – "Sammā sāmañca sabbadhammānaṃ buddhattā pana sammāsambuddho", referring to the Visuddhimagga's Buddhānussati exposition for the detail.
