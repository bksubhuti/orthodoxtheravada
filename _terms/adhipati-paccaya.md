### Grammatical Analysis
**Adhipatipaccaya**: [m.] Predominance condition. Formed by *adhipati* (overlord, sovereign, supreme ruler) + *paccaya* (condition).

### Orthodox Definition
*Adhipati-paccaya* is the third of the 24 Conditions. The Visuddhimagga defines it: "a phenomenon that is helpful by way of being the chief is the predominance condition (*jeṭṭhakaṭṭhena upakārako dhammo adhipatipaccayo*); it is of two kinds, conascent and object" (Vism-ii 921:1). The Atthasālinī and its Mūlaṭīkā explain *adhipati* as that which "occurs by overcoming" (*abhibhavitvā pavattanaṭṭhena*): the conascent dominant makes its concomitants follow its sway, and the object-dominant prevails over the states that take it as object (As-mt 131:1). 

This condition manifests in two distinct ways:
1. **Object Predominance** (*Ārammaṇādhipati*): When the mind takes an object that is so exceptionally desirable or powerful that the mind becomes utterly captivated and dominated by it.
2. **Conascent Predominance** (*Sahajātādhipati*): This relates to the four Bases of Spiritual Power (*Iddhipādas*): Desire (*[chanda](/glossary/chanda/)*), Energy (*[viriya](/glossary/viriya/)*), Consciousness (*[citta](/glossary/citta-base/)*), and Investigation (*[vīmaṃsā](/glossary/vimamsa/)*). The four are predominance conditions "but not all together" (*no ca kho ekato*): when the mind occurs with desire as chief, only desire is dominant, not the others (Vism-ii 921:2-3). The Paṭṭhāna gives each in turn (Paṭṭh-i 13:1-4), and the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha gives the same two kinds: a weighty object for the object-dominance, and the fourfold conascent dominance (Abhidh-s 725:1).

### Quote
> **Adhipatipaccayoti – chandādhipati chandasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ adhipatipaccayena paccayo. Vīriyādhipati vīriyasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ adhipatipaccayena paccayo. Cittādhipati cittasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ adhipatipaccayena paccayo. Vīmaṃsādhipati vīmaṃsasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ adhipatipaccayena paccayo.**  
> 3. Predominance condition: Predominant desire is related to the states associated with desire and to the materiality produced by them, by predominance condition. Predominant energy is related to the states associated with energy and to the materiality produced by them, by predominance condition. Predominant consciousness is related to the states associated with consciousness and to the materiality produced by them, by predominance condition. Predominant investigation is related to the states associated with investigation and to the materiality produced by them, by predominance condition.  
>

### Quote
> **Jeṭṭhakaṭṭhena upakārako dhammo adhipatipaccayo , so sahajātārammaṇavasena duvidho. Tattha “chandādhipati chandasampayuttakānaṃ dhammānaṃ taṃsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṃ adhipatipaccayena paccayo” tiādivacanato (paṭṭhā.1.3.3) chandavīriyacittavīmaṃsāsaṅkhātā cattāro dhammā adhipatipaccayoti veditabbā, no ca kho ekato. Yadā hi chandaṃ dhuraṃ chandaṃ jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā cittaṃ pavattati, tadā chandova adhipati, na itare.**  
> 597. A phenomenon that is helpful by way of being a chief is a dominant condition (adhipatipaccayo); it is of two kinds: co-nascence and object. Therein, because of the statement, “Predominant desire is a condition for states associated with desire and for mind-originated forms by way of dominant condition,” and so on, the four phenomena known as desire, energy, consciousness, and inquiry should be understood as dominant conditions, but not simultaneously. For when the mind occurs making desire the primary focus and making desire the chief, then only desire is the dominant factor, not the others.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (Ch. XVII, §597) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=165&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NTk3LiBKZeG5reG5rWhha2Hhua3hua1oZW5hIHVwYWvEgXJha28gZGhhbW1vIGFkaGlwYXRpcGFjY2F5byAs)

### Quote
> **Tattha garukatamārammaṇaṃ ārammaṇādhipativasena nāmānaṃ, sahajātādhipati catubbidhopi sahajātavasena sahajātānaṃ nāmarūpānanti ca duvidho hoti adhipatipaccayo.**  
> 27. Therein, a prominent object is a condition for mental states by way of object dominance; the fourfold co-nascence-predominance is a condition for co-arisen mind-and-form by way of co-arising. Thus dominant condition is of two kinds.

### Quote
> **Abhibhavitvā pavattanaṭṭhena maggo adhipati etesanti maggādhipatino.**  
> By way of occurring by overcoming (abhibhavitvā pavattanaṭṭhena), the path (maggo) is the dominant factor (adhipati) for these, therefore they are having the path as dominant (maggādhipatino).  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)-aṭṭhakathā (Atthasālinī) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_01&page_number=88&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QWJoaWJoYXZpdHbEgSBwYXZhdHRhbmHhua3hua1oZW5hIG1hZ2dvIGFkaGlwYXRpIGV0ZXNhbnRpIG1hZ2fEgWRoaXBhdGluby4%3D)

### Quote
> **Abhibhavitvā pavattanaṭṭhenāti sahajātādhipatipi pubbābhisaṅkhāravasena jeṭṭhakabhāve pavattamāno sahajāte attano vase anuvattayamāno te abhibhavitvā pavattati, ārammaṇādhipatipi tadārammaṇe dhamme tatheva attānaṃ anuvattayamāno te dhamme abhibhavitvā ārammaṇabhāvena pavattati, na paccuppannabhāvena, tasmā adhipatidvayampi saṅgahitanti veditabbaṃ.**  
> 'In the sense of occurring by overcoming' (abhibhavitvā pavattanaṭṭhenā) means that even the co-nascent predominance, occurring in the state of being a chief due to previous preparation, occurs by overcoming the co-nascent while causing them to follow its own will; the object-predominance also, while causing the co-nascent phenomena that have that as their object to follow itself in the state of being a chief, occurs by overcoming those phenomena by way of being an object, not by way of being present. Therefore, it should be understood that both types of predominance are included.  
>  
> — [Dhammasaṅgaṇī](/glossary/dhammasangani/)-mūlaṭīkā · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_bi_01&page_number=46&name=dhammasa%E1%B9%85ga%E1%B9%87%C4%ABm%C5%ABla%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QWJoaWJoYXZpdHbEgSBwYXZhdHRhbmHhua3hua1oZW7EgXRpIHNhaGFqxIF0xIFkaGlwYXRpcGkgcHViYsSBYmhpc2HhuYVraMSBcmF2YXNlbmEgamXhua3hua1oYWthYmjEgXZlIHBhdmF0dGFtxIFubw%3D%3D)
