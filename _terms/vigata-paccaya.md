### Grammatical Analysis
**Vigatapaccaya**: [m.] Disappearance condition. Formed by *vigata* (gone away, disappeared; past participle of *vigacchati*) + *paccaya* (condition). In the Paṭṭhāna it is used of *samanantaravigatā cittacetasikā dhammā*, the mind and mental factors that have "just disappeared" (Paṭṭh-i 60:1).

### Orthodox Definition
*Vigata-paccaya* is the twenty-third of the 24 conditions (Paṭṭh-i 8:1, 60:1).

The Mūla defines it in one line: *samanantaravigatā cittacetasikā dhammā paṭuppannānaṃ cittacetasikānaṃ dhammānaṃ vigatapaccayena paccayo*, mind and mental factors that have just disappeared are a condition for the mind and mental factors present now (Paṭṭh-i 60:1). The Aṭṭhakathā adds the mode of help: *te eva vigatabhāvena upakārakattā vigatapaccayo*, "those very same (*te eva*, the phenomena of the absence condition) are the disappearance condition because they help by the state of having disappeared" (Paṭṭh-a 1943:1; Vism-ii 946:1, XVII 615).

So the phenomena are the same as in the absence condition (*[Natthi-paccaya](/glossary/natthi-paccaya/)*, Paṭṭh-a 1942:1); the two differ in the way of help. In the absence condition the immediately ceased formless phenomena help by giving the opportunity for occurrence (*pavattiokāsassa dānena*); in the disappearance condition they help by their state of having gone (*vigatabhāvena*). The Ṭīkā likewise states it as help by way of disappearance (*vigatabhāvena upakārakā*, Abhidh-s-t 1673:1). It is the opposite of the non-disappearance condition (*avigatapaccaya*), the twenty-fourth.

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Vigatapaccayoti – samanantaravigatā cittacetasikā dhammā paṭuppannānaṃ cittacetasikānaṃ dhammānaṃ vigatapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 23. Disappearance condition: the just disappeared phenomena consciousness and mental factors are related to the present consciousness and mental factors by disappearance condition.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paṭṭhāna-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Te eva vigatabhāvena upakārakattā vigatapaccayo. Yathāha – samanantaravigatā cittacetasikā dhammā paṭuppannānaṃ cittacetasikānaṃ dhammānaṃ vigatapaccayena paccayoti.**  
> Those same things, because of being helpful due to their state of having disappeared (vigatabhāvena), are disappearance condition (vigatapaccayo). Just as he said: ‘Mind and mental factors that have just disappeared (samanantaravigatā) are a condition for mind and mental factors that are presently active by way of disappearance condition.’  
>  
> — Paṭṭhānappakaraṇa-aṭṭhakathā (on the vigatapaccaya) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_bi_03&page_number=352&name=pa%C3%B1capakara%E1%B9%87aa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGUgZXZhIHZpZ2F0YWJoxIF2ZW5hIHVwYWvEgXJha2F0dMSBIHZpZ2F0YXBhY2NheW8u)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Te eva vigatabhāvena upakārakattā vigatapaccayo. Yathāha – “samanantaravigatā cittacetasikā dhammā paṭuppannānaṃ cittacetasikānaṃ dhammānaṃ vigatapaccayena paccayo” ti.**  
> Those very same states, because they assist through their disappearance (vigatabhāvena), are the disappearance condition (vigatapaccayo). As it is said: “Mind and mental factors that have just disappeared (samanantaravigatā) are a condition for present mind and mental factors by way of disappearance condition.”  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, Paṭṭhānapaccayakathā, 615) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=172&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=VGUgZXZhIHZpZ2F0YWJoxIF2ZW5hIHVwYWvEgXJha2F0dMSBIHZpZ2F0YXBhY2NheW8u)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī)
> **Attano sabhāvāvigamanena appavattamānānaṃ vigatabhāvena upakārakāyeva dhammā vigatapaccayo.**  
> Phenomena that are helpful by way of their own disappearance to subsequent phenomena that do not occur because their own nature has not disappeared, are the disappearance condition (vigatapaccayo).  
>  
> — Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī-ṭīkā (on the vigatapaccaya) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_06&page_number=245&name=abhidhammatthavibh%C4%81vin%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=QXR0YW5vIHNhYmjEgXbEgXZpZ2FtYW5lbmEgYXBwYXZhdHRhbcSBbsSBbmHhuYMgdmlnYXRhYmjEgXZlbmEgdXBha8SBcmFrxIF5ZXZhIGRoYW1txIE%3D)
