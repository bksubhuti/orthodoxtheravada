### Grammatical Analysis
**Therīgāthā**: [f.] Verses of the Elder Nuns. Formed by *therī* (elder nun) + *gāthā* (verse, poem).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Therīgāthā* is the ninth book of the Khuddaka Nikāya, in the order given by the Paramatthajotikā on the Khuddakapāṭha (Khuddakapāṭha, Dhammapada, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Suttanipāta, Vimānavatthu, Petavatthu, Theragāthā, Therīgāthā, ...; Pj-i 24:5). Its closing verses give the count: "four hundred verses, eighty, and fourteen more" (*gāthāsatāni cattāri, asīti puna cuddasa*), spoken by one hundred and one elder nuns (*theriyekuttarasatā*), "all of them with the destruction of the taints" (*sabbā tā āsavakkhayā*) (Thī 1783-1784).

Like the *[Theragāthā](/glossary/theragatha/)*, these are poems of liberation. The nuns recount their past sufferings, such as the loss of a husband and children (Paṭācārā, Thī-a 1658-1659) and the decay of a once-beautiful body (the former courtesan Ambapālī, Thī-a 3238), and how the [Dhamma](/glossary/dhamma/) brought them freedom.

The commentary on these verses is the *Therīgāthā-aṭṭhakathā*. Its formal title is the *[Paramatthadīpanī](/glossary/paramatthadipani/)* ("Elucidation of the Ultimate Meaning", Thī-a 4534), and its colophon says it was composed by the elder Ācariya Dhammapāla, who lived at the Badaratittha monastery (Thī-a 4545). It supplies the occasion (*aṭṭhuppatti*) of each elder nun's verses, after first telling how the bhikkhunīs received going forth and higher ordination (Thī-a 6:2).

### Textual References
* **Canonical**: *Paṭācārātherīgāthā* (Thī 449, "10. Paṭācārātherīgāthā"; Thī-a 1658-1672) – her husband and two sons died on one journey, grief drove her mad, and the Buddha taught her; later, while washing her feet with water she had poured, she practised insight (Thī-a 1672:4).
* **Canonical**: *Ambapālītherīgāthā* (Thī 931, "1. Ambapālītherīgāthā"; Thī-a 3237-3238) – the former courtesan of Vesālī describes the impermanence of her once-beautiful body (verses 252 ff.).
* **Commentary**: *Therīgāthā-aṭṭhakathā* (formally titled *[Paramatthadīpanī](/glossary/paramatthadipani/)*, Thī-a 4534) – Dhammapāla's commentary, which gives the background narrative of each elder nun's verses.
