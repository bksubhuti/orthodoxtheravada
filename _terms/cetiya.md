### Grammatical Analysis
**Cetiya**: [nt.] shrine. The Aṭṭhakathā derives it: "*cayitabbanti cetiyaṃ, pūjetabbanti vuttaṃ hoti, citattā vā cetiyaṃ*", it is a *cetiya* because it is to be heaped up (*cayitabbaṃ*), that is, to be venerated; or because it is built up (*citattā*) (Pj-i 1068:1).

### Orthodox Definition
In the Mūla *cetiya* is a shrine as a place: "delightful, Ānanda, is Vesālī, delightful is the Udena shrine, the Gotamaka shrine, the Sattamba shrine, the Bahuputta shrine, the Sārandada shrine, the Cāpāla shrine" (D-ii 413:2). The Khuddakapāṭha speaks of gifts placed "in a shrine, or in the Saṅgha, or in an individual, or in guests" (Khp 214:1).

The Aṭṭhakathā says there are three kinds of *cetiya*: *paribhogacetiya*, *uddissakacetiya* and *dhātukacetiya*, and gives examples: the Bodhi tree is a shrine of use, a Buddha image is a shrine of indication, and a stupa containing a relic chamber with relics is a shrine of relics (Pj-i 1068:2-3). The Ṭīkā to the Vinaya commentary gives a different list of three: *paribhogacetiya*, *dhātucetiya* and *dhammacetiya* (Sp-t-i-a 711:6), and explains the *paribhogacetiya* as a shrine made by enshrining articles that were used (*paribhuttūpakaraṇāni*) (Sp-t-i-a 711:5; Sv-nt-ii 243:8). Another Ṭīkā adds that the bodily-relic shrine is weightier than the shrine of use, which is why "having saluted the shrine" is said first (Sv-pt-i 772:8).

### Quote (Mūla: Dīgha Nikāya)
> **Ekamantaṃ nisinnaṃ kho āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ bhagavā etadavoca – “ramaṇīyā, ānanda, vesālī, ramaṇīyaṃ udenaṃ cetiyaṃ, ramaṇīyaṃ gotamakaṃ cetiyaṃ, ramaṇīyaṃ sattambaṃ cetiyaṃ, ramaṇīyaṃ bahuputtaṃ cetiyaṃ, ramaṇīyaṃ sārandadaṃ cetiyaṃ, ramaṇīyaṃ cāpālaṃ cetiyaṃ.**  
> 167. While sitting on one side the Gracious One said this to venerable Ānanda: “Delightful, Ānanda, is Vesālī, delightful is the Udena shrine, delightful is the Gotamaka shrine, delightful is the Sattamba shrine, delightful is the Many Sons’ shrine, delightful is the Sārandada shrine, delightful is the Cāpāla shrine.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paramatthajotikā)
> **Tattha cayitabbanti cetiyaṃ , pūjetabbanti vuttaṃ hoti, citattā vā cetiyaṃ. Taṃ panetaṃ cetiyaṃ tividhaṃ hoti paribhogacetiyaṃ, uddissakacetiyaṃ, dhātukacetiyanti. Tattha bodhirukkho paribhogacetiyaṃ, buddhapaṭimā uddissakacetiyaṃ, dhātugabbhathūpā sadhātukā dhātukacetiyaṃ.**  
> Therein, a shrine is so called because it is to be worshipped; it is said to be something to be venerated, or a shrine is so called because it is constructed. Now, this shrine is of three kinds: a shrine of use, a shrine of indication, and a shrine of relics. Therein, the Bodhi tree is a shrine of use; a Buddha image is a shrine of indication; a stupa containing relic-chambers is a shrine of relics.  
>  
> — Paramatthajotikā (Khuddakapāṭha-aṭṭhakathā, Pj-i 1068) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_01&page_number=188&name=khuddakap%C4%81%E1%B9%ADhaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VGF0dGhhIGNheWl0YWJiYW50aSBjZXRpeWHhuYMgLCBwxatqZXRhYmJhbnRpIHZ1dHRh4bmD)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Sāratthadīpanī)
> **Paribhogacetiyaṭṭhānanti ettha paribhuttūpakaraṇāni nidahitvā kataṃ cetiyaṃ paribhogacetiyanti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Tividhañhi cetiyaṃ vadanti paribhogacetiyaṃ dhātucetiyaṃ dhammacetiyanti. Tattha paribhogacetiyaṃ vuttanayameva.**  
> Regarding the reading “a site for a paribhogacetiya,” a paribhogacetiya should be seen as a cetiya made having enshrined the articles used. For they speak of three types of cetiyas: paribhogacetiya, dhātucetiya, and dhammacetiya. There, the paribhogacetiya is as stated above.  
>  
> — Sāratthadīpanī (Sp-t-i-a 711) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_vi_01&page_number=172&name=s%C4%81ratthad%C4%ABpan%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=UGFyaWJob2dhY2V0aXlh4bmt4bmtaMSBbmFudGkgZXR0aGEgcGFyaWJodXR0xatwYWthcmHhuYfEgW5pIG5pZGFoaXR2xIEga2F0YeG5gyBjZXRpeWHhuYM%3D)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: Sīlakkhandhavagga-ṭīkā)
> **Paribhogacetiyato sārīrikacetiyaṃ garutaranti katvā “cetiyaṃ vanditvā”ti pubbakālakiriyāya vasena vuttaṃ.**  
> Thinking the bodily relic shrine is weightier than the object of enjoyment, he said “saluted the shrine” by way of preceding action.  
>  
> — Sīlakkhandhavagga-ṭīkā (Sv-pt-i 772) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_di_01_01&page_number=244&name=s%C4%ABlakkhandhavagga%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81&note=&selected_text=UGFyaWJob2dhY2V0aXlhdG8gc8SBcsSrcmlrYWNldGl5YeG5gyBnYXJ1dGFyYW50aSBrYXR2xIEg4oCY4oCYIGNldGl5YeG5gw%3D%3D)
