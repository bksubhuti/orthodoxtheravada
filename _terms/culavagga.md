### Grammatical Analysis
**Cūḷavagga**: [m.] The Minor Division; The Lesser Chapter. Formed by *culla* (small, minor, lesser) + *vagga* (chapter, division).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Cūḷavagga* is the second volume of the *[Khandhaka](/glossary/khandhaka/)* section of the [Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/) Piṭaka, containing 12 chapters. Despite being named the "minor" division, it is just as legally critical as the *[Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/)*.

It deals heavily with the internal discipline, architecture, and historical crises of the [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/). It outlines the grueling procedures for placing monks on probation (*parivāsa*) for severe offenses, the rules for building and furnishing monasteries (*[senāsana](/glossary/senasana/)*), and the seven legal methods for settling disputes (*[adhikaraṇasamatha](/glossary/adhikaranasamatha/)*).

Historically, the *Cūḷavagga* contains three defining narratives of the Theravāda tradition: the founding of the [Bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/) Saṅgha (and the Eight Heavy Rules), the schism caused by Devadatta's assassination attempts, and the detailed accounts of the First and Second Buddhist Councils, cementing the textual authority of the Piṭakas.

### Quote
> **Ubhayāni pātimokkhānīti bhikkhubhikkhunīpātimokkhavasena.**  
> Both Pātimokkhas refers to the [Bhikkhu](/glossary/bhikkhu/) [Pātimokkha](/glossary/patimokkha/) and the Bhikkhunī Pātimokkha.  
> 
> **Dve vibhaṅgānīti bhikkhubhikkhunīvibhaṅgavaseneva dve vibhaṅgāni.**  
> Two Vibhaṅgas means two Vibhaṅgas in terms of the Bhikkhu [Vibhaṅga](/glossary/vibhanga/) and the Bhikkhunī Vibhaṅga.  
> 
> **Dvāvīsati khandhakānīti mahāvaggacūḷavaggesu āgatāni dvāvīsati khandhakāni.**  
> Twenty-two Khandhakas refers to the twenty-two Khandhakas that appear in the Mahāvagga and Cūḷavagga.  
> 
> — *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)*

### Textual References
* **Vinaya**: *Cūḷavagga* – The final two chapters are the primary canonical source for the history of the First Council at Rājagaha and the Second Council at Vesālī.
* **Commentary**: *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* – Providing the orthodox interpretation of how schisms and disputes are to be legally extinguished.
