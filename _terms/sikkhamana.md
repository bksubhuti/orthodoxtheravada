### Grammatical Analysis
**Sikkhāmānā**: [f.] Probationary nun; a woman in training. Derived from *sikkhati* (to train, learn) + present participle suffix *māna*. 

### Orthodox Definition
A *Sikkhāmānā* represents an intermediate status unique to the female monastic order, situated between a novice (*[sāmaṇerī](/glossary/samaneri/)*) and a fully ordained nun (*[bhikkhunī](/glossary/bhikkhuni/)*). The Vinaya defines her as one who has trained in the six *dhammas* (*chasu dhammesu*) for two years (Vin-ii 1162).

Before she can receive higher ordination, she is given the Saṅgha's agreement to train (*sikkhāsammuti*) for two years in the six rules, and she undertakes, rule by rule, for two years not to transgress them (Vin-ii-b 3292, 3296). The Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī identifies the six *dhammas* as the six training rules, beginning with abstinence from killing and ending with abstinence from untimely eating (Kkh 2560:2). The Vinaya lists them: abstinence from killing, from taking what is not given, from unchastity, from false speech, from liquor and spirits (the occasion of heedlessness), and from eating at the wrong time (Vin-ii-b 3296:2-7).

A nun who ordains a probationer who has not trained (*asikkhitasikkhā*) for two years in the six rules incurs a *pācittiya* offence. In the word-analysis, "has not trained" means either that the training was not given, or that it was given but is interrupted (*kupitā*; Vin-ii-b 3298, 3300). The Samantapāsādikā adds that these six trainings must be given even to a woman gone forth who is sixty years old, and that one not trained in them should not be ordained (Sp-ii-b 1348:3).

The reason for the training period, in the Samantapāsādikā, is that a woman is impulsive: if she tries to fulfil the precepts without having trained for two years in the six rules she suffers, but having trained first, she will not be distressed later and will overcome the difficulty (Sp-ii-b 1347:2). The commentary on the rule also explains the name: she gets it "because of training in those six dhammas" (Kkh 2560:4).

> **Sikkhamānā nāma dve vassāni chasu dhammesu sikkhitasikkhā.**  
> ‘A female probationer’ means: one training in the six rules for two years.

Abrahmacariyā veramaṇiṃ dve vassāni avītikkamma samādānaṃ samādiyāmi. Musāvādā veramaṇiṃ dve vassāni avītikkamma samādānaṃ samādiyāmi. Surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇiṃ dve vassāni avītikkamma samādānaṃ samādiyāmi. Vikālabhojanā veramaṇiṃ dve vassāni avītikkamma samādānaṃ samādiyāmī” ti.**  
> That probationer should be told: ‘Speak thus:’ ‘I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from onslaught on creatures. I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from taking what is not given. I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from unchastity. I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from false speech. I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from occasions for sloth (arising from) fermented liquor and spirits and strong drink. I undertake for two years not to transgress the resolution of abstinence from eating at the wrong time.’  
>

### Textual References

* **[Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/)**: *Bhikkhunīvibhaṅga*, Pācittiya (§1077-1081, Vin-ii-b 3292-3300) – the allowance of the *sikkhāsammuti*, the six undertakings, and the offence of ordaining one who has not trained.
* **Commentary**: *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* (Sp-ii-b 1347-1348) and *Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī* (Kkh 2560).
