### Grammatical Analysis
**Asekha**: [m.; adj.] a non-learner; one beyond training; an adept; master. Formed by the negative prefix *a* (not/no more) + *[sekha](/glossary/sekha/)* (learner/trainee). Signifies one who has fully completed the entire spiritual curriculum.

### Orthodox Definition
An *Asekha* is the absolute antithesis of the *[puthujjana](/glossary/puthujjana/)* and the ultimate fulfillment of the *[sekha](/glossary/sekha/)*. This designation applies exclusively to the eighth noble individual: the **[Arahat](/glossary/arahat/)** who stands firmly in the Supramundane Fruition of Arahatship (*arahatta-phalaṭṭho*).

An asekha has completely finished the task of spiritual purification. Because they have uprooted all ten fetters and destroyed the last traces of ignorance and influxes (*āsavakkhaya*), they no longer have any further training rules to master, defilements to suppress, or insight paths to cultivate. 

The mind of an asekha operates in a state of flawless, unshakeable equanimity. Their choices no longer construct *[kusala](/glossary/kusala/)* or *[akusala](/glossary/akusala/)* [kamma](/glossary/kamma/); their mental actions are classified entirely as functional (*[kiriya-citta](/glossary/kiriya-citta/)*), passing away cleanly without generating any karmic residue inside [saṃsāra](/glossary/samsara/).

### Quote
> **‘“Asekho asekho ’ ti, bhante, vuccati. Kittāvatā bhante, bhikkhu asekho hotī” ti? “Idha, bhikkhu, bhikkhu asekhāya sammādiṭṭhiyā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāsaṅkappena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāvācāya samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammākammantena samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāājīvena samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāvāyāmena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāsatiyā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāsamādhinā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāñāṇena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāvimuttiyā samannāgato hoti. Evaṃ kho, bhikkhu, bhikkhu asekho hotī” ti.**  
> “Venerable sir, they speak of ‘one beyond training’, ‘one beyond training’. How is it, venerable sir, that a bhikkhu is one beyond training?” “Here, bhikkhu, a bhikkhu is possessed of the one beyond training’s right view, possessed of the one beyond training’s right intention, possessed of the one beyond training’s right speech, possessed of the one beyond training’s right action, possessed of the one beyond training’s right livelihood, possessed of the one beyond training’s right effort, possessed of the one beyond training’s right mindfulness, possessed of the one beyond training’s right concentration, possessed of the one beyond training’s right knowledge, possessed of the one beyond training’s right liberation. “Here, bhikkhu, a bhikkhu is possessed of the one beyond training’s right view, possessed of the one beyond training’s right intention, possessed of the one beyond training’s right speech, possessed of the one beyond training’s right action, possessed of the one beyond training’s right livelihood, possessed of the one beyond training’s right effort, possessed of the one beyond training’s right mindfulness, possessed of the one beyond training’s right concentration, possessed of the one beyond training’s right knowledge, possessed of the one beyond training’s right liberation. “That is how, bhikkhu, a bhikkhu is one beyond training.”  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Paṭhamaasekha Sutta* (AN 10, A-x 1019-1021) – The one beyond training is possessed of the ten *asekha* factors: the eight path factors plus right knowledge (*sammāñāṇa*) and right liberation (*sammāvimutti*).
* **Sutta**: *Asekha Sutta* (AN 5, A-v 798-800, CST §108) – The five *asekha* aggregates: virtue, concentration, wisdom, liberation, knowledge and vision of liberation.
* **Sutta**: A-ii 80:5 – "There are two worthy of offerings in the world: the learner and the one beyond training" (*sekho ca asekho ca*).
