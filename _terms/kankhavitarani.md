### Grammatical Analysis
**Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī**: [f.] The Overcomer of Doubt; The Dispeller of Uncertainty. Formed by *kaṅkhā* (doubt, uncertainty) + *vitaraṇī* (overcoming, crossing over).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī* is Venerable Buddhaghosa's specialized commentary dedicated exclusively to the *[Pātimokkha](/glossary/patimokkha/)* (the bare code of 227 rules for monks and 311 rules for nuns). 

While the *[Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/)* covers the entire massive [Vinaya](/glossary/vinaya/) Piṭaka, the *Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī* serves as a highly concentrated, rapid-reference manual specifically for the fortnightly recitation days (*[uposatha](/glossary/uposatha/)*). It provides precise definitions of key terms, explains exactly what constitutes a breach of a rule, and outlines the correct method for confessing offenses. By clearly defining the boundaries of monastic behavior, it fulfills its title by removing all "doubt" from the minds of the monastics regarding their purity.

### Quote
> **Tattha sañjātakaṅkhānaṃ, bhikkhūnaṃ tassa vaṇṇanaṃ; Kaṅkhāvitaraṇatthāya, paripuṇṇavinicchayaṃ. Mahāvihāravāsīnaṃ, vācanāmagganissitaṃ; Vattayissāmi nāmena, kaṅkhāvitaraṇiṃ subhanti.**  
> I shall expound an explanation for those bhikkhus who have developed doubts, in order to overcome those doubts, with complete and definite decisions. Relying upon the lineage of recitation of the residents of the Great Monastery, I shall expound the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī, which is beautiful in its elaboration.  
>
