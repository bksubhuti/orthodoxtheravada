### Grammatical Analysis
**Khuddakapāṭha**: [m.] the Minor Readings. A compound of *khuddaka* (minor, small) + *pāṭha* (reading, recitation). The Paramatthajotikā places it as the ninefold *khuddakapāṭha* among the *khuddakāni*, the "minor teachings" (Pj-i 21:1-2; 25:7).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Khuddakapāṭha* is the first book in the list of the Khuddaka Nikāya (Pj-i 24:5). Its nine texts, as the TPR headings and the Paramatthajotikā give them, are:
1. *Saraṇattayaṃ* (the Three Refuges, Khp 4:1)
2. *Dasasikkhāpadaṃ* (the Ten Training Rules, Khp 15:1)
3. *Dvattiṃsākāro* (the thirty-two parts of the body, Khp 27:1)
4. *Kumārapañhā* (Khp 36:1)
5. *Maṅgalasuttaṃ* (Khp 48:1)
6. *Ratanasuttaṃ* (Khp 87:1)
7. *Tirokuṭṭasuttaṃ* (Khp 153:1)
8. *Nidhikaṇḍasuttaṃ* (Khp 193:1)
9. *Mettasuttaṃ* (Khp 244:1)

The Paramatthajotikā says that the ninefold Khuddakapāṭha is "the beginning according to the lineage of teachers, placed in the stream of recitation, not according to what was spoken by the Blessed One" (*ādi ācariyaparamparāya vācanāmaggaṃ āropitavasena na bhagavatā vuttavasena*, Pj-i 25:7). See *[Khuddaka-nikāya](/glossary/khuddaka-nikaya/)*.

### Quote (Mūla: Khuddakapāṭha, Saraṇattaya)
> **Saraṇattayaṃ Buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi; Dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi; Saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.**  
> The Three Refuges. I go for refuge to the Buddha. I go for refuge to the Dhamma. I go for refuge to the Saṅgha.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Paramatthajotikā)
> **Imāni suttantapiṭakapariyāpannāni atthato vivarituṃ vibhajituṃ uttānīkātuñca adhippetāni khuddakāni, tesampi khuddakānaṃ saraṇasikkhāpadadvattiṃsākārakumārapañhamaṅgalasutta- ratanasuttatirokuṭṭanidhikaṇḍamettasuttānaṃ vasena navappabhedo khuddakapāṭho ādi ācariyaparamparāya vācanāmaggaṃ āropitavasena na bhagavatā vuttavasena.**  
> The minor teachings that are included in the Sutta Piṭaka and are intended to be elucidated, analysed, and made plain are these minor teachings; among them, the ninefold Khuddakapāṭha—consisting of the Refuges, Training Rules, Thirty-two Parts, Kumārapañhā, Maṅgala Sutta, Ratana Sutta, Tirokuṭṭa Sutta, Nidhikaṇḍa Sutta, and Mettā Sutta—is the beginning, having been placed into the stream of oral transmission by the lineage of teachers, not by the Blessed One's own speaking.  
>  
> — [Paramatthajotikā](/glossary/paramatthajotika/) (Khuddakapāṭha-aṭṭhakathā, Ganthārambhakathā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_ku_01&page_number=2&name=khuddakap%C4%81%E1%B9%ADhaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=SW3EgW5pIHN1dHRhbnRhcGnhua1ha2FwYXJpecSBcGFubsSBbmkgYXR0aGF0byB2aXZhcml0deG5gyB2aWJoYWppdHXhuYMgdXR0xIFuxKtrxIF0dcOxY2E%3D)
