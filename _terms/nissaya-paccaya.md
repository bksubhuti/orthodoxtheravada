### Grammatical Analysis
**Nissayapaccaya**: [m.] Dependence condition; support condition. Formed by *nissaya* (support, foundation, reliance) + *paccaya* (condition). TPR's translation of the Paṭṭhāna renders it "dependence condition".

### Orthodox Definition
*Nissayapaccaya* is the eighth in the list of conditions at the head of the *[Paṭṭhāna](/glossary/patthana/)* (Paṭṭh-i 8:1: *hetupaccayo, ārammaṇapaccayo, adhipatipaccayo, anantarapaccayo, samanantarapaccayo, sahajātapaccayo, aññamaññapaccayo, nissayapaccayo ...*). In the Paccayaniddesa it is explained by these cases: the four immaterial aggregates are conditions for one another; the four great elements for one another; at the moment of descent into the womb, name-and-form for each other; consciousness and mental factors for the materiality arisen from thought; the great elements for derived materiality; and the sense bases for the consciousness elements, for example "the eye sense base is a condition for the eye-consciousness element and its associated states by dependence condition" (Paṭṭh-i 35-36).

The *Visuddhimagga* defines it: "A phenomenon that is helpful by way of standing ground (*adhiṭṭhānākārena*) and by way of support (*nissayākārena*) is *nissayapaccaya*, like the earth for trees and the canvas for paintings" (*tarucittakammādīnaṃ pathavīpaṭādayo viya*). The *Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha* gives three kinds: mind and mental factors as conditions for one another and for co-arisen forms; the great elements for one another and for derived forms; and the six bases for the seven consciousness elements.

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Nissayapaccayoti – cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā aññamaññaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ aññamaññaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Cittacetasikā dhammā cittasamuṭṭhānānaṃ rūpānaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Mahābhūtā upādārūpānaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Cakkhāyatanaṃ cakkhuviññāṇadhātuyā taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo. Yaṃ rūpaṃ nissāya manodhātu ca manoviññāṇadhātu ca vattanti, taṃ rūpaṃ manodhātuyā ca manoviññāṇadhātuyā ca taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 8. Dependence condition: The four immaterial aggregates are mutually related to one another by dependence condition. The four great elements are mutually related to one another by dependence condition. At the moment of descent into the womb, name-and-form are mutually related to each other by dependence condition. The states of consciousness and mental factors are related to materiality rooted in thought by dependence condition. The great elements are related to derived materiality by dependence condition. Eye sense base is related to eye-consciousness-element and its associated states by dependence condition. Depending on that materiality, mind-element and mind-consciousness-element arise; that materiality is related to mind-element, mind-consciousness-element, and their associated states by dependence condition.  
>

### Quote (Abhidhamma: Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha)
> **Cittacetasikā dhammā aññamaññaṃ sahajātarūpānañca, mahābhūtā aññamaññaṃ upādārūpānañca, cha vatthūni sattannaṃ viññāṇadhātūnanti ca tividho hoti nissayapaccayo.**  
> 30. Support condition is threefold: mind and mental factors are a condition for one another and for co-arisen forms; the great elements are a condition for one another and for derived forms; and the six bases are a condition for the seven consciousness elements.  
>  
> — [Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha](/glossary/abhidhammatthasangaha/) (Paṭṭhānanaya, §30) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_05&page_number=56&name=abhidhammatthasa%E1%B9%85gaho&note=&selected_text=MzAuIENpdHRhY2V0YXNpa8SBIGRoYW1txIEgYcOxw7FhbWHDscOxYeG5gyBzYWhhasSBdGFyxatwxIFuYcOxY2EsIG1haMSBYmjFq3TEgQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga)
> **Adhiṭṭhānākārena nissayākārena ca upakārako dhammo nissayapaccayo tarucittakammādīnaṃ pathavīpaṭādayo viya. So “cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo” ti evaṃ sahajāte vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Chaṭṭho panettha koṭṭhāso “cakkhāyatanaṃ cakkhuviññāṇadhātuyā … pe … sota … ghāna … jivhā … kāyāyatanaṃ kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ nissayapaccayena paccayo.**  
> 601. A phenomenon that is helpful by way of standing ground and by way of support is a dependence condition (nissayapaccayo), like the earth for trees and canvas for paintings. It should be understood in the very manner stated for co-nascence, such as: “The four formless aggregates are conditions for one another by way of dependence condition.” The sixth section here is analyzed thus: “The eye sense base is a condition for the eye-consciousness element … ear … nose … tongue … the body sense base is a condition for the body-consciousness element and its associated phenomena by way of dependence condition.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, Paṭṭhānapaccayakathā, 601) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=167&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=NjAxLiBBZGhp4bmt4bmtaMSBbsSBa8SBcmVuYSBuaXNzYXnEgWvEgXJlbmEgY2EgdXBha8SBcmFrbyBkaGFtbW8%3D)
