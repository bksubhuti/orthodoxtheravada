### Grammatical Analysis
**Paṭisandhi**: [f.] Rebirth-linking. Formed by *paṭi* + *sandhi* (joining). The Visuddhimagga: "*pacchimaṃ bhavantarādipaṭisandhānato paṭisandhīti vuccati*", the later consciousness is called *paṭisandhi* because it links to the beginning of the next existence, while the earlier is called *cuti* because it passes away from the former existence (Vism-ii 1047).

### Orthodox Definition
*Paṭisandhi* is the first consciousness of a new existence, the rebirth-linking consciousness. In the Abhidhamma handbook it is performed by nineteen resultant consciousnesses, which also perform the functions of life-continuum (*bhavaṅga*) and death (*cuti*): the two investigating consciousnesses accompanied by equanimity, the eight great resultants, and the nine fine-material and immaterial resultants (Abhidh-s 20). It is of four kinds: woeful, fortunate sense-sphere, fine-material and formless (Abhidh-s 15). For example, the unwholesome-resultant investigating consciousness "becomes the rebirth-linking at the moment of descent in the woeful plane, then life-continuum, and at the end it is death" (Abhidh-s 16). Including the non-percipient beings' material rebirth-linking, the Visuddhimagga counts twenty (Vism-ii 1007).

The Visuddhimagga teaches that rebirth-linking occurs immediately after (*anantarameva*) the death-consciousness, which has a past or a not-to-be-stated object, and that there is no death-consciousness with a present object (Vism-ii 1008). It also teaches that nothing migrates: "this consciousness has not come here from the former existence, nor has it manifested without causes such as kamma, volitional formations, the inclination of craving and the object" (Vism-ii 1047; see also 1044-1045: the arising of mere material and immaterial states is not a being or a soul). The Paṭṭhāna speaks of "the moment of rebirth-linking" (*paṭisandhikkhaṇe*): dependent on one resultant aggregate, three aggregates and kamma-produced materiality arise, and so on (Paṭṭh-i 160). In the Paṭisambhidāmagga rebirth-linking is listed as suffering (*paṭisandhi dukkhaṃ, appaṭisandhi sukhaṃ*, Paṭis 125). The handbook arranges kamma by order of ripening: weighty, proximate, habitual and done (*garukaṃ, āsannaṃ, āciṇṇaṃ, kaṭattākammaṃ*, Abhidh-s 51).

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭṭhāna)
> **Abyākataṃ dhammaṃ paṭicca abyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā – vipākābyākataṃ kiriyābyākataṃ ekaṃ khandhaṃ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṃ, tayo khandhe paṭicca eko khandho cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṃ, dve khandhe paṭicca dve khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṃ; paṭisandhikkhaṇe vipākābyākataṃ ekaṃ khandhaṃ paṭicca tayo khandhā kaṭattā ca rūpaṃ, tayo khandhe paṭicca eko khandho kaṭattā ca rūpaṃ, dve khandhe paṭicca dve khandhā kaṭattā ca rūpaṃ;**  
> Dependent on an indeterminate state, an indeterminate state arises by way of root condition – dependent on one resultant indeterminate or functional indeterminate aggregate, three aggregates and mind-produced materiality arise; dependent on three aggregates, one aggregate and mind-produced materiality arise; dependent on two aggregates, two aggregates and mind-produced materiality arise; at the moment of rebirth-linking, dependent on one resultant indeterminate aggregate, three aggregates and kamma-produced materiality arise; dependent on three aggregates, one aggregate and kamma-produced materiality arise; dependent on two aggregates, two aggregates and kamma-produced materiality arise;  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Paṭisambhidāmagga)
> **Paṭisandhi dukkhaṃ, appaṭisandhi sukhanti abhiññeyyaṃ.**  
> Rebirth-linking is suffering, non-rebirth-linking is bliss; this is to be directly known.  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga, after the death-consciousness)
> **Evaṃ tīsu ārammaṇesu pavattamānā pana paṭisandhi yasmā atītārammaṇassa vā navattabbārammaṇassa vā cuticittassa anantarameva pavattati. Paccuppannārammaṇaṃ pana cuticittaṃ nāma natthi. Tasmā dvīsu ārammaṇesu aññatarārammaṇāya cutiyā anantarā tīsu ārammaṇesu aññatarārammaṇāya paṭisandhiyā sugatiduggativasena pavattanākāro veditabbo.**  
> However, although rebirth-linking occurs with these three kinds of objects, it occurs immediately after (anantarameva) a death-consciousness (cuticittassa) that has either a past object or a not-to-be-stated object. There is indeed no such thing as a death-consciousness with a present object. Therefore, it should be understood how rebirth-linking occurs with one of three objects immediately after a death with one of two objects, by way of happy and unhappy destinations.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, Vism-ii 1008) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=181&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=RXZh4bmDIHTEq3N1IMSBcmFtbWHhuYdlc3UgcGF2YXR0YW3EgW7EgSBwYW5hIHBh4bmtaXNhbmRoaQ%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Visuddhimagga, no transmigration)
> **Tadevaṃ pavattamānaṃ taṇhāvijjānaṃ appahīnattā avijjāpaṭicchāditādīnave tasmiṃ visaye taṇhā nāmeti, sahajātasaṅkhārā khipanti. Taṃ santativasena taṇhāya nāmiyamānaṃ saṅkhārehi khippamānaṃ orimatīrarukkhavinibaddharajjumālambitvā mātikātikkamako viya purimañca nissayaṃ jahati, aparañca kammasamuṭṭhāpitaṃ nissayaṃ assādayamānaṃ vā anassādayamānaṃ vā ārammaṇādīhiyeva paccayehi pavattatīti. Ettha ca purimaṃ cavanato cuti. Pacchimaṃ bhavantarādipaṭisandhānato paṭisandhīti vuccati. Tadetaṃ nāpi purimabhavā idhāgataṃ, nāpi tato kammasaṅkhāranativisayādihetuṃ vinā pātubhūtanti veditabbaṃ.**  
> As it thus proceeds, because craving and ignorance have not been abandoned, craving inclines that consciousness toward that object—which has the danger hidden by ignorance—and conascent volitional formations impel it. That consciousness, being inclined by craving and impelled by volitional formations by way of continuity, relinquishes its former support—like a man crossing a ditch by hanging onto a rope tied to a tree on the near bank—and operates by way of conditions such as the object, whether or not it relishes the next support produced by kamma. And here, because it passes away from the former existence, it is called "passing away" (cuti). Because it links to the beginning of the next existence, it is called "rebirth-linking" (paṭisandhi). It should be understood that this consciousness has not come here from the former existence, nor has it manifested without causes such as kamma, volitional formations, the inclination (of craving), and the object.  
>  
> — [Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/) (XVII, Vism-ii 1046-1047) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=annya_bi_02&page_number=187&name=visuddhimaggo+%28du%29&note=&selected_text=VGFkZXZh4bmDIHBhdmF0dGFtxIFuYeG5gyB0YeG5h2jEgXZpamrEgW5h4bmDIGFwcGFoxKtuYXR0xIEgYXZpamrEgXBh4bmtaWNjaMSBZGl0xIFkxKtuYXZlIHRhc21p4bmD)
