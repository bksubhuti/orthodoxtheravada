### Grammatical Analysis
**Passaddhi**: [f.] tranquility; calming down; serenity; quietude. The Aṭṭhasālinī derives it from *passambhati*: "that by which those states become tranquil, become free from agitation (*vigatadarathā*), and attain complete calm" (As 1333:2). The Dhammasaṅgaṇī defines it with the series *passaddhi paṭipassaddhi passambhanā paṭipassambhanā paṭipassambhitattaṃ*.

### Orthodox Definition
In the Theravāda [Abhidhamma](/glossary/abhidhamma/), *passaddhi* is a beautiful mental factor split into a pair, *kāyapassaddhi* and *cittapassaddhi*, among the nineteen mental factors common to all beautiful consciousness (*sobhanasādhāraṇa*, which "are found in all fifty-nine beautiful consciousnesses", Abhidh-s 129:1):
1. *Kāya-passaddhi*: Tranquility of the feeling, perception and formations aggregates (Dhs 430:2; "body" here means these three aggregates, As 1249:3).
2. *Citta-passaddhi*: Tranquility of the consciousness aggregate itself (Dhs 431:2).

It functions as the fifth factor of enlightenment (*passaddhi-sambojjhaṅga*), which in the Ānāpānassati Sutta follows rapture: "For one whose mind is full of rapture, the body is tranquil, the mind is tranquil" (*pītimanassa kāyopi passambhati, cittampi passambhati*, M-iii 460:1). The *[Atthasālinī](/glossary/atthasalini/)* says the two together have the characteristic of calming the fever of body and mind (*kāyacittadarathavūpasamalakkhaṇā*), the function of suppressing that fever, and the manifestation of unshakeable coolness; they are to be understood as opposed to defilements such as restlessness (*uddhacca*) that make body and mind unrestful (As 1249:4-5).

It leads on to concentration (*[samādhi](/glossary/samadhi/)*), for as the Buddha stated in the same sutta: *"Passaddhakāyassa sukhino cittaṃ samādhiyati"* (In one whose body is tranquil and who feels pleasure, the mind becomes concentrated; M-iii 461:1).

### Quote
> **Katamā tasmiṃ samaye kāyapassaddhi hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye vedanākkhandhassa saññākkhandhassa saṅkhārakkhandhassa passaddhi paṭipassaddhi passambhanā paṭipassambhanā paṭipassambhitattaṃ– ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye kāyapassaddhi hoti. 41. Katamā tasmiṃ samaye cittapassaddhi hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye viññāṇakkhandhassa passaddhi paṭipassaddhi passambhanā paṭipassambhanā paṭipassambhitattaṃ – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittapassaddhi hoti.**  
> 40. What is bodily tranquility at that time? Whatever tranquility, subsidence, calming (passambhanā), special calming (paṭipassambhanā), the state of having specially calmed (paṭipassambhitattaṃ), exists at that time in the feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, the volitional formations aggregate—this is bodily tranquility at that time. 41. What is mental tranquility at that time? Whatever tranquility, subsidence, calming (passambhanā), special calming (paṭipassambhanā), the state of having specially calmed (paṭipassambhitattaṃ), exists at that time in the consciousness aggregate—this is mental tranquility at that time.  
>
