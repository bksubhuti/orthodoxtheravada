### Grammatical Analysis
**Parivāra**: [m.] Accessory; appendix; retinue. From *pari* + *vāra*. The Aṭṭhakathā's opening verse places the Parivāra "immediately after the Khandhakas" (*khandhakānaṃ anantarā*, Sp-v 7). The Aṭṭhakathā calls it "*anuloma*", the accordant, among the terms *sutta, vinaya, anuloma* (Sp-v 302).

### Orthodox Definition
The *Parivāra* is the fifth book of the Pāḷi Vinaya in TPR (Pārājika, Pācittiya, Mahāvagga, Cūḷavagga, Parivāra: Vin-i to Vin-v), and the third division of the Vinayapiṭaka after the two Vibhaṅgas and the Khandhakas. The Samantapāsādikā counts it in the Vinaya that Mahākassapa questioned and Upāli answered at the first council: "the Vinayapiṭaka consisting of both Vibhaṅgas, the Khandhakas and the Parivāra" (Sp-i 111). The Aṭṭhakathā explains the terms in a Vinaya passage thus: "*sutta*" is the two Vibhaṅgas, "*vinaya*" is the Khandhaka, and "*anuloma*" is the Parivāra (Sp-v 302).

Its first chapter, the Soḷasamahāvāra, asks of each rule where it was laid down, concerning whom, and in what case, whether there is a laying down, a supplementary laying down, or one not yet occurred (Vin-v 8). The Ṭīkā describes the plan: the Paññattivāra, Katāpattivāra, Vipattivāra, Saṅgahavāra, Samuṭṭhānavāra, Adhikaraṇavāra, Samathavāra and Samuccayavāra, repeated again by way of condition (*paccaya*), together making the sixteen sections of the Mahāvibhaṅga (Sp-t-i-a 250).

### Quote (Mūla: Parivāra)
> **Yaṃ tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena paṭhamaṃ pārājikaṃ kattha paññattaṃ, kaṃ ārabbha, kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ? Atthi tattha paññatti, anupaññatti, anuppannapaññatti? Sabbatthapaññatti, padesapaññatti?**  
> 1. Where was the first offence involving Defeat laid down by that Lord who knows, who sees, the Perfected One, the fully Self-Awakened One, concerning whom, and on what subject? Is there there a laying down, a supplementary laying down, a laying down that has not occurred? A laying down (that holds good) everywhere, or a laying down (that holds good only) for certain districts?  
>

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: the Parivāra at the first council)
> **Evaṃ bhikkhunīvibhaṅgaṃ saṅgahaṃ āropetvā eteneva upāyena khandhakaparivārepi āropesuṃ. Evametaṃ saubhatovibhaṅgakhandhakaparivāraṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṅgahamārūḷhaṃ sabbaṃ mahākassapatthero pucchi, upālitthero vissajjesi.**  
> Having thus redacted the Bhikkhunīvibhaṅga, they also redacted the Khandhakas and the Parivāra in this same manner. In this way, the Elder Mahākassapa questioned and the Elder Upāli answered regarding the entire Vinaya Pitaka, which includes both Vibhaṅgas, the Khandhakas, and the Parivāra (saubhatovibhaṅgakhandhakaparivāraṃ), all of which was included in the collection.  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Pārājikakaṇḍa-aṭṭhakathā, Bāhiranidānakathā, Sp-i 111) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_01_01&page_number=12&name=p%C4%81r%C4%81jikaka%E1%B9%87%E1%B8%8Daa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=RXZh4bmDIGJoaWtraHVuxKt2aWJoYeG5hWdh4bmDIHNh4bmFZ2FoYeG5gyDEgXJvcGV0dsSBIGV0ZW5ldmEgdXDEgXllbmE%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: sutta, vinaya, anuloma)
> **Sutte vinaye vātiādīsu suttaṃ nāma ubhatovibhaṅgo. Vinayo nāma khandhako. Anulomo nāma parivāro. Paññattaṃ nāma sakalaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ.**  
> In the phrases “in Sutta, in Vinaya,” and so on, “Sutta” is the two-fold analysis. “Vinaya” is the Khandhaka. “Conformity” is the Parivāra. “Laid down” is the entire Vinaya Piṭaka.  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Parivāra-aṭṭhakathā, Sp-v 302) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_05&page_number=200&name=pariv%C4%81raa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=U3V0dGUgdmluYXllIHbEgXRpxIFkxKtzdSBzdXR0YeG5gyBuxIFtYSB1YmhhdG92aWJoYeG5hWdvLg%3D%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: the opening verses of the Parivāra-aṭṭhakathā)
> **Visuddhaparivārassa, parivāroti sāsane; Dhammakkhandhasarīrassa, khandhakānaṃ anantarā. Saṅgahaṃ yo samāruḷho, tassa pubbāgataṃ nayaṃ; Hitvā dāni karissāmi, anuttānatthavaṇṇanaṃ.**  
> The Parivāra—which belongs to the teaching of the Blessed One who is pure and has an Arahant as his attendant—is a supplementary rule. It is immediately after the divisions of the Blessed One, whose body is the collection of the Dhamma. I shall now explain the meaning of that (Parivāra), which has been elevated into a compilation, by the method that went before. Having abandoned (the old way), I will now perform the explanation of the meaning that was not previously clear.  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Parivāra-aṭṭhakathā, opening verses) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_05&page_number=137&name=pariv%C4%81raa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=VmlzdWRkaGFwYXJpdsSBcmFzc2EsIHBhcml2xIFyb3RpIHPEgXNhbmU7)

### Quote (Ṭīkā: the sixteen sections)
> **Soḷasaparivārāti soḷasahi parivārehi upalakkhitattā soḷasaparivārāti vuttaṃ. Tathā hi parivārapāḷiyaṃ “yaṃ tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena paṭhamaṃ pārājikaṃ kattha paññattan” tiādinā (pari.1) paññattivāro, tato paraṃ “methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevanto kati āpattiyo āpajjatī” tiādinā (pari.157) katāpattivāro,“methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevantassa āpattiyo catunnaṃ vipattīnaṃ kati vipattiyo bhajantī” tiādippabhedo (pari.182) vipattivāro,“methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevantassa āpattiyo sattannaṃ āpattikkhandhānaṃ katihi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā” tiādippabhedo (pari.183) saṅgahavāro,“methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevantassa āpattiyo channaṃ āpattisamuṭṭhānānaṃ katihi samuṭṭhānehi samuṭṭhantī” tiādinā (pari.184) samuṭṭhānavāro,“methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevantassa āpattiyo catunnaṃ adhikaraṇānaṃ katamaṃ adhikaraṇan” tiādinā (pari.185) adhikaraṇavāro,“methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevantassa āpattiyo sattannaṃ samathānaṃ katihi samathehi sammantī” tiādippabhedo (pari.186) samathavāro, tadanantaraṃ samuccayavāro cāti aṭṭha vārā vuttā.**  
> “Sixteen Parivāras” is so called because it is characterized by sixteen Parivāras. For, in the Parivāra text, eight chapters (vāra) are stated, beginning with the Paññattivāra, defined by “Where was the first Pārājika prescribed by that Blessed One?”; the Katāpattivāra, defined by “How many offenses does one commit when consuming sexual intercourse?”; the Vipattivāra, defined by the classification of failures such as “How many of the four failures does sexual intercourse incur?”; the Saṅgahavāra, defined by categories such as “In how many of the seven classes of offense are the offenses of sexual intercourse included?”; the Samuṭṭhānavāra, defined by “By how many origins do the offenses of sexual intercourse arise?”; the Adhikaraṇavāra, defined by “Which of the four legal issues is the offense of sexual intercourse?”; the Samathavāra, defined by categories such as “By how many methods of settlement are the offenses of sexual intercourse settled?”; and following that, the Samuccayavāra.  
>  
> — Sāratthadīpanī (Pārājika-ṭīkā, Sp-t-i-a 250) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=tika_vi_01&page_number=67&name=s%C4%81ratthad%C4%ABpan%C4%AB%E1%B9%AD%C4%ABk%C4%81+%28pa%29&note=&selected_text=U2%2FhuLdhc2FwYXJpdsSBcsSBdGkgc2%2FhuLdhc2FoaSBwYXJpdsSBcmVoaSB1cGFsYWtraGl0YXR0xIEgc2%2FhuLdhc2FwYXJpdsSBcsSBdGkgdnV0dGHhuYMu)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: the opening question explained)
> **Tattha yaṃ tena bhagavatā … pe … paññattanti ādinayappavattāya tāva pucchāya ayaṃ saṅkhepattho – yo so bhagavā sāsanassa ciraṭṭhitikatthaṃ dhammasenāpatinā saddhammagāravabahumānavegasamussitaṃ añjaliṃ sirasmiṃ patiṭṭhāpetvā yācito dasa atthavase paṭicca vinayapaññattiṃ paññapesi, tena bhagavatā tassa tassa sikkhāpadassa paññattikālaṃ jānatā, tassā tassā sikkhāpadapaññattiyā dasa atthavase passatā;**  
> 1. Therein, this is the brief meaning for the inquiry that proceeds by such method as: 'Where was the first offence involving Defeat laid down by that Blessed One…?' The Blessed One—who, for the sake of the long-standing stability of the dispensation, having been entreated by the Commander of the Dhamma (Sāriputta) who had placed his hands—the gesture raised by the force of reverence and great respect for the true Dhamma—upon his head, laid down the Vinaya rules after focusing on ten reasons—knowing the time of laying down for each of those training rules, seeing the ten reasons for the laying down of each of those training rules;  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Parivāra-aṭṭhakathā, Soḷasamahāvāra, Paññattivāravaṇṇanā, Sp-v 10) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_05&page_number=137&name=pariv%C4%81raa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=MS4gVGF0dGhhIHlh4bmDIHRlbmEgYmhhZ2F2YXTEgSDigKY%3D)
