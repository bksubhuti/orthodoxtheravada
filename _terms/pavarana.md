### Grammatical Analysis
**Pavāraṇā**: [f.] Invitation; the ceremony at the end of the rains retreat. From *pavāreti* (to invite, offer, give choice).

### Orthodox Definition
*Pavāraṇā* is a formal [Saṅgha](/glossary/sangha/) ceremony held at the end of the Rains Retreat (*[Vassa](/glossary/vassa/)*). The Buddha allowed it after a group of monks in Kosala, who wished to spend the rains "in harmony, on friendly terms and without quarrelling", had agreed to keep silence; he rebuked this as the "silence-practice of other sects" (*mūgabbata*, Vin-iii 1230-1231). He then said: "I allow monks who have kept the rains to invite in regard to three matters: what has been seen, heard or suspected. That will be for you a being agreeable to one another, a removal of offences, an aiming at the discipline (*vinayapurekkhāratā*)" (Vin-iii 1231).

Each monk therefore "invites" (*pavāreti*) the Saṅgha to speak to him about any fault seen, heard or suspected, "out of compassion", and he promises to make amends on seeing it. The Aṭṭhakathā says the day is either the fourteenth or the fifteenth of the fortnight, and the monk determines it so: "Today is my Pavāraṇā, the fourteenth" or "the fifteenth" (Sp-iii 569); it also mentions the Pavāraṇā of the Kattika four-month period (Sp-iii 575).

The formula for a junior monk is: *"Saṅghaṃ, bhante, pavāremi diṭṭhena vā sutena vā parisaṅkāya vā. Vadantu maṃ āyasmanto anukampaṃ upādāya. Passanto paṭikarissāmi."* ("Venerable sirs, I invite the Saṅgha in respect of what has been seen, heard or suspected. Let the venerable ones speak to me out of compassion. Seeing it, I will make amends"). An elder uses "*āvuso*" in place of "*bhante*" (Vin-iii 1233).

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, the allowance)
> **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vassaṃvuṭṭhānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ tīhi ṭhānehi pavāretuṃ – diṭṭhena vā sutena vā parisaṅkāya vā. Sā vo bhavissati aññamaññānulomatā āpattivuṭṭhānatā vinayapurekkhāratā. Evañca pana, bhikkhave, pavāretabbaṃ.**  
> I allow, monks, monks who have kept the rains to ‘invite’ in regard to three matters: what has been seen or heard or suspected. That will be what is suitable for you in regard to one another, a removal of offences, an aiming at the discipline. And thus, monks, should one invite.  
>

### Quote (Mūla: Mahāvagga, the motion and the formula)
> **“Suṇātu me, bhante, saṅgho. Ajja pavāraṇā. Yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, saṅgho pavāreyyā” ti. Navakena bhikkhunā ekaṃsaṃ uttarāsaṅgaṃ karitvā ukkuṭikaṃ nisīditvā añjaliṃ paggahetvā evamassa vacanīyo – “saṅghaṃ, bhante, pavāremi diṭṭhena vā sutena vā parisaṅkāya vā. Vadantu maṃ āyasmanto anukampaṃ upādāya. Passanto paṭikarissāmi.**  
> “Honoured sirs, let the Order listen to me. Today is an Invitation day. If it seems right to the Order, the Order may invite.” A newly ordained monk, having arranged his upper robe over one shoulder, having sat down on his haunches, having saluted with joined palms, should speak to it thus: “Honoured sirs, I invite the Order in respect of what has been seen or heard or suspected. Let the venerable ones speak to me out of compassion, and seeing I will make amends. And seeing I will make amends.  
>  
> — [Mahāvagga](/glossary/mahavagga/) (Pavāraṇākkhandhaka, 210) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=mula_vi_03&page_number=223&name=mah%C4%81vaggap%C4%81%E1%B8%B7i&note=&selected_text=MjEwLuKAmOKAmCBTdeG5h8SBdHUgbWUsIGJoYW50ZSwgc2HhuYVnaG8u)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā)
> **Ajja me pavāraṇāti ettha sace cātuddasikā hoti,“ajja me pavāraṇā cātuddasī” ti sace pannarasikā “ajja me pavāraṇā pannarasī” ti evaṃ adhiṭṭhātabbaṃ.**  
> 218. Regarding the phrase “Today is an Invitation day for me,” if it is a fourteenth-day, one should determine: “Today is my fourteenth-day Invitation,” and if it is a fifteenth-day, “Today is my fifteenth-day Invitation.”  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Pavāraṇākkhandhakavaṇṇanā, 218) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_03&page_number=358&name=mah%C4%81vaggaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=MjE4LiBBamphIG1lIHBhdsSBcmHhuYfEgXRpIGV0dGhhIHNhY2U%3D)

### Quote (Aṭṭhakathā: Samantapāsādikā)
> **Kattikacātumāsiniyā pavāraṇāya pana sace paṭhamaṃ vassūpagatehi mahāpavāraṇāya pavāritehi pacchā upagatā adhikatarā vā samasamā vā honti, pavāraṇāñattiṃ ṭhapetvā pavāretabbaṃ.**  
> On the Invitation day of the Kārttika-four-month period, if those who arrived later are more numerous or equal to those who arrived in the first period and invited on the Great Invitation day, they should invite having set the Invitation motion.  
>  
> — [Samantapāsādikā](/glossary/samantapasadika/) (Pavāraṇākkhandhakavaṇṇanā) · [TPR](https://tpr.pali.tools/open?book_id=attha_vi_03&page_number=359&name=mah%C4%81vaggaa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhakath%C4%81&note=&selected_text=S2F0dGlrYWPEgXR1bcSBc2luaXnEgSBwYXbEgXJh4bmHxIF5YSBwYW5hIHNhY2UgcGHhua1oYW1h4bmDIHZhc3PFq3BhZ2F0ZWhp)
