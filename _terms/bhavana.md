### Grammatical Analysis
**Bhāvanā**: [f.] mental cultivation; development; meditation. Derived from the causative root of *bhū* (to be, become), meaning "causing to exist," "producing," or "cultivating."

### Orthodox Definition
*Bhāvanā* is the Theravāda term for what the modern world calls "meditation," but its orthodox meaning is far more active and structural. It does not mean emptying the mind or passively relaxing; it means the deliberate, energetic cultivation and multiplication of wholesome mental factors (*kusala-cetasika*).

It operates structurally across two distinct systems:
1. *[Samatha](/glossary/samatha/)-bhāvanā* (Cultivation of Tranquility): Developing concentration (*[samādhi](/glossary/samadhi/)*) to suppress the hindrances and achieve absorption (*[jhāna](/glossary/jhana/)*).
2. *[Vipassanā](/glossary/vipassana/)-bhāvanā* (Cultivation of Insight): Developing wisdom (*[paññā](/glossary/panna/)*) to penetrate the three characteristics of reality (*[anicca](/glossary/anicca/)*, *[dukkha](/glossary/dukkha/)*, *[anattā](/glossary/anatta/)*) and uproot defilements permanently.

As a basis of merit, *bhāvanā* is one of the three bases of meritorious action (*dānamayaṃ, sīlamayaṃ, bhāvanāmayaṃ puññakiriyavatthu*, A-viii 418:3). The two things to be developed are tranquility and insight (*samatho ca vipassanā ca*, Paṭis 179:2).

### Quote
> **“Bhāvanaṃ anuyuttassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno viharato kiñcāpi na evaṃ icchā uppajjeyya – ‘ aho vata me anupādāya āsavehi cittaṃ vimucceyyā ’ ti, atha khvassa anupādāya āsavehi cittaṃ vimuccati. Taṃ kissa hetu? ‘ Bhāvitattā ’ tissa vacanīyaṃ. Kissa bhāvitattā? Catunnaṃ satipaṭṭhānānaṃ, catunnaṃ sammappadhānānaṃ, catunnaṃ iddhipādānaṃ, pañcannaṃ indriyānaṃ, pañcannaṃ balānaṃ, sattannaṃ bojjhaṅgānaṃ, ariyassa aṭṭhaṅgikassa maggassa.**  
> “When a mendicant is committed to development, living thus, even if such a wish does not arise for them: 'Oh, if only my mind were freed from the defilements without clinging!' even so, their mind is freed from the defilements without clinging. Why is that? It should be said: 'Because they are developed.' Developed in what? Of the four establishments of mindfulness, the four right efforts, the four bases of psychic power, the five faculties, the five powers, the seven factors of enlightenment, and the noble eight-factored path.  
>

### Textual References
* **[Sutta](/glossary/sutta/)**: *Vitakkasaṇṭhāna Sutta* (MN 20) – Providing active, vigorous techniques for cultivating wholesome thoughts to crowd out unwholesome ones.
* **Canonical**: *Bhāvanā Sutta* (AN 7.71) – Comparing mental cultivation to a hen sitting on her eggs; if she incubates them properly, the chicks will hatch regardless of whether she wishes for it or not.
* **Commentary**: *[Visuddhimagga](/glossary/visuddhimagga/)* (Chapter III) – The foundational chapter opening the massive treatise on mental development.
