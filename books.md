### Ajahn Sujin Boriharnwanaket Reference Library

* **A Survey of Paramattha Dhammas**  
    A comprehensive and concise account of the Buddha's teachings on ultimate realities.  
    [Download PDF](https://kh.dhammahome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/A-Survey-of-Paramattha-Dhammas.pdf)

* **The Perfections Leading to Enlightenment**  
    An exploration of the *pāramīs* and their development in daily life.  
    [View Books / Download PDFs](https://www.dhammahome.com/book-en/topic/15)

* **Taking Refuge in Buddhism**  
    Focuses on building true faith through the understanding of the Dhamma.  
    [Download PDF](https://kh.dhammahome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Taking-Refuge-in-BUDDHISM.pdf)

* **Mettā: Loving-kindness in Buddhism**  
    A direct approach to the practical application of *mettā* as a reality, not just words.  
    [Download PDF](https://archive.org/download/buddhism-engl/BUDDHISM%20ENGL.rar/BUDDHISM%20ENGL%2FNOVICE%2FOKEY%2FNINA%20VAN%20GOORKOM%2FNew%20folder%2FNina_van_Gorkom-Loving_Kindness.pdf)

* **Realities and Concepts**  
    Extracted from *A Survey of Paramattha Dhammas*, focusing on the distinction between truth and naming.  
    [View Books / Download PDFs](https://www.dhammahome.com/book-en/topic/15)

* **Buddhism in Daily Life** *(Ajahn Sujin)*  
    Practical guidance on seeing *dhammas* in ordinary experience rather than special postures.  
    [View Books / Download PDFs](https://www.dhammahome.com/book-en/topic/15)

### Nina van Gorkom Reference Library

* **Abhidhamma in Daily Life**  
    An essential introduction to the Abhidhamma, defining absolute realities and encouraging their recognition in ordinary experience to eradicate wrong views.  
    [View / Download PDF](https://www.dhammahome.com/book_en/topic6-Abhidhamma-in-Daily-Life.html)

* **Cetasikas**  
    A detailed study of the 52 mental factors (*cetasikas*) that accompany the mind, helping the reader identify defilements and develop wholesome qualities.  
    [View / Download PDF](https://www.dhammahome.com/book-en/topic/20)

* **Buddhism in Daily Life** *(Nina van Gorkom)*  
    Written in Thailand, this book explores how the Buddha's teachings on generosity, sincerity, and tolerance are lived in every moment.  
    [View / Download PDF](https://www.dhammahome.com/book-en/topic/14)
